**2. The prompt's ending list stood against the page in six places and carried one figure of fact wrong.** The band file's `closing_images` had been corrected in the writer's second pass and the prompt had not, so the file a writer reads first printed a straight edge of broken concrete at 0826 where the chapter ends on a square hole with air in it, a stack leaning true at 0827 where the chapter ends on ivy hanging off the top rail with its bottom six inches in the air, bands lying on top of the boards at 0828 where he put them back flat in the sand, a bearer at the height of the bar at 0830, a window frame on a stack at 0831 where the chapter ends on the opening, and **six feet of privet stub at 0832 where the chapter and every state block say four**. **The prompt's list and its three-word line now read as the page does, and the prompt says in place that they were corrected after the prose and where the first wordings are kept.** Same for the four-ends-left-behind sentence higher up, which still said the bands lay on top of the boards and that 0831 ended on the mend.

**3. Card 0830's stop was the opposite of what the chapter does.** The card said *the height of the bearer, which is set by the length of the bars and by nothing else*. The chapter has the reach between the two cross timbers set by the length of the longer bar and by nothing else, and the height of the rack set by her hands and by nothing else — which is both what the page says and what a rack is. **The card now reads as the chapter reads in both the prompt and the band file; the band file keeps the first wording beside it and the prompt's banner names the change and says where that record is.** The card's second half was wrong in the same way: it called the bar she stood on in 0795 *the first bar*, when on the page that bar is a third length lying over by the far wall and neither of the two on the rack is it. That ambiguity was already fixed in the prose; the plan was not fixed with it. **The prose was not touched, on the 0063 review's ground: the chapter is right and the record was wrong.**

**4. 0825's closing arithmetic did not close.** Sixteen stones off six feet of run at the width of a brick on end is right, eleven good and five bad is right, and **the eleven are all whole bricks, because a half fails on the arris test in the same paragraph that explains why, and eleven whole bricks at four and a half inches come to four foot one and a half inches and not four feet and a half.** The chapter said *about four feet and a half*, which overstates the shortfall the paragraph exists to point at. **It now says the eleven are whole bricks and come to four foot and an inch and a half.** This is exactly the class of error no measure in this directory can see.

**5. 0832's repair had left the same error standing in new wording.** The old text put the four posts in *a bed she had just taken the turf off* — the turf came off the front bed in 0829 and the privet is on the side wall — and it was replaced with *a bed two foot wide against a wall she has not dug at this end of*, which is not a thing a wall is, and the next paragraph still read *the front wall of that side bed*, so the false geometry stood. **The posts are now in a bed two foot wide against the side wall in ground not opened this year, and the bed runs down to the low spot at the door end where the water goes.**

**6. The second person was being reported, not acted.** In four places the prose quoted the block's own visitor rule instead of showing what the visitor did: *she does not pick one up, and she does not look at the passage twice* (0825), *she does not touch a board and does not ask about them* (0828), *is not there for the frame and does not look at it twice* (0831), and *no two hands on anything* (0831), with *there is nothing asked about the passage from either of them* (0825) and *she does not offer and nothing is asked of her* (0832) in the same class. **All six are now action.** The sister at twelve keeps a hand on the gate with her bag hooked over her arm, looks out at the road, and shifts her weight when the near wheel finds the edge of a flag. The woman from two doors down stands with her back against the cabin and comes out of his way each time he goes past with an end in his hands. The woman with the bucket is gone up the path before the architrave is off the wall, and the hour with two people in that yard now ends with a bucket going out of the gate. The rule is now a rule in the plan and not a sentence in the prose, which is where it belongs.

**7. The band file's own claim about arithmetic was overstated.** It said *nothing is added up, nothing is priced, nothing is counted and no number is asked to agree with another*. The page counts sixteen stones, eleven and five, four foot one and a half, thirty bricks with nineteen ringing, six feet and about five of bar, four feet apart, a foot overhanging at each end. **The claim is corrected in the file, the first wording kept, and what the rule actually forbids is set out plainly: no figure-mismatch spread, no telephone, no sum worked out to reveal something, and no number asked to agree with a number in another chapter.** Counting physical things in one place and letting the count carry the chapter is the trade, and a ban read literally would have produced a chapter that avoided counting.
