## Findings, and all four are in the next prompt and not in the chapters

**1. Two of the ten cards re-break the prohibition the same prompt sets.** The 0060 prompt says *every job is dry work* and *a card that cannot be written without a cement bed must be rewritten before it is used, because 0059 had to leave one and it is still open for a human* — and then card 0786 takes the frost wall *down to the footings* and rebuilds it *with a footing course*, and card 0787 sets the post *in concrete*. Three wet jobs would have arrived where one flag is standing, and the flag this phase raised would have been buried instead of reaching a human. **Both cards are now dry** — 0786's wall is packed with broken wall and chippings, trodden in layers, and built back up with every perpend packed and the coping bedded on it, and 0787's post is packed with broken stone and hardcore and pinned plumb with two hardwood wedges cut off flush — and in both cards the fault in the person, which is the family's whole content, is left standing exactly where it was.

**2. A third card breaks the same prompt's ground rule.** 0791 put the sister *on the roof* and had her fit a cowl, against *every job is done from the ground*, against *the ladder is not opened*, and against a flue whose head this block has no lawful way to reach. **The sweep is now done at the hearth end on the floor** with a rod, a coil of rope and a flue brush, no roof, no ladder, no cowl, and the fault in the person — the stove lit in the first cold week with the smoke coming back into the room — is unchanged.

**3. The rotation paragraph contradicted itself in one clause.** It read *the lock-up carries late forties and not the band the lock-up carried in 0059, which was also late forties*, so the sentence cancelled itself. The band belongs to the person and not to the position, and the lock-up is a woman in her late forties here as she was in 0776 and 0781. **The clause is gone and the three orders it claims to differ from are now printed** — 755–764 ran twelve, unit, lock-up, nine, ten; 765–774 ran lock-up, ten, twelve, nine, unit; 775–784 ran nine, lock-up, ten, unit, twelve; this block runs lock-up, twelve, unit, nine, ten — so the claim can be checked instead of believed.

**4. One card stands on a job the state files call unfinished.** Card 0787's good repair put the wire back on the strap side, and its fault in the person was the owner hooking the latch back over the gate within a month because the strap and the hinge were on the same side again — which needs the staple on the gate frame, and the staple is not done and will not be done until April, because the man from the cottage said so, and April is not on this page. **The card now works the post, the wire and the packing and nothing else, and its fault in the person is his own.** He stops leaving the gate standing where the frost left it and shuts it hard every time he comes through it, and by the spring the post is going over at the height of his hip again, because of what he did to it and not because of what she did. The staple is named as closed in three places in the prompt and no card may stand on it.
