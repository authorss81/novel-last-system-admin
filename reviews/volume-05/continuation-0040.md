**1 — `chapter-0587.md` stages a day after its own dateline. The most serious finding in this repository since 0038, and NOT repaired.** Dated Wednesday the nineteenth of August 2020; the body puts the writing and posting of its letter on *the second Wednesday she had been gone*, which is the twenty-sixth. **A letter posted at a last collection of half past four on a Wednesday cannot come on the Wednesday** — it comes on the Thursday. The only self-consistent reading posts it on Tuesday the twenty-fifth and delivers it Wednesday the twenty-sixth, **and the card it was written from asked for nine days from the Sunday she said it, and the sixteenth plus nine is the twenty-fifth, which is a Tuesday. Dateline, body and card are three figures and cannot all be true.**
