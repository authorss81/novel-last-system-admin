## The six findings

**1 — The volume has swallowed four planned volumes. Real, and a human's. Not repaired.** `outline/series.md` gives Volume 05 as 193–240; `chapters/volume-05/` holds 195–424, which is 230 chapters against 48, and the on-page block now contains the whole of the ranges the outline assigns to Volumes 06, 07 and 08. **Fixing this by moving files or naming a boundary is a re-cut of the plan, so nothing was moved and no outline was touched.** The block's own framing of it as a bookkeeping nuisance is fair enough as a description of what a writer may do about it and is not an answer. Nineteen batches have now carried this flag. It is the review's judgement that it must be settled, and the review is right, and it is still not a writer's to settle.

**2 — The male lead has been off the page for 161 chapters. Real, and a human's. Not repaired.** Verified independently: **Jonas Mercer's last appearance in Volume 05 is 0263**; the last appearance of any outline-named character is **0294 (Nell Ardent)**; and chapters 0300–0424 — one hundred and twenty-five chapters — carry no human personal name at all, only place names and the four tags. The bible's named figures were each checked in the chapter files and none appears in 0300–0424. **One thing was checked rather than assumed and is not a discrepancy: Evan Mercer is the missing father and the subject of the central mystery, and he is not the lead.** Returning a named character is a plot decision, so nothing was returned, no reunion was invented, and no name was added to any chapter. The finding is now stated at the head of the 0024 prompt, where a writer will meet it before writing rather than in a review after.

**3 — `fortnight` was printed at two, in 0422 and 0424. Real, and wrong in both halves. Repaired in the state file; the chapters were not touched.** The true count across the ten chapters is **six** — 0415, 0416, 0418, 0420 and 0422 twice — and **0424 contains none at all**, so the printed pair was wrong in its count and in five chapters out of ten in its location. The half of the line that stood is that cards 0422 and 0424 are the two cards that ask for one, and that half now sits beside the corrected figure.
**Two of this finding's own claims are withdrawn.** The review says the wrong pair is "repeated in `state/current.md` … and the batch summary". It is not: the wrong pair appears in **one place**, `state/continuity.md` §13. The `current.md` sentence quoted, *none of the three was raised in a fortnight by anybody*, is narrative prose about three faults on a parade and is not a figure claim, and the 0023 block of `state/batch-summaries.md` contains no fortnight figure at all. Both were checked for the phrase and neither carries it.
**And the count exposed a card that was not met.** Card 0424 prints *the same finding it was a fortnight ago* twice and 0424 uses the word not once, carrying the substance of the card in other terms instead. **That is now flagged at §12D item 4 and no word was put into the chapter**, because 0424 was cut back and rewritten from nothing at §12C item 4 and a word added after the fact would be the third version of a chapter already twice found faulty with. A human may rule that a card figure bears a word and not only a shape; a writer did not.

**4 — Repetition the account's own detector cannot see. Real, and larger than the review stated. Repaired in the chapters.** The review's two figures reproduced exactly — eleven shared nine-token spans between 0416 and 0421 and nine between 0415 and 0417 — and measuring the ten datelines **as a set of their own** gives **37 shared spans across ten pairs** before the repair. The cause is named at §12A item eight: a dateline is one of the method's three excluded classes, which is right for a date and a place and wrong for the frame this account builds around a date. **That is why all six runs came out nil while every chapter in the batch opened on the same construction, and it is the most useful thing in this review.** It is now run seven at §13A, at 37 raw and nil after, and it is carried into the 0024 prompt as a rule for a writer rather than as a figure for a filing clerk.
The repairs were five dateline frames (0415, 0417, 0419, 0421, 0422), which broke the shape without moving a date, a clock time or a place; the closing construction *AND NOBODY HAS ———*, which stood in five of the ten chapters, reworded in three of them so that two remain; and one genuine cluster of filler in 0418, where *thought about* appeared five times in three consecutive paragraphs and now appears once. Cross-chapter nine-token reuse across all ten chapters is **nil without the dateline exclusion**, and so is run seven.
**Ruled on and not applied: the densities.** `nobody` and `about` are real, and `about` is at about 19 per thousand words. Almost all of it is the account's hedged measurement — *about thirty foot*, *about half past six* — which is load-bearing for every figure this volume refuses to state exactly, and cutting it would break the voice rather than a fault. Only the mechanical uses, the prepositional *about*, were touched. A volume whose subject is that nobody is asked anything is not going to have a low count of *nobody*, and driving it lower would be a costume, not a repair. This is the review's item 4 and it is **partly right**, and the part that is right has been repaired.

**5 — State files are growing without bound. Partly real, and the review's own figure is wrong. Partly repaired.** The five superseded editions of the header paragraph in `state/current.md` total **4,483 characters, about 4.4 KB**, not the fifteen-odd kilobytes claimed; three of the five are already bare marker lines and the 0009 body had already been reduced to a 492-character correction note by an earlier pass. The real fault in them was duplication, not retention: a standalone 0022 marker sitting above a body that already carried its own marker, and the 0021 marker appearing twice. **Those two lines were removed and nothing else was.** Every superseded edition still has a marker, every body survives, and `PHASE_SYSTEM.md` prescribes archiving and indexing over deletion, which is the rule this followed.
**Ruled on and not repaired: the method apparatus.** §12 through §12E of the block is roughly 9 KB of detector documentation rewritten from scratch every batch, and it is now a larger share of the hand-over than the plot is. That is a true and serious observation about where this series has spent eighteen batches. It is also not a repair pass's to change, because the apparatus is what caught upwards of two hundred and eighty real reuses in this batch alone, and rewriting the method mid-series would invalidate the figures every earlier block stands on. It goes to a human with finding 1.

**6 — Minor. Repaired.** 0415's *she has not been up that street since except in the August* was a sudden jump to a future tense in a chapter dated 29 May. The retrospective reach is the account's own and is used in 0418, 0420 and 0423, so the fact stayed; the sentence was put on the same footing as its neighbours.
**Not repaired: the review's note that `reviews/volume-05/` has no file for 0021 or 0022.** Both are genuinely absent, and a review cannot be written for a batch that was not read. The log for this phase is the only one in the repository, and it is not a licence to compose two others.
