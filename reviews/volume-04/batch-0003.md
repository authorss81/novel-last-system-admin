### Three further defects found while repairing, not in the review

- The Batch 0004 prompt gave the blank line under the nineteenth as **248 days on 31 July 2015**. Recomputed from Wednesday 26 November 2014 it is **247**. Corrected in the prompt, with the arithmetic left visible.
- **Chapter 0174 misstated a canon figure.** The laundry round of 21 May was *granted in about four seconds*; it was granted in **about eleven seconds**, and that is where the figure entered the account — the woman who granted it worked the cost out loud in about four seconds *before* she said yes, then said yes in about eleven. 0174 had taken the first figure and used it for the second. **Cut from 0174 rather than corrected to *eleven seconds*, because *about eleven seconds* is the most protected figure in the volume and a recap is the wrong place to spend it twice.**
- **The Batch 0004 prompt told the next writer that *in about four seconds* is a one-use callback not to be re-used. The chapters used *four seconds* ten times** — four in 0168, one in 0172, four in 0173, and one in 0174 — **and there are nine of them now, the tenth having been cut by the repair above. The census was rewritten from the chapters.** A binding rule the delivered prose has already broken ten times is worse than no rule.
