## 4. The method, which Volume 03 has to keep

Ask for a state and not for a list. A person may refuse to be counted and the count says so. A duty is an act and not a place. A record with one name in it cannot be argued with. A form is the last thing a person writes and the first thing an institution keeps. **A person may not be required even to be told, and that sentence is the load-bearing wall of everything since 3 April.** Findability is not answerability.
