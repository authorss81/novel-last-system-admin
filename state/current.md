## The Volume 02 mechanism (canon, established in Chapters 50, 51, 54, 57 and carried unchanged through 60–90)

**CivicCore's Schedule 4 to the Saint Orra service agreement of 2019 — continuity pricing. Valid since 1 March 2014 and unread by anybody in this city until 22 April.**

- Paragraph 11's contractual failure definition — a node in failure where its civic term has not been continuously in force for more than twenty-four consecutive hours — is taken up and turned into a market.
- A node in failure is an **unfunded node**. An unfunded node **is not disconnected; it is deferred.**
- A deferred node **serves its last verified essential use, and if none is recorded, the use is the node's maintenance.**
- A node in failure may be placed on a **continuity schedule** by the district it stands in, on payment of a **continuity fee**, at a **tier**. The party holding the standing continuity authorisation may place nodes on a schedule at any time and may take a node off a schedule under its own holding.
- **The order of tiers is set by the fee. A higher tier outranks a lower tier in any precedence question. An unfunded node is outranked by every node that is not.**
- **A tier may be challenged by a service district or by a node in the same tier. It may not be challenged by a person. A person may not be required to do anything and may not be required even to be told.**
- **Fees, 2014–15, per node: Tier 1 £1,880 · Tier 2 £4,200 · Tier 3 £11,400.**
- **A claim may be assigned, in whole or in shares, to a party having authority (Schedule 4, page 219). An assignment does not create a new claim or a new reference. The assignee takes the claim, and takes what is owed on the claim, and takes the duty of the claim. A share of a claim carries the tier in proportion and is enforceable against the assigning party in respect of the tier only.**
- **Participation-agreement clause 4(c):** a signature on a participation agreement is a consent mark and **may not be withdrawn except on the same date next year.**
- The Lattice **cannot publish a narrowing.** The city can, on its own paper.
