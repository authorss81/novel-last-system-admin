## The Volume 02 mechanism (new canon, established in Chapters 50, 51, 54, 57)

**CivicCore's Schedule 4 to the Saint Orra service agreement of 2019 — continuity pricing. Valid since 1 March 2014 and unread by anybody in this city until 22 April.**

- Paragraph 11's contractual failure definition — a node in failure where its civic term has not been continuously in force for more than twenty-four consecutive hours — is taken up and turned into a market.
- A node in failure is an **unfunded node**. An unfunded node **is not disconnected; it is deferred.**
- A deferred node **serves its last verified essential use, and if none is recorded, the use is the node's maintenance.**
- A node in failure may be placed on a **continuity schedule** by the district it stands in, on payment of a **continuity fee**, at a **tier**. The party holding the standing continuity authorisation may place nodes on a schedule **at any time**.
- **The order of tiers is set by the fee. A higher tier outranks a lower tier in any precedence question. An unfunded node is outranked by every node that is not.**
- **A tier may be challenged by a district or by a node in the same tier. It may not be challenged by a person.**
- **Fees, 2014–15, per node: Tier 1 £1,880 · Tier 2 £4,200 · Tier 3 £11,400.**
- **Participation-agreement clause 4(c):** a signature on a participation agreement is a consent mark for the purposes of the Lattice and **may not be withdrawn except on the same date next year.** Consent is graded by date. There is no appeal against a date.
- The Lattice **cannot publish a narrowing** — the drawing layer draws a shape, a term, a date and a claim, and has never drawn what a thing stopped doing. **The city can, on its own paper.**
