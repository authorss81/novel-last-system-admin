# Chapter Summaries

## Volume 01 — The Permission Fault

### Chapter 0001 — The Door That Owes Nothing

Jonas replaces a worn River Stacks relay, then reaches Saint Orra bridge as an ambulance is rejected by conflicting east/west requests. He uses a 25-minute attended bypass rather than a suppression lead, serves the ambulance, and records an unresolved permission conflict. The old I. Vey seal and a downstream west-clinic request establish the service chain; Jonas remains Stage 0 and wants both permanent status and fuller archive clearance.

### Chapter 0002 — A Borrowed Key

At the west clinic, Sanaa needs Alma Reyes admitted while the gate rejects a valid care claim because the night-watch duty is missing from the local record. Ruth Nwosu, the working night steward, holds the physical key. Under an attended escort procedure, Alma enters observation four at 10:56, the route closes at 10:57:41 after 1 minute 41 seconds, and Sanaa makes Jonas record exactly what remains unverified. A separate municipal office order suspends Jonas’s independent gate use pending local duty review; it is not a second Lattice notice. Ruth’s duty is established here as ending at the 11:00 morning-watch handover. No civic term is mended.

### Chapter 0003 — The Clause in the Wall

Oren Pell closes the bridge under a valid public-safety order but invokes the separately inspected Assembly Emergency Service Test for one ambulance lane. Jonas completes the physical relay reset; afterward, the limited Fault-reader view shows the obsolete empty-crossing term linked to the ambulance conflict, and he names the fault class for the first time — a seam. Sensory bleed forces him to anchor on Nora Bell and the plate. The 90-minute lane test closes successfully but incompletely, and a separate unlisted shuttle arrives.

### Chapter 0004 — The Unlisted Stop

The green shuttle has no transit record, but it carries a child with a hand-marked token, courier Pia Santos, Alma Reyes after a worsening return journey, and an older driver who remains with the vehicle. Jonas refuses to treat the route as a rogue bridge effect or impound the vehicle while the patient deteriorates. A clinic clerk dispositions the child and patient while Pia remains with the vehicle; Jonas signs only a vehicle safety check, and the driver retains custody. The patient returns toward the old-canal shelter, where the door asks for a post-arrival duty.

### Chapter 0005 — The Price of a Door

Selah Mora refuses Jonas’s convenient offer to put his own name on an undefined shelter claim. Rafi, Mina Okafor, and Selah instead name a bounded escort: room, medication, follow-up, and closing time. Alma returns to the shelter, the route closes after a complete handoff, and Jonas recognizes the temptation to turn responsibility into control. A valid dawn closure order then forces the shelter’s residents to seek another address.

### Chapter 0006 — The Registrar’s Rule

Oren’s order is valid: the old building’s wall, canopy, and water supply are unsafe. Jonas refuses to take an inspection he is not qualified for and helps Selah move residents, possessions, and keys to Bellow Market under Mara Holt’s care. Alma and her medication reach the receiving shelter, the building is sealed, and Tomas rejects Jonas’s urge to sign a blank personal guarantee. Oren schedules the separate pump inspection.

### Chapter 0007 — No One Signed the Map

Emeka Rusk demonstrates that a real shared-maintenance duty survives without a current municipal term. Jonas separates the physical leak from the missing term but cannot write one into the other. Under pressure from an official headcount request, he refuses to trade resident identities for a route record; Vann resolves the emergency tank by metered consumption instead, so the refusal costs nothing. The pump remains closed, bottled water and a clinic tank are arranged, and management orders the bridge incident misclassified as hardware-only.

### Chapter 0008 — The Man Who Filed a Fault

Jonas identifies the I. Vey seal as part of the original Continuity Project maintenance series and finds a partial lowland service code matching hardware in Evan’s family file. Selah keeps the complete route and identities in a sealed local log; the NDC receives only a redacted custody reference with consent, purpose, and expiry limits. Tomas warns against a continuity waiver. Jonas files an unresolved permission conflict, leaving his appointment pending.

### Chapter 0009 — The First Night Shift

A clinic/shelter hinge makes the urgent patient route and Omar Sadiq’s nonurgent shelter return compete. The local review leaves Jonas’s gate-use suspension active but allows attended maintenance only after Ruth’s named watch initiates the route, and only while that watch stays in place. Jonas refuses to create a cross-node term; the urgent transfer crosses, while Omar waits exactly 43 minutes and then completes by the street route. The shelter route itself opened at 10:16 and closed unused at 10:19 because the vehicle had already been committed. Sanaa makes Jonas disclose the cost, earning limited trust without making her his ally. Tomas then requires every witness log and a visible responsibility bond before any shared-node civic edit.

### Chapter 0010 — The City Needs a Name

Under the west-lane Assembly test, a real ambulance, clinic admission, and Bellow Market shelter-to-clinic handoff run within a 90-minute attended window from 10:00 to 11:30. Ruth Nwosu’s duty ends at 11:00 and no successor exists to take the key, because the local record never carried her as a holder, so she stays to the expiry as a named witness, unpaid, while Mina keeps the paper and corridor from 11:00 and Dr. Sethi’s day team still comes on at noon. At the 9:58 round Selah reads the scheduled clinic run back to Jonas and names that it crosses the failed south span; he prices the taxi alternative at 38 minutes, writes the risk onto the scope, but cannot re-book her local claim and will not spend the shelter’s water money, so he leaves the run in place. When the physically stopped side service gate blocks it at 10:17, he makes Nina record the delay as three causes — the permission conflict, the stopped gate, and his own scope. Unsafe side-hinge pressure leads to formal isolation at 10:31 under Oren’s order. The shelter vehicle and handoff leg end at 10:55, while the formal shelter authorization closes at 11:30 on the test’s expiry rather than the patient’s condition. Jonas records the unresolved conflict instead of accepting a hardware-only report. The Old Ward board reveals an empty central root holder field with a Reclamation Season review line. Tomas blocks the next shared-node attempt until Jonas faces a visible responsibility bond.

## Volume 01 — Chapters 11–20

### Chapter 0011 — The Bond

Tomas Renn puts a seven-clause responsibility bond on a chair in the River Stacks shop and makes Jonas read it aloud, with the Chapter 6 transport record beside it so the difference is visible: the transport record moves a stretcher, the bond lets him write a rule. Jonas first offers a broad guarantee (“any person who requires it… until the underlying fault is resolved”). Bea Osunde asks who decides who requires it and what happens at six o’clock when the watch has gone home. On a decommissioned bench plate the broad promise returns **A promise with no end is not a term. / No bearer named. No expiry. / Nothing to hold**, and nothing happens. Jonas drafts the bond himself, adds clause seven — that it cannot touch his civil-service appointment — and Ruth Nwosu arrives for an hour paid by the shop’s dispute fund, writes her own refusal condition in the margin in block capitals, and signs as witness. The bond is posted on the shop’s yellow board, named to the west clinic care entrance, and accepted rather than approved.

### Chapter 0012 — The Door’s Terms

Ruth initiates the attendance in writing at 21:20, satisfying the narrowed gate-use suspension. The plate carries the engraved **NO UNATTENDED ACCESS — WEST CLINIC CARE ENTRANCE** and a pencilled window, 23:00–06:00, that is six hours short of her real 22:00–11:00 watch. Jonas maps the six parts of a permission on a form and finds four real, one questionable, and the **precedence** box empty. Sanaa lists three clinical needs; Ruth insists urgent only; Mina insists on a definition of verification; Selah Mora, by telephone at 23:38, consents to the return-of-care limb alone, 22:00–06:00, and refuses everything else. Ruth tapes the emergency doors and times a trolley to the ward turn — eleven minutes, blocking the emergency approach — which converts urgency from a judgment into a measurement. A dry run of a real non-urgent claim fails and the plate returns **Scope unclear. Precedence not stated. / Which promise answers is not written down.** The term is written on the back of a shift sheet, signed by all four, and the sixth line — *if no bearer is on duty, the route is held by the issuer* — is the only one the plate will take.

### Chapter 0013 — The First Mended Term

Ruth wakes Jonas at 04:50 and has written a bearer-initiated maintenance request by 05:42. With Ruth on the probe and Sanaa witnessing, Jonas restores **one field** — the precedence — and nearly loses the edit; Ruth, not Mina, calls the stop. The plate prints a narrow strip instead of a verdict, and the strip carries a line nobody discussed: **Window on record: 23:00–06:00. Window declared by the holder: 22:00–11:00**, and a sixth line, **IF NO HOLDER IS ON DUTY: the route is held by the issuer. ISSUER FALLBACK — MERCER, J. AVAILABILITY: acknowledged per shift.** Ruth reads the issuer fallback aloud and calls it a man with a door. Two real transfers succeed at 07:04 and 08:15; the plate correctly refuses a laundry trolley. Ruth hands over at 11:00 without staying a minute, and reminds Jonas that her hours are still wrong.

### Chapter 0014 — The Useful Mistake

The gate works and the crews begin using it; Jonas says yes to the service lift because the schedule does not say no, and checks. Sanaa comes down the ramp and tells him he has built a gate that no longer needs anybody. Tomas refuses a continuity rider that would reclassify the door as equipment, and reports six people who cannot honestly hold it. This time Jonas changes a decision: he moves the Bellow Market clinic run off the isolated south span onto the clinic vehicle and pays forty-one minutes out of the department budget, logged beside the earlier delay. At 21:09 he acknowledges two one-hour gaps in the night window from his kitchen. At 02:40 the next morning Ezra Mbeki moves a fall-risk patient alone because the plate believes the night watch is on duty; **Marta Sowle, ninety-one, goes down in the corridor and is found at 02:53 by a cleaner, eleven minutes later**, and the safeguarding form records the record’s own contribution. Ezra tells Jonas the acknowledgement helped nobody, because the middle of the night was never the problem. Jonas drops a screwdriver, is sent home at 12:50, says “nothing needs me until five,” and sleeps through a transfer arriving at 15:22.

### Chapter 0015 — The Price of an Open Door

**Lorna Vessey, sixty-one**, arrives with a verified claim, an assigned bed, a named receiving nurse and a 15:40 cardiac slot. The plate says **HOLDER NOT NAMED. GATE CLOSED.** Sanaa refuses to call the case an emergency to save the slot, and says why. The slot is released at 15:41; the next available date is the fourteenth. At 15:58 Jonas acknowledges the day shift and opens the door at 16:03 in knowing breach of his suspension, with Mina writing the line in the exact words he gives her. He states three things out loud: the gate worked for him and nobody else; the same page also let a patient be moved alone; and the people who can stop the next use are Ruth, Sanaa and Mina. Sanaa certifies the hardware and writes on the same form that she does not certify the permission. Nerys Vessey says her mother will be told there was a door and it was open and nobody was standing behind it, and insists her mother’s name stays off the public board while the slot stays on it.

### Chapter 0016 — What the Claim Requires

Gunnar Halvorsen, the west site’s service manager, offers a hospital continuity waiver that would have opened the ramp and would let *the issuer or a designated municipal officer* hold any gap. Jonas refuses it in nine words — a category cannot be told no — and has the refusal and the offer minuted. **Tomas names four prerequisites for a real day watch — a payroll line, a paid training afternoon, a clinic-side naming authority, and a written rule for when two people on one rota both refuse — and says the fourth is the question that will kill the plan in a fortnight. It is never answered in this batch.** Ruth refuses to have the night watch erased so the schedule can look clean, and Sanaa produces the patient notice, including the 02:40 event, which the ninety-one-year-old patient wants to read before it goes up. In the records room, Nina surfaces a registrar’s letter asking the department to classify Jonas’s two solo acknowledgements. Nell Ardent arrives as the Vessey family’s lawyer, gives no remedy and one free piece of advice, and refuses to write that the repair was sound. Bea Osunde produces the fourteen-day private rota Jonas wrote and keeps it. He decides to renegotiate the schedule and accept the debt.

### Chapter 0017 — The Existing Bearers

Six people define *available* and none of them agrees. The roster is written: Bram Osei 06:00–11:00, Ezra Mbeki 13:30–22:00, Ruth Nwosu 22:00–11:00, Selah Mora for the return-of-care limb 22:00–06:00 with the line **retainer requested in writing of Saint Orra west: not yet agreed** — she makes Mina read that line aloud and says the words stay until the hospital writes the cheque — and a named, published, unfilled gap from **11:00 to 13:30**. Ruth reads the carried cost on the board and signs the roster before she leaves. Tomas leaves his stop-work instruction and does not ask a second time about his fourth prerequisite — what happens when two people on one rota both refuse — which the roster answers for the worse. Ezra comes on at ten past one, reads the term aloud alone as the page requires, and asks whether the sixth line is still true. Marta Sowle tells Jonas to take the issuer off the door. At 14:00 Jonas makes a **second, separate edit** at the same plate, with Ezra on the probe: the issuer is removed from the fallback and a declared emergency reduction written in by the on-call clinician. The plate prints **Term unchanged. Issuer unchanged. Claim unchanged.** The seam mark extends to below the elbow. At 16:10 the Reclamation Season series 6 maintenance request arrives.

### Chapter 0018 — The First Bearing Trail

Bea opens drawer four. The series 6 chain runs from a River Stacks cabinet through the Saint Orra bridge to the clinic gate and on to **6-L/14 and 6-L/19, “Canal Lowline,” address not indexed**. The code LO/6-L/14 is the Chapter 8 lowland code and matches Evan’s family file; Jonas stops himself from reading the past backwards and says so. Four records, four blanks: the library street index, the water authority’s standpipe map, a school catchment office that needs a reference he cannot obtain. ### Chapter 0019 — Another Map

Pia sets four conditions on the towpath: no names, no photographs, no promises without local agreement, no use of the sealed log, and he leaves the tool roll at the shop. He meets Aurrie Nkemdirim, Margo Doré and Bo Trimble, learns that the Weir stand runs twenty-two minutes a day against a rota of forty because the lowland main’s silt trap packs every surge, and finds a civic plate on the loft wall with its lower face scraped, carrying **LOWLAND WARDS — WATCH ESTABLISHED FOR THE FLOOD YEARS / ANY HOUSE MAY CALL. THE WATCH ANSWERS.** Nobody knows who made it or who scraped it. He asks about a child’s brass token and does not ask twice. At the top of the Weir they find a wet patch on the inner face of the bank the size of a doorway, and Bo says nothing at all.

### Chapter 0020 — The Flood-Gate Choice

Jonas states only what he has seen from the path and refuses to be quoted for anything else. Three positions are argued and none is chosen for him: apply and be counted, take a county pump for one tide, or carry on unlisted. He prices the work — pumping, four days inside the tide table, six people who will get hurt — and names the three things any future term would need, then refuses to be any of them. He and Bo pack the sill with fresh compound, which buys a season and not a surge; he agrees to bring a coupling and a gasket on Thursday and nothing else. Bo explains that the loft’s own plate was taken down and dropped in the canal because it kept asking who was on duty at two in the morning, and tells Jonas not to make it into a story. On the towpath Pia says the number came out of his own drawer four days after he fixed the door, and that she does not know what to do with him.

## Volume 01 — Chapters 21–30

### Chapter 0021 — Thursday, After Six

Jonas reads the Old Ward board at ten past five on Thursday the thirteenth and finds four gate-log lines he did not write: Bram’s 06:00 tick, the 11:00 closure, a 12:41 emergency opening declared by the on-call clinician and returned at 13:02, and Ezra’s 13:30 tick. ### Chapter 0022 — The Registrar’s Answer

In the records room: Nina Calder refuses to draft, Royden Achebe offers a version that costs the city nothing and withdraws it by name at 10:09 when Oren Pell points out that a department cannot be the holder of a plate; Tomas Renn insists the answer goes public; Sanaa Iqbal brings the clinical annex. The filed answer splits the two acknowledgements: **21:09 is a breach; 15:58 is ratified as an emergency opening and the department accepts the claim.** Consequences: Jonas stays issuer, the maintenance claim is neither discharged nor transferred, he is removed from all gate-initiation at 6-41 and from cabinet 6-R-14, the lowland nodes’ history “cannot be produced” with a physical inspection proposed, and his appointment review is deferred. Oren warns that an inspection requires a location, and a location in a form becomes an address. Back in the records room, consequence three’s form has a box that wants six words, and at 12:41 Jonas writes **6-L/14 — NON-CIVIC PLANT. 6-L/19 — NON-CIVIC PLANT**, true, and the worst thing he does this month. On the board, the term was **renewed at 22:00 on the thirteenth** with Ruth Nwosu’s written objection about her wrong hours in the box beneath it.

### Chapter 0023 — The Tide Table

Saturday, 05:05 ebb. Jonas and Bo go down the chamber on a rope. Ordinary inspection only: the sill is rotten through the depth in the middle third; the pintle is solid; and there are **two sets of bolt holes**, so the current pintle was fitted into holes that do not match the frame. The chamber wall carries a silted civic plate — **6-L/14, LOWLAND WARDS, FLAP CONTROL, LOWLAND DIP, HOLDER: —** — whose one legible condition line reads **IF THE FLAP IS NOT SHUT, THE BANK IS NOT A WALL.** Jonas tells the room in full that his own filed answer has put a number on their street. Margo reads the tide table: floods on the 8th, 15th, 22nd, 29th and 6th; springs 12th–22nd; usable window the morning ebb, 05:05–07:25. Otie names the hazard: two hours of ebb a day for four days is nine hours and ten minutes of work, and “there are two kinds of person at three in the morning.” The job is priced: crew £3,600, pump £1,900, stone £340, skip £410, materials £280, mason £600 = **£7,130**; they have £913. Bo names where the hurt is: the bank. Margo catches that Aurrie has just agreed to four neap mornings that are in fact spring mornings. Bo then proposes asking the *city* for a pump, because the department’s own proposed inspection will hire one — and the price of the pump is that somebody writes a location in a box. Aurrie pins three positions on the loft wall with a name against each and the line **THE BANK — no name yet.**

### Chapter 0024 — What the Count Is For

Sunday. **Hector Mbatha produces a 32-year door count** he has kept in a pocket diary: 61 in April 1994, 59 last April, recounted every April in eleven minutes. Aurrie admits she has wanted a district since 1994 and that she never knew he had the number. Margo produces the second number: the stand’s board says **SLOTS: 40**, and 40 is *minutes of draw a day*, not households — a water authority man put 40 into *households served* in a Saltmouth report nine years ago and it is still there. Otie’s rule: a form does not know what it asks, it only knows the shape; the leash is the *name under the number*, not the number. **Pia refuses to be a spokesperson** and refuses Jonas the room’s consent. Jonas writes a location description for the inspection that is true and unusable and reads it aloud; Aurrie makes him strike *no address* and write **NO MADE-UP ROAD. ACCESS BY FOOTPATH ONLY, ON FOOT.** Jonas states in front of them that he is not on their side and that the reason the form exists is that he opened a drawer. Mina tells him the Sunday day-porter box has been empty three Sundays running and nobody read it because nothing happened.

### Chapter 0025 — A Term at the Water Point

The stand pillar carries a second unindexed plate: **6-L/19, WEIR STAND, DRAWING POINT, HOLDER: —** with a legible duty and no precedence. Anchored on it with Bo as witness, Jonas reads a seam: a rota claim and a mains premises claim with no order between them. He proposes mending one field — precedence. **Tomas Renn telephones and stops the work**, because the bond is named to the west-clinic care entrance and Jonas is not on a rota, a clinic-side naming authority, or anything that authorises a second hinge. Tomas’s condition: bring Bea, and do not put his own name in the issuer line. Bea drives ninety minutes, stops the work a second time over the *holder field* (“you have written a name in the holder line… a rota stops meaning *these people, today, and they can put it down*”), and the fix is that the holder is **the rota**, not a person. A plate will not take a role without a name, so Margo Doré consents to **ISSUER: THE HOLDER ON THE WEIR STAND ROTA, KEEPER M. DORÉ.** Jonas performs the bounded edit. The plate prints precedence, refusal rights, an unpaid claim, a 14-day term or completion of the barrier work whichever is sooner, and renewal only by the holder on the rota reading it aloud to the next holder. **Wesley Kpomah refuses his Wednesday slot in writing on the board**; the stand runs 12 minutes instead of 22 and the 10 minutes are not made up. Jonas completes a civic repair for the first time without being its issuer.

### Chapter 0026 — The Terms of a Pump

Monday night in the loft kitchen, nine o'clock until nearly four. The arithmetic: £7,130 all-in, £4,630 without pump and mason, £1,030 without the marine crew, £913 in hand, a £117 gap. **Margo Doré refuses box four in writing** — *a person who cannot be reached at 03:00 must not sign for machinery* — and says she would do it badly. **Hector Mbatha refuses** because a name in that box gives the street an owner, and an owner is the one who is on the bank at three in the morning, and he will not be that and neither should anybody else. **Aurrie Nkemdirim is the only one who could sign and refuses anyway**, because the day her name is in that box the street has an owner and the *owner* is responsible at three in the morning instead of the street, and belonging to nobody is the only civic technology this street has had since before she came to it. Bo removes the skip from the price because an unpumped chamber is not cut. **Perdita Sow**, a borough road sweeper who has swept that road on Sundays for eleven unpaid years, takes a seat — for the habit, not for bravery. Jonas offers to go on the bank and is refused by five people separately. Margo reads out the bottom line of the paper, **THE BANK — anyone who will take it, and there is no name on that line because there is no name yet**, and then Jonas, told to leave the rest blank and put a ring round the blank on purpose, writes: **THE 22ND, AT 03:00, ON A BANK. NO NAME.** He also corrects the wall paper's hour — 04:00 is the muster, not the working time, and the water is out at 05:05. Jonas confesses, unasked, that he was the man in the van in the coat in 2019 who told Bo the gasket was the wrong size, and that Hector had told him the day before, at the kitchen table, that the man had his face and he had said nothing.

### Chapter 0027 — What Is Under the Sill

Friday the twenty-first, 04:10, the fourth and last working morning. Jonas corrects Margo on the tide table: in a neap the low is 1.5 m and the sill top is 1.10 m below datum, so a neap chamber never clears the beam; only a spring low empties it. Margo accepts it in one paragraph and says she has been wrong about the ebb for 29 years. The 05:05 ebb empties the chamber to eight hundred millimetres of freeboard. The flap is found to have been shut **by hand on the ebb for 29 years** — the operating error behind the boil. The rotten middle third comes out in two hours and forty minutes. Under the frame: a **lead service seal stamped 6-L/14 · SERVICE · LOWLAND WORKS · XI·17 · S&D IW**, eleven years old. Under that, paint on the concrete — **DO NOT CLOSE ON THE EBB. CLOSE ON THE FLOOD. 6-L/14 — LOWLANDS — 8·III** — and on the sound cheek of the beam a cold-chisel **E·M**. Jonas wants it to be Evan; it cannot be, because the seal is eleven years old and Evan died eighteen years ago and never worked for a district ironworks. **Bo refuses to carry the letters up the path for him** and says he has a date on it. The new ash beam (£140, of which £39 was Aurrie’s own) and the new pintle go in; the flap closes itself on the 02:10 flood. The barrier holds for four hours and ten minutes, will not hold a surge, and has no name on it for the next flood. The seal and the beam go into an oilcloth envelope in a tin. **Hector asks for the tin and refuses it** — *a thing that is held is a thing that is yours.* **Ottie Beck takes it**, into her spares tin, unlabelled. Nobody may tell the county or the department it exists.

### Chapter 0028 — A Separate Kind of Pressure

Saturday the twenty-second. At 01:00 on Friday the trap packed and **Delphine Ofori**, holder in the first rota slot, closed the stand herself and crossed the slot. At 02:30 Ottie needed water for a four-year-old with a fever and could not get it, because the mended precedence required *the holder* and the holder was in bed. Bo: *you wrote a rule that needs a person.* At 02:10 the flood comes over the path; the flap shuts itself; Perdita is alone on the bank and rings the loft to say two words. The barrier holds. **Ottie Beck pins THE LOWLAND DIP — WHAT WAS DONE AND WHAT IT COST on the Old Ward public board** — the first thing from that street on a civic board — refusing a list of houses and refusing any number the city did not put on itself. At 14:10 **Royden Achebe’s classification of 6-L/14 and 6-L/19 as non-civic plant** is lodged into the series 6 file, and it is the six words Jonas wrote himself on the fourteenth. Oren Pell calls it wrong and gives Jonas the only move that does not put his name on a lie: a **refusal to classify**, on the ground that the local custodians were asked for a statement of function and declined. **Pia Santos refuses to help him withdraw it** and states the demand that the batch ends on — not a count, not a survey, not a statement: *who is the other end of it.*

### Chapter 0029 — The Second Map

Monday the twenty-fourth. She tells him he has put three things under his own name and there are now eleven on the Weir under somebody else’s, and that he has stopped being the answer there without noticing. Beside it, clipped to the frame, is **Hector’s pencil map: THE WEIR. 59 DOORS. DRAWN BY H. MBATHA, 24 MARCH.** At 12:02 a stretcher comes up the Saint Orra ramp; the plate says HOLDER NOT NAMED; Mina telephones the on-call clinician; **Sanaa does not answer for nine minutes because she is in a resuscitation room** — the day she named. At the shop, **Tomas Renn asks his fourth question in person, once**, and tells Jonas not to answer it.
