# Chapter Summaries

## Volume 01 — The Permission Fault

### Chapter 0001 — The Door That Owes Nothing

Jonas replaces a worn River Stacks relay, then reaches Saint Orra bridge as an ambulance is rejected by conflicting east/west requests. He uses a 25-minute attended bypass rather than a suppression lead, serves the ambulance, and records an unresolved permission conflict. The old I. Vey seal and a downstream west-clinic request establish the service chain; Jonas remains Stage 0 and wants both permanent status and fuller archive clearance.

### Chapter 0002 — A Borrowed Key

At the west clinic, Sanaa needs Alma Reyes admitted while the gate rejects a valid care claim because the night-watch duty is missing from the local record. Ruth Nwosu, the working night steward, holds the physical key. Under an attended escort procedure, Alma enters observation four, the route closes after 1 minute 41 seconds, and Sanaa makes Jonas record exactly what remains unverified. No civic term is mended.

### Chapter 0003 — The Clause in the Wall

Oren Pell closes the bridge under a valid public-safety order but invokes the separately inspected Assembly Emergency Service Test for one ambulance lane. Jonas completes the physical relay reset; afterward, the limited Fault-reader view shows the obsolete empty-crossing term linked to the ambulance conflict. Sensory bleed forces him to anchor on Nora Bell and the plate. The 90-minute lane test closes successfully but incompletely, and a separate unlisted shuttle arrives.

### Chapter 0004 — The Unlisted Stop

The green shuttle has no transit record, but it carries a child with a hand-marked token, courier Pia Santos, and Alma Reyes after a worsening return journey. Jonas refuses to treat the route as a rogue bridge effect or impound the vehicle while the patient deteriorates. A clinic clerk dispositions the child, patient, and courier; Jonas signs only a vehicle safety check. The patient returns toward the old-canal shelter, where the door asks for a post-arrival duty.

### Chapter 0005 — The Price of a Door

Selah Mora refuses Jonas’s convenient offer to put his own name on an undefined shelter claim. Rafi, Mina Okafor, and Selah instead name a bounded escort: room, medication, follow-up, and closing time. Alma returns to the shelter, the route closes after a complete handoff, and Jonas recognizes the temptation to turn responsibility into control. A valid dawn closure order then forces the shelter’s residents to seek another address.

### Chapter 0006 — The Registrar’s Rule

Oren’s order is valid: the old building’s wall, canopy, and water supply are unsafe. Jonas refuses to take an inspection he is not qualified for and helps Selah move residents, possessions, and keys to Bellow Market under Mara Holt’s care. Alma and her medication reach the receiving shelter, the building is sealed, and Tomas rejects Jonas’s urge to sign a blank personal guarantee. Oren schedules the separate pump inspection.

### Chapter 0007 — No One Signed the Map

Emeka Rusk demonstrates that a real shared-maintenance duty survives without a current municipal term. Jonas separates the physical leak from the missing term but cannot write one into the other. Under pressure from an official headcount request, he refuses to trade resident identities for a route record. The pump remains closed, bottled water and a clinic tank are arranged, and management orders the bridge incident misclassified as hardware-only.

### Chapter 0008 — The Man Who Filed a Fault

Jonas identifies the I. Vey seal as part of the original Continuity Project maintenance series and finds a partial lowland service code matching hardware in Evan’s family file. Selah keeps the complete route and identities in a sealed local log; the NDC receives only a redacted custody reference with consent, purpose, and expiry limits. Tomas warns against a continuity waiver. Jonas files an unresolved permission conflict, leaving his appointment pending.

### Chapter 0009 — The First Night Shift

A clinic/shelter hinge makes the urgent patient route and Omar Sadiq’s nonurgent shelter return compete. Jonas refuses to create a cross-node term; the urgent transfer crosses, while Omar waits exactly 43 minutes and then completes through a local route. Sanaa makes Jonas disclose the cost, earning limited trust without making her his ally. Tomas then requires every witness log and a visible responsibility bond before any shared-node civic edit.

### Chapter 0010 — The City Needs a Name

Under the west-lane Assembly test, a real ambulance, clinic admission, and Bellow Market shelter-to-clinic handoff run from 10:00 to 11:30. Unsafe side-hinge pressure isolates the failed route; the shelter return takes a 38-minute street alternative and completes. Jonas records the unresolved conflict instead of accepting a hardware-only report. The Old Ward board reveals an empty central root holder field with a Reclamation Season review line. Tomas blocks the next shared-node attempt until Jonas faces a visible responsibility bond.

## Rolling Handoff

The opening batch ends at Stage 1 with no active supernatural seam mark or personal claim. The bridge, clinic, and shelter routes were attended and closed; the old shelter and pump remain closed for physical reasons. Chapters 11–20 begin with Tomas’s bond and the first bounded west-clinic civic repair, then begin the separate Chapters 19–20 Lowglass route work.
