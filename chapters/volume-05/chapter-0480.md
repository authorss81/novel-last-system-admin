# Chapter 0480 — Five Weeks of a Building

The Saturday of the twenty-third of March 2019, and the five weeks before it, from about eight in the morning until about a quarter to twelve. A building at the top of a street, and a scaffold that came down the day before, in Tideglass.

**FOR FIVE WEEKS A MAN IN HIS THIRTIES WALKED PAST A BUILDING ON THAT STREET EVERY MORNING AND WATCHED THE FRONT OF IT, AND ON THE SECOND DAY OF THE SIXTH WEEK HE STOOD ON AN EMPTY PAVEMENT FOR ABOUT FOUR MINUTES AT THE PLACE WHERE THE LADDER HAD BEEN, AND THE THING HE HAS BEEN WATCHING IS A ROW OF THREE OPENINGS ON THE TOP FLOOR WHERE EVERY FLOOR BELOW IT HAS TWO, AND HE STILL CANNOT PUT A NAME TO WHAT THEY ARE FOR.**

He is in his thirties and he does building work and odd jobs out of a place off Corrance Lane, and he goes out at about half seven most days and is done by about four, and that building is at the top of a street he does not have any business on and goes past it twice a day.

It is a building of four floors with a shop that shut years ago on the ground floor and a door beside it that has not been locked in about a decade.

The scaffold went up along the front of it on the fifteenth of February and it came down yesterday.

That is the shape of the five weeks, and it is worth setting out because the whole of what follows is a man looking at a building from a pavement and what he could and could not get out of it.

The first week, the front of it was opened. The whole face above the shop, and the openings came out of it where the openings had been, and the inside of it was visible from the street for the first time in about a hundred years.

The second week, nothing much. They were inside.

The third week, the back half of that building was stripped. He could see it from the corner at the top because there is nothing between, and about nine days later there was new timber going in at the back.

The fourth week, the front was boarded over from the ground floor to the second floor and the top floor was open again.

The fifth week, the back was finished and there was a hoist going up and down the front of the top floor all morning every morning, and about nine lengths of something came down on the fourth and went away.

And on the top floor, from about the first week, anybody standing at that corner could see three openings where every other floor has two.

---

He has got to the same place with them about nine times and the place is this.

They are the right size for a stair going up. He is not guessing at that; he does this for a living and he has built about as many openings as he would care to say, and he knows what an opening for a stair looks like from the outside on a building of that age, and those are three of them, side by side, with the same head height and the same width and the same two inch of hard brick either side of them.

They are also the right size for a stair that used to be there and was not, and there are about a hundred ways for a building like that to have had a stair taken out of it in about 1975 and a cupboard put where it was, and every one of them puts a bit of an opening in the same place with the same head.

He has been at it for five weeks. What he has never been able to do is get from one to the other, because both of them are consistent with everything he has seen, and the only thing that would settle it is a person who was in that building about half a century ago, and there is nobody in that street he can ask that.

He has looked at the fourth floor from the corner. The fourth floor of that building has two windows, and they are about a foot narrower than the ones on every other floor and they are not in line with them, and that has told him nothing at all, because a building of four floors put up in about 1870 has two windows on the top floor that are narrower than the rest in a very large number of cases and none of them mean anything.

He has looked at the nine lengths that came down on the fourth. They were not new. He could tell from the kerb at thirty yard and he cannot tell anything else at thirty yard, and he has not walked over to look at them and he is not going to.


There is a woman in that building and he is not going to say which floor and he could probably work it out.

She has been going in and out of it for five weeks at about half seven in the morning and about four in the afternoon, and at about half seven in the morning she carries something down to the door and at about four in the afternoon she carries something up, and on two or three days she has come out at about twelve and stood at that door for about ten minutes with a bag between her feet.

He has worked out that she is on something that starts early and finishes at four and that there is a child in that building, and he has not worked out anything else about her, and he is not going to.

On the fourth day of the fifth week he came past at about half past four and she was standing at that door in the sunshine with a carrier bag and she looked up at the top floor, where two men were on the boarding, for about a minute and a half.

He went on. He goes past twice a day and he has gone past her about thirty times in five weeks and she has looked up at that top floor in front of him twice.

---

The reason he does not go and knock on a door on that street is not that he is a stranger.

It is that in 2013 he was up the top of a house on a different street with a torch in his hand while the next house was being re-covered and nobody in either of them knew he was in there, and in 2016 he went up a scaffold at four in the afternoon and put his hand into the head of a tie and there was nothing in it at all, and he was paid nothing for either and he has never told anybody about either.

He is not a man who is frightened of a door. He is a man who has twice been inside a place that was not his with nobody in it, and he has never been able to give a person an account of how he knows what he knows, and that is the whole of the reason and it has nothing to do with that building.

On the day before, the scaffold came down.

He got there at about half past seven, before work, and there was nothing on the pavement where it had been, and the front of that building was boarded from the ground to the second floor and above that it was open and dark.

He stood at the point where the ladder had been, which he knows because there is a gully off to one side of it and he watched them put it there on the first day, and he stood there for about four minutes.

What he wanted was to see the three openings from underneath. He could not see them from underneath.

He has been in that street about thirty times in five weeks and he has not been able to decide whether the three openings upstairs are a stair that is being made or a stair that is being taken out, and he has had five weeks of it and about nine goes at it and no nearer than he was on the first morning.

He went to work at about ten to eight.

**A MAN WALKED PAST A BOARDED BUILDING AT THE TOP OF THAT STREET ON THE MORNING OF THE TWENTY-THIRD OF MARCH 2019 AND DID NOT LOOK UP, AND A WOMAN WENT IN AT HALF PAST SEVEN WITH A CARRIER BAG AND STOOD IN A DOORWAY FOR ABOUT TEN MINUTES LOOKING UP AT THREE OPENINGS SHE HAS LOOKED AT EVERY DAY FOR FIVE WEEKS AND HAS NEVER ASKED ANYBODY WHAT THEY ARE FOR.**
