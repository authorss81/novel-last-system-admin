# Chapter 0493 — Two Bowls

Thursday the twenty-third of May 2019 — half past five in the morning until nine at night. The backs of about eight houses, in Tideglass.

**A BOWL WENT OUT ON THE BACK STEP OF ONE OF THOSE EIGHT HOUSES EVERY NIGHT FOR ABOUT SIX YEARS AND A SECOND BOWL WENT OUT ON THE BACK STEP OF ANOTHER OF THEM EVERY NIGHT FOR MOST OF THOSE SIX YEARS, AND THE SECOND ONE STOPPED IN THE FIRST WEEK OF NOVEMBER, AND ON THE TWENTY-THIRD OF MAY 2019 A MAN CARRYING HIS OWN BOWL ACROSS HIS OWN YARD CAME ROUND THE BACK OF THE ROW AT HALF PAST SIX AND A WOMAN CAME OUT OF HER OWN BACK DOOR AT THE SAME MOMENT WITH A BOWL IN HER HAND, AND NEITHER OF THEM STOPPED.**

Her dog went out of the front of that house on the seventh of June 2013 and was not seen again by anybody in this borough.

She was in the house. He was on his way home from somewhere else. There is nobody else in that street who could have let a dog out of a front that has a knob on it and not shut the door behind it, and there is not one person in this borough who has ever been able to give that dog an account of itself.

Nobody went looking for it after about the middle of that June. Not properly. There was a week of it, and then there was another week of it, and then there was the rest of everybody's lives.


He has never stopped.

He put a bowl out on his own back step on the night of the seventh of June 2013 at about ten at nine and he has put one out every night since, and he has not missed one, and it has gone out every night of them with him having filled it at about ten at nine at the side of that house and put it back with him having turned the water off again.

It is a plastic bowl. It is a blue one with a mark on the rim where it has been dropped once, in about 2016, and it has been the same bowl the whole time.

He washes it every morning. That is the whole of his system and it has not altered since 2013 and nobody in that row has ever asked him about it and he has never said.


She put one out too, and this is the part of it that nobody has ever known about and that she has never said to anybody, including him, in six years.

She began in about the fourth week of June 2013 and she did it at about half past ten, half an hour after he did his, and she put it on her own back step, and the two steps are about twenty yard apart with two yards between them and nobody in this borough has ever been at the back of that row after dark except the two of them.

She has never known why she started it. She has been over it a great many times and the honest answer is that she started it because hers was an empty house and hers was not the only one that was empty that night, and putting a bowl out on that step in the dark was a thing she could do.

She washed that one as well. Hers was a red one and about half the size of his.


And then she stopped in the first week of November, and here is the whole of why, and it has stayed inside that house with her.

Her mother died in 1996 and her mother had a dog, and that dog went out of that house in about 1981 and was not seen again, and her mother put a bowl out on that step every night for a long time afterwards and never once said to anybody in that family what she was doing it for, and her mother died with that never having been asked about.

She worked that out in the first week of November, at about half past ten at night, with a bowl in her hand, and she put it away inside the back door in a cupboard under the stairs where it is now, and she has not taken it out since.

---

He noticed.

There was a hard frost in the first week of November and the ground on that side of that row was white at about ten at nine and every other thing out there had about half an inch of it on it, and there was one bowl on that side of that row and it was his.

He stood on his own back step and looked across at about eight yards of white ground and one blue bowl and he stood there for about two minutes.

He has not said one word to her about it. He has not gone to the back of her house. He has not knocked and he has not asked and he has not mentioned it to anybody in this borough, and he has put a bowl out every night since that week and it has been about two hundred nights.

And she has not asked him why he looks at her back step any more than he has asked her about the bowl, and neither of them ever asked the other about it while it was happening either.


What each of them has decided about it, and neither will say it, is this.

He has decided that it is not his business. He has decided that in six years he has never put a single question to her about herself and he will not start at the back of a house in the dark over a plastic bowl, and that whatever a person does with a bowl at the back of her own house is hers, and that he is fairly sure she knows he puts one out and he is fairly sure she does not and he has not decided which of those he would rather be true, and he has noticed that he would rather both.

She has decided that if he ever says one word about it then he has noticed, and that a man who notices has counted, and that she was counting too, and that being found out by a person who counts is the one thing in this borough she could not bear.

So she carries a bowl in a press under the stairs.


Here is the thing that actually happened on the twenty-third of May.

He had moved his own out about seven weeks ago.

That is all the change there has been in six years. He had worked out from his own kitchen one evening in March that the ground at the back of that row is in the shade of the building behind it from about eight in the evening in the summer onwards, and that by the middle of the summer the water in that bowl on that step is warmer than a dog would want to drink.

So he began putting it out at about half past six instead of at ten at nine, and he has done it every night since the middle of March, and nobody in that row has noticed, because nobody else goes out at the back of that row after about six in the evening.

---

She came out of her own back door at about half past six on the Thursday of the twenty-third of May.

She has not come out at the back of that house in the evening since the fourth of November and she has not come out at that door for any reason anybody knows of. There is nothing at that house that needs doing out there.

What she did was hear it.

That is the whole of it and she is not going to pretend otherwise in her own head. She heard a sound she had not heard since about March, which was the particular noise a plastic bowl makes when it is put down on concrete, about thirty yard from her own back door.

And she went out at half past six to see what it was.

And he was at the corner of the yard turning round from the step with his hand still on it.


Neither of them stopped.

He said, "Morning."

And she said, "Morning."

And she had a bowl in her hand and he has never seen her with a bowl in her hand in his life, because he has only ever known her from about ten at nine and she has only ever been out of that door at about a quarter past ten, and that was in 2013 and it was for about four months.

He looked at the bowl in her hand.

He did not ask. There was about two seconds in that yard in which he could have asked what it was and why she was carrying it at half past six, and she saw the two seconds go past on his face, and she has thought about those two seconds every day since and she is fairly sure of what went through them.

And then he said, "That's six years of them now."

And she said, "Is there."

And he said, "Since the frost."

And she said, "Right."

And she turned round and went back in and shut her own back door.


He went back round the side of his own house at about a quarter to seven and stood in his own front room.

Here is what he has got and he is not going to be told any other part of it.

She had a bowl. It was red and it was small and she was carrying it out at half past six, which is about an hour and a half earlier than she used to, and she has not done it once in six months, and he has put one out every night of those six months.

There are about seven things he could put those four facts together into and he has put them together into one, and it is that she has been doing what he has been doing.

And he does not know what that means and he has not asked and he never will, and if she has been stopping and starting all through the cold months then that is hers alone, and he is not the man who will shove his face into it at half past six in the morning.

**A MAN STOOD IN HIS OWN FRONT ROOM ON THE EVENING OF THE TWENTY-THIRD OF MAY 2019 AND ADDED FOUR FACTS TOGETHER AND GOT ONE ANSWER OUT OF THEM, AND A RED BOWL IS UNDER THE STAIRS AT NUMBER THIRTY-FOUR AND HAS NOT BEEN OUT SINCE THE FOURTH OF NOVEMBER.**
