# Chapter 0832 — Cut At The Level Of The Grass

Sunday the thirtieth of July 2023, along the side wall of that house at number nine from the middle of the morning until there is no light on the grass, and four feet of privet that is going with the path is the whole of that Sunday.

**THERE ARE FOUR FEET OF PRIVET ALONG THE SIDE WALL AT NUMBER NINE AND SHE MEANS TO CUT IT BACK, AND THE WOMAN WHO DOES HER SHOPPING PASSES ALONG THE FRONT AT THE KERB IN THE MIDDLE OF THE DAY AND DOES NOT COME IN.**

That privet is along the side wall on four posts with wire between them, about chest high, with the bottom two feet gone to wood where the light never got in.

It is going with the path when the path goes.

She starts with shears and a saw and a sheet on the flags to catch the cuttings, working from the front to the back.

The tops come off first, down to where the leaf is thick. Privet cut to a bare stem puts out two where there was one, so she cuts to leaf and no lower through the middle of the work, leaving green on every stem she leaves.

Then the sides, back to the wire, a little at a time, with the sheet pulled along as she goes.

Here is the whole of the decision, and it comes at the end with her knees on the sheet.

Where the cut goes at the level of the ground. Everything above can be got wrong by eye and put right again, a branch too high or too low, a side too full. The cut at the ground cannot be got wrong by eye because there is only one place for it, flat to the soil, so the saw leaves no snag for rain to sit on.

She puts the saw to the foot of the first stem and cuts across, and the stem comes away clean with a pale face showing. Then the other three in turn, each one low and flat, until the four posts stand in a row with wire between them and nothing else.

The woman who does her shopping passes along the front of number nine in the middle of that Sunday and stops at the kerb to look at the heap on the sheet, and does not come in through the side gate. She does not offer and nothing is asked of her.

“The bus did not come,” she tells her from the kerb.

“Then you will be late for your dinner,” she tells her, and she walks on without another stop.

The four posts are left in the ground where they were put in, and the wire and the ties are left on them, and the cuttings go in the barrow in two loads.

---

When there is no light left on the grass she folds the sheet and stands at the wall and looks down it. Four feet of stub is in the ground along the side wall with the cut at the level of the grass and not a bit above it, pale faces to the sky, and the posts still in it with the wire on them.
