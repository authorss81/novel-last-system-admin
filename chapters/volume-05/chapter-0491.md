# Chapter 0491 — He Knew

Monday the thirteenth of May 2019, an afternoon and then an evening, from about two until about nine. One street, and the back of a building off a parade, in Tideglass.

**A MAN SAID TWO WORDS IN A ROOM OFF THAT PARADE IN THE SECOND WEEK OF FEBRUARY 2016 AND FOUR PEOPLE WERE WITHIN EARSHOT OF HIM AND EACH OF THE FOUR TOOK SOMETHING DIFFERENT OUT OF IT, AND ON THE THIRTEENTH OF MAY 2019 THE LAST OF THE FOUR WALKED DOWN A STREET TO ASK HIM WHAT HE HAD MEANT, AND HE GAVE HER AN ANSWER HE HAD NOT GIVEN ANY OF THE OTHER THREE, AND HE KNEW AS HE WAS GIVING IT THAT HE WAS DOING IT.**

The room is at the back of a building off that parade and it has been a store room since about 2016, and before that it was a canteen and before that it was a room where people sat, and about nine people from that building and two from the street drank some of what was in a plastic crate on the middle of a table that night.

Nobody had organised it. There was no word for it and nobody used one.

He was the one who locked up.

That is the whole of what he was in that building. Nine years, on the front, last out, and he has never had a title and there is no title and he would not know what it was called.

He went at about half past four and went home at about half past seven.


Here is what happened at about half past eight on that evening.

The man who had run that building for about nine years had gone in about a year before, and that had not been talked about at all in that building, and about nine people were in that room that night and at least four of them were saying, in three different corners, the same sentence in different shapes.

And a woman who had driven that building's van for six years said, to nobody, that he had been let go like that.

And a man who came in with the bread said something about a thing having been arranged.

And the one who locked up put his glass down on the table and said, "He knew."


Four people were within about nine foot of that table.

The woman who had driven the van heard it as a man telling her that the man who had run it had known he was going to be told, which is one thing and is a different thing from knowing a thing and saying nothing about it.

The man who came in with the bread heard it as the man who locked up saying the whole thing had been arranged beforehand, which is a second thing and is not what was said.

A woman who had worked on that counter until about a year before heard nothing in it at all. She had been talking to somebody about a bus timetable and she has since said, and she is quite clear about it, that he was not talking to anybody and she was not listening and it means nothing that he was in the room.

And a porter heard it as the man who locked up saying that he himself had known, about something else entirely, and had then decided to say it in a room where it would land on somebody else.

Those are four. Nobody has ever put them next to each other and nobody in this borough has ever asked anybody else which one of them is the right one.


He gave an answer to the first of them in about September 2016.

She got him at the end of that street outside the shop that shuts early, and she said, "What did you mean, in that room? Saying he knew."

And he said, "He knew what he was doing."

And she said, "Do you think he did?"

And he said, "I think he did."

And she said, "Right," and went, and she has thought about that for three years and has never asked anybody whether it is a load of rubbish.

He gave an answer to the second of them in about the January after that.

The man came into the shop and said, "Everyone's saying you said he knew."

And he said, "He didn't."

And the man said, "Didn't know what?"

And he said, "Any of it."

And the man said, "Right."

And that is a completely different thing from the first answer and it is a completely different thing from what he said at the table, and he said both of them within about four months of each other to two people who were in the same room on the same night.

He gave an answer to the third of them in the third week of October last year, on a telephone, when she rang him up because she had not seen him for a while.

And he said, "He knew he'd have to leave."

And she said, "Everybody knew that."

And he said, "Yes."

And she said, "That's not the same as knowing, though."

And he said, "No. It isn't."

---

And that is three.

What he has never done is go back to any of them, and none of them has ever asked him a second time, and the three of them are spread across three streets in this borough and none of the three knows that the other two have asked.

He has been asked about those two words four times in three years and he has said four things and he has never once said the same thing twice.

He has not thought about that much until the telephone went on the table at about twenty past two on the thirteenth of May.


She had not asked in three years either.

She was the last of the four. She was at that counter on and off for about nine years before 2015 and she had not been in that building since, and she had not been in that room that night at all, and she has said more than once that she was not listening.

She has thought about those two words more than any of the other three and she has never told anybody that she thought about them, and the reason she rang him is that in the second week of April somebody in the street where she lives said, in a shop, that a man who locked up had said *he knew*, and it got back to her.

And she has thought: there is one version of that and I have got another one, and there are about nine people in this borough who have got a version, and not one of us can say which.

---

She came down his street on the Monday afternoon and she had walked from the bus stop and she had got there early and had been standing about thirty yard off for about four minutes deciding.

She knocked.

He said, "Come in."

And she said, "You've got a minute."

And he said, "Go on."

And she said, "I'm going to ask you a stupid thing and you're to tell me if it's stupid and I'll go."

And he said, "It's not stupid."

And she said, "I wasn't there. I want you to know that first. I was on that counter and I'd left and I was not in that room."

And he said, "I know you weren't."

And she said, "And for three years I have not been listening to about four people tell that story and every single one of them has it different, and I am not interested in whose, I have decided that. I have just — I have got my own one and it has been sitting in me for three years."

And he said, "What's yours."


And this is the part he will not be able to put down afterwards.

He stood in his own front room with the door open and he could see her in it, and he had the four of them ready in about a second, the way you have four keys on a ring, and he picked one.

He said, "I was saying to the room that he knew what he was being told."

And she said, "Right."

And he said, "That's all."

And she said, "And it's true."

And he said, "It's true."

And she said, "Right," and stood in his own front room for another about four minutes and did not ask him a second thing.


Then she went.

And he shut his own front door at about twenty to five and he stood behind it and he put his hand flat on the inside of it and he thought, and he got it right the first time.

That is not what he meant.

Not that it is not true. It is true. That is the part he is going to have to sit with. He has just told a woman in his own front room a thing that is correct, and he said it because it was the one of the four that would get her out of his front room fastest and would not put anything in her head about him, and he picked it in about a second.

And he said something else to the woman who drove the van, which was that the man who ran it knew what he was doing, and something else to the man who brought the bread, which was that the man who ran it knew nothing at all.

Three years. Three people. Three different answers from one man who locked up a building, and he is the only person alive who has any idea that they are three.

And he will take that to the three of them when he is dead, if then, and in the meantime there is nothing in his hands that would carry it.

**A MAN STOOD BEHIND HIS OWN FRONT DOOR ON THE MONDAY OF THE THIRTEENTH OF MAY 2019 WITH HIS HAND ON THE INSIDE OF IT AND WORKED OUT WHICH OF FOUR THINGS HE HAD ALREADY SAID, AND HE WENT AND PUT THE BINS OUT AT ABOUT SIX.**
