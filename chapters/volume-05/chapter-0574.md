# Chapter 0574 — Three Things That Will Keep

The Friday of the twenty-sixth of June 2020, and the whole of the day, in three places in this borough that have nothing to do with one another.

**A FLAG ON A FOOTPATH HAS LIFTED AT ONE END, AND A FRONT DOOR IN ONE BLOCK OF FLATS DOES NOT LATCH UNTIL IT IS LIFTED, AND THE SAME FAULT IS ON A BACK DOOR IN A BLOCK A MILE OFF THAT ONE, AND NONE OF THE THREE WILL BE MENTIONED AGAIN.**

Behind a parade of shops there is a footpath about sixty foot long between the backs of the buildings and a line of low units. Halfway along it, one of the concrete flags has lifted at one end.

It was put down in about 1996 and the material underneath it has gone. That end is up about a quarter of an inch and if you put your heel on it the flag rocks. It has been that way since before the middle of June.

There is nothing in it for anybody. Nobody in that parade has reported it and nobody has a reason to. The man who brings the deliveries walks over that end about thirty times a day and has not once looked down, and the people who come to those units on a Thursday come in the other entrance.

---

The first block is off a road that has no shops on it and it was built about 1971.

The front door of the flat on the second floor does not latch until you lift it. You have to put your hand under the bottom edge and lift and push at the same time, and if you only push it swings back and stands open.

It has done that since before the middle of 2018. Three people have to go through it every morning and every evening, and every one of them can do it without thinking about it, and it costs about half a second, and not one of them has ever mentioned it to anybody, including the two of them who have lived there longest.

The second block is about a mile off. It was built about 1968, and it is not the same building, and the two blocks share nothing at all except a street name about half a mile away.

The back door on the ground floor does not latch until you lift it.

Same make of door. Same hanging side. Same drop on it, same finger on the handle. It has done that for longer than anybody there can remember, and the man who is on that floor has been there since 2004 and has never once wondered whether that is a thing about the door or about his own hands.

There is nobody in this borough who has been inside both of those two blocks.

Nobody who lives in the first block knows the man on the ground floor of the second, and nobody who lives in the second block knows anybody in the first. If somebody sat the two of them down together they would have it inside a minute, and neither of them has any occasion to be in the same place as the other, and there is nothing on any sheet about either.

The flag will get worse and the doors will go on needing a hand. Nothing will be done about any of the three and nobody will be annoyed for long, and by this time next year that end will have turned up properly and the person who rocks it will have a habit of it.

None of the three is in anybody's way and none of them is anybody's business, and there are about two hundred more of them in this borough at any moment, and the three on this page are only the three somebody happened to walk about on a Friday.

**AND WHATEVER IT IS THAT NOBODY IN THIS BOROUGH HAS MANAGED TO GET INTO WORDS BY THE END OF MARCH IS STILL IN THE BOROUGH SOMEWHERE, AND THERE IS NOBODY HERE WHO HAS A REASON TO GO AND LOOK FOR IT, AND NOBODY HERE WOULD KNOW WHAT TO DO WITH IT IF IT WERE PUT IN FRONT OF THEM.**
