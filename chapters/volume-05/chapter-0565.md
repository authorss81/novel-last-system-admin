# Chapter 0565 — The Bucket on the Turn

The Friday of the fifteenth of May 2020, and the nine days before it, in a building of four floors in this borough that has a tank on the ground floor and nothing above it.

**HE IS A MAN IN HIS EARLY SIXTIES AND HE HAS HAD THE SAME MORNING EVERY DAY SINCE 2009, AND THE WOMAN IN THE FLAT NEXT TO HIS HAS KNOWN HIM FOR THE WHOLE OF IT AND HAD NEVER ASKED HIM ONE QUESTION, AND ON THE SIXTH OF MAY HE PUT A BUCKET DOWN ON THE TURN AND DID NOT CARRY IT UP AGAIN, AND IT WAS STILL ON THAT TURN ON THE FOURTEENTH AND NOBODY IN THAT BUILDING HAD SAID ONE WORD ABOUT IT.**

The building is four floors and there are eight flats in it and it was put up about a hundred years ago by people who were building for themselves. There is a tank on the ground floor at the back behind a door, and the tank feeds the two flats on the bottom, and there is nothing above the second floor at all.

He is on the top and has been there since 2009. He goes down at twenty past six.

He has a bucket. He fills it at the tank, which takes about three minutes because the tap is old and runs slow, and he carries it up one flight, which takes him about a minute and a half with it full, and he puts it in the bathroom, and he goes to work. At about half seven in the evening he carries it back down, empties it into the outside gully, rinses it out and leaves it outside his own door on the turn.

He has done that every morning and every evening since 2009. Nobody asked him to. There is no list of it and no sheet with his name on it, and nobody in that building has ever once said a word about where the water comes from, and he has never once brought it up.

Nobody can see that turn from the bottom of the flight. You come up off the ground floor and there is one run of treads, and then a wide bit where that run doubles back on itself, and then another run of the same length to the top. There are two doors on that wide bit and nobody has ever used either of them for anything.

On the sixth of May he was coming up with the bucket in his right hand and a carton in his left, and he put the bucket on the wide bit against the plaster and went on up with the carton. Then he went down at half five, which is earlier than he has ever gone down.

The bucket was on that wide bit every day from the sixth to the fourteenth.

It was not in anybody's way. It was against the plaster and it was not in anybody's way. On the eighth a woman came up with a carrier and put it down on that wide bit to rest it while she got her keys out, and she moved the bucket about a foot to get round it, and then she took her carrier up and he was not there, and she did not think about the bucket again. Nobody said anything about it on the eighth or on any of the other days.

On the fifteenth it was gone.

He went down at twenty past six and filled it and carried it up and it was not on the wide bit.

There were seven buckets in that building on the sixth of May and there are six now. He has not counted and he is not going to count and he has not asked anybody, and the woman who lives next door to him would not put it past her to have walked off with it herself, and neither of them has mentioned it.

He went down again at half five that evening and rinsed the other one out and left it by his own door, and there was nothing at all on that wide bit.

---

She was inside the front door at about six and he came in behind her, and there were three other people about and none of them said anything at all.

She waited until the other two had gone up.

"Where's your bucket gone?"

"There are plenty of buckets."

"There isn't one on the second that's mine."

He did not answer that, and she did not stand there waiting for him to, and one of them said something about the afternoon being warm and she answered him about that instead, and then she went up.

---

**THE NINE DAYS WERE THE WHOLE OF IT AND HE PUT IT DOWN WITH HIS OTHER HAND FULL AND HAS NEVER SAID WHY AND IS NOT GOING TO ASK HER, AND SHE HAS ASKED HIM ONE QUESTION IN ELEVEN YEARS AND WILL NOT ASK HIM A SECOND, AND ON THE FIFTEENTH OF MAY HE WENT DOWN AT TWENTY PAST SIX AND CARRIED UP NOTHING AND STOOD AT THE BOTTOM OF HIS OWN FLIGHT FOR A MOMENT AND THEN CARRIED ON UP.**
