# Chapter 0554 — Three Things in Three Places

The Monday of the twenty-third of March 2020, and most of the day, in four streets of this borough.

**A WEEK OF ACCOUNTING IN A PLACE OFF THE LONG ROAD COMES UP SHORT BY SOMETHING AND NOBODY HAS LOOKED, SOMETHING THAT COMES EVERY WEEK CAME ONCE ON THE WRONG DAY, AND A MAN IN HIS EARLY THIRTIES OPENED A DOOR HE HAS NEVER LOCKED AND WHAT HAD BEEN BEHIND IT IS NOT THERE, AND NONE OF THE THREE WILL EVER BE MENTIONED.**

There is a place off the long road where people go to get a seat, some wheels, a bath and a small number of other things that people need once and for a very short time, and you go there for an hour and you take something home in a van that somebody else drives.

The woman in her mid forties has worked in that office since about 2016. She does the booking and she does the account at the end of every week, and the account is a page with the week's name on it and the money taken and the money owed, and it is a long-established habit of that office to take two off the total for anybody who does not pay before the Friday.

She has taken two off the total for the first time in all the time she has done it, in the week of the second of March, for a delivery on the sixteenth, and she wrote it against the name of a man who came in on the Tuesday and paid in the way he always pays, which is in the middle of the following week.

The money came out of that week's take on the Thursday. She did not see it come out. She signs the page on a Friday and she did not see the two, and on the Monday of the sixteenth she wrote the next week's account from the page and the page was right and the total was short by one whole figure, and there was nothing in it about a mistake, and she has not found it.

She has still not found it. She will find it in about three weeks, in a week when she is busy, and she will put it in, and she will not say anything to the man, because the man did what he has always done and there is nothing to say to a man about that.

The man who pays in the middle of the following week has done that since about 2014 and everybody in that office knows it and the woman in her mid forties has never once mentioned it to him, and there is a good reason for that, which is that the whole of that office's habit of taking two off is built on exactly what he does.

Nobody asked him to pay sooner. Nobody told him he could. He worked out on his own, in about the third year, that the week he pays in is the week he is thought of as not having paid, and he decided that was fair, and he has gone on paying at the same point in the week ever since.

So there is a rule in that office that nobody wrote, and a man in that office who obeys it, and a week in the March when the two of them cost a woman in her mid forties two pounds she has not found yet. That is all that happened. Nobody was dishonest. The page was signed. The total was short.

---

The second thing happened in a street off the long road to a woman in her late twenties who has had a delivery on a Tuesday for about nine years, and the delivery is four of something, and it is the same four every week, and it has never once been late.

In the February it came on a Wednesday. That is the whole of what is unusual about it and it was unusual for four days, because on the Tuesday of the March it had not been there, and by the Wednesday of that week the woman in her late twenties had stopped opening her door until about half past five on a day of not knowing what day it was going to be.

It came back on the Tuesday. It has come on the Tuesday ever since and there have been four of those Tuesdays since, and she has not said one word to the man who brings it, and he has not answered her, and neither of them has any idea whether the February happened.

She has not asked whether the February happened, because the answer is yes, and because a woman in her late twenties who has had the same delivery for nine years does not ask a man about a Wednesday.

She has thought about putting a note on her own door. She will not put a note on her own door, and that was settled in the second week of the March and has not been looked at since.

There were four of them before the February and there have been three since, and the woman in her late twenties has not gone to a shop once about it, and there is a shop four streets away where a woman sells the same four on a Wednesday if anybody wants them on a Wednesday.

She has not gone to the shop. She has thought about going to the shop perhaps four times. She does not want the same four on a Wednesday; she wants the four that come on a Tuesday, and there is no version of wanting that which a shop four streets away can answer.

---

The third thing is a box of tools and a man in his early thirties who has been at the same employer for six years and has been in a locker in the workshop at the back for the whole of that time.

The lockers are in a small building at the back, and that building is not locked in the daytime because nobody locks it, and the man in his early thirties has never once worried about it in six years and has never once checked his box in the morning.

On the second Monday of March he opened it at about ten to seven and the door was standing open and what had been behind it for six years was gone.

There is no way of saying that anybody took it. The door was open. That is the whole of it. The box had hand tools in it and it was not worth very much and it was the sort of thing that goes in a skip in a fortnight.

He stood in there for a couple of minutes and then he shut the door and went to work and did his day and told nobody, and he has not mentioned it since, and there are no tools in that building now belonging to him, and the fitter he works with has never asked him why he has not had a box since that Monday.

He has not told his mother. He has not told the man he works with. He has not told the woman he shares a house with, and the woman he shares a house with asked him on the Sunday whether he had moved his things out and he said he hadn't and she said right, and that is the end of the only conversation anybody has had about it.

**ONE FIGURE IS MISSING FROM A PAGE THAT A WOMAN IN HER MID FORTIES SIGNS EVERY FRIDAY, A THING A WOMAN IN HER LATE TWENTIES OPENED HER DOOR FOR FOUR DAYS IN FEBRUARY, AND A BOX OF TOOLS IS IN A HOPPER SOMEWHERE IN THIS BOROUGH OR IS NOT, AND NOBODY HAS ASKED ANYBODY ANYTHING AND NONE OF THE THREE IS GOING TO BE RAISED.**
