# Chapter 0554 — Three Hours, Three Places

The Monday of the twenty-third of March 2020. Three places and three hours, and none of them in the same street as either of the others.

**A WOMAN WHO HANDS OUT SMALL WAGES EVERY WEEK IS ONE WHOLE FIGURE SHORT AND HAS NOT BEEN TOLD, A DELIVERY A WOMAN HAS HAD FOR NINE YEARS ON ONE MORNING OF THE WEEK WENT OUT ONE DAY EARLY AND WAS NOT BACK BY THE MORNING IT HAS ALWAYS COME, AND A MAN OPENED A DOOR HE HAS NEVER LOCKED AND WHAT WAS BEHIND IT WAS NOT THERE, AND NOT ONE OF THE THREE IS ANYBODY ELSE'S BUSINESS.**

There is a place off the long road where people go to get a seat, some wheels, a bath, and a small number of other things that people need once and for a very short time. You go there for a day and you take something home in a van that somebody else drives.

The woman in her mid fifties works in the office of that place. She has been there since about 2016. She hands the wages out on a Friday morning in envelopes, six of them, and she does it at nine, and she has done it every Friday for as long as she has been there.

The money for the six is counted into the six on the Thursday afternoon, out of the day's take, and the day's take is counted on the Thursday afternoon, and the two counts are done by the woman in her mid fifties and by nobody else.

She is one whole figure short.

It has been going on since the middle of February. The money leaves that office short by one whole figure every Friday, and the six envelopes are lighter than the six should be by that figure, and one of the six people who opens one has not said a word about it and will not.

She has not noticed.

She counts the money out of the day's take on a Thursday and she counts it in again on the Friday, and both numbers are right, and the difference between them is the figure, and it has been on that Friday every week since the middle of February without the two counts ever disagreeing in front of her.

She will find it in about three weeks, in a week when she is busy, and she will put it straight, and she will not say anything to any of the six, because not one of the six did anything.

---

The second thing happened in a street off the long road to a woman in her late twenties who has had a delivery on a Tuesday for about nine years.

It is four of something. It is the same four every week. It has never once been late and it has never once been on a different day.

In the February it came on a Wednesday.

That is the whole of what is odd about it, and it was odd for four days, because by the Tuesday of the following week it had not been there, and she had been at that door at about half past four on four mornings running by the time she gave up on it.

The man who brings it has been doing that round for a good many years and has never been asked anything by anybody on that street. He did not say anything on the Wednesday and he has not said anything since, and whether he knows that she opened her door four times he has never told anybody, including himself.

She came back on the Tuesday. It has come on the Tuesday ever since, and there have been four of those Tuesdays since, and she has not said one word to him about the Wednesday and has not asked him why.

She has not asked, because the answer is that something had gone wrong that week on a delivery that was going somewhere else, and there is nothing to ask a man about a Wednesday.

She thought about putting a note on her own door. She will not put a note on her own door, and that was settled in the second week of March and has not been looked at since.

There is a shop four streets away where a woman sells the same four on a Wednesday if anybody wants them on a Wednesday. She has thought about going to it perhaps four times.

She does not want the same four on a Wednesday. She wants the four that come on a Tuesday, and there is no shop four streets away that has those.

---

The third thing is a box of hand tools and a man in his early thirties who has been at the same employer six years and has had the same place in a workshop at the back for the whole of that time.

The places are in a small building at the back. That building is not locked in the daytime because nobody locks it, and the man in his early thirties has never once worried about it in six years and has never once opened it in the morning.

On the second Monday of March he opened it at about ten to seven and the door was standing open and what had been behind it was not there.

There is no way of saying that anybody took it. The door was open. That is the whole of it and that is the only fact there is about it.

The box had hand tools in it and it was not worth a great deal, and it is the sort of thing that goes in a skip in a fortnight, and a man in his early thirties is not going to a building and saying that to anybody.

He stood in there for a couple of minutes and then he shut the door and went to work and did his day.

He has told nobody. He has not told the man he works with. He has not told the woman he shares a house with, and she asked him on the Sunday whether he had moved his things out and he said he hadn't and she said right, and that is the whole of the only conversation anybody has had about it.

**NOT ONE OF THESE THREE WILL BE RAISED WITH ANYBODY, AND THE ONLY PEOPLE WHO KNOW ANYTHING ABOUT THEM ARE THE PEOPLE WHO LIVE WITH THEM, AND NONE OF THE THREE WILL BE MENTIONED AGAIN.**

He has been coming in at about ten to seven every morning since the second Monday of March, a few minutes earlier than he used to, and he has never once said why, and nobody in that building would understand it if he did.
