# Chapter 0466 — The Floor Above the Second

Thursday the tenth of January 2019, from about twenty past two in the morning until about twenty to three. Three floors of one building, in Tideglass.

**IN A BUILDING OF THREE FLATS TWO PEOPLE HAD SPENT YEARS DECIDING THAT THE WOMAN ON THE TOP FLOOR WAS ASLEEP, AND IN JANUARY EACH OF THEM FOUND OUT SHE WAS NOT, AND NEITHER OF THEM WILL RAISE WHAT THEY LEARNED, AND SHE IS ON THE OTHER SIDE OF THAT CEILING AND AWAKE.**

It is a building of three floors and a shop that is shut, and there is one flat on each of the three floors and a stair in the middle of the plan, so that anybody going in or out of any of them goes past the landing of the one below.

He has the ground floor. He came into that building in 2013 because it was cheap and because the shop underneath was already shut and would stay shut.

She has the second floor. She has been there about eight months.

There is a third floor and there is somebody in it, and nobody in that building has ever met her.

Here is what each of those three people has believed, and it is worth setting out before the morning, because none of the three beliefs is stupid and two of them are the kind that a building of three flats makes for you whether you want it or not.

He has believed for about six years that the woman on the top floor is not in at night.

He arrived at that in the ordinary way. There is a noise through that ceiling about one night in three, at about half past two in the morning, which is a bed frame or a wardrobe door or a person walking about four steps and stopping. And in six years he has never once gone up those stairs at that hour, and he has never once heard her come in or go out, and he has therefore settled on the most ordinary explanation available, which is that she is asleep and something in her room is making the noise.

She has believed for about eight months that the woman on the top floor is in all night.

She arrived at that by counting. The noise is not every night. It is three nights in seven, and it is always at about half past two, and it lasts about four minutes, and once in the first fortnight she had come up out of her bed and gone to the top of her own stairs and stood there in the dark and listened to somebody moving about above her for four minutes at half past two in the morning, and she has slept badly ever since on the grounds that somebody in a room four feet above her head is awake at half past two and has been for a long time.

They have never spoken about it. There has never been any occasion. It is not a thing anybody raises in a stair of a building where the three of them are strangers.

---

On the Thursday of the tenth, at about twenty past two in the morning, he came in.

He comes in late most nights and he has a key and he does not turn anything on. He came through the front door, shut it, and was in the passage of the ground floor with his coat half off when he heard the stair move.

Not much. Somebody on the second landing going up. And then, at about two minutes to three, a noise on the top floor that was not a bed frame and was not a wardrobe door.

He stood in that passage for about four minutes and then went up.

He has never spoken to the woman on the top floor and he did not know what he was going to say, and he did not have to say anything, because the door of the second flat was about six inches open and the switch by it had been used.

He went past it. There is a landing about five foot deep on the top floor and a window at the end of it, and she was at that window with her coat on and her shoes in her hand in her left hand and her handbag over her arm, and she was looking down at the front of that building rather than out at anything.

He did not say anything. She did not say anything. He came back down the stairs past the open door of the second flat and the switch by that door went off about four seconds after he got to the bottom, which told him that the woman on the second floor was awake and had been awake and had heard all of it.

She got out of her bed at about two minutes to three because she heard somebody go up past her door.

She had heard that stair before. She had a whole account of that stair built out of eight months of it, and the account said that the ground floor was a man who came in late and went straight to bed.

It was not that. It was somebody going up at about ten to three, and the switch by that door had been used, which she had never known it to be in the eight months she had been there.

She went out onto the landing and looked up. The stair to the top floor is eight steps and there is no turn on it, so from the second floor landing you can see the top floor landing from the third step up.

What she saw was a woman on that top landing at the window with her shoes in her hand and her handbag on her arm, and the woman was not getting dressed to go anywhere. She was dressed and she was leaving.

She stood on that landing for about ten seconds and then she went back into her own flat and shut her door and sat on the edge of her bed.

Here is what she has taken from it, and she has taken it from it correctly. She has believed for eight months that the woman above her is in her flat at half past two in the morning, awake, moving about, every week or so, and that nobody in that building is awake except the two of them, and that they are both lying in the dark listening to each other not know.

And on the tenth of January at about ten to three in the morning she watched that woman go out of that building with her shoes in her hand.

---

Neither of them put it to her.

He is not going to. He went up eight stairs at twenty to three in the morning on a Thursday because he heard a stair move and he has now seen the woman on the top floor dressed with her shoes in her hand, and the only question open to him is the one that would take him up there and say it.

The reason is six years old. He has been lying in a bed on the ground floor of that building since 2013 listening to a noise through the ceiling about one night in three at half past two in the morning and building a woman out of it, and the woman he built was asleep, and the woman on the top floor is not asleep and has not been asleep for as long as he has been lying under that noise.

If he goes up there and asks her what she is doing, then in a fortnight's time somebody in that building has spoken to her about her own habits, and he cannot do that. He has been the man under the noise. He knows what the noise has been doing to a person on the other side of it for six years without a word of it, and he is not going to be what makes a person on that top floor start watching a stair.

She is not going to either, and hers is the worse of the two, and she has been through it.

What she has to give up if she asks is not a habit. It is the eight months. She has been angry about that noise since before she moved in. She came into that flat in the summer, she was on a rota with an early start, and within about three weeks of being there she was awake at half past two in the morning three nights in seven because somebody above her was moving about, and she has never said one word about it to anybody, and she has not said one word to the woman on the top floor, and she has not said one word to the man downstairs, and she has not said it to the woman who owns the building.

And she decided in the October of last year that she was not going to say it. She decided that having said nothing for eight months, saying it now would be a thing she had done at a quarter to three in the morning to a person she had never spoken to, and that a person who has been lying awake since about half past two for eight months is entitled to have that left alone.

And then the noise stopped being the thing and became somebody going out, and being angry about a person going out is a different and worse thing than being tired, and she is not going to be that either.

So she is in that flat, and she is awake, and the top floor is above her, and on the night of the tenth of January she lay in the dark for about an hour afterwards and then got up and did not go back to bed until about four.

He has not gone up those stairs at that hour since. He goes in at about half past one most nights and he does not listen, and if the stair moves he is in bed and asleep, which he is not.

She has not gone up those stairs at that hour since either. She goes up in the daytime to sign for things and she does not knock.

And neither of them has any idea what the other one knows.

On the Saturday morning she came down to take her post off the mat and the man on the ground floor was in his kitchen eating something standing up, the pair of them said good morning, he asked her whether anything had come for her, and she said that nothing had, and that was the whole of it.

**ON THE SATURDAY MORNING A WOMAN CAME DOWN THOSE STAIRS AT ABOUT HALF PAST EIGHT AND A MAN ON THE GROUND FLOOR ASKED HER WHETHER THE POST HAD COME AND SHE SAID IT HAD NOT, AND UPSTAIRS A WOMAN ON THE THIRD FLOOR WAS AWAKE AND STAYED AWAKE, AND NONE OF THE THREE SAID ANYTHING ELSE.**
