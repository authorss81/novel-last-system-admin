# Chapter 0567 — The Small One at the Front

The Monday of the twenty-fifth of May 2020, and the three months before it, in a building at the back of a parade of shops in this borough.

**HE HAS OPENED THAT BUILDING EVERY MORNING SINCE 2013 AND HE HAS BROUGHT THREE BOXES OUT OF THE STORE AT THE BACK AND SET THEM DOWN IN ONE WAY, AND SINCE THE FEBRUARY A WOMAN WHO WORKS THERE HAS BEEN IN FIRST ON SOME MORNINGS AND PUTS THEM DOWN THE OTHER WAY, AND NINETY DAYS WENT BY WITH NEITHER OF THEM SAYING ANYTHING, AND ON A MONDAY IN MARCH HE PUT THEM DOWN HER WAY WHEN SHE WAS NOT THERE, AND SHE HAS NOT FOUND OUT.**

The building is one space deep with a store at the back, and about a dozen people work in it doing repairs to other people's things. It has two sets of doors at the front. The man who opens it is in his late fifties and has opened it since 2013.

There are three wooden boxes in that store and they are three sizes. There is a big one, a middling one and a small one, and they have been in there since before either of them came.

He brings them out the same way every morning. The big one first, at the office end, then the middling one along from it, then the small one at the far end.

There is a reason for that and it is not a good one. The big one is the one that fouls the doors if it is at the front, and if it is at the far end there is about nine feet of clear floor in front of it, and everybody has walked round it for seven years without any of them deciding to.

He has never asked anybody to help him with it and there has never been any question about whether he should be the one. He came in at about twenty past six for seven years and the boxes came out at about ten past and everybody else came in at about a quarter to eight.

---

She is a woman in her early thirties and she came to work there in the February.

On the mornings she is in first she opens it now. She has never asked him whether she is to, and she has never told him she was going to, and he has never been in on a morning when it happened, and nobody in that building has mentioned it.

She puts the small one at the front, then the middling one, then the big one at the far end.

The two ways have been going in and out of that building since the February. Nobody has ever asked which of them is right and there is nobody in there it would matter to, and the boxes are heavy enough that both of them move them by taking hold of the base.

On the mornings she is not in he does it his way and on the mornings she is in she does it hers, and about a dozen other people walk past three wooden boxes whatever way round they are in.

Neither of them has said anything about it. Not once, and not in the way people say nothing by agreeing not to, which is a thing he would have noticed and she would have noticed him not doing.

**NINETY DAYS IS THE WHOLE OF IT, NOTHING WAS AGREED IN THEM, NOBODY PUT ANY OF IT INTO WORDS, AND EVERY MORNING SINCE THE FEBRUARY THE WAY ROUND THREE BOXES HAVE BEEN DECIDED BY WHICHER OF THE TWO OF THEM WALKED IN AT TEN PAST.**

---

On the Monday of the sixteenth of March she was not there and he put them out her way.

He has not said why. He was not asked and he has not brought it up, and if anybody were to put it to him he would have to stand still for a second before he answered, which is not a thing he does.

He did it that morning, and then he did it his way on the Wednesday and the Friday of that week and on the Monday following, and she came back on the Wednesday of the eighteenth and did hers, and neither of them looked at the other one about it.

Since the middle of March he has been going in at ten on most days, which means she is in first on most days, which means the boxes go down the way she puts them on most days. There has not been a morning since the February on which anybody in that building has said which way round they were.

He has not moved a box back. She has not asked him why. She does not know that he ever did it at all, she has no way of finding out, and he is not the sort of man who would say.

On the morning of the twenty-fifth she was in first again. She came in at about ten past and put the small one at the front and hung her hat by the door, and neither of them said anything.

**HE PUT THEM DOWN HER WAY ON THE SIXTEENTH OF MARCH AND HAS NEVER SAID WHERE HE GOT IT FROM, AND SHE FOUND HIM DOING IT ON THE NINETEENTH OF MAY AND HAS NEVER SAID SHE SAW, AND NEITHER OF THEM HAS ASKED THE OTHER FOR ANYTHING ONCE IN NINETY DAYS.**
