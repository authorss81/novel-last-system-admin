# Chapter 0570 — Fifty Minutes Off and Back

The Saturday of the sixth of June 2020, and the April before it, on a hill about fifty minutes off, three of them, and the same short grass at the top.

**SIX TIMES IN SIX YEARS THE THREE OF THEM HAVE DONE IT, AND IN THE APRIL ONE OF THEM TOLD THE OTHER TWO PLAINLY IN HER OWN KITCHEN THAT THERE WOULD NOT BE A SEVENTH, SAID IT ONCE, WAS NOT ASKED WHY, AND THE OTHER TWO SAID RIGHT AND NOTHING ELSE, AND ALL THREE OF THEM DID IT.**

The hill has a tower on the top of it and a cut path up the bank from a road at the bottom, and from the top you can see three counties and about nine miles of nothing much in between them. There is no charge for it. You drive up the road and walk up the cut and stand at the low edging at the top and go back down.

Nobody in the three of them knows who started it. It has no occasion. None of the three of them has a birthday that comes near the first week of June, and none of them has ever given anything to the other two on that day, and there is nothing written down about it anywhere, and they have done it six times.

---

She is in her early forties and it was she who said it.

The other two are a man in his late thirties and a man in her late forties, and the two of them have known each other longer than either of them has known her, and that has never come up either.

She said it in her own kitchen on the twenty-second of April, with the tea made and two of them sitting there and the middle of the afternoon about two thirds gone.

"This would be the last time," she said.

And then she did not say anything else for about a minute and a half, and one of them said, "Right," and the other one said, "Right."

She said, "There's no need to ask," and that was the end of that afternoon.

Nobody asked. Nothing else happened in that kitchen that afternoon, all three of them have been carrying the twenty-second of April since, and not one of the three has put a question to her about it, and she has not offered it.

**A THING THAT GOES ON FOR SIX YEARS IS NORMALLY STOPPED BY ONE PERSON NOT TURNING UP. THIS ONE WAS STOPPED BY ONE PERSON SAYING OUT LOUD, IN FRONT OF THE OTHER TWO, THAT SHE MEANT IT, AND NEITHER OF THE OTHER TWO HAS SINCE MANAGED TO GET AS FAR AS THAT BEING THE REASON.**

They met at the road at the bottom at about half past ten.

She got there first and waited on the grass bank and the two of them came up together in the same car and one of them got out and said the traffic on the far road had been slow, and she said it usually is on that road, and the three of them went up.

They did not talk much going up. The cut is about three hundred of the tread of them and it takes about half an hour and there are two places along it where anybody would sit.

At the top the three of them stood at the low edging.

There was about half an hour up there. She looked at the far county and he looked at the ground at his feet and the other one took about six photographs of nothing in particular, the same three hundred yards of the same middle distance, and put his phone away.

At about half twelve they went back down the cut and got into the two cars and drove back the way they had come.

They were back at about one.

The two of them went into a place in this borough and had something on plates, because that is what they do when they have done it, and she sat in her car in the road outside for about twenty minutes and then drove home.

She has not rung either of them since. They have not rung her.

One of them said in the place, "Same in September, then," about something else entirely, and the other one said something back, and neither of them said anything about a June.

What she is carrying is not the hill.

**AND NEITHER OF THE TWO MEN HAS PUT IT TO HER AND NEITHER OF THEM EVER INTENDED TO, AND SHE SITS IN HER CAR OUTSIDE A PLACE THAT IS NOT HERS FOR TWENTY MINUTES AND THEN DRIVES HOME, AND THAT IS WHERE THE SIXTH TIME ENDED.**
