# Chapter 0499 — Stepping Over It Again

Monday the seventeenth of June 2019, that evening only, from about half past five until about ten at night. One house, and a passage inside it, in Tideglass.

**IN THE JANUARY AND FEBRUARY OF 2015 A MAN STAYED IN THAT HOUSE FOR EIGHT WEEKS AND IN THE SECOND WEEK OF THE FEBRUARY HE LIFTED THREE SQUARE TILES OUT OF THE FLOOR OF THE PASSAGE AND PUT THEM BACK THE WRONG WAY ROUND, AND THERE IS A PLACE IN THAT PASSAGE ABOUT FOUR FOOT LONG WHERE THE FLOOR GIVES, AND A WOMAN WHO LIVES IN THAT HOUSE FOUND OUT ABOUT IT IN THE THIRD WEEK OF MARCH 2019 FROM A MAN WHO WAS NOT HIM, AND HE HAS SINCE BEEN IN THAT HOUSE TWICE.**

The house is at the low end of a street of nineteen. It is an old house with a passage straight through it from the front to the back, which was a thing about it that the agent had mentioned, and the passage has a hard floor of square tiles laid in a grid and no door on it at either end except the two front and back doors, and it is about nine foot wide.

Number sixteen.

The woman who lives there has lived there since the summer of 2016. Nobody else lives there. She works at a place out of town and she is out from about seven in the morning until about six, and there is a cat.

The man had been in that house for eight weeks in the January and February of 2015 and had left in the March, and nobody in that house at that time had known him at all, and he had been there because he was between one place and the next and the owner of the house was away in the country for those two months.

He had slept in the top room. He had left a treadle sewing machine in a wooden cover in the top room when he went, and had told the owner he would come back for it, and had not come back for it.

---

Here is the floor, and it is a very small thing and it is the whole of this.

In the second week of February 2015 he was in that passage at about nine in the evening putting a length of pipe—no.

He was in that passage in the second week of February 2015 because there was a gap under the floor by the side of the back door where you could put your thumb into it, and he had a flat bar and a claw hammer, and he is the sort of man who does not leave a gap.

He lifted three of the square tiles up. They are about six inch tiles and they come up whole and they go back in the way you would guess, and he put three of them back the wrong way round, so that the bevel that is meant to go down is going up, and the tile sits about a third of an inch proud of the ones either side of it, and if you put a foot on that stretch of floor it goes down, and if you put a wheel on it, it catches.

It is a bad piece of work by anybody's standard. It is the only bad piece of work he has left anywhere that anybody knows about.

Here is what she has done about it, which is nothing, and it is the nothing of two different people.

The woman at number sixteen found out in March.

She found out from a man who had been in that house in the summer of 2015, after he had gone, and who had been in a shop in the parade in the third week of March 2019 buying a thing, and who had seen her and had said, without being asked and in the middle of something else, that whoever had laid that floor had laid three of the tiles in the passage upside down and that they would need doing before anybody put a fridge through.

That is all he said. He did not say when. He did not say who. He said "whoever laid it" and he went back to what he was buying.

She went home and knelt down in that passage on the Sunday afternoon and put her hand on it, and she found it in about four seconds, and she sat on the bottom step in that passage for a while.

And then she did the thing that has brought her to the middle of June.

She went and found out when the floor was laid. Not the man. The floor.

And the floor was laid in about 1981, and the tiles in it are the same tiles, and the three that are wrong are three of the same tiles, and they are not three tiles that somebody else put in later. They are three of the same floor. The man in the shop was wrong about the age of the whole thing being a different job, and right about the three, and she worked out in about a minute in that passage that there is no way of telling which of those two things is true for three tiles, and that the only person alive who could tell her is the man who lifted them.

She has not asked him. She has not seen him since she moved in. She does not know that he has ever been in that house at all.

What has happened since March is this, and there is a list of it and the list is four lines long.

In the summer of 2018 a man came to that house in the middle of a Sunday afternoon and asked the woman who was there at the time whether there was a bag of his in the top room, and there was, and he took it and went, and that was the first time she had seen him without knowing what she was looking at.

On the second of June 2019 he telephoned and asked her whether the top room was clear and whether the front door was on the latch, and she said it was, and he said he would come on the seventeenth, and she said the seventeenth was a Monday, and he said it was, and there was nothing else in the conversation.

That is all of it. That is the whole of the contact there has been between them in those eight weeks of 2015 and in the three years since.

Here is what happened on the Monday.

He came at about half past five in the evening with a handcart, because a treadle sewing machine in a wooden cover weighs about ninety pounds and there is no way of getting it down a staircase otherwise.

She was in the house. She was in because she had told him the front door would be on the latch and she had put a dish of water down for the cat and she was sitting at the top of the stairs with a book when she heard the cart.

They said four things. Here is all four of them, and the order they were said in.

"You're late, I thought you said six."

"I said six, I came at half five because I knew what this weighs."

"Mind the top stair, it's the same as it was."

"That'll be the man's, is it."

He said that last one over his shoulder, in the top room, not as a question, and she said, "It's not mine," and he said, "Right," and that was the fourth thing.

Then he worked on it for about an hour and a half.

The cover came apart in four pieces and the machine came out of it and he had to get the machine itself up and over his shoulder and down two flights and through that passage, and he tried it sidewise first and it would not go through the door frame of the back door because of the cover behind it, and he had to take the cover apart into two and take the parts down separately.

She came down with the second piece of the cover at about half past seven and put it in the passage by the front door, and then she went and stood in the kitchen doorway and watched him come down.

Here is the whole of the next two minutes, and it is about thirty seconds of it.

He came down that passage with the machine on his shoulder and one hand on the frame of the back door, and at about two-thirds of the way along, at the place where three tiles have been the wrong way round since February 2015, his right foot went down and the floor gave about a third of an inch and he put his weight onto it and the machine came down onto his hip.

He did not fall over. He said something that was not a word.

And he went out through the front door with the machine and put it in the cart and stood in the front area for about a minute looking at the cart, and then he came back in, and she was in the front area, and he had to come past her to get to the cover at the back of the passage.

And as he came past her he put his own foot on that place again, deliberately, the way a man puts a foot on a step he is about to go down, and the floor went again, and this time he looked down at it.

He looked down at it for about two seconds.

And the woman stood in the front area of her own house about four feet from him and did not say one word, and he did not look at her, and after the two seconds he said "right" to himself and went out to the cart.

Then he loaded the rest of it, which took another half an hour because the belt had perished and he had to cut it with a knife on the front step, and he did that on the step and not in the passage, and she noticed that he did that on the step and not in the passage.

He said goodnight at about a quarter to ten. She said goodnight. He took the cart out and up the street and she shut the front door and stood in her own front area for a while.

Here is where she is, and the point of this is that it is not a decision.

She has not told him. She has not said anything to him about the floor, and she did not say anything on that Monday, and she had about thirty seconds in which the whole of it was there in a front area with a man in it and she did not use any of them.

It would have been four words. It would not have been an accusation. It could not have been an accusation, because she does not know that it was him, and because there is no way of putting a hand on three tiles in 2015 and being believed.

And here is the part that is not a decision either.

She has not decided not to say it. She has thought about not saying it, on and off, since about half past seven that evening, and she has got as far as a position, and the position is that she does not want to be a woman who tells a man in a doorway that she knows what he did in 2015, because that would make it a thing she had been carrying for three years for him, and she has not been carrying anything for him. She has been carrying a passage.

And she has also got as far as noticing that there is no third way. It is either that she says it, or it is that she goes on kneeling on a Sunday afternoon in five years' time to look at a floor, and she has not chosen either of them.

**A MAN CAME DOWN A PASSAGE WITH A SEWING MACHINE ON HIS SHOULDER ON THE EVENING OF THE SEVENTEENTH OF JUNE 2019 AND THE FLOOR UNDER HIS RIGHT FOOT WENT DOWN A THIRD OF AN INCH AND A WOMAN FOUR FEET AWAY WATCHED IT HAPPEN AND SAID NOTHING, AND HE LOOKED DOWN AT THE FLOOR FOR TWO SECONDS AND WENT OUT AND CAME BACK AND PUT HIS FOOT ON IT AGAIN ON PURPOSE.**
