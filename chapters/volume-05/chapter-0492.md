# Chapter 0492 — Six Minutes in the Same Place

Friday the seventeenth of May 2019, from about half past five until about nine in the evening. One street, and two houses on the same side of it, in Tideglass.

**A LORRY STOPPED IN THAT STREET AT ABOUT TWENTY PAST SIX ON THE MORNING OF THE SEVENTEENTH OF MAY 2019 IN THE SAME PLACE IT STOPPED IN MAY 2016, AND A WOMAN STOOD IN THE PASSAGE OF NUMBER THIRTY AND WATCHED IT AND A WOMAN STOOD IN THE PASSAGE OF NUMBER THIRTY-FOUR AND WATCHED IT, AND THE TWO OF THEM HAVE NEVER PUT THAT MORNING TO EACH OTHER, NOT ON THE DAY AND NOT ONCE IN THREE YEARS, AND NEITHER OF THEM IS GOING TO.**

They are about eight houses apart and they have been in that street about the same length of time and they were both at the front of their own houses on that morning in May 2016.

Neither has ever put it to the other.


What stopped there on the second Monday in May 2016 was a lorry, and there was a boat on the back of it.

A boat. That is the whole of it. A flat-bottomed sort of thing about fifteen foot long with three seats in it and a short outboard, standing up on its side on the deck of an articulated lorry, roped down at four corners.

It came along that street at about ten past six in the morning with nobody walking and nobody about, and it stopped at about a quarter to six, in the road outside number thirty, and the engine was shut off, and nothing else happened for about six minutes.

And then it went on to the top of that street, where there is a turning place, and was gone in about four minutes.

Nobody in that street has ever been given one word about it.


What the woman at number thirty has thought about, in three years, is this.

She has got a version and the version has got three things in it. A boat on a lorry going through that street at ten past six in the morning on a Monday, and no person watching it, and the lorry stopping for as long as it stopped.

She decided inside about a year that the length of it was the point. Not the boat. If it is a boat on a lorry then that is a thing that happens and it happens to somebody else's business and it does not stop anybody's day.

But six minutes in the road outside her own house is somebody deciding to be outside her own house.

She has never worked out who. She has never wanted to. She has worked out that if she ever worked out who then she would have to decide whether to do something about it, and she is not a woman who does anything about anything after a lorry has stood outside her own front.

---

What the woman at number thirty-four has thought about, in three years, is that same stopped lorry and a completely different thing.

She was not in that street in 2016 for about four months of that year. She knows about the morning because of the woman at number thirty, who told her about it on the Friday, and who said the whole of it in about nine sentences and did not ask her a single question about it.

And she has been carrying that since about June 2016.

What she has carried is not the boat. It is that she was told, and that she has never once asked, and that she has worked out in the three years since that there is not going to be a day when she does, and that she would not know what to do on that day.

And she has worked out something else, which is the part she does not like.

She thinks the other one has told nobody. She thinks that in three years that street has not so much as had a cup of tea out because of a boat.


Here is the thing about the two of them, and neither has ever said it, and it is the only thing about this that is actually unusual.

They do not disagree.

That is not a thing anybody would notice. Two women on one side of a street who have been there the same length of time and who nod, and who have never once argued about anything in this borough.

But there is a version of that morning in each of them, and neither has ever heard the other's, and after about two years of not asking it became a thing neither of them could begin, because the first question would be *what did you see*, and neither of them is going to stand in a passage and ask another woman that.

So what has happened in three years is that those six minutes have gone from being a thing they both saw and become a thing that each of them has alone.

On the morning of the seventeenth of May 2019 the lorry came along that street at about twenty past six.

It was the same road and it was about the same time and it came up past number thirty-four first, because thirty-four is lower than thirty, and there is one of these a week on that route and nobody in that street looks at any of them.

This one slowed.

It slowed the way a lorry slows when the driver is looking at a thing, and it came to a stop in the road outside number thirty at about four minutes past six, and put its hazards on, and the engine went off.

Number thirty is the house of the woman who has been thinking about that morning since 2016. Number thirty-four is the house of the woman who has been carrying being told about it since 2016.


The woman at number thirty came out of her front at about ten to six because she was putting a bag out and she saw it going past and she stopped on her own step with the bag still in her hand.

It went past her and stopped.

That is all she saw. She did not see the driver get out and she did not see him look up and she did not see him get down into the road and stand in the middle of it. She was already back inside by the time the hazards stopped and she was standing in her own front room for about four minutes afterwards.


The woman at number thirty-four came out of her own front at about six because she had heard it change.

She came out and stood in her passage, which is the side of that house and not the front, and she was about eight house-lengths down the street from where the lorry was, and she could see about two thirds of it and she could see the hazards going and she could see that nobody was out on the front there.

She went back in at about six minutes past and shut her own front and stood in her own front room.

She stayed in that front room for about six minutes and then she made the mistake of going to the front again and looking at it, and there was nothing there.


At about ten to nine that morning the woman at number thirty walked across to number thirty-four.

That is not a thing either of them does. They nod. She has not been across that road to that house since about 2017 and not for a reason.

She had a reason when she started out. She is not able to say now what it was and she was not able to say it at the gate.

She stood at that gate for the best part of a minute and there was nothing about the boat in her head while she was standing there, and then, when she turned round and went back across, it came into her head all at once and she was about six foot into that road.

And she did not go back.

**A WOMAN STOOD AT A GATE ON THE OPPOSITE SIDE OF THAT STREET AT ABOUT TEN TO NINE ON THE SEVENTEENTH OF MAY 2019 FOR THE BEST PART OF A MINUTE AND SAID NOTHING, AND WALKED BACK TO HER OWN FRONT DOOR, AND THE WOMAN INSIDE THAT HOUSE STOOD AT HER OWN OPEN FRONT DOOR AND WATCHED HER GO ALL THE WAY AND SAID NOTHING EITHER.**
