# Chapter 0557 — The Friday Line

The Sunday of the fifth of April 2020, and most of the afternoon, in the ground behind a row in this borough.

**HE HAS BROUGHT A LOAD INTO THAT PLACE EVERY FRIDAY SINCE 2014 AND HE DID NOT BRING ONE IN FEBRUARY, AND THE LINE ON HER SHEET THAT HAS READ FRIDAY SINCE 2014 IS BLANK FOR THE WHOLE OF THAT MONTH, AND SHE HAS ASKED HIM ONE QUESTION, AND SHE HAS DONE NOTHING WITH IT SINCE.**

The ground behind the row is not much bigger than the houses it belongs to. There is a building on it with two wide doors and a slope up to them, and a pit at the far end with sheeting round it, and the building has belonged to a man in his early sixties since before that row went up.

There is one more entrance, off the gap between two of the houses, and it is the entrance everybody uses.

She is a woman in her late forties and she has worked in that place since about 2016. She is on her own in the office most of the day, which is one end of that building with the door open and a radio going most afternoons.

Everything that goes into that pit goes on a sheet. What came in, what day it came in, what it weighed, and what is charged for taking it. She writes all of it. Nobody else writes any of it, and he has never asked her for a copy and has never asked her for anything at all.

---

He is a man in his mid forties. He has a van and he works at a place about four miles off where about nine people do nothing but mend other people's things, and the rubbish off that place has gone into that pit in his own van every Friday since 2014.

His has been the smallest figure on that sheet every week since she started on it. It has been the same figure since 2014 and it has not moved once. She has never asked him why a man who runs a place like the one he runs gives away the same thing every week for six years at the same figure, and she has never asked him anything else either.

In February there is no Friday on her sheet.

She did not go back to February until the Wednesday of the first of April, when a woman she knows asked her in the ordinary way how many loads had gone in that month.

There had been none.

---

She had him in her head for two days after that and on the Friday of the third of April she caught him at the top of the slope on his way in.

"Have you stopped coming?"

"Since when?"

"Since February."

He looked at the pit for a bit.

"I had a hip done in January," he said.

Which was true, and it was not the answer, and she knew both of those things standing there, and she has not been able to get them into the right order since.

Nothing else was said about it. He went up and put it in and she went back inside and wrote it on the sheet with the day against it at the ordinary figure, because that is what the sheet says when a load comes in, and nobody has ever asked her to do anything else.

She has not asked him again. She has not asked the man who owns the building about it. She has not told either of the two women who come in on a Tuesday. She has not been back into February to put anything in it, and that Friday line for that month is still blank, and it is the only blank one the sheet has ever had.

On the Sunday afternoon she was in the office with the door open and the place empty and the sheet in front of her, and she ran a finger down the Friday line for 2019 from the top of it to the bottom, which took about a minute and which she had not meant to do.

There are fifty-two of them and every one is the same figure and none of them is anything except his.

She could put the two of them side by side now and it would not mean anything, and she has known that since the Friday, and she went and looked at the February page again anyway about half an hour later, standing up, with the sheet in her hand.

It is a good sheet. The writing on it is small and even and there is not one thing on it since she started that she would call wrong, and the Friday line for the twenty-eighth of February is the only place on the whole of it where there is nothing at all. She has been asked twice about putting her on a computer for it and has said that she can do it like this and that it suits her, and neither of the two who asked has asked her again since.

She put it back in the same place and went out and shut the door. There is nothing on that sheet for the twenty-eighth of February and there is not going to be anything, and she did not go out and stand in the pit to look at it, and she did not ask him either.
