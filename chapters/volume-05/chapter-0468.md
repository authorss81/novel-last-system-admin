# Chapter 0468 — The Wrong Side of the Landing

Saturday the nineteenth of January 2019, from about half past three in the afternoon until about half past four. A landing and two doors, in Tideglass.

**A WOMAN HAS STOOD ON ONE SIDE OF A MAN ON A LANDING SINCE 2013 FOR A REASON SHE HAS NEVER EXPLAINED, AND IN JANUARY HE TOLD HER THAT THE SIDE SHE CHOSE IS THE ONE HE HEARS ON, AND SHE IS GOING TO GO ON STANDING THERE, AND NOBODY ELSE IN THAT BUILDING IS AWARE OF ANY OF IT.**

He has the second floor. She has the third.

It is a narrow building, four flats to a floor, and the stair is at the back, and the landing on the second is about nine foot deep with a window at the end of it, and anybody coming up or going down that stair crosses it.

She has lived on the third for about three and a half years and he has been on the second for about six, and they have spoken across that landing probably a hundred and fifty times, mostly about parcels, mostly in about ten words, and he has never once been in her flat and she has never once been in his.

Where she stands when she talks to him has been fixed since the March of 2013.

The reason is a thing he said in 2013 and has entirely forgotten saying.

She had something to say to him, and it was not a small thing, and it was about the first floor, and she did not want to say it twice. She worked out at the time that he was hard of hearing and that the ear it was worst in was the one on his left, and she reasoned her way through it in about a minute on that landing and decided that she was not going to stand on his good side and talk at him like a woman addressing a man across a room.

So she stood on his left, at the wall side, about four foot from him, and she said it once, at volume, and he answered it, and it took about a minute and a half, and she went back up to the third floor.

He has never mentioned it. She has thought about the fact that he has never mentioned it about once a fortnight for nearly six years.

And what she has never worked out, and could not work out if she tried, is whether that minute and a half was a conversation or a performance.

---

What happened on the nineteenth of January was that he finally lost the near ear altogether.

Not suddenly. It has been going since about the September before, and what happened on the Saturday was that it arrived at the point where he turned his head to the other side.

She was coming down to the bins and he was coming up with a parcel in a hand, and they met on that landing, and she did what she has done about six hundred times and stood on his left against the wall, and said, "Is that you, then."

And he said, "That's me."

Which is the wrong answer, and she heard that it was the wrong answer, and she is not a fool and she has not been able to leave it where it was since about ten past three on that Saturday.

He stopped on that landing. He put the parcel down on the sill of the window, which he has never done in six years. And he said:

"I can hear you perfectly well on this side. It's the other one that's gone. You've been saying everything twice for about two years and I've been saying it back twice and neither of us ever thought to say so."

And he said it the way a man says the time, with no edge on it whatever.

That is what she has been unable to get past. He was not aggrieved. He was not standing there doing the sum about two years. He put the parcel down and he said it the way a man tells you the time, and then he picked the parcel up and said, "Sorry. It's worse than I said it was," and went up two steps and then stopped again and came back.

What he came back for was the other half of it. He said, "It was there in 2013 as well, you know. You used to stand over there. I could hear every word of that."

And he went up.

---

She has been on the third floor since ten past four and she has not gone down.

She has been through it four times since and arrived in the same place, and the place is that she is not going to change sides of that landing.

Not because of the ear. She would change sides tomorrow if the only thing in it was the ear.

The reason is 2013.

If she moves to his other side, then she is a person who has moved to his other side, and the only reason anybody ever moves is that they have worked out that the side they were on was a choice and that the choice was a thing they did on purpose. And she did it on purpose. She chose that side in about a minute, on that landing, in that March of 2013, because she wanted to say a thing once.

And she has never known whether he heard it.

And all of that is six years old, and she cannot go and find out now without telling him why she is asking, and she is not going to do that on a landing, and she is not going to do it at all.

There is a man on the ground floor of that building who would have noticed.

He came into that building about three years back and he is in it more than he is out of it, and he goes up and down that stair four or five times a day, and he has seen those two on that landing more times than he can count, and he has seen that she always stands on the same side, and he has not once thought about it, and if you had told him about it on the Saturday he would have said it was a thing about a hearing aid and gone back to what he was doing.

There is a woman on the first floor who has not been in that building since about October.

Nobody else knows. Nobody on that landing knows it but the two of them, and one of them has not decided anything at all, because he is a man who told the truth and went up two steps, and the other has decided something and is not going to tell anybody she has decided it.

**AT ABOUT HALF PAST FOUR ON THAT SATURDAY SHE CAME DOWN THAT LANDING TO GO OUT AND SHE STOOD ON HIS SIDE OF IT FOR THE FIRST TIME AND HE WAS NOT THERE, AND SHE WENT BACK UP AND CLOSED HER DOOR.**
