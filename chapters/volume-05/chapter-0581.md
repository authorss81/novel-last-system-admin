# Chapter 0581 — I Knew When You Asked Me

The Thursday of the twenty-third of July 2020, from about half past seven in the evening until about half nine, at the wall of a playing field and on one street in this borough.

**SHE TOLD A MAN THAT HE KNEW SHE WOULD SAY NO BACK IN THE FEBRUARY, AND HE SAID HE DID, AND NEITHER OF THEM HAS PUT ONE WORD ON WHAT THAT DID TO THE HOUR AFTERWARDS, AND THE HOUR AFTERWARDS IS MOST OF THE CHAPTER AND NOTHING AT ALL HAPPENED IN IT.**

The wall is about two feet high and runs along the edge of a playing field where, in July, four and five-year-olds are put down on the grass in pairs at about seven in the evening by whoever can be got.

There were about a dozen of them out and about nine grown-ups standing along the wall in the last of the light, and a woman of seventy-one was sitting on the wall with a bag of shopping beside her and a man in his mid forties was standing about three feet along from her with his hands in his pockets, and they had been there a while.

She has lived on that street since about 1968 and he has been on it since the January, and she is seventy-one and he is in his mid forties.

In the February, before he had a key or a word for the place, he asked her whether she would take him to the hospital twice a week for a bit. He had nobody here. She said that she would.

She did it for four months. Twice a week, always the same two days, and he paid her in cash into her hand every single time so that there was no occasion to write anything down, and he stopped at the end of June because it was finished.

On the Thursday evening he asked her whether she would do it again in the August.

She said no.

He said, "Right," and stood there, and then she said the thing.

"You knew when you asked me in the February," she said. "You knew I'd say no."

He did not take a moment. He said, quietly:

"I did."

That was all. He did not apologise, and she did not ask him why he had asked, and neither of them has since brought the February up, and it is not going to be brought up.

---

There was nothing else to say and neither of them said anything else, and what the two sentences did to the rest of the evening was not visible from outside.

She could not have put it into words if somebody had asked her on the pavement. What had happened was that for about a minute the two of them were the only people in the borough who knew what they were talking about, and that had not been true ten minutes before and could not be made true again by anybody else.

At about ten to eight she picked her bag up and said she was going down for her tea things and he said he would walk with her as far as the corner, and they went.

They went the length of that street at the speed of two people with nowhere in particular to be. They looked at a hedge. They talked about a road closure at the end where a sewer had been left open for three weeks, and about a dog on the corner that had started coming out on its own, and about a woman three doors down whose window had been painted over by somebody else's mistake. Nothing that mattered.

At the shop he waited outside. She was in there about six minutes. She came out with a bag of biscuits and a paper of something for a cat and he said, "You've got a cat?" and she said, "I've had a cat," and he said, "Right," and that was that.

They went back up the street and stopped at the bus stop, which has a timetable nobody reads and a shelter with one side of the glass gone.

At about twenty past eight a bus came and went. At about half past eight another came and went. Neither of them had anywhere to be and both of them stood there for both of them, and neither of them said so, and there was nothing in that to be said.

At about ten to nine he said he was going to put his bins out and walked off up the road, and she let herself in and left the light on over the stairs for a while because she was not tired yet.

She has not rung him. She did not think of ringing him.

**THEY SAID TWO SENTENCES ON A WALL AND BOTH OF THEM WERE ACCURATE AND NEITHER OF THEM WAS ANYTHING, AND THE ONLY CONSEQUENCE IS THAT THE TWO OF THEM ARE MORE ALIKE THAN THEY WERE AT HALF PAST SEVEN AND NEITHER HAS FOUND THE WORDS FOR THAT.**
