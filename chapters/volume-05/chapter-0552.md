# Chapter 0552 — The Fourth One

The Thursday of the twelfth of March 2020, and the four days before it, in a small place at the end of a street of this borough and in a front part four miles away.

**HE IS IN HIS EARLY FORTIES AND HE KEEPS THINGS AND HE HAS PAID LESS FOR EVERY ONE OF THE FOUR THINGS THAT HAVE COME THROUGH THAT DOOR SINCE 2018, AND IN MARCH HE PAID WHAT HE PAID THE FIRST TIME AND HE DID IT ON PURPOSE, AND THE OTHER MAN HAS NOT ASKED HIM WHY.**

The place at the end of the road is barely wide enough to turn round in and it has a name on the side of it that is not the name of what goes on inside. It sells second-hand. It has sold second-hand since 2016 and the man who runs it is in his mid thirties and he has never described what he does as anything at all.

He buys. He goes to houses and to clearances and to a man by the water who has too much of it, and he pays what he can get away with, and then he puts a figure on it and waits. He has been doing that for seven years and he has never had anybody complain, and there is nobody in that street who would know how to complain of him properly and one man who would have tried.

The man in his early forties has been buying off him since 2018. He is a man who keeps things. He has the sort of house where a person cannot open a wardrobe without finding something that was there in 1974, and he buys the things other people have given up on, and he pays when they turn up and he pays what is asked, and he has never once tried it.

The first one was a chest of hand tools in the March of 2018 and it was a hundred and twenty pounds and it was worth more than that.


What he does with what he buys is not storage. He has been the sort of man who takes things apart and puts them back together since he was a boy, and everything that has come through that door in all that time has been worked on in his own front part, and the chest of hand tools in 2018 is the reason he was able to do the trestle, and the trestle is part of the reason he could take the weights.

That is not an answer to anything and it is not offered as one. It is a fact about a man who has spent nine years of his life finding out what a thing is by taking it to bits, and it is the only reason he can afford to pay a hundred and twenty pounds for a bench with a vice on it, and neither of the two men in that place has ever mentioned it.

The strip says two hundred and ten pounds. The man in his early forties paid a hundred and twenty. The difference is ninety pounds, which he worked out in the fourth week of March, standing in his own front part, and he has not written it down and he has not told anybody and he came back on the Tuesday to see whether the strip was still on the side of the bench.


The second was a trestle in the June of 2019 and it was seventy-five pounds and it was worth more than that.

The third was a set of cast weights in the November of 2019 and it was fifty pounds and it was worth more than that.

He knows what the difference is on each of them. He worked it out at the time and he has gone over it since, more than once, and the going over is the part of this that has taken two years.

What he has worked out is that the man in his mid thirties was not being unfair. The chest was worth about a hundred and thirty to him and he took a hundred and twenty because the man asked and because he wanted the sale. The trestle was worth about a hundred and he took seventy-five. The weights were worth about seventy and he took fifty.

There is no reason in that and it is not a cheat and it is not a trick, and it is the same decision three times with three different people, and the man in his early forties has never been able to make it into anything else, and he has never once said any of it out loud.

Nobody else in that street has paid the first price twice, and nobody else would know to, and there is no reason in the world why he should have.

He has thought about that as well. He has gone round the four of them in his own head more than once and he could not find a pattern and he has not looked for one, and the reason he has not looked for one is that he knows what he would find, which is nothing at all, and a man who is in his early forties and has been keeping things since 1974 does not want to find nothing at all in the middle of a March.

---

The bench came in on the Monday and it is a bench with a vice on it and it is the best of the four by some way, and the man in his mid thirties priced it on the Thursday morning at two hundred and ten and wrote that on a strip and stuck it on the side of it.

The man in his early forties saw the strip at about two hours in.

He stood there with his hands in his pockets for a while, and then he went home, and he came back at about half past four with the money for the first one, which was a hundred and twenty pounds, and the notes were counted out on his own kitchen surface at home and folded once, and that was where they came from.

The man in his mid thirties counted it and put it in the place where the money goes and gave him change and there was no change, because there was not any.

"That was the price of the first one," the man in his early forties said.

"It was," the man in his mid thirties said.

"I know what it's worth," said the man in his early forties. "That's not what I'm saying."

"What are you saying?"

"I'm saying that's what I paid you the first time."

The man in his mid thirties did not answer that. He has not answered it in the twenty minutes since, and he was not going to answer it, and the two of them stood in a place barely wide enough to turn round in for about half a minute with a bench between them and the strip still on the side of the bench saying two hundred and ten pounds on it.

Then the man in his early forties said, "I'll take it down on Thursday," and went out, and the strip is still on the side of the bench, and he will take it down on Thursday because he said he would, and neither of them has said anything about the other three and neither of them is going to.

The man in his mid thirties has got ninety pounds more than the whole of the four of them together would have brought at the first rate, and nobody has been told, and he is not a bad man, and he has thought about the strip on the side of that bench every day since the Thursday and has not taken it down himself.
