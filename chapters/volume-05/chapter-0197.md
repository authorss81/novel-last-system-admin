# Chapter 0197 — The Kerb

Tuesday the sixth of October 2015, from about ten past six in the morning, a yard entrance in Tideglass.

**HE WAS TOLD AT TEN PAST SIX IN THE MORNING BY A MAN HE HAD MET ONCE, WITH HIS SLEEPING BAG ALREADY IN THE CAB, AND HE WENT, AND HE IS ALL RIGHT, AND HE IS GONE.**

There is a way people have of turning up at six in the morning for something that was settled on Friday, and this yard had it. A tipper was backed in with its lights off and a flatbed behind it and about nine people standing about in the dark on the other side of it, not talking much, two of them smoking and one of them a young man in a high-visibility jacket who had the clipboard of somebody who had gone home.

Jonas Mercer got there at about ten past six because he had come down the street at ten past six and there were headlights in the yard entrance, and nobody had sent him, and there was nothing that could have stopped him coming at half past six instead.

The man of about thirty-seven put his bag in the cab of the flatbed. He had a kettle, a charger, two clean shirts in a plastic bag and a job's worth of tools, and the tools went in the footwell and the kettle went on the parcel shelf, and he did it in an order he had done before. He has his own tipper and a flatbed and he does work for whoever pays him, and he is not on a rota and he is not on anything, and there is no list in this city with his name on it and there has not been one since he was on a school register.

**THE ONLY LATTICE NOTICE AT THAT YARD ENTRANCE THIS MORNING, printed and fixed to the gate post at about the height of a person of average height, and it defines nothing, and nothing on it defines *holder*:**

> **A PERMISSION SAYS A PERSON MAY GO SOMEWHERE. IT DOES NOT SAY THE PERSON HAS TO GO.**
> **IT ALSO DOES NOT SAY THE PERSON HAS TO COME BACK.**
> **NOTHING ON THIS NOTICE IS HELD AGAINST ANY PERSON.**

The man who told him was a man of about sixty-seven with a waterproof and a lanyard from the water side, who had met him once in June when a pallet of pipe had gone into a pond. He had a folded piece of A4 with a line on it, and he read the line, and then he put the paper in his pocket.

"There's fourteen days."

"Right."

"It's a fortnight. It says fortnight."

"Right."

"Pay's in the cab. It's in an envelope, it's the whole of it, it's before you start, and it's the going rate for that work over there, not the going rate here, and it's more than the going rate here."

"Right."

"And you've got a room."

"I've got a room."

Nobody said anything for about a minute. A woman of about twenty-seven who lived in one of the flats over the yard gates said, to Jonas Mercer and not to anybody else, that she had looked after his washing for about two years and that she had a box of it and would keep it, and that he would be back on the twentieth and could have it then.

"That's fine," said the man of about thirty-seven.

"You'll be back on the twentieth?"

"They've got me for fourteen days. I said yes last Saturday."

"You said yes on Saturday and it's Tuesday morning," said the woman of about twenty-seven, and then stopped, because there is no way of asking a person a question about that which is not a question about the answer, and nobody in that yard was going to do it.

**AND WHAT A PERMISSION DOES TO A PERSON WHO CANNOT DECLINE IS MAKE HIM FINDBLE FIRST, AND FINDBLE IS NOT THE SAME AS BEING HELPED.**

He was findable because he has a telephone. Not because he is on anything, and not because anybody decided to look, and not because a shape in a room has him in it. He is a man who answers a telephone, and that is the whole of his address, and on Saturday a man from the water side wrote that number on a folded piece of A4 and put it in his pocket, and this morning at ten past six he said fourteen days out loud at a gate and the man of about thirty-seven was standing there to hear it.

There are two ways a person in this borough gets to be findable, and this morning was one of them and the other one happened in August, and the two are not going into one sentence. A man a price makes findable is a different man from a man a shape in a room makes findable. The first has a rate printed next to his name in another city's schedule and an envelope in the cab before he starts. The second has four things in a field and one of them is the hour he went home, and he cannot get out of it and does not know that he is in it.

The other one is owed a bad back and is not going to be asked about it, and he is on nothing either, and he has never been in a yard at ten past six in the morning in his life. This man of about thirty-seven is owed a fortnight's work and he took it. Nobody was sent for. Nobody is a rescue. And no part of what happened this morning would have happened if there had been an emergency.

The tipper came off the yard at about twenty past six. There was no speech. The young man in the high-visibility jacket wrote something on a clipboard that had not belonged to anybody, and Jonas Mercer could not see what it said from where he was standing, and the man of about thirty-seven said *cheers* to nobody in particular and got in, and the gates came open by themselves because the man who opens that yard on weekday mornings was on the wall looking at the water, and the tipper went out into Selby Row and turned left toward the water.

**AND HE WENT, AND HE IS ALL RIGHT, AND HE IS PAID, AND HE IS GONE, AND THIS BOROUGH HAS NEVER HAD A MECHANISM FOR A PERSON COMING BACK AND THERE IS NOT GOING TO BE ONE.**

Clause four of the permission gives him a right of return, on the day the work ends, without asking anybody for anything. That is the best clause in the document and it is worth reading twice, and Jonas Mercer read it twice on a table in a room over a shop last Wednesday while a woman of about fifty-two waited for a train, and it is a right to come back and it is not a way of being brought back, and the difference between those two things is the whole of the morning.

A right of return needs nothing. A mechanism for a return needs somebody to be in it. If this borough had one — a list, a name, a box, a counter, a person standing in a doorway with a piece of paper — then somebody in this borough would be able to say out loud that a man of about thirty-seven was due back on the twentieth, and that sentence about a person would then exist, and the man would be a person somebody could ask about. A man a price makes findable and a man a sentence in a box makes findable are two different men, and one of them chose to be findable and the other one had it done to him in August.

The tipper was gone. The yard went quiet at about half past six. The woman of about twenty-seven went back in with a box of washing under her arm, and the man on the wall said that the water was up about a foot on the low side and would be more by Wednesday, and nobody asked him how he knew, because he looks at that wall every weekday morning and has done since he was seventeen and there is no instrument in this city that could ask a man what he knows from a wall.

Jonas Mercer walked back up the street with nothing in his hands, which is the condition he has been in since the twenty-fifth of February.

The envelope was in the cab. Fourteen days at the going rate, in money, in full, before the work begins, and there is no field and no hour and no thing the person was called for, and nobody in this borough is going to write down that a man of about thirty-seven said yes to a fortnight on the Saturday and was told on the Tuesday, and if the whole thing were cancelled tonight the only record anywhere in either city would be a line on a borrowed clipboard in the hand of a man in a high-visibility jacket who was going home.
