# Chapter 0564 — Four Small Things

The Sunday of the tenth of May 2020, from about eight in the morning until about two in the afternoon, in four places in this borough that have nothing to do with each other.

**A WINDOW IN A ROW OF THREE WILL NOT OPEN AND HAS NOT SINCE THE JANUARY, AND A FOOT OF THE EDGE OF A PATH HAS GONE WHERE THE CEMENT CAME AWAY, AND A GUTTER JOINT DRIPS ONTO THE SAME PIECE OF PAVING EVERY TIME IT RAINS, AND NONE OF THE THREE WILL BE MENTIONED AGAIN.**

On the ground floor of a building off a street in this borough there are three windows in a row that are the same make as each other, and two of them open and one of them does not, and has not since about the second week of January.

The wood is sound and the glass is sound. What holds the moving part came away about four months ago in the way they do, and it has been sitting loose in the rebate ever since, and it will come out one day in a high wind and go onto whatever is below it.

There is a woman in her early thirties who works on that floor and who has tried to open that window by force about thirty times since January and has stopped trying. There is nothing on any sheet about it and nobody has asked about it. The other two windows in that row open and shut all day and nobody in that building has ever mentioned the difference.

Behind a school there is a made path about a hundred foot long that people take because it is shorter than the road. At the near end of it, on one side only, about a foot of the edging has gone.

The cement came away from the block underneath it. That is all. There is no hole and nothing under it and that length of it is not going to get any worse on its own, and it will be the same in a year and it will be the same in ten.

A man in his late sixties who cuts that grass has walked past the end of it twice a week since about the March and has never once looked down at it, and neither has anybody else, and nobody in that borough knows it is there.

On a row of about nine houses off a road in this borough, one joint in a length of guttering has come apart where two lengths meet, and the water that comes down that side does not reach the pipe. It goes onto the piece of paving outside number four, at the angle, and it has been doing that since before Christmas.

It is not a great deal of water. It has made a mark about the width of a hand on the paving, where the colour is different, which a person sees once and then does not see again.

The woman who lives at number four has swept it. She has swept that angle about once a fortnight since about the New Year and has not looked up at the guttering while she did it, and has not said anything to anybody, and is not going to, and it is not going to be mended by anybody in particular.

---

In a building by the water there is a working book two inches deep and it lies open on the top of the grey thing by the door, face down at the outer edge where nobody turns the pages.

Under the last entry in it there is a line and it is still empty and was not touched.

A woman in her mid fifties works in that building and comes in on a Sunday because a Sunday is when it gets done. She has done that for about six years. Nobody works with her on a Sunday and nobody has for about three years.

She sees that book every Sunday. It is not on a list of anything and it is not on anybody's way and the only person in that city who could say what it is for is not in the building and has not been for some time.

There are nineteen entries going down one side of it and under the nineteenth there is a line ruled across the page and nothing at all in it, and it has been sitting there since the September of 2015, and nothing in that building has ever shifted by so much as a day.

The last thing written in that book was written on the fourteenth of September 2015. Underneath it the count now stands at one thousand nine hundred and ninety-two days, and the line under that is empty, and she did not put anything in it this morning and she is not going to this afternoon.

**AND WHATEVER IT WAS THAT NOBODY HERE HAS EVER MANAGED TO GET INTO WORDS BY THE END OF MARCH IS STILL IN THAT BUILDING SOMEWHERE, AND NOT ONE PERSON IN THIS BOROUGH HAS A REASON ANY MORE TO GO LOOKING FOR IT, AND THE BOOK IS STILL ON THE TOP OF THE GREY THING WITH THE PAGE TURNED DOWN.**
