# Chapter 0442 — Four Inches

Sunday the twenty-third of September 2018, from about half past eight in the morning until about four in the afternoon, one flat above a shop off that parade, in Tideglass.

**A MAN TOOK A BED OUT OF THE WAY OF A WINDOW HE HAD SPENT TWO DAYS FITTING SO THAT IT WOULD OPEN, AND THEN HE PUT THE BED BACK EXACTLY WHERE HE HAD GOT IT FROM, AND THE WINDOW OPENS FOUR INCH, AND HE KNEW THAT ON THE LAST MORNING AND SAID NOTHING.**

The flat is the top one. She has been in it six years and he has been in it in four days in the last two years, for the shutter on the front, which stuck, and that was a whole conversation.

The job this time is a window in the back room, where the sash rotted through along the bottom and the rain came in onto the sill and down the wall behind the bed, which is the reason the bed is where it is.

The bed is under that window. It has been under that window since she moved in, because that is where it fitted when the room was empty, and the room has never had anything else in that space, and she has never tried anything else in that space. It is an old iron bed with a high foot, the kind that was in this room before she was in it, and she has said none of that and he has not asked.

He took the old sash out on the Monday morning and started cutting the new one to size. He got the joints done by the Tuesday lunchtime and hung it on the Wednesday. It went up well. He put new cords on and the weights in and got the pulley boxes out and got them back, and it came down about eight inch on the pull and stopped, and it came down all the way on the pull, and he left it up about four inch so that he could get at the pulley boxes behind the beads.

On the Thursday he let the beads down, closed the window, and came to shift the bed.

The bed is an old one and it is heavier than it looks. It took him about ten minutes to get it out far enough to open the window, and he took the bottom pillow off and stood it against the wall, and he put the mattress back on afterwards the way he had found it, and the blanket was on it folded and he folded it back the way it was. The two legs on his side went into the dip in the carpet on the first attempt. The other two did not.

With the bed out, the window goes out and up and goes back about a foot and a half, which is as far as that window will go. He went out into the yard in the middle of that afternoon and stood in the open and was out of the wind for the first time in three days. He came back in and said that the back of that shop had always been the same and the window was the reason it was not.

She said, "You'll have all of it now."

She meant the window, and she said so later, and it was not wrong.

She said, "It's more than I had."

---

He came back on the Friday and the Saturday to the front room and the kitchen, and came out to that back room twice.

The bed is against the wall under the window, the same way it was. The window comes down on the bottom sash and then stops against the top of the bed's foot end, four inch, and then the top sash comes down on the weight and that is that.

If the bed were six inch further out from the wall the window would go the whole way. He has worked that out. He did it in his head standing in the doorway on the Saturday morning before he started on the front room, and he has not said it and he is not going to, for three reasons, and the third of them is the real one. It is not her bed to move. It is not a thing a man says to a woman on a Saturday morning. And if he said it, he would be saying it because of what it would get her, and he is not a person who wants to be the one who said that in a room where nobody had asked.

He said nothing about it on the Friday and nothing about it on the Saturday and nothing about it on the Sunday, and she did not ask either, and neither of them has been in that room together since the Sunday afternoon.

There are two marks in the carpet where the legs of that bed have been standing since April. The pile is crushed pale in two rectangles about two inch by three, and the rest of the pile under the bed is the colour it was when the carpet was new. When he put the bed back he put it back about half an inch to the left of where the marks are.

It is visible in the afternoon light from that window, and it is the sort of thing a person sees once and then sees every day.

---

He was there from half past eight until about four on the Sunday.

He did the two shelves in the front room, which is not what he was asked for, and which he mentioned, and she said, "Go on, then," and that was that.

He had the back of that room to himself from about midday until about one and spent a lot of that time on the sill, and came down and went and stood at the window instead.

At about half past three he went out to the back room to put his bag down, and the bed was there with the window four inch open on it, and there was a sheet of newspaper on the sill under the open gap because the frame is old and it weeps at that corner. The newspaper was one she had put there herself at some point in the summer and it was soaked through in the middle.

He picked it up and looked at it and put it back the way it had been, with the wet side up, because that was the way it was.

He put his hand flat on the top of the foot end of that bed and left it there for a second, and it was cold, and he took his hand off it, and he did not know he was going to do that until it had happened.

At four o'clock he shut the door and it shut, and the light went on the landing from the shop's own light through the fanlight for about a minute after he had gone down.

**A BED IS UNDER A WINDOW OFF THAT PARADE AND A WINDOW THAT WAS MEANT TO OPEN ALL THE WAY OPENS FOUR INCH, AND THE MARKS IN THE CARPET ARE HALF AN INCH TO THE RIGHT OF WHERE THE LEGS ARE.**
