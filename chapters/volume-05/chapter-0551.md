# Chapter 0551 — The Tuesdays at Seven

The Thursday of the fifth of March 2020, and from about ten past six in the morning until about half past seven in the evening, in one street of this borough.

**A MAN ON THE FIRST OF THAT BLOCK HAS DONE ONE THING ON THE SAME MORNING EVERY WEEK FOR TWO YEARS AND HE STOPPED IT AFTER SOMEBODY CAME TO HIS DOOR, AND THE ONE PERSON WHO HAS NOTICED IS ON THE FLOOR ABOVE AND HAS SAID NOTHING ABOUT IT.**

The block is thirty-eight flats in three storeys and it is on the same system as every other block in that street, which is that it was built in 1963 for people who worked on the water and it was converted about 1990 and the water in it has never been right.

The cold is on the ground floor. The cold is on every Tuesday at about seven in the morning, and it lasts about an hour, and it comes back on its own at about eight, and it has done that for long enough that a dozen people in that block could tell you about it and every one of them still can.

He is a man in his late forties. He has been in the flat on the first since 2016 and he has been writing it down since the March of 2018, on the day after, in a book, in a column, with the day and the hour and what the tap did.

He has never shown it to anybody. Nobody in that block has ever asked to see it. The woman in her early sixties on the second floor has seen him with it, because she has seen him with a pen in his own door on a Tuesday, and she has said nothing, and she has said nothing on any of them.

Nobody knows what is in that book. It is a column of days and hours and a note of what the tap did, and it is about a third of a way down a first column and it stops there.

---

The twelfth of February was a Wednesday and it is the only day in two years that anything came to that door about this.

A man came at about half past ten in the morning carrying more than he had any reason to be carrying, and the woman in her early sixties let him in because the lock on that door sticks and it sticks for everybody, and he was in for about twenty minutes and he went into the space under the window at the end of the flat and opened the door of it and looked inside and shut it again.

He did not look at the tap. He did not turn it. He did not ask what the problem was, and he did not ask the man in his late forties a single question, and he wrote nothing down, and when he went he said that he would be in touch and he has not been in touch and nobody has heard from him since.

After he had gone, the man in his late forties did not write anything down. He has not written anything down on a Tuesday since.

There have been three Tuesdays since. The eighteenth of February, the twenty-fifth, and the third of March. On all three of them the cold came at about seven and went at about eight, and on all three of them the man in his late forties got up, made a hot drink, sat in his own front part, and did not write the line.

What he has not done is gone and looked.

He did that once, in 2018, in the first month of writing them down, and he went round the whole of that block at about a quarter past eight on one of them and put his hand on the outside of every door on the way, and on six of them somebody answered, and on four of them nobody did, and on two of them the person who answered said the same thing he would have said.

He never did it again. That was the whole of the exercise and it lasted one morning.

What he does instead is get up. On the eighteenth of February he was up at ten to seven and sat in his own front part with the hot drink until about a quarter to eight. On the twenty-fifth he was up at five to seven and did the same. On the third of March he was up at ten past eight, which is not a thing he does, and that was the one of the three that was not like the other two.

---
On the Tuesday the woman in her early sixties noticed that she had heard him up at ten to seven and down again, and she thought about going up and knocking and did not, and she has thought about it on every one of them since.

She has not knocked. There are four ways of asking a man about a thing like that and all four of them are worse than not asking, and she worked all four of them out on the evening of the eighteenth and put them away and has not taken them out again.

The block is thirty-eight flats and there is no way in it to say to a man: you have written that down for two years and a man came and looked at a cupboard and now you have stopped. There is no sentence, and she is a woman who has never once in six years made a sentence she could not finish, and she has not made this one.


On the third of March he got up at ten past eight, which is not a thing he does, and went and stood at the tap in the kitchen and turned it and there was water in it.

He stood there for a minute. Then he made the hot drink again and sat back down.

The woman in her early sixties came up at about half past four on the Thursday, which is the day after, and knocked on his door, and he opened it.

"You were up early," she said.

"I couldn't sleep."

She said, "It's been doing it three weeks now, that." And she said it as a thing she was telling him about the weather, and she was not asking him anything, and she has never once in six years asked him anything.

"I know," he said.

And that is the whole conversation, and she went back down, and he shut the door, and the book is where it always was, and it is closed, and the last line in it is the fourth of February, and there is a month of blank after it, which is the first blank of any kind in the whole of that column.

He has not told the woman in her early sixties that he has stopped. He has not told anybody in that block. He is not going to, and if anybody asked him why he had stopped he would have to say that a man came, and that would be the whole of the answer, and he would rather have no answer at all.

**THE COLUMN STOPS AT THE FOURTH OF FEBRUARY AND A MAN LOOKED INSIDE A CUPBOARD ON THE TWELFTH AND LEFT, AND THREE TUESDAYS HAVE GONE BY UNWRITTEN, AND THE ONLY PERSON WHO HAS SAID ANYTHING ABOUT IT IN THAT BLOCK SAID IT ABOUT THE WEATHER.**
