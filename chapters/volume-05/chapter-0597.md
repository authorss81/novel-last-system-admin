# Chapter 0597 — The Long Ones With the Handle

The Tuesday of the sixth of October 2020, from about ten in the morning until about half past four in the afternoon, at a house on an estate in this borough.

**SHE SAID ONE SENTENCE ABOUT A MAN WHO IS NOT IN THIS BOROUGH ON THE WEDNESDAY BEFORE, AND ON THE SATURDAY SHE WENT AND BOUGHT A THING FOR HIM AND BROUGHT IT HOME AND PUT IT IN THE DRAWER WHERE HIS ARE, AND IT WORKS ON A CUP AND IT WILL NEVER DO WHAT SHE BOUGHT IT FOR, AND IT IS NOT THROWN OUT, AND NEITHER OF THEM CAN SAY WHERE HIS WENT.**

They have been married thirty-one years. He is her brother's boy and came to that house in his twenties and has not left, and he is a man in his mid fifties, and she is a woman in her mid fifties, and the two of them are the same age within a few months of each other and neither of them has ever said so.

The sentence was about her brother, who lives a long way off and whom she has not spoken to since the June.

---

Here is the sentence.

She said it on the Wednesday before, at about half past nine at night, and he was in the chair and the programme was on and she was not looking at him.

She said, "He's started having trouble with his hands."

And he said, "Right."

And she said, "His hands, not his legs. I want that said."

And he said, "Right," and that was all of it, and they watched the rest of it and went to bed.

The whole of what the woman in her mid fifties did, and of what the man in her mid fifties did, is the next three paragraphs.

She thought about it for three days and said nothing more about it, and on the Saturday she got the bus into town and went to a shop that sells the sort of thing, and she was there about an hour and a half, and this is what happened in it.

The thing she went in for is a pair of long tongs with rubber ends, for picking things up off the floor without going down to them. She had seen them in a window in the August and had not gone in. She went on the Saturday because she had a reason now.

She tried two pairs. The first one she could not get closed, because her grip is not the grip they are for. The second one she could close and could open, and she could pick a cup off the floor with them, and she stood in that shop for about twenty minutes with a tin of biscuits in them doing that, twice, and it works.

She paid for the second pair and was given a paper bag and she carried it home on the bus.

She got in at about twenty past twelve and unpacked the bag in the kitchen and put the tongs in the drawer of the sideboard where his are, at the front, so she could get at them.

She had a go with them on the Sunday in the front room, on her own, on a cushion that had gone over the arm of the chair. She could pick a cup up. She could pick a remote up off the carpet, which is what she wanted them for.

And on the Monday morning she went to that drawer for them and they were not in it.

She looked for about an hour. She looked in the other drawers and in the cupboard and on the top of the fridge and under the sink, and she looked in the box in the garage that has his things in it that she has never opened, and she did not find them, and neither pair was thrown out, because neither pair is the sort of thing anybody throws out, and the bin went as usual.

She asked him, once, last night, whether he had moved the sideboard.

He said, "No."

And she said, "Have you had a go in that drawer?"

He said, "No."

And she said, "All right," and that was the whole of it.

Here is the other half of it, which is that the new ones do not do the thing.

And they will not do the thing. The thing is her brother's hands, and her brother's hands are in another county, and a pair of tongs does not travel and cannot be sent and cannot be given, and she bought them for a man about a hundred and thirty miles away who does not know she has been thinking about his hands and who would not know what to do with a pair of tongs if they arrived, because tongs are for a person who cannot bend down, and he can bend down perfectly well.

He has not mentioned them. He was not told she had gone into town.

Here is the part about his pair, which is the other half of what the drawer is.

His came into that house in the June in a carrier bag with a bit of tape on the handle. They are the same make and a slightly different colour. They are the ones he left behind in the March, and he has never asked for them and has never mentioned them and has never once said he left them.

She has never wondered where they were. She has used them maybe six times since the June, mostly for a cup that had gone over, and she has never moved them out of that drawer.

---

The new ones were in that drawer on the Saturday afternoon when she put them there, because she put them there, and she is certain of that, and he is not a person who goes in a sideboard.

Here is what that leaves, and it is worth putting down plainly, because there are only three things it can be and none of them is a good one.

She put them in and has forgotten which drawer. There are four in that sideboard and she has used the top one for years and the other three are for things she has not needed since the March. That is the ordinary answer and she does not believe it, because she is a person who knows which drawer she has just used.

He moved them. That would take about four seconds, and he was in that kitchen on the Saturday and the Sunday and the Monday and last night, and he does not go in the sideboard, and he said no last night without any pause in it at all, which she noticed and has not mentioned.

Or they are behind the sugar, which is where things go in that house, and which she did not look behind, because looking behind the sugar in that sideboard is a thing she has never had to do in her life.

That is three. The tongs are a good make and they work on a cup and they work on a remote and they will never do the thing they were bought for, and neither pair is in a drawer in this borough, and nobody has thrown either of them out, and she will go back into town in about six weeks for the same thing and put it in the same place.

**SHE BOUGHT A THING FOR A MAN A HUNDRED AND THIRTY MILES AWAY WHO HAS NOT SPOKEN TO HER SINCE THE JUNE, AND IT WORKS ON A CUP, AND HIS ARE NOT THROWN OUT AND ARE ALSO NOT IN THAT DRAWER, AND NEITHER OF THEM CAN SAY WHERE THEY WENT, AND SHE WILL DO IT AGAIN.**
