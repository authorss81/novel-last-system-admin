# Chapter 0230 — Four

Sunday the twenty-seventh of March 2016, from about ten in the morning, a room over a shop on a parade in Tideglass, a trade counter on the same parade, a cabin on a trade estate, and a forecourt four miles out.

**THE TRADE IS A WEAPON AND IT IS ONE BECAUSE NOBODY HAS TO USE IT AS ONE, AND A BOROUGH THAT DECIDED ITS CREWS DID NO MORE WORK OVER THE WATER WOULD FIND, IN ABOUT THREE WEEKS, THAT ITS OWN INSURANCE, ITS OWN PARTS, ITS OWN HIRE AND SOMETIMES ITS OWN FUEL WERE MORE EXPENSIVE, AND NOBODY WOULD HAVE HAD TO DO ANYTHING HOSTILE, AND THE PEOPLE WHO WOULD HAVE ENJOYED IT ARE PEOPLE THIS BOROUGH HAS NEVER MET.**

The question was whether somebody in this borough who wanted to stop could stop, and the honest answer was that they could, and that it would not feel like stopping.

He put it in the room over the shop on the Saturday night before, as a question and not as anything else, and Hanna Wray had the answer before he had finished the second half of it.

"Four," she said. "It's four, and it's been four for years, and there's nothing secret about it, they're just the four that come up the road."

Corrance Supply. Sarn Aggregates for the stone and the sand and the cement. Lowdale Hire for the plant and the skip and the van. And the fuel, which was Marchmont, who had the account on the two trucks that stand on that hard standing and had terms on it, and who did not ask what the trucks did with the rest of the day.

"And all four of them sell into that yard," said Jonas Mercer.

"All four of them sell into that yard, yes."

"Since when."

"I don't know. Long time. There's a trade out there you've never heard of and everybody in it has got the same four and there's a price on everything and it's the cheapest price most of us have ever seen, and that's not a favour, that's a yard that buys by the pallet." She stopped. "Why?"

"No reason. Go on with your Friday."

---

Corrance Supply has a trade counter at the back of a shop on the same parade and it opens at eight on a Sunday for builders and shuts when they have all been in. The woman on the counter had been there about nine years and did not need to be told anything and was not going to be impressed by a man in a good coat with a question.

"Sorry — are you trade, love?"

"I'm asking about trade."

"Right." She put a box down. "We supply that yard. Everybody supplies that yard, they're the biggest single account on my book and they've been on it since before I had this counter. Two thousand one and six, whatever they've ordered, on the Friday, on an account, thirty days, and they've never been late, not once, in nine years, and I will tell you something for nothing, we have never asked them for a schedule and there is no schedule."

"Would you give them a better rate if they were buying more."

"They're buying plenty."

"Would you give anybody else in this county a better rate than you give them."

She thought about it properly, which took longer than the question had deserved, and Jonas Mercer has never been able to stop being glad of the way people do that.

"I don't know what the rate is. There's a book in the office and I don't know what's in it and I'm not going to go and find out for a man in a coat on a Sunday." She said it without any edge at all. "What I do know is that if that yard went somewhere else in the country tomorrow, it would be a big hole in a week, and they haven't gone anywhere, and they've not been difficult, and there is nothing anybody's done."

**AND THE ONLY LATTICE NOTICE IN A TRADE COUNTER ON A SUNDAY MORNING, printed on a card behind the till and dated to no year anybody could read, and it defines nothing, and nothing on it defines *holder*:**

> **A PRICE IS A THING A PERSON CAN SEE.**
> **A SCHEDULE IS A THING A PERSON CAN ASK FOR.**
> **NEITHER OF THOSE IS A DECISION.**

---

Lowdale Hire is a cabin and a yard and a man in it, and Jonas Mercer had driven out to it because it was the one that had a telephone and the telephone was the only thing that could be got out of it.

"Can I ask you a question that isn't about prices."

"You can ask."

"If this borough stopped sending crews over there, entirely, in the summer — what happens to your plant hire in this borough?"

The man in the cabin looked at him for about four seconds.

"It'd go up," he said.

"How much."

"I couldn't tell you. I don't price it, I'd have to ask the office, and if I asked the office today they'd tell me the same as everybody." He turned the radio off, which he had not needed to do. "But I'll tell you the reason, because you asked nicely and it's not a secret. About half my turnover in this borough is trade that goes out of this borough. Van, breaker, mixer, the lot. It goes out on a Friday and it comes back on a Sunday. It comes back on a Sunday because the same crews take it out again, and those crews are the ones you mean, and I don't write them down and neither does anybody, and they ring me when they need a breaker and I send it and it goes out with whoever's got the van." He shrugged. "Take the crews away, the van trade doesn't go with them. That's not a threat, that's a Tuesday."

"And the insurance man."

"He'll tell you himself. He rings me. Half the trades in this borough that he writes for are trades that cross that bridge, and about a third of that is the crews, and he's got a renewal book the size of a telephone directory and I don't doubt he's done the sums on it. He'll put it the way brokers put it, he'll call it exposure, and he'll be right, and it won't be a word anybody in your borough has said to him, because you don't say it."

The forecourt card on the way back said MARCEMONT, and the price per litre on it was about a tenth above what the shared yard gets, and the forecourt had been there fourteen years and the card had been repainted twice and nobody had ever asked the man on the till about it, and he was not going to be asked about it, and Jonas Mercer did not ask him about it.

He sat in the car on the parade for about ten minutes and did not put the engine on.

Nobody had done anything. There had been no approach, no letter, no meeting, no pressure and no conversation in which anybody said anything to anybody. Four companies had supplied a yard for twenty-nine years, and a supply is not a favour, and being the cheapest thing on a shelf is a fact about a price, and a man with a book in an office is a man with a book in an office. And in about three weeks a borough that stopped sending crews over the water would find its own cover dearer and its own parts dearer and its own plant dearer, and some weeks its own fuel dearer, and there would be nothing anybody could point at and nothing anybody would have had to do.

**AND A REGION CAN BE WEAPONISED BY ARITHMETIC, AND HE SAID THE OTHER HALF OF IT OUT LOUD IN A CAR ON A PARADE AND SAID IT TO HIMSELF AND TO NOBODY, IN A VOICE THAT DID NOT GO ABOVE A NORMAL ONE:**

*They did not do anything to us. They sold us what we asked for.*

He did not write it down. He had the notebook in the inside pocket and it was still in the inside pocket at the bottom of the parade, and he had the two halves of it on the back of an order pad in a handbag in a shop and a piece of paper with a number on it in a coat, and there was nobody in this borough he could have put them in front of that would have been able to hold both of them, and there was a version of the argument that would have settled three things at once and it rested on a document that could not be produced, and the eleventh refusal stood where it had stood since September, and a twelfth did not arrive, and nobody in this borough was going to be able to say that anybody had threatened them.

**AND A CAR STOOD ON A PARADE FOR ABOUT TEN MINUTES WITH THE ENGINE OFF AND A MAN IN IT, AND FOUR COMPANIES IN A COUNTY WENT ON SUPPLYING A SHARED YARD ON THE SAME TERMS, AND A PLACE WITH NOTHING IN FORCE IN IT IS NOT A PERSON WITH NO ADDRESS, AND HE HAD ALMOST WRITTEN THAT DOWN ON THE THURSDAY AND HAD NOT WRITTEN IT ON THE THURSDAY.**
