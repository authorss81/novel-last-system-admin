# Chapter 0566 — Two Years of One Figure

The Wednesday of the twentieth of May 2020, and about ten minutes of it, in one kitchen in this borough and in two bank accounts that have never been in the same place.

**THEY HAVE PUT THE SAME MONEY INTO THE SAME THING SINCE 2017 AND ONE OF THEM HAS PAID TWICE IN ONE MONTH AND NOTHING IN TWO OTHERS SINCE MARCH, AND THE OTHER ONE WORKED THAT OUT ON THE TWENTIETH OF MAY BY ADDING UP TWO YEARS OF HER OWN ENTRIES, AND THE WHOLE OF WHAT SHE KNOWS IS STILL ONLY HERS.**

The plot is ten rods off a road in this borough and it is one of about thirty in that part and it has belonged to the two of them since 2017. There is a gate at the top end, a water butt beside it, a path down the middle that has not been re-laid since either of them can remember, and six lengths along the high side that have never been anything but beans.

There is a rent and it goes up most years and a water charge on top of it. Both of those come out of one account, and for three years that account has had the same two figures going into it on the same day of the same month.

The two of them are sisters. The older is in her mid forties and the younger is in her late twenties, and they have shared that plot since 2017 and have never once had a conversation about the money, because there has never been anything to have a conversation about.

On the twentieth of May the older one was in her kitchen going through her own account for something that had nothing to do with it, and having got there she ran her thumb down two years of it, which is about what anybody does at that point.

For two years there had been two amounts going in on the same day, and they were the same amount, and they came from different people.

Going down that stretch she found that in March there was one. In April there were two. In May there was one. Two years of pairs, and three months at the end of it that were not pairs.

She read it twice and then she sat with her hands flat in front of her and worked the rest of the year out in her head, and it came to exactly what her sister's share had been every month since 2017, and it did not come to a penny more or a penny less than that, and it came to the same thing again when she did it the second way.

The younger sister had not stopped paying in April. She had paid twice in April because nothing had gone in for March and she would not leave a month standing open in the middle of a year.

---

Nothing about it was said.

They were at the plot on the Sunday before, about ten in the morning, at the top end by the butt, and the younger one carried a can of water the whole length of the path without putting it down once, and the older one weeded the two ends of the third length and did not ask, and they talked about a neighbour's dog for about a minute and then did not talk.

On the Sunday before that they were there again and the same thing happened, and on the Sunday before that as well.

The rent came out on the first of the month, as it has since 2017, and it came out short by one share, and nobody rang anybody.

She is not going to say it. There is no sentence in her for it, because the money is not hers and the plot is not hers either, and in three years not one of them has ever wanted a return from the other, and by the time the money had been missing three months it had stopped being anything she could put into a sentence.

She has not mentioned it to the younger one and she has not mentioned it to anybody else, and she has not gone back into March or April or into the twentieth to look at it a second time, and she has not once said out loud, in her own kitchen, that she has noticed.

What she does is that she goes to the plot on the Sundays, and she stays longer than she used to, and she does the two ends of the third length, and she does not let the conversation get anywhere near it. The younger one comes at ten and stays an hour and puts her hand on the gate on the way out, both times, and neither of the two of them has said one word about the three months.
