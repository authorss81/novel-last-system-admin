# Chapter 0505 — Seven Days of It

The Saturday of the thirteenth of July 2019, and the whole of it lay between about a quarter past nine and about four in the afternoon. A terrace of fourteen houses, and the hill that goes down from it to the water, in Tideglass.

**A MAN WENT DOWN THAT HILL EVERY MORNING AT ABOUT HALF PAST SIX AND INTO THE BATHS AND WAS BACK ON THAT TERRACE BY NINE, AND HE DID IT EVERY MORNING SINCE THE SUMMER OF 2015, AND ON THE FRIDAY OF THE FIFTH OF JULY HE DID NOT, AND HE HAS SAID NOTHING TO HIS WIFE SINCE, AND SHE FOUND THE NAIL BARE ON THE SATURDAY SIXTH, AND THERE IS NO WAY FOR HER TO RAISE IT, BECAUSE THE ONLY VERSION OF THAT QUESTION WHICH WORKS TELLS HIM WHAT SHE HAS BEEN DOING WITH HER EARS, AND WHAT HE GAVE FOR IT IS IN HIS OWN SPINE AND WHAT SHE IS CARRYING IS SEVEN DAYS OF KNOWING WITH NOWHERE TO SPEND IT.**

He was in the building trade until 2008 and came off it in a fortnight and has done a bit of this and that since without it amounting to anything anybody could call work. He has two bad discs and one of them is worse than the other, and he has known for about two years that swimming is the only thing he has found that keeps him able to get out of a bath at night.

He goes down that hill at half past six because the baths shut at half past nine and it is twenty-five minutes with the last quarter of it steep.

He has done it every morning since the summer of 2015, bar a dozen days for the weather and once when he was ill.

He has not missed one since 2015 because he had decided to. That is what makes it different from a habit, and it is what his wife has been able to build a thing out of without ever saying so.


She has been in number nine since 1988 and has not worked since 2011.

What she has is the ordinary shape of a morning, and what she has built inside the ordinary shape of a morning is this.

She is not asleep when he goes. She has not been asleep when he goes for about two years, and before that she was, and the difference between the two eras is not a thing she has ever had to think about because it has never come up.

She hears the door. She hears his boots on the steps down from their own front room to the front of the house, which she can count and has counted without once meaning to. Then she hears the front door shut and after that she hears nothing for a bit over two hours, because he is down there and the water is loud enough on its own.

Then the front door again, and the boots on the steps, and a door at the back of the house, and then him in the kitchen, and then the front room, and then a long stretch of nothing whatever.

On the Saturday of the sixth of July, the boots went down at about half past six and the front door went, and at about ten past nine that morning she got up and went into the front room.

There was a nail by the door with nothing on it.

The towel lives on that nail. It has lived on that nail since 2015 and it is a bath sheet, an old one, a brown one that was her mother's, and it goes on the nail wet and comes off it about the middle of the morning and goes out on the line behind the house.

There was nothing on the nail. The line behind the house had nothing on it either. The towel was in the cupboard on the top, which is where it goes when it has not been used.

She stood in that room for about a minute.

Then she made the bed and did the things she does, and at about half past nine she went down and sat on the front step for ten minutes or so in the sun.


And here is the whole of what he did on the Friday, and it took him about four seconds to decide and it will take him a long time to put into words.

He got up at about six as he does. He put his socks on. He got to the top of the steps and put his hand flat on the wall the way he does, because the wall is what he puts his hand on, and it had not rained since the Tuesday and there was a pipe on that wall that is cold whatever the night has done.

He stood at the bottom and worked out that the bottom of that hill and the top of it again were both finished for him, and that what he actually felt was not in his hip and was not the thing he has told the doctor about.

It was the second disc.

It had been going for about a fortnight, and the two or three weeks before that it was the kind of going a man can talk himself through, and on the Friday morning of the fifth of July it was not that any more, and he knew it was not any more about two thirds of the way down the first hundred yard, before the hill turned.

What he thought about, standing there with a sock on one foot, was that there is no version of coming back up that hill tomorrow and no version of going down it after that, and that in a week or so he is going to have to stand in front of a person in a room and say he cannot manage that hill any more, and that that sentence, in this house, with this woman in it, is not a sentence about a back.

It is a sentence about being a man who cannot get about, and everything she has ever done about him in twenty-six years was to do more of it.

That is the reason. It took him about four seconds and he has said none of it.

He went back up and hung the towel on the nail himself, because he is the one who hangs it, and because if it was off the nail with nothing on it she would see it before she had made a cup of tea, and he thought about that for about half a second on the Friday morning and has not been able to put it down since.

She saw it at about twenty past six.

She stood at the bottom of those steps and looked at the nail for the count of four, and then she went back up and made the tea and said nothing about it, and she has said nothing since.

---

And on this Saturday, the thirteenth, seven days after she found out, here is what happened.

He got up at about six as he does, and he did not put his socks on, and he sat on the edge of the bed until he had counted sixty, and then he got up and went down and put the towel on the nail where it lives, wet, because it had been in the cupboard since Friday and he had run it under the hot tap in the kitchen at about ten to six that morning and it was running over the side of the bowl and onto the floor, which she has not mentioned and has been hearing since.

And she came down at about seven and saw it and stood behind her and said nothing.

He said, "That's gone down with the water."

And she said, "Right."

And that was the whole of it, and about half of that was about a towel.


What she has been doing about it is nothing. What she has been doing inside it is a great deal, and here it is.

She has worked out that she cannot ask him why he stopped, and the reason is not kindness and it is not manners.

If she asks, then from that moment the two of them are two people who have been keeping a count. And he has not kept a count. He has got out of bed and gone down a hill. She has listened to steps and a door and a door again since 2015 and that is a thing she has done without ever once putting her hand on it or looking at it, and it is hers and she has never had to be a person who does it.

Asking would hand it to him. It would say: I have been listening to those steps since before we were married, and I know what a missing morning sounds like.

So she has not asked. And she has not said anything else about it either. And she has been getting up at half past six anyway.


On the Sunday she asked him a different question.

She came down a little after seven and he was in the front room and she said, "Are you going to see somebody about it?"

And he said, "No."

And she said, "Right."

And she went and made the tea and that was the whole of the Sunday.

He has had three appointments in his life about his back and has told her about one of them.

What he has not said, and what she does not know, is that he wrote the number of the surgery on the reverse of a bit of paper in the cupboard under the stairs in about three seconds in June, and that it is still in there, and that he has gone to that cupboard twice since the Friday and has not gone in.

---

And in the two hours after that on the Saturday morning, this Saturday, he did the thing he has done every morning for seven days.

He goes down and sits on the front step with a cup of tea from about half past seven until about half past nine, in the sun, on the left, facing out.

He was out there from about a quarter past nine, which is the hour he is normally back, and she came down and stood behind him for about half a minute and went in again.

Here is what he does in the two hours after that, and she has never asked him about it and has watched it out of the front room maybe three times in all those years.

He sits and looks at the hill for ten minutes or so and does not want to go down it.

That is the whole of it, and there is nothing in it about the water.

It took him about three weeks to work out what he had lost. It was not the swimming. It was being a man walking towards something at six in the morning.


At about four o'clock on the afternoon of the thirteenth a man from the top of that terrace walked over to number nine with a drill and put a new washer on the back door of number seven, which has been swinging loose since the Thursday, and the two of them were out on the step for about half an hour while he did it.

She came out with two cups of tea on a tray and gave one to her husband and one to the man, and none of them said anything about anything.

At about twenty past four the man went up with the drill and she went in, and her husband stayed out on the step for another half an hour, and that is the whole of the thirteenth of July 2019 in that house.

**A MAN SAT ON THE FRONT STEP OF NUMBER NINE ON THAT TERRACE FROM ABOUT A QUARTER PAST NINE UNTIL ABOUT HALF PAST FOUR ON THE SATURDAY OF THE THIRTEENTH OF JULY 2019 WITH A CUP BESIDE HIM ON THE BRICK, AND AT ABOUT TEN TO SEVEN THAT MORNING HIS WIFE HAD CAME DOWN AND STOOD BEHIND HIM AND LOOKED AT A WET TOWEL ON A NAIL AND SAID NOTHING.**
