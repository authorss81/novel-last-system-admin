# Chapter 0253 — The Wrong Direction

Tuesday the nineteenth of July 2016, from about ten in the morning, the yard at Penhale, and the trade counter at the back of a shop on the parade of shops in Tideglass.

**A QUESTION WENT OUT OF THIS BOROUGH TO THE OTHER SIDE OF THE ESTUARY INSIDE SOMEBODY ELSE'S FOLDER, AND WHAT CAME BACK WAS CORRECT, AND IT WAS NOT A BILL AND NOT A DEMAND AND NOT A CLAIM, AND IT WAS A SHEET, AND SOMEBODY PUT IT IN A DRAWER, AND THAT IS THE WHOLE OF WHAT HAPPENED.**

The boat went out over the water on the Tuesday at about half past seven in the morning and came back on the Friday, and it is one of about four things that left this borough that week that nobody arranged, and the other three were the engine and a lorry of pipe and a man from the works on the far side of the river, and the two of them that came back came back when they came back.

The yard at Penhale is a bend in a road and it owns nothing, and the reason it owns nothing is the reason it is any use, and the man in his forties who keeps the book under Haldane Perry said so to about four people in the yard that morning without being asked and without appearing to enjoy it.

The folder is a job wallet. It is brown card with a press stud and a great many dockets in it and it belongs to the boat, and it goes out and it comes back, and the man who carries it does not read it, and there is nobody in this borough who could tell you what is written on any of the dockets in it at any time and that has never once mattered.

Somebody had written on the back of the last docket, in a hand that was not a tradesman's hand:

*Who printed the first one, and are they still doing it.*

There was no name on it. There was no address. There was no company and no date and no reference, and it went into a brown card wallet in a boatman's inside pocket at about ten o'clock in the morning, and the boatman said one sentence about it, and the sentence was that it was a terrible way to do a thing, and then he put it in the wallet and did the day's work.

---

On the Friday, in the cabin at Lowdale, the man in it watched a van come onto the forecourt from the direction of the bridge and watched two men get out of it and one of them go round to the back and come back with a brown card wallet, and he said nothing about it, because he has never once been asked about anything by anybody in this borough and he is not going to start, and his whole trade is waiting to be asked.

Four companies supplied the yard at Penhale and the four of them were not involved in any part of this, and had no idea that any part of it was happening, and would have supplied the same week at the same rate whether it had or not, and the man in the cabin at Lowdale has got half his turnover in this borough out of work that leaves on a Friday and comes back on the Friday after because the same crews take it out again the following week, and that is the whole of what a week in this borough has been for four months.

A van came back off the bridge on the Friday at about ten past four and went on up the parade and stopped outside a shop that shuts at five.

The wallet came to the trade counter at about a quarter to five, and the man who brought it put it on the counter face down, which is a thing you do with a wallet, and said, "There was a bit of paper in it that wasn't mine," and went out.

Hanna Wray turned it over.

There was no docket on top of it. There was a single sheet of A4, and the side that was showing was blank, and she turned it round.

One line of printing across the top, in about six inches of type, and under it one line, and that was all of it, and there was nothing at the bottom and the back was blank.

*Any part of any system in this region may be used by any person.*

And under that, on its own, centred, one place.

The woman of about fifty-five, who has the room at the end of the first-floor corridor in a building by the water, was in the shop and read it over Hanna Wray's shoulder and said one word.

"Us."

"Us," said Hanna Wray.

---

They took it up the parade to the Methodist hall on Carrow Lane, because that is where a thing like this ends up in this borough, and about seven people were in there and by half past seven there were about nine, and the gas was on because somebody had put a pound in, and nobody saw who.

"That is this borough," said the man in his sixties, standing up, which he does. "That is the name of this borough, spelled right, printed under a line that says that any part of any system in this region may be used by any person. Now I am going to say four things about it and then I am going to stop, and I have said three of them in this hall before and one of them is new.

"First: it is correct. Every word of it. It is as correct as the sheet you have all seen with eight places under the same line, and it is the same six inches of type off the same kind of machine, and if the two of them were printed a fortnight apart in a room with one window, I could not tell you which was which. Second: it is not a bill, it is not a demand, and it is not a claim, and no money moves because of it and nobody owes anybody anything because of it, and I would like all three of those said out loud in this room tonight because that is what everybody in this borough has been imagining for six weeks. Third, and this is the new one: it is not about us at all. It is about a place. It says a place is one of the places from which a thing may be used. It does not say anybody is coming, it does not say anything is owed, it does not say anybody has to do anything, and it does not say that a person who lives there has been counted or heard from or written down anywhere. It is a printed place name under a printed sentence, and a printed place name is a fact about printing."

"And the fourth thing," said the young woman of about twenty.

"The fourth thing is that it does not answer the question," said the man in his sixties. "That is the entire finding and I have been in this borough for six years and I have never had less to say. A lad wrote two questions on the back of a docket about who printed the first one and whether they are still doing it, and somebody over there read two questions about a person and answered with a place, and that is a perfectly good answer to a question nobody asked, and it is correct, and it went in a wallet and across an estuary and it cost a boat a day of a man's time, and there is nobody on either side of that water who has done anything wrong or anything clever or anything hostile, and if anybody in this room goes home tonight and starts building a thing out of that, I will say to their face that they have made it up and they will have had to."

Nobody in that hall said who printed the first one, and nobody said whether they were still doing it, and the man of about eighteen was in the room and did not ask it again, because he had asked it once, on the fourth of June, and a man had told him in front of nine people to say it again slower and slower, and the second half of that instruction was not about speed.

The woman of about fifty-five wanted to know whether it came from the same machine as the two pieces of A4 that are in a handbag in a building by the water.

"I couldn't tell you," said Hanna Wray, "and I wouldn't, and I'm not going to hold it up to the light in front of people, because that's what you do to a document when you want it to be a document."

It went in the drawer under the trade counter at about a quarter to nine that night, face up, on top of the other one, and the lock went round twice.

**AND A QUESTION WENT OUT IN SOMEBODY ELSE'S FOLDER AND A SHEET CAME BACK WITH THE NAME OF THIS BOROUGH PRINTED UNDER A SENTENCE THAT ANYBODY MAY USE ANY PART OF ANY SYSTEM IN THIS REGION, AND IT IS CORRECT, AND IT ANSWERS SOMETHING ELSE, AND HANNA WRAY TURNED THE KEY TWICE.**
