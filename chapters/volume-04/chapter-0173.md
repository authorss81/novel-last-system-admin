# Chapter 0173 — The Hose Does Not Reach

Thursday the sixteenth of July 2015, from about ten in the morning, a service yard behind a block of flats in Tideglass, and a porters' lobby off it.

**A correct request, granted in one sentence, in a room, with no paper on it, and a bill. The bill is not on a person in a different street this time. The bill is on a man of sixty-three who was standing in that room, who was not asked for anything, and who could not say no because nothing had been put to him. He carried water up a flight of steps four times, in daylight, in front of about nine people, and nobody asked him to and nobody thanked him. He asked no question of anybody. Nobody died and nobody was rescued and it is a different person and a different street and a different summer.**

---

The block has four flats a side and a stairwell and a service yard at the back with a cupboard in the wall, and the cupboard has a standpipe in it and the standpipe is locked and the key is on a board in the porters' lobby, and the board has had a key on it since before anybody in this borough started writing things down.

**The keyholder is a man of about sixty-three who has been the porter of that block for about nine years and is on no rota and there is no rota and is paid at the end of a month out of a book in an office, and the only thing that makes him findable is that he is standing in a doorway holding a key, and that stops the moment he goes inside.**

---

"**I need the yard tap for an hour,**" the man of about forty-two said. "**That machine has to be filled before it will start. Yours are all off, are they.**"

"**All off since Monday. They are re-piping the riser and they are not saying when they are doing the other side.**"

"**Right. So I need about forty litres, and I am not going to put a hose through a flat, and I have got a van and I have got the other one in at two.**"

The man of about forty-two said all of that without asking for any of it, and there were two of them in that doorway and neither of them had come to ask the other for anything, and it took him about nine seconds. The machine is in the hall of the first floor and it came out of the flat this morning on a sack truck and it is a white one and it is not connected to anything.

**Nobody in that yard was asked for anything. It was a doorway and two people in it and a key on a board.**

"**Yes,**" the man of sixty-three said. "**You can have the tap.**"

"**That is it?**"

"**That is it. There is no paper for it. It is a tap.**"

---

**AND THAT IS THE WHOLE OF THE GRANT, AND IT TOOK ABOUT FOUR SECONDS, AND HE WROTE NOTHING DOWN, AND NOBODY IN THAT YARD ASKED HIM TO WRITE ANYTHING DOWN.**

And here is the cost of it, and it was not on anybody's list, and it is not anybody's fault, and it landed in about four seconds and it landed on the man who said yes.

**The standpipe is in a cupboard on the wall of the yard. The tap is about two feet out from the wall and the hose on the reel does not reach the foot of that stairwell and he did not know that until he had the cupboard open.**

There is one hose, on a reel, and it is for washing down the yard, and it is not long enough, and he could have run it along the wall and up the outside of the building and in through a kitchen window and did not do that.

So he filled a bucket from the standpipe and carried it up a flight of steps and put it in the machine, and he carried it up four times.

---

**AND HE CARRIED IT FOUR TIMES, IN DAYLIGHT, AT TEN IN THE MORNING, IN FRONT OF ABOUT NINE PEOPLE, AND EVERY ONE OF THOSE NINE PEOPLE SAW HIM DO IT.**

A woman of about forty-one with a shopping trolley came out of the side passage and stopped. A boy of about sixteen who was sitting on the step got up off it. Two men at the far end of the yard. A woman at a first-floor window. A man who came out of number four to ask about the riser and did not ask about the riser.

**The man of about forty-two with the van did not help him and did not offer and did not say anything about it, and he is right, and it is not on an invoice, and a man who fits washing machines for a living does not carry buckets up a stairwell for free at ten in the morning, and both of those things are correct.**

He put a towel down on the step at the bottom before he started so the yard would not be tracked across, and he did not carry anything up, and he did not offer, and the towel is the whole of what he did and it took him about two seconds and it is the only thing a man of forty-two did in that yard that cost him nothing.

**And a man of thirty-two noticed the towel, and the towel is not on anything either.**

The porter is sixty-three and he is on no rota and nobody in that block asked him to carry anything and nobody in that block knew he was going to carry anything, and he could not have said no, because saying no is a thing you do to a request, and nobody made a request of him, and by about the third trip somebody at the far end of the yard said *let me take that* and he said no and he was right to and he did not explain why and the man at the far end did not ask again.

---

**AND HE DID IT BECAUSE HE SAID YES TO FOUR SECONDS' WORTH OF SOMETHING, AND THE THING HE SAID YES TO WAS A TAP, AND NOBODY HAS SAID THE WORD TAP SINCE.**

**Nobody is a price. Nobody is on a list. There is no form in this borough on which anybody could write that a man of sixty-three carried a bucket of water up a flight of steps four times on the sixteenth of July, and there is not going to be one, and the reason there is not going to be one is that it would have to have a name at the top of it and the name at the top of it would be the name of the person who put the name at the top of it.**

**And there is no way in this borough to stop a thing being handed over.** He was handed a thing. He was handed a tap. And the price of a price is that somebody else pays it somewhere you can look. The price of a correct request is that somebody else pays it here, in a doorway, in daylight, and the difference is that this time the person paying it is close enough to hand the thing over, and he handed it over four times, and nobody asked him to.

---

**AND A MAN OF THIRTY-TWO WAS AT THE BACK OF THAT YARD FOR THE WHOLE OF IT, AND HE ASKED NOBODY ANYTHING, AND HE SAID NOTHING, AND HE DID NOT CARRY A BUCKET.**

He has a word for it. There is a person of nineteen in this borough who has a word for what that is, and she had it in May, on a footbridge, about something else entirely, and she was right about it, and it is her word and it is not his.

**He has got it once and he is not going to use it again. That is not a principle and it is not a rule he has set himself. It is that the word belongs to a person of nineteen who is owed nothing and is owed it and got it for nothing, and saying it here would make it his, and if it were his then he would be the man who named what a porter of sixty-three did with his morning, and he is a man of thirty-two who was standing in a yard with nothing in his hands and who carried nothing, and the word costs the person who carries nothing, and he cannot pay for it.**

So he did not say it. He stood at the back of that yard for about twenty minutes and said nothing and carried nothing and the word stayed where it was.

---

**Nobody in that yard was thanked and nobody refused to be thanked. The machine is connected and it is running and the van went at ten past two and the riser is still off and nobody has said when it goes back on. The register of narrowings is at nineteen and the blank line under the nineteenth is two hundred and thirty-two days old. The card in the slot is a hundred and forty-six days old. The letter in the tray is a hundred and forty-eight days old and it has two pieces of post on it and it has not been opened in front of a second person and is not going to be.**

**And a person who is not counted is not a person who is helped, and a correct request was granted in about four seconds in a doorway on the sixteenth of July, and the whole of the bill fell on the person who said yes, and he is not owed anything by anybody, and there is nobody in this borough he could ask.**
