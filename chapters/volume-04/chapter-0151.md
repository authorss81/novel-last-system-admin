# Chapter 0151 — One Narrow Thing

Thursday the twenty-first of May 2015, from about ten past nine in the morning, a corridor and a board, the east wing, Saint Orra Civic Hospital; and about eleven o'clock, the laundry at the back of that site; and about half past two in the afternoon, the same corridor.

**He asked no question of anybody on that day and nobody asked him anything. One person asked for one thing in writing and got it, in about eleven seconds, from a person who could have said no, and that is the first time in nine months that anybody in this borough has been given something they asked for by a person who could say no. Nobody thanked anybody.**

---

**THE ONLY LATTICE NOTICE IN THAT BUILDING, taped inside the door of the laundry at the back of that site, and it defines nothing and nothing on it defines *holder*, and it requires nothing of anybody:**

> **THE LAUNDRY AT THIS SITE RUNS ON ITS OWN HOURS AND IS NOT PART OF ANY ROTA PUBLISHED IN THIS BOROUGH.**
> **NO PERSON IS ASKED FOR ANYTHING IN ORDER TO HAVE THEIR LINEN COLLECTED.**
> **IF A ROUND IS CHANGED, NO PERSON IS REQUIRED TO BE TOLD WHY.**

---

**And here is the number, and it is the whole of the morning, and it is not a discovery.**

At the end of that corridor there is a piece of hardboard and it says eighty-one, and it is what that building is held to, and on the Thursday morning it was thirty-one degrees outside and there was still no mechanical extract in that corridor, and about two thirds of the eighty-one had been awake since half past four.

Nobody in that building had asked him anything. He had been in the corridor twice since Monday and he was not on a rota and there was no record of it and there was not going to be one, and the whole of the arrangement is that he is a person who is somewhere, and being somewhere is not on a list.

---

**And then, at about eleven o'clock, at the back of that site, in a room the size of a garage with a door that is open and a fan on a stand that was bought by somebody, about five people were in it and one of them was asking for something.**

The laundry is a van, a round, two drivers, and a linen supervisor. **Winifred Crabbe, 62, linen supervisor, nineteen years,** has been reading a laundry list aloud to a room for nineteen years, and on the thirteenth of January in a hall in daylight she said a number out loud and it went into a minute in a building two miles from here, and she is one of the seven filled seats of a body that has not met since the middle of January, and that body is not required to meet, and it has no chair of its own and there never was one, and it cannot require anybody to do anything including her. **She is not on a rota of that laundry. There is not one. She is not owed anything by anybody in this borough and she is not owed anything by the woman who asked her.**

**The Friday round is the big one. Twenty-two stops. The van leaves the site at about two and the round is built in an order and the order has been the same for years, and the last four stops are the ones that get the linen worst, because a loaded van in the sun in May is a warm box and the last four are at the end of the round and not near the site.**

The driver of the Friday round is a woman of about forty-four who has been on that round nine years and who is not on any list in this city and who is not a thread and is not owed anything by anybody.

She had written it out on the back of a delivery note. That is all it was on.

"**I want the last four done first.**"

Winifred Crabbe read it standing up, the way she reads things.

"**Why.**"

"**Because stop eighteen has got a woman in it who's ninety-one and she cannot get to a laundrette and she is not on any round but ours, and stop twenty-one is a house with nobody in it in the day, and the linen sits in a porch on stop twenty-two until about half past three, and on Friday the fifteenth the van thermometer on that round read thirty-one at the last four, and I took them off the back of the van at about ten past five and they were not linen, they were hot towels.**"

---

**And then she said the second half, and the second half is the part that makes it a correct request, and it is correct.**

"**I am not asking for a new round. I am asking for the order of the four. Eighteen stops stay exactly where they are, same day, same times, same driver, same van. I want four of them moved to the front. That is all it is. It is on the back of a delivery note because I did not know whether you would take it off a piece of paper.**"

---

**AND WINIFRED CRABBE COULD HAVE SAID NO. THAT IS THE WHOLE OF WHAT HAPPENED AND IT IS THE WHOLE OF WHY IT MATTERS.**

There is no meeting. There is no minute. There is no form and no box and no name and no reference, and nobody in that room is obliged to be there and nobody in that room is owed anything, and the body the linen supervisor sits on has not met since the middle of January and cannot require her to do a single thing, and the woman who asked is owed nothing by the borough and is owed nothing by anybody in that room.

**A person who could say no, said yes, in about eleven seconds, to one narrow thing, and that is how long it takes to hand a man a piece of paper with a date on it, and nobody in that room found that remarkable and nobody in this borough is going to.**

"**Right. The last four first. From Friday.**"

"**From Friday.**"

"**Stop eighteen is ninety-one.**"

"**Stop eighteen is ninety-one.**"

"**Then I will do it and I will not write it down, because if I write it down then it is a change of round and a change of round is a thing that somebody can point at in four years, and I have got a word for that and the word is not pleasant.**"

---

**AND HERE IS WHAT IT COST, AND SOMEBODY HAS TO LIVE WITH IT, AND IT IS NOT THE PERSON WHO ASKED.**

Winifred Crabbe did the arithmetic out loud, once, in about four seconds, standing up, and nobody stopped her.

**The east wing of that hospital is stop nine on the Friday round. Nine of twenty-two. If the last four go to the front then nine goes to about nine in the evening, and the linen comes off the van at about nine at night in a heat and goes into a cupboard in a corridor with no mechanical extract, and the sheets are on a bed at about ten.**

She said: "**So bed nineteen is going to have a warm sheet on it on Friday night and it will still be warm at six in the morning.**"

"**Yes.**"

"**And Mrs Adeyemi in bed nineteen said it to me on the Monday and I have written nothing down about it, and there is nowhere for me to write it, and I am not going to.**"

"**I know you haven't.**"

"**Then you had better not either.**"

"**I have not and I am not going to and I could not if I wanted to, because nobody in this borough has ever given me a piece of paper with a box on it and I have not got a way of asking for one and I would not know what to write in it.**"

---

**AND HE WAS IN THAT ROOM FOR THE WHOLE OF IT, AND HE ASKED NOBODY ANYTHING, AND THE FINDING IS HIS, AND A CORRECT THING IN THIS BOROUGH HAS NOT LOOKED LIKE THE SHAPE OF ANYTHING FOR AS LONG AS HE CAN REMEMBER, AND IT IS NOT A SENTENCE AND IT IS NOT GOING ANYWHERE.**

**A person asked for one narrow thing. It was correct. Somebody who could say no said yes, and nobody in that room made it into anything, and there is no minute of it, and nobody wrote it down, and the only two people who know why it happened are the two of them, and he was in that room for the whole of it and did not know it was happening until a woman told him the linen was going to be warm.**

**And that is a shape.**

He could see it from the doorway. He could see it exactly.

**It is not the shape of anything he knows. It is the shape of one correct request being granted once, by a person with the power to refuse, for no reason, on their own time, and it holding because nothing on earth is holding it. And there is no instrument anywhere in this city that can see that shape, because the instrument that would see it is a form, and a form would have turned it into a change of round, and a change of round is a thing that can be pointed at in four years.**

**And the cost of it does not go to the person who asked. It goes to a woman of about ninety-one who will get her linen at half past three, and to a woman in bed nineteen who will have a warm sheet on it, and neither of them is in the room and neither of them is on anything and neither of them is going to be told.**

**And he did not write it down. That is the whole of what he did. He did not write it down, and the reason is not a better reason than any other: there is no box on it, and a thing with no box on it in a book at the end of a street is a thing that one person is the only keeper of, and he has been the only keeper of that book since the fourth of September, and in a few years somebody is going to be able to describe exactly what that book contains.**

---

**And it is not a service, and the finding of that is the last line of it: the counter is not the one who grants it. A counter in that city gives a person what was true on a day and a date, and it cannot give anybody anything, and it does not scale, and it works because a person is standing at it who is not findable. A linen supervisor on her own time in a room the size of a garage can say yes to one narrow thing once, and there is no way at all to ask her for a second one, because asking her for a second one would be requiring her, and the only body in this borough that could require anybody has not sat since January. And that is not a service and it is not a scheme and it is not going anywhere.**

---

**And the school on Tarn Street closed at one o'clock that afternoon, because it has no water above the first floor, and about ninety letters went home in the pockets of about two hundred and forty children, and about half of them came back on the Friday, and the ones that came back were about the heat and about the water and about whether the school would be open on Monday. The ones that did not come back are not in any record, and there is nowhere in this borough for a child's name to be written next to a letter that was not brought back, and a child who did not bring a letter back is a child who is fine.**

**Nobody was harmed. Nobody was helped. The register of narrowings is at nineteen entries and the blank line under the nineteenth is a hundred and seventy-six days old and it did not move, and a heat wave is not a reason to write a form.**

**AND THIS IS THE LAST OF IT, AND IT IS A THING HE CAN SEE AND CANNOT HOLD AND IS NOT GOING TO PUT DOWN.**

**A correct request that is granted once is a precedent, and a precedent does not need anybody to decide anything, and the whole of the danger of a precedent is that the next one is easier and the one after that costs less, and there is no form, and he is standing in a corridor on a Thursday afternoon watching it happen to a woman with a delivery note, and he is the only person in this borough who can see the shape of it, and he cannot say a word.**

**He has not written it down. He is not going to. And the reason is not a better reason than any other: a pad that goes in a shop at the end of a street is a piece of paper, and a piece of paper with a delivery note in it is a piece of paper that somebody can produce.**
