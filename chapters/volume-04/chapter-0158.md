# Chapter 0158 — Nobody Can Put You In It

Thursday the eighteenth of June 2015, from about half past five in the afternoon, a canteen at the back of the Saint Orra Civic Hospital site.

**A man who stood in a corridor in this borough in May and read a sheet and asked a question it was never written to answer came back on his own. Nobody followed him up, nobody looked for him, he is not a thread, and he came because he had a question of his own. He asked Jonas Mercer one question, and it is the only question anybody has put to him in four weeks, and he answered it, and the answer is worth nothing and it is correct. Nobody was helped. Nobody was harmed. Nothing was written down.**

---

He had come off that corridor and gone into the canteen because there is a fan in the canteen.

That is the whole of how he was there. He has been in the east wing of that hospital three times since Monday in a heat and nobody has asked him anything on any of the three visits and nobody is going to, and there are eighty-one beds on a board at the end of that corridor and the wing has no mechanical extract, and the fan in the canteen is the reason he went in and not the corridor.

There were about ten people in it and a hatch and a counter and a man of about thirty-three at a table by the wall with a tray he had not touched.

---

**AND HE KNEW HIM, AND HE DID NOT ASK HOW, AND THE REASON HE DID NOT ASK IS THE ONLY THING HE DID IN THAT CANTEEN THAT WAS NOT PASSIVE.**

Two tired men who are not on anything read a term of six days aloud to each other and there is no date on it, and a man who stands at walls has been in rooms where nothing is said all spring.

**SOLOMON ADJEI, 33.** He works nights and has been on nothing since he was twenty, and on the second of May a man at work told him there was a minute you could have for nothing and that you did not have to ask anybody for it, and he came in on a Saturday morning and stood in a corridor on the side with the fire door on the wedge and read the fourth item and read it again, because in the middle of that item there is a sentence about what this floor is for, and the sentence names a class of people, and the class has no members in it that anybody can point at.

He asked two questions at that counter. He was told the wall. The woman who told him the wall was correct and she told him the same thing twice.

He did not say the name of the person he was asking about, and that was correct, and nobody in this borough is going to be told about it either.

"**You were at the wall,**" the man said. "**In May. I didn't know you were there that morning. I know who you are now and it's taken me six weeks, which I think is about right.**"

"**I was at the wall.**"

"**You didn't say anything.**"

"**No.**"

"**I know. That's why I'm sat here.**"

---

**AND HE SAID WHY HE CAME, AND IT WAS A REASON OF HIS OWN, AND IT WAS NOT A THREAD, AND NOBODY HAD SENT HIM.**

"**I got the sheet again on the Thursday. Same sheet, it comes round, it's free, there's a notice about it. And I read the fourth item again in a canteen in here and I worked out that I had already worked the answer out in May and that the answer isn't on the sheet.**"

"**Right.**"

"**It's in a corridor. That's where I worked it out. On a Saturday morning, standing up, at about half past ten, and a woman told me the wall, and she was right, and I put the paper in the inside of my coat and I went down the stairs and out into the street with it.**"

"**And there is no minute of that corridor.**"

"**There is no minute of it because there is no minute of anything in this city that isn't in a room, and I was in a corridor.**"

---

**AND THE RESISTANCE, WHICH IS THE WHOLE OF WHAT HAPPENED TO HIM, AND IT IS FIVE SENTENCES.**

**What happened to him happened to a corridor. A corridor in this borough has no record, and it cannot have one, because a record of a corridor would require a name on it, and the name is the whole of the difficulty, and the reason he did not say the name in May is the only reason he cannot be helped now.**

He had the correct act and it worked. In a room he would have said the name and it would have gone on a sheet and in a few years there would have been a person in this city who could be pointed at as a person a service floor was for. He did not do that, and he was right, and the price of being right is that there is now no record anywhere in this borough that a man stood in a corridor on the second of May and did the right thing.

**And there is no way to write that record down now without a name in it, and the name is the thing he will not say, and he is not going to be asked for it, and nobody is ever going to be asked for it, including the person it belongs to.**

---

"**So I've got a question,**" he said, "**and it's the only one and I'm going to ask it once.**"

"**Go on.**"

"**Is there anybody in this city who is allowed to put me in that sentence without writing anything.**"

---

**And he answered it, and it is the only question he answers in the next three weeks, and the answer is worth nothing and it is correct.**

"**No.**"

The man waited, and Jonas Mercer did not improve it for about as long as it takes to be sure a person has heard you.

"**And there is not going to be one. The reason is not that anybody is keeping it from you and the reason is not that nobody has thought about it. The reason is that anybody who could put a person inside that sentence without writing anything would have to be written down in order to do it, because that is the only way it gets done, and a person who is written down to do a thing is a person somebody can ring about it, and after that they cannot do it for you or for anybody else, and then one day they will not want to, and then they will have to pretend to.**"

"**That's what she said.**"

"**I know it's what she said. I was nine feet away and I did not hear her say it, and I have thought about it every day since, and it is the same answer and it is a better one than mine and I have not got a third.**"

---

**AND THE COST OF THAT ANSWER LANDED WHERE THE COST OF A CORRECT ANSWER LANDS, ON A PERSON WHO IS NOT IN THE ROOM, AND IT IS THE FIRST TIME HE HAS WATCHED IT LAND ON SOMEBODY HE HAS SPOKEN TO.**

"**Right,**" the man said.

"**Right.**"

"**Can I say something that isn't a question.**"

"**You can say it.**"

"**I've said it at work.**"

"**To who.**"

"**To two people. On nights, in the break room, in May, and I have got it wrong both times, and both of them are people I sit in a room with for eight hours. What I've said is *a floor's for a group of people* and I've said it like it's a rule, and one of them said *so which group*, and I said the group that hasn't got anybody in it that I can point at, and he said that's a very clever way of saying nothing.**"

"**Yes.**"

"**It is. And I have said it twice and both times it turned into a thing about me.**"

---

**And there it is, and he did not say it, and it is the sentence a woman of nineteen named on a footbridge in this city in May and it is not his and he is not going to take it and say it back to a man in a canteen.**

A sentence said in a room is a thing a room knows, and it does not last, and the only thing that happens to it is that it goes to a person. He watched it go to a person on the second of May, in a corridor, in about fifteen seconds, and it was standing up and folded and put in a coat, and six weeks later the same sentence is in a break room on a night shift and it has got his face on it.

He said nothing. He has a true sentence available and he has had it available since the twentieth of May and it is not his and he is not going to use it to make a man in a canteen feel better about May.

---

**THE MAN DID NOT STAY. HE HAD A SHIFT, HE SAID, AND HE TOOK HIS TRAY WITH HIM, AND HE LEFT IT ON THE TABLE, AND NOBODY HAS TO GO BACK FOR IT BECAUSE IT IS HIS.**

At the door he stopped and said the last of it, and it was not a question, and it was the correctest thing anybody said in that canteen, and Jonas Mercer has thought about it since and has not improved it.

"**You could have told me in May. You were standing there and you knew what it was going to cost and you didn't come and find me after, and I've worked out why, and I think it's because if you'd found me then I'd have been a person you'd found.**"

"**Yes.**"

"**So I wasn't found. And I still don't know whether that's a good thing.**"

"**Neither do I.**"

"**Right,**" the man said. "**I'll be in on Wednesday.**"

---

Nobody thanked anybody. Nobody refused anything.

Nobody was helped. Nobody was harmed. **A man of thirty-three asked a correct question and got a correct answer and it is worth nothing and he has to live with it, and that is the correct outcome, and the answer was given by a man who is not on anything and is not a member of anything and is not a party to anything and has no department and no grade, and who has been standing at the back of rooms in this borough since the fourth of September because that is where he has decided to be.**

**And nothing was written down. There is no minute of that canteen. There is no form on which a man of thirty-three could be recorded as having asked and a man of thirty-two as having answered, and if there were one then in a few years a piece of paper would be able to say that this borough had asked a man with nothing on it whether it had a place for him, and the true answer would be on the paper, and the true answer is no.**

The register of narrowings is at nineteen and the blank line under the nineteenth is blank. The card in the slot is a hundred and eighteen days old and nobody has taken it out. The letter in the tray is a hundred and twenty days old and has not been opened in front of a second person. The rota has still not been rebuilt and cannot be and the row from eleven o'clock to half past one is still empty and still does not say *vacant*.

**And a person who is not counted is not a person who is helped, and a man of thirty-three came back to a canteen on his own and asked one question and was told the truth and has not been helped, and the reason is a name he will not say and will never be asked for, and the reason he will not say it is the only thing he did right in May, and both of those things are true at once and neither of them can be put in a column on a delivery sheet.**
