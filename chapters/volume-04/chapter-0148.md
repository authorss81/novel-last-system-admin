# Chapter 0148 — What The Number Is

Monday the eighteenth of May 2015, from about half past two in the afternoon, a corridor, and a day room off it, the east wing, Saint Orra Civic Hospital; and about four o'clock, the same corridor.

**She asked him one question. It was the first question anybody has asked Jonas Mercer since the twenty-seventh of April, it was in a room and not in a public room, and he answered it, and it is the only question anybody is going to ask him and get an answer out of in the next nine days. He asked no question of anybody on that afternoon and has not asked one since the twenty-fifth of February. Nobody was helped and nobody was harmed.**

---

**AND BEFORE ANY OF IT, THE WAY HE GOT THERE, WHICH IS THREE LINES AND IS NOT A REASON.**

On the Friday, in a shop at the barrier end of a street, a man she has never spoken to and did not ask anything of put a paper bag on the counter and said that his mother had been on a ward on the east side of the hospital for nine days and that the place was like a greenhouse in the afternoon and that the people in it had been asleep all day and awake all night.

Beatrice Osunde did not say he was brave. She does not. She has refused to say that to him since the fourth of September and she has been right about it every time.

She said, "**Then you had better go and look at it,**" and she went back to the bench oil, and she did not tell him where the ward was, and she did not say anything else about it, and neither of them mentioned anything about the other after that, on that afternoon or afterwards.

---

The east wing is a corridor with four doors off it and a day room at the end with two chairs in it and a hatch in the wall where the tea used to be.

**It has no mechanical extract. There is no fan in the ceiling of that corridor and there has not been one since it was built, and there is a line in that building's budget that has said for six years that the mechanical extract for the east wing is not urgent, and the line has no name against it, and a line with no name against it has nobody to draw on it, and she cannot find out who is allowed to draw on it because finding out would be a thing a person has to be asked about, and nobody in that building is going to be asked about a line in a budget on a Monday in May.**

She was on the ward at half past two with a trolley and a clipboard and the particular way of moving down a corridor that is about nine degrees faster than anybody else's and that is not impatience.

They had not spoken since before September.

**It is not a quarrel. It has never been a quarrel. That is what makes nine months of not speaking different from nine months of a quarrel, and one of the two is recoverable and one of the two is not, and he has not worked out which one he is in and neither has she.**

---

She saw him at the end of the corridor and did not stop and did not change her speed, and she went into the day room and put the trolley down and came back out, and he was still standing there.

"**How many have you got.**"

That is the first question anybody has asked him since the twenty-seventh of April, and it is in a room and not in a public room, and it is a fair question and it is not a kind one, and it was asked before he had said one word.

He knew what she meant, and she waited, because she is a physician and she does not repeat herself.

"**Three,**" he said. "**As in three projects. That is the whole of the answer and it is the honest one, and there is no part of it that is yours.**"

"**I know it is not mine,**" she said. "**I wanted to hear whether you had come to tell me it was not mine. People come down here to be relieved of it, and you get the same three words and a very relieved face, and then in about a week you are back in a different coat asking me for something you cannot have.**"

"**Right.**"

And she went past him into the day room and pulled the door half to, and he came in, and there were two chairs and a hatch and a bin and a window that gives on to a brick wall about a foot away, and neither of them sat down.

"**I have not been going to ask you anything and now I have asked you something, and I would like it noted that I did not want to.**"

"**Noted.**"

"**No. Don't do that. There is nowhere for that to go.**"

---

**AND THEN SHE GAVE HIM THE PROBLEM, AND SHE GAVE IT TO HIM IN HER OWN WORDS, AND IT IS THE THING THAT HE DID NOT EXPECT AND IS THE REASON HE CAME.**

The wing has eighty-one beds on the board at the end of the corridor. The board is a piece of hardboard with a number on it and it is what that building is held to.

**Eighty-one beds. In a heat, on the eighteenth of May, at four in the afternoon, with no mechanical extract in a corridor and a wall on one side of it that holds the heat until about seven in the evening — the number of people who can be in that wing and be all right is not eighty-one, and it is not any number anybody in that building can put on that board, because the number that would go on that board is a number of beds and the number they need is a number of people, and those are two different numbers and only one of them is a thing the borough is able to hold.**

She said the whole of that in about a minute and a half, in a day room with a bin in it, without sitting down, and she did not ask him for a solution and she did not ask him whether there was one.

Then she said:

"**The extract on this side comes off a thermostat in a plant room, and it was set in February by a contractor who had been in the building for a day, and it is on a dial, and anybody in this city can walk into that plant room and read that dial. That is the whole of what I have found in nine days. A number on a dial that anybody may read, and no instrument in this borough that could go and read a thing like that and ask what it is for.**"

"**You have not read it.**"

"**I have not read it. I have sent two people and one of them could not find the plant room and the other one found it and told me it was on and then left.**"

---

**AND HERE IS THE CLOSE OF THE CORRIDOR, AND IT IS THE WHOLE OF WHAT HE TOOK OUT OF THAT AFTERNOON, AND HE DID NOT WRITE IT DOWN.**

**A number on a board is what a building is held to, and eighty-one beds is a number of beds, and the number they need on the eighteenth of May is a number of people, and the difference between those two numbers is a human being, and a human being does not go in a box.**

That is the sentence in that building and it is not a sentence anybody has said out loud, and it is not going in a minute, and he is not going to say it to her and he is not going to write it in the pad, and it is a thing a records supervisor would say if she were here, and she is not here and is not going to be told about it.

**Nobody was helped. She did not get eighty-one beds' worth of air out of a wing, and nobody in that corridor was any cooler at four o'clock than they were at half past two, and a man read a sentence off a board and was not given anything and gave nothing and there was no instrument anywhere in it.**

---

**AND THEN SHE DID THE OTHER THING, AND SHE DID IT BEFORE HE HAD ASKED FOR IT, AND IT IS THE ONLY REFUSAL IN THAT AFTERNOON AND IT IS NOT ONE OF THE ELEVEN AND IT IS NOT A TWELFTH.**

She came out of the day room and went to the hatch and put the trolley down and said:

"**You have not asked me about the room.**"

"**No.**"

"**You are going to. You came to a building in a heat and you stood in a corridor for an hour and a half and you have not asked me about the room, and you are not going to, because you have decided that it is not about a room. So I am going to say no now, and then neither of us has to spend the afternoon finding out how you were going to ask it.**"

"**All right.**"

"**No. Listen to the half of it, because I am not being kind, I am being quick. I am not going to help you find the man who did that sheet.**"

---

He did not say anything.

**She was right. It is the most exact refusal anybody has given him in nine months and she had it ready in a day room at about ten to three on a Monday afternoon in a heat, and she did not know he was in the building, and she is not going to be told that she refused him, and he is not going to be able to prove that she did.**

"**He did it for about eleven weeks, on his own time, with no name against it, and then he stopped, and that was in October, and he has not been asked about it, and he is not going to be asked about it by me.**"

"**I was not going to ask you about him.**"

"**I know you were not. That is why I am saying it now.**"

"**All right.**"

"**I am not going to be asked about that room, and I am not going to be asked about the sheet, and I am not going to be asked who used to do it, and I am not going to have it come back through a corridor in a fortnight in a different coat with a different question in it. And if it does, you will get the same answer, and I am telling you what the answer is now so that the first time and the second time are the same answer.**"

"**They usually are.**"

"**Not usually. Once. And the second one is cheaper.**"

---

**And that is what he has. A refusal that is correct, that is about a person, that he did not ask for, that he cannot appeal, that is not the eighth of anything and is not a twelfth of anything, and that cannot be improved on, because the only way to improve on it would be to ask her a second question.**

**Eleven refusals in this borough and the count is eleven and has been eleven since the thirteenth of March. A person who has been refused twice is one refusal and not two. What she has just done is hers, applied to a man instead of to a duty, and it is not a new request and it is not a twelfth, and nobody in this borough is going to add it to anything.**

---

He went down the corridor past the board with eighty-one on it and past the hatch and past the four doors, and about a third of the way along, outside the plant room, which was a door like the other four and had a sign on it that said what was behind it, he stopped and went in and read a dial.

**It was set to twenty-two. It is set to twenty-two now. It is a thermostat with a number on it and a number can be wrong and a number can be read by anybody and that is the only thing in this whole business that anybody in this building can do about it without asking anybody to do anything.**

He put the door back the way it was and went out into the corridor and down it and out into the car park and it was about four o'clock and it was still hot and nothing that happened in that corridor on the eighteenth of May made one single person in that building any cooler.

**A man who is not on anything read a dial on his way out. That is not an instrument, and it is not a service, and it is not a form, and there is no way in this borough to be held to it, and there is nobody in that building who is going to be asked whether he was there, and he is not going to be told that he was.**
