# Chapter 0163 — Agreed

Thursday the twenty-fifth of June 2015, from about ten in the morning, a small meeting room at the water office; and from about one o'clock in the afternoon, a corridor in the same building, and then a lane at the end of a street that nobody in that building is ever going to walk down.

**A correct request was made in writing and granted in a sentence by a person who could have said no, and it cost a person in a different street something physical. It is a person. He is not on a list. He was not rescued and he did not die. The difference between this and every other correct thing this borough has done since the fourth of September is that somebody wrote it down. He found out afterwards and said nothing, and the whole of what he did about it was say nothing.**

---

**PRITI SANDHU, 37**, of number twelve, one of the four streets, came in at about ten with a single sheet of paper in her own hand, and she is not on a list, she is not on a rota, she is owed nothing by anybody, and nobody has ever put a box in front of her.

The standpipe at the bottom of her street runs off the tank and the tank is metered on a timer, and the meter is correct, and the timer is set for eleven till two on weekdays and not at all at weekends, and none of that is anybody's fault and all of it is a page of what is in a tank this afternoon.

She wrote it out herself. One page. A sentence saying what she wanted. Her own name at the top because it is her paper.

"**If the standpipe at the bottom stays on overnight, for this week, then the four of us at the bottom can fill after eleven and before six in the morning instead of at two in the afternoon in the sun with the school letting out. That is all it is. It is this week. I am not asking for it to go on after.**"

"**Why the bottom.**"

"**Because it is the top everybody can see. The four at the bottom have got my mother at number nine and she cannot get up that hill twice a day in this. And because it is the bottom that everybody assumes is fine, since it is near the tap.**"

---

**AND IT WAS GRANTED IN ONE SENTENCE BY A PERSON WHO COULD HAVE SAID NO, AND IT COST THIS BOROUGH NOTHING AT ALL, AND THAT IS EXACTLY WHY IT IS THE ONE THAT MATTERS.**

"**Yes. It can stay on overnight. I'll put the timer back at about eleven tonight.**"

"**Thank you.**"

"**It is all right. And I will tell you why that is not a favour, because you will want to know whether it is one. The water is metered on the tank and the tank is your school's, so the extra is yours, and the labour is the same labour, so as far as this borough is concerned you have asked me to leave a tap open and I have said yes, and that is the whole of it.**"

Nobody in that room found it remarkable. There is no minute, there is no agenda, there is no reference and no crest, and nobody in that room is obliged to have been in that room.

---

**AND HERE IS THE DIFFERENCE, AND IT IS FOUR WORDS AND A DATE, AND HE WROTE THEM HIMSELF.**

Callum Nkemdirim turned the sheet over and wrote on the back of it, in his own hand, the word **Agreed**, and the date.

**He wrote it down. That is the whole of the difference between this and the laundry round and the tank and the nine answers in a car park and the two hours at a school. Every one of those was correct and every one of those happened in a room with nothing on it afterwards, and this one is correct, and now it is a piece of paper.**

**And a piece of paper has to be paid for out of something.**

---

**AND THE THING THAT IT WAS PAID FOR OUT OF WAS NOT A DECISION. IT WAS A LINE ON A LIST, AND THE LINE IS THE ONE NOBODY CLAIMS.**

The allowance for that week was fixed on the Thursday before. The overnight water on one street is a quantity, and the quantity comes off a total, and the total is fixed, and the only item on the whole of that list that has never once had anybody stand in front of it and ask for it was the filling round that goes down Collier Lane, because that round fills butts and standpipes at the end of a lane of terraced houses and garages with no houses on it, and there is no demand against it and there never has been, and it is the last line and it is always the line that goes.

Denise Hallam, 44, who manages the contract and who is the person the sheet goes out on, said the arithmetic out loud once and said it correctly and did not enjoy it.

"**There is no argument. That is the thing. If somebody at number twelve on that street says overnight, then overnight is in, and the total is the same, and the round at the end of Collier Lane is the round that is not in anybody's mouth, so it is the round that gets the Wednesday instead of the Friday.**"

"**Can it move a day?**"

"**It can move a day. It cannot move a fortnight. It cannot move a fortnight and nobody has asked for a fortnight and nobody is going to.**"

---

**AND THEN SOMEBODY NAMED HIM, AND IT WAS THE FIRST TIME IN THIS BOROUGH THAT A PERSON WHO WAS IN A CORRIDOR ON THE MORNING OF SUNDAY THE TWENTY-FOURTH OF MAY HAD A NAME IN ANYBODY'S MOUTH, AND IT WAS NOT TO THE BOROUGH, AND IT WAS TO ONE PERSON, AND IT WAS IN A ROOM, AND IT HAD A REASON.**

Nobody in that room had said anything for a moment and a woman of about forty-three with a folder had come in late and had been standing at the back, and she said:

"**Does anybody lose by it.**"

**And Denise Hallam said it, once, to Callum Nkemdirim and not to the room, in one sentence, and the sentence had the reason in it.**

"**Colin Wharton. He's sixty. He is at number thirty-one at the end of that lane and he fills a butt under his own downpipe and he has filled it for about six years and he pays for every drop of it out of his own pocket, and he is not on anything and has never been on anything, and he does it because there is a woman at number twenty-nine who cannot get to the top of the lane, and that is the whole of the arrangement and it is his own, and he does not know anything about that round and nobody tells him anything about that round, and when it does not come he will work that out and he will not ask anybody.**"

And nobody said the name again, and nobody wrote it down, and it is in one person's mouth in one room in one building and it is not going to be in anybody else's.

---

**And that is the bill, and it is the whole of it, and it is not anybody's fault, and it is not going in a document.**

The round at the end of Collier Lane comes on a Thursday at about eleven, and it did not come on this one, and at about eleven that morning a man of sixty put his hose on the tap at the end of the lane and there was nothing in the pipe. He carried four containers from the standpipe two hundred yards away at about one o'clock, on his own, and again at about four, and the water in the second lot was warm by the time he had finished with it because it had been standing in a plastic container in the sun since one o'clock.

**At about four o'clock in the afternoon he could not lift the fourth one. He left it standing full on the kerb at the bottom of the lane and he carried three up, and he is sixty, and he has a bad grip on his right hand and it has been like that since about February.**

**Nobody has told Colin Wharton about the timer. Nobody is going to. There is no instrument in this borough that would put a man of sixty on a list because his butt did not fill on the Thursday, and there is not going to be one, and the reason there is not going to be one is the same reason there is no form in this borough on which a person could write that they are not a series.**

---

**HE WAS AT THE BACK OF THAT ROOM FOR THE WHOLE OF IT AND HE ASKED NOBODY ANYTHING AND HE LEFT AT ABOUT HALF PAST TEN AND HE DID NOT WRITE ANYTHING DOWN.**

Nobody refused anything, nobody was helped, and nobody died, and nobody was lucky.

**At about one o'clock, in a corridor outside the canteen at the back of a hospital site, a man of thirty-eight told him, in about six words and unasked, that the round at the end of Collier Lane had been taken off that week, and that there was a man at number thirty-one at the end of it who filled a butt under a downpipe, and that he was sixty.**

He was not asked. He did not ask. He said nothing.

---

**AND HERE IS WHY HE SAID NOTHING, AND IT IS ABOUT HIM, AND IT IS NOT A PRINCIPLE, AND IT IS THE WORST THING HE HAS DONE THIS MONTH.**

**He could find Colin Wharton in about an hour.** That is the difficulty and it is the whole of it and it is the same difficulty as a yard behind a market on the sixteenth of May. There is a street name and a house number and a man of sixty with a bad grip, and there is no instrument standing between him and that house except the one that has been there all along, which is that going there is the whole of what he does.

So there are two things he could have done, and he did not do either, and both of them are wrong.

**He could have gone and told a man of sixty that he is on a piece of paper in a building in the city.** That is how people get found, and he has watched it happen three times in nine months in three rooms that were his, and he said the whole of that out loud once, to one person, on the thirteenth of April, and he is not going to say it again.

**Or he could have gone and carried the fourth container up that lane himself.** And that is what a woman of nineteen called a kindness on a footbridge in this city in May, and she was right about the word, and the word is the reason he did not do it: it goes to a person, and there is no box on it, and in a few years the only sentence anybody in this borough could say about Colin Wharton would be that a man came and carried his water, and he would not know who, and neither would anybody, and there would be a man in this city whom a man of sixty on a lane of terraced houses and garages would be grateful to, and that man could not be asked about it by anybody, ever, which is the freedom, and it is also the whole of what he is, and it is not going to be enough and it is not supposed to be.

**So he said nothing in a corridor, to a man who had not asked him anything, about a man he had never met, over a butt under a downpipe.**

---

**And the standing of that, on that Thursday afternoon, is this: a woman of thirty-seven asked for one thing in writing and got it, and it was correct, and it was granted in a sentence, and a man of sixty carried four containers two hundred yards twice on his own and could not lift the fourth one, and the reason is a word and a date on the back of a sheet of paper in a building, and not one of the three of them is going to be told that the other two exist.**

**And this is the first correct thing this borough has done since the fourth of September that has a piece of paper on it, and the piece of paper is a bill, and nobody is going to be given the bill.**
