# Chapter 0171 — Nothing Is Wrong With It

Monday the thirteenth of July 2015, from about half past two in the afternoon until about half past five, one of the four streets.

**A street where nine people gave the same correct answer in the second week of June, in a cool week, with nothing at all wrong with it. The correct document that came out of those nine answers says *no requirement stated* about this street, and it says it correctly, and it is in a folder in a building, and nobody in that street knows it exists. The two kinds of man on that street are both at it again and neither of them is in the document and one of them has a price and one of them cannot be found. He asked no question of anybody. Nothing was helped and nothing was refused and nobody was thanked and no name went in a box.**

---

It is a street of terraces about four hundred yards long, and you can walk the whole of it in about ten minutes, and it has a shop at one end and a postbox at the other and about sixty doors and a pavement that is in shadow from about two o'clock in the afternoon because of the houses on the other side of it.

**There is nothing wrong with it.** That is the whole difficulty with the thirteenth of July and everybody in this borough knows it and nobody says so. No main is off. No drain is blocked. No collection has failed. Nobody is carrying anything for anybody. There has been no heat for three weeks. The pavement is dry, the walls are not warm to the touch, and the nine lines in a printed document in a folder in a building on another street say, twice, *no requirement stated*.

**And nobody in that street has ever seen the document. It has a heading and the nineteenth of June on it and four streets down the left in a column headed *location* and nine lines beside them, and it came round in a tray on a Friday, and one of those buildings is a mile from here, and there is no way in this borough to walk into a street and tell it what a building has said about it.**

---

**HE WALKED IT AT ABOUT HALF PAST TWO AND HE ASKED NOBODY ANYTHING AND HE WAS ASKED NOTHING BY ANYBODY, AND HE IS NOT ON THE AGENDA OF ANYTHING AND HE HAS NO DEPARTMENT AND NOBODY CAN RING HIM.**

At about three o'clock a white van was outside number twenty-nine and a man of about fifty-three was on his back at the top of a flight of steps hauling a fridge down through a window, and he had a card on the dashboard with a telephone number on it and a list of eight things under the number, and the list of eight things included *fridges* and *wardrobes* and *lofts* and nothing else.

He had done four of the nine doors on that street since the twenty-eighth of June. He had charged forty pounds at two of them and thirty at one and he had been paid in cash at all three, and he had left his card at six, and he had knocked at every door on the street including the nine.

**He is findable. He is the most findable man on that street and he has made himself so in about nine days by writing a telephone number on a piece of card, and there is no instrument in this city that would stop him and no reason anybody would want to.**

---

**AND AT ABOUT FOUR O'CLOCK A MAN OF ABOUT SIXTY-SIX CAME UP THE STREET FROM THE OTHER END AND DID A JOB AT NUMBER NINETEEN, AND HE DID NOT CHARGE FOR IT, AND HE DID NOT LEAVE ANYTHING BEHIND HIM, AND NOBODY IN THAT STREET CAN SAY WHO HE IS.**

A man at number nineteen was putting an armchair out on the pavement because he is moving on the twenty-second of this month and cannot get it down the steps and cannot get it into the van on his own.

The man of about sixty-six came past with a folded moving blanket under his arm, which means he had come prepared, and he did it in about ten minutes with the two of them and he did not take any money and he would not take a cup of tea, and at about ten past four he picked his blanket up off the pavement and went back down the street, and the van that came for the chair at half past four was not his.

**Nobody knows where he came from. Nobody in that street can say whether he is on a street of his own or two streets away or out of this borough entirely, and nobody asked, and there is no way in this borough of finding him, and he has not been on a rota, on a list, on a board, in a minute or in a document in his life, and the reason he came is that he came.**

**There is no price on him, nothing has ever put one there, and he has not put one there himself, and there is no version of a man turning up with a blanket under his arm that anybody in this borough can charge anybody for.**

**And he knocked at none of the nine and the man with the card knocked at all of the nine, and every one of the nine let the man with the card finish his sentence on the doorstep, and not one of them said no and not one of them said yes, and one of them gave him a glass of water and one of them told him they had already said, and both of those are correct, and one of them is a glass of water.**

---

**AND RONNIE PEART, 52, OF NUMBER FIFTY-EIGHT, WHO WENT ROUND NINE DOORS OVER THREE EVENINGS IN THE SECOND WEEK OF JUNE AND ASKED ONE QUESTION AT EACH OF THEM AND GOT THE SAME CORRECT ANSWER AT ALL NINE, OPENED HIS FRONT DOOR AT ABOUT TEN PAST FOUR AND LOOKED DOWN THE STREET AND SHUT IT AGAIN.**

He has a dog. He has no position of any kind in this borough and he is on nothing and he is not going to be.

**He did not go down the street. He did not offer. He did not call anybody. The envelope with the nine answers on the back of it is in a drawer in a kitchen in number fifty-eight and it has been in that drawer since the second week of June and it has not been asked for and it is not going to be asked for, and a man of thirty-two walked past that door at about ten past four and neither of them knew the other was in the street.**

---

**AND HERE IS WHAT THE DOCUMENT IS RIGHT ABOUT, AND IT IS RIGHT ABOUT ALL OF IT, AND IT IS NOT A FLAW IN THE DOCUMENT AND IT IS NOT A SUCCESS EITHER.**

A right document about a street does not stop two men working on it. It never does. It says what a building is going to do, and what this building is going to do about this street is nothing, and nothing does not stop a man with a card, and nothing does not stop a man without one.

**The man with the card is not contradicting the sheet. The man with the card is doing more than the sheet says, and a document that says *no requirement stated* does not forbid a man from turning up with a fridge, and that is a sentence about how strong a correct document is, and it is not a finding, and nobody is going to be told it.**

**The sheet is correct. The street is fine. Nine people said the correct thing and it was printed and the printing did not touch anybody's life, and in the second week of July a man of sixty-six carried an armchair down a flight of steps for free, and the sheet says nothing about that either, in either direction, and it was never asked to.**

Nobody in that street was helped in a way that lasts and nobody in that street was refused anything, and one man of fifty-three has taken four payments in cash and one man of sixty-six has taken none, and both of them are working on a street that a building has recorded as requiring nothing, and the building did not know about either of them and is not going to.

**There is no form in this borough on which anybody could write *a man of sixty-six moved an armchair at number nineteen on the thirteenth of July*, and there is not going to be one, and nobody is required to write it, and the armchair is in a van and the blanket went back down the street and the man at number nineteen is going to a different house on the twenty-second.**

Nobody in that street was thanked and nobody refused to be thanked. The register of narrowings is at nineteen and the blank line under the nineteenth is two hundred and twenty-nine days old. The card in the slot is a hundred and forty-three days old. The letter in the tray is a hundred and forty-five days old, it is under two pieces of post, and it has not been opened in front of a second person and is not going to be.

**And a person who is not counted is not a person who is helped, and a street that is not on anything is not a street that needs something, and two men worked on one this afternoon and neither of them is a person anybody in this borough is going to be helped about.**
