# Chapter 0193 — The Field

Saturday the nineteenth of September 2015, from about nine in the morning, a parade of shops in Tideglass.

**A valid instrument has been in force since the middle of August and this morning nobody in this borough is looking at it. It has no case list and no report and no date anybody can point at except the one on the last page. About thirty people were on this pavement yesterday because a hole in the road was dug on Wednesday, and the hole is a hazard and the thirty are the mechanism, and they are the same thing and they are the same week. This morning a man of about twenty-three went past the trench at half past nine with a bag in his hand, and he is one of the nine, and nobody saw him, and he is all right, and nobody is going to ask him.**

---

**THE ONLY LATTICE NOTICE ON THAT PARADE THIS MORNING, printed and fixed to the barrier at about the height of a person of average height, and it defines nothing, and nothing on it defines *holder*:**

> **A HAZARD IS A HAZARD UNTIL SOMEBODY PUTS A THING IN IT.**
> **A PERSON WHO PUTS A THING IN IT GOES HOME AT THE HOUR IT GOES HOME.**
> **NOTHING ON THIS NOTICE IS HELD AGAINST ANY PERSON.**

---

**HE CAME AT NINE BECAUSE NOBODY SENT HIM AND BECAUSE THERE IS NOTHING IN THIS CITY THAT COULD HAVE STOPPED HIM.**

The main came off under this parade at about ten to nine yesterday morning. There is a hole of about four feet by three where it came off and a trench running out from it for about a hundred yards along the flags, and there is a length of blue pipe lying beside the trench, and a stopcock key on the kerb, and a coil of hose, and a man in a hired jacket on a machine at the far end, and a barrier at each side of the trench with a piece of A4 fixed to one of them and an hour written on it in a hand anybody in this borough would recognise.

**That is the whole of the outside of an instrument that has been in force since the thirteenth of August.**

---

**AND NOBODY ON THIS PARADE THIS MORNING IS HERE BECAUSE A DOCUMENT EXISTS.**

There is no list and no board and no clerk and nobody whose day it is. There are five people on the flags. A man from a drainage firm. A man from the water side with a stopcock key, waiting on somebody to tell him where to put it. A man in the apron of the shop next door, who has come out twice and gone in twice. A man who lives above the chemist and can see the hole from his kitchen and has not been asked why he is here. And the man of about thirty-eight, who is the only one on the street who appears to know what the day is for, and who is not in charge of anything.

**They are standing there because a hole is in the ground, and the hole is in the ground because a main came off, and the main came off, and that is a Saturday.**

---

**AND IF A PERSON CAME DOWN THIS PARADE IN A YEAR'S TIME AND ASKED WHAT HAD HAPPENED ON IT IN SEPTEMBER, THERE WOULD BE NOBODY TO ASK, AND THE ROAD WOULD BE MENDED, AND THE ANSWER WOULD BE THAT A MAIN CAME OFF.**

That is what an instrument looks like when nobody is watching it. Not a building and not a queue and not a notice with a name on it. A trench, and five people, and a piece of A4 with an hour on it, and a hundred yards of blue pipe.

**A copy of the drawing that has been in forty places since Friday morning is gummed inside the window of the shop next door, at eye level, with the squares and the arrows and the lines between them. About nine people in this borough have stood in front of one of them this week. Not one of them has come down this parade to look at the hole in the road, and the hole is the only thing on this street that is dangerous.**

---

**AND THE PEOPLE TOLD HIM THINGS, BECAUSE THEY ARE NOT REQUIRED TO AND BECAUSE HE IS THE MAN PEOPLE TELL.**

A woman was standing in her own doorway with a box of washing powder she had not gone in for.

"**They'll have it open till Friday, they said.**"

"**Friday,**" he said, and that was the whole of what he said to her all morning.

Nobody knows which of them said it. He could have asked. **He did not, and there is nobody in this city he could have asked, and that is not a prohibition; it is that a woman standing in her own doorway with a box of washing powder is not a source and has not been asked to be one, and the only way anybody in this borough becomes a source is by being written down as one, and nothing in this borough writes anybody down as anything.**

The man in the apron said this road has been dug twice before, and the men who dug it before are gone, and the men who dug it yesterday are here. Nobody wrote that down and nobody asked the man in the apron how he knew, and he knew because he has watched this parade out of a first-floor window for about nine years.

---

**AND THE FINDING OF THE MORNING, WHICH IS ABOUT THE DOCUMENT AND NOT ABOUT THE ROAD, IS THAT THE DOCUMENT HAS SOMEBODY IN IT AND NOBODY ON THE STREET.**

Seven clauses. Precedence, decided by a person. The field, with four things in it. The call, which a person may not decline. The closure, which is posted on the street it closes. The transfer, which stands until somebody writes. The second call, which is not being applied. The renewal, which is on the last page with a date on it.

**Not one of the seven says who is in charge of a street. Clause one decides what happens first between two services and it is decided by a person, and it does not say that the person is on the pavement. This morning there are five people on a parade of shops and a man with a stopcock key waiting to be told where to put it, and the man who could tell him is on a telephone about something else, and none of that is a breach of anything, because the document does not have a clause in it about a street.**

---

**AND THE SECOND FINDING IS ABOUT THE THIRTY, AND IT IS NOT A FINDING ABOUT THE ROAD.**

About thirty people were on this ground yesterday at half past ten because somebody rang a telephone. There are five on it this morning, and the reason there are five is that the field is closed, and a closed field cannot take anybody who is not already in it, and the second call did not go off, and the work is half done and will be finished toward the middle of next week.

**The man of about thirty-eight is the one who did not make the calls this morning and he is the one who is not saying why, and nobody has asked him, because nobody is going to be asked anything. Two facts are on the same pavement: a man of thirty-two said a sentence here on Friday in front of thirty people, and there are five people here this morning. They have nothing to do with each other on the page. There is no page.**

---

**AND AT ABOUT HALF PAST NINE A MAN OF ABOUT TWENTY-THREE WENT PAST THE TRENCH CARRYING A CARRIER BAG.**

He is one of the nine. He said yes in a doorway on the twenty-second of August in the time it took to be asked, and carried four boxes of water and then a chair up two flights, and he has been called five times since the second of September and has not declined one, and he was on this parade yesterday at about half past ten for the fifth time. The work is not skilled and takes two days and yesterday was the first of them.

**He was not on it this morning. He was going past, on his way somewhere, at about the pace a person goes at who has not slept much and is carrying nothing.**

He was carrying that bag the way a person carries a bag when they have been carrying something heavier than a bag this week. His shoulders were not turning into it. He did not stop and he did not look at the trench, and nobody in that street said good morning to him, because four of the five people on the flags had not seen him before this morning and the fifth had seen him yesterday and was on a telephone.

**Jonas Mercer was about thirty feet from him and he saw all of it, and he has spent nine months watching people carry things up stairs, and he knew what yesterday had done to a man of twenty-three in about a second and a half.**

He did not ask. He did not cross the road. He stood where he was and let a man of twenty-three walk past him, go into the chemist's, come out of the chemist's with the same bag, and go on, and the man of twenty-three is all right, and he will be all right by Thursday, and it is a bad back and not a life, and he is not going to be asked about it.

---

**AND THE PART OF THIS MORNING THAT MATTERS IS WHY NOBODY CAN BE TOLD, AND IT IS NOT BECAUSE ANYBODY IS BEING BRAVE.**

The man of about thirty-eight is on a pavement about forty yards away with a telephone. He called nine people in a doorway on the twenty-second of August and thirty on this parade yesterday. He is not the employer of a man of twenty-three and is not his supervisor and is not a party to anything, and there is no clause in the whole of it that says what happens to a person who is called on and then cannot do it.

Suppose somebody told him. He is competent and he would do the one thing that is available, which is to ring the man and say not this time, and he would be right, and the man of twenty-three could not say no to being called on, and could not say no to not being called on either, because the order has no place anywhere in it for a man who says no. There is a column headed *date seen again* on a printed sheet in nine other buildings with *no name, no initials* printed under it, and there is nothing in it, and it has been empty since the tenth of July and nothing that has happened since was going to put anything in it.

**So the hurt would arrive in the only form this city can hold, which is a decision about whether to go on using a person, and the decision would be wrong and right at once, and that is not a reason to do it. It is the reason there is nowhere in this arrangement for a person whose back has gone.**

The field holds a name, the hour the person was called, the hour the person went home, and the thing the person was called for. **Everything that happened to him yesterday is in that field and every part of it is correct. His name is in it. The hour he arrived is in it. The hour he went home is in it. The thing he was called for is in it. It is a complete record of a man who was there. It is not a record of a man who is hurt, and the field has four things in it and there is no clause that says there could be a fifth.**

Nobody is required to report anything about themselves in this borough, and that is not cruelty, it is the arrangement: a person who is on a list can be sent to a street, and sent twice, and cannot be sent at all if the person is asleep. The price of that is that a hurt person has nobody to hand the hurt to. **There is one person in this city a man of twenty-three could not say no to, and that is the man with the telephone, and there is one person who is on no list and on nothing, and that is a man of thirty-two who has not asked one question of anybody since the twenty-fifth of February, and there is nobody else.**

The register of narrowings is at nineteen and a back is not a narrowing. A doctor is a thing a person goes to in their own time on their own account, and nobody is going to be told which doctor. **And the two people in this city who have a department have not written, and have not been asked to write, and will not be asked, because there is no body that can require anybody to do anything and a body that cannot require anybody cannot be told what it will do. Those two sentences are on a card on a wall in a Methodist hall in this borough and neither can be argued out of the other one, and a man with a bad back on a parade of shops in September is what the two of them mean.**

---

**AND HE HAD A QUESTION HE WANTED TO ASK SINCE HALF PAST NINE AND HE DID NOT ASK IT, AND THE REASON IS NOT COURAGE.**

If he had crossed the road he would have got an answer, and that is the whole difference between the two of them. A man of thirty-two can be answered. **A man of twenty-three has been asked five times in a month whether he could come and has not once been asked one other thing, and a man who has only ever been asked whether he can come has not got a way of saying that something is wrong with him.** Giving him one here, on a pavement, outside a chemist's, would be a way of asking him about it on Saturday and then on Monday, and then whenever anybody in this city wanted to know whether he was all right — and a man who has a way of being asked whether he is all right is a man somebody can ring, and this city does not have instruments and it has telephones.

**So nobody asks, and the man of about thirty-eight does not know, and the man of twenty-three is all right, and there are two people on a parade of shops this morning who know what happened to him and neither of them has a place to put it and neither of them is going to look for one.**

---

**AND AT ABOUT TEN SOMEBODY ASKED HIM FOR SOMETHING, AND IT WAS THE FIRST TIME IN NINE MONTHS THAT ANYBODY HAD ASKED HIM FOR ANYTHING.**

"**Take that end.**"

Jonas Mercer took that end.

It was a length of pipe about nine feet long and there were four other men on it and the man of about thirty-eight was not one of them, and the trench was about three feet down, and the pipe went in on the count of two and lay along the bottom of it, and the five of them stood in there for about a minute and a half and nobody said anything, and then the man who lives above the chemist said "**that'll do**" and the five of them came out of the trench one at a time.

**Nobody wrote it down. There is no list and there is no field and there is no hour recorded anywhere, and he has not been given a name, a grade, a department, a chair, a seat, a membership, a form or a rota row, and there is no instrument in this city that could record the fact that a man of thirty-two held one end of a length of pipe for a minute and a half on the morning of the nineteenth of September.**

---

**AND THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT SAID THE ONE THING HE SAID ALL MORNING THAT WAS ABOUT THE DOCUMENT, AND HE SAID IT MOSTLY TO HIMSELF, AND JONAS MERCER WAS CLOSE ENOUGH TO HEAR IT.**

"**Nothing from either of them. It goes back on Tuesday unless one of them does something about it.**"

Nobody has written to that man from either of the two places in this city that have a department. Nobody is going to be asked to. Two places that have spent nine months learning what a blank does have not been sent a paragraph, a form, a telephone call or a question, and on Friday, in front of thirty people, he said it starts again on Tuesday unless one of them says it does not, and it is Saturday, and nothing has come, and nothing is going to.

**It is holding. That is not the same as settled, and nobody in this city is going to mistake one for the other, and nobody won anything on this parade this morning, and a sentence said once on a pavement has not been turned into a reason by anybody, including the man who said it.**

---

**AND THE ORDER HAS NO CASE LIST AND NO REPORT AND NO DATE ANYBODY CAN POINT AT EXCEPT THE ONE ON THE LAST PAGE, AND THAT IS WHAT AN INSTRUMENT LOOKS LIKE FROM THE INSIDE, WHICH IS THAT IT IS A THING THAT WORKS AND A THING THAT CANNOT BE CHECKED.**

The seventh clause says the order stands for ninety days from the date of making, and page two says it is renewed on the eleventh of November 2015, or it is not renewed, and under that it says *agreed by*, and there is a line, and the line stops about two inches from the bottom edge of the page and there is nothing after it.

**Thirty-seven days. There is nothing in the whole of it about a hole in a road and nothing in the whole of it about a man of twenty-three, and the only part of it anybody has ever written anything in is a field with four things in it, and one man writes in that, and the number of names in it is not given to anybody and is not going to be.**

Nobody has asked whether anybody is going to renew it. Nobody has been asked. There is a date on a piece of paper in a building and a line under the date, and the line is empty, and it has been empty since the thirteenth of August, and no body in this city can require anybody to put anything in it.

---

The register of narrowings is at nineteen and the blank line under the nineteenth has been blank for two hundred and ninety-seven days, and a hurt back is not a narrowing, and an order that bounds a call is not a narrowing, and a date in November is not a reason. The card in the slot on the ground floor is two hundred and eleven days old and nobody has taken it out of it. The letter in the tray on the floor above is two hundred and thirteen days old, with two pieces of post lying on it, and it has not been opened in front of a second person and is not going to be.

**And a person who is not counted is not a person who is helped, and this morning the counting of one man turned out to be the reason nobody was able to ask him how he was. He was found in a doorway in August because a person asked him in a doorway whether he could come, and being found is what a field is for, and a man who can be found at six in the morning has already said yes to being found, and a man who has said yes to being found cannot then be asked whether he is all right, because the asking would be the second thing anybody has ever asked him, and the first was whether he could come. It is the nineteenth of September and it is still true and nothing in this borough is going to answer it.**
