# Chapter 0178 — Not For The Bags

Wednesday the twenty-second of July 2015, from about half past three in the afternoon, a block of flats off a parade of shops in Tideglass, and a stairwell with a half-landing and a window at the turn.

**Somebody is carrying something for somebody in daylight in front of people, and it is the second time in a month that this borough has seen that shape, and the shape is the same and the person is different. The carrying is done and then the person who did it is thanked for something else, and the thanking does not attach to the carrying. He asked no question of anybody and nobody asked him anything and he carried nothing. Nothing was written down and nothing is going to be.**

---

She came in at the bottom of that stairwell at about half past three with four plastic bags on her arms, and she is about sixty-seven, and she lives on the fourth floor, and there is a lift in another block and not in this one.

**The bags were full. They were from a supermarket on the far side of the market and they had been carried about two hundred yards from a car, and the handles had been in her hands long enough for her fingers to have gone white at the ends of them.**

There is no man in that stairwell helping her and there is nobody on that landing she can call, and she was going to do it in four goes.

---

**AND A MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY-NINE WENT DOWN THE STAIRS PAST HER AND CAME BACK UP WITH HIS HANDS OUT, AND HE DID NOT ASK HER WHETHER SHE WANTED A HAND, AND SHE DID NOT ASK HIM EITHER.**

He lives on the second floor. He came down because he was going to the shops and she was in his way, and then he stood there and did not go, and then he took two bags off her without saying anything about it and carried them up.

"**That is very kind.**"

"**It is four floors.**"

"**It is still kind.**"

"**It is four floors and a bag each,**" he said. "**I have to go down again anyway.**"

**He is fifty-nine. He is not her husband and not her son and not her brother and not anybody's anything, and there is no arrangement of any kind, and there is no rota in that block and there is no list of who does what for whom on that stairwell, and nobody is required to carry anything for anybody, and he said yes to a thing that was not put to him as a request and it took him about two seconds and it was granted in a stairwell with no paper on it.**

---

**AND HE CARRIED THE OTHER TWO UP AS WELL, AND HE CARRIED THEM IN DAYLIGHT, AND HE CARRIED THEM IN FRONT OF PEOPLE, AND HE DID IT AT HALF PAST THREE IN THE AFTERNOON ON A WEDNESDAY WHEN EVERYBODY IN THAT BLOCK IS AWAKE.**

A man with a pushchair was on the half-landing and had to wait. Two people were standing at the bottom of the stairs and one of them said *go on then* and stepped aside. A woman at a window on the second floor looked down and then looked away.

**Nobody said anything else about it. Nobody offered. Nobody came up behind him with a third bag.**

This is the second time in a month that this borough has seen this shape. Sixteen days ago a man of about sixty-three carried four buckets of water up a flight of steps in a service yard in Tideglass in front of about nine people, and nobody had asked him to, and he could not have said no, and it was in daylight, and the whole of the cost of that landed on him.

**The shape is the same. The person is different, and the street is different, and it is a different week, and whether it is the same thing is not said.**

---

**AND IT IS ALSO NOT THE SAME, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS THE ONLY INTERESTING PART OF IT, AND IT IS ONE WORD.**

The man of about fifty-nine did it because she was in his way and because he was going down anyway. Nobody asked him to. Nobody could have made him. He did not put a towel down, and he did not say no when a man at the bottom offered, and he did not carry a thing for a person who had asked another person to carry it.

**Nobody is a price. Nobody is on a list. And a bag of shopping is the cheapest thing anybody in this borough has ever been asked to carry, and the reason it costs nothing is that it is worth nothing, and that is the entire mechanism, and it has been worked out independently in about nine places in nine months and it has never been written down in any of them.**

He put the four bags on her landing floor, and stood up, and said:

"**I am going down now and I am going to the shops, so I would be two minutes ahead of you if you came now.**"

"**I will come in a minute.**"

"**Do not bother.**"

---

**AND AT THE TOP OF THE STAIRS SHE SAID THANK YOU, AND IT WAS NOT FOR THE BAGS.**

"**I am going to say something,**" she said, "**and it is not about the bags.**"

"**All right.**"

"**In June you told me the water was going to be off on the Wednesday, and you told me on the Monday, and I would not have found out until the Tuesday night and I would not have been able to do anything about it then, and I did not have to fill a kettle for a fortnight because you told me on the Monday.**"

"**It was on a board.**"

"**It was on a board in a corridor and you read it and you told me. That is all. And nobody has ever asked you for that and nobody is going to.**"

"**No.**"

"**So thank you for that.**"

"**You are welcome.**"

---

**AND SHE WENT INTO HER FLAT AND SHUT THE DOOR, AND THE THANKING WENT TO THE PERSON WHO HAD CARRIED FOUR BAGS UP FOUR FLOORS ON A WEDNESDAY AFTERNOON, AND THE CARRYING WAS STANDING RIGHT THERE, AND NOBODY PUT THEM TOGETHER, INCLUDING THE MAN WHO WAS CARRYING.**

She thanked him for a corridor in June. He was paid for it in four bags and she never knew that, and he never knew that she had not known, and he would not have wanted her to.

**And the carrying goes on being a thing that happened, and there is nowhere in this borough to put it, and it is not going to be mentioned again by anybody in that block except possibly by her, in about a year's time, in a different conversation, as a story about a man who carried her shopping, and by then the two things will have been in the same story for so long that the person telling it will not know which one of them was the favour.**

---

**AND A MAN OF THIRTY-TWO WAS ON THE HALF-LANDING GOING DOWN WITH NOTHING IN HIS HANDS AND HE SAW ALL OF IT, AND HE CARRIED NOTHING.**

He was going the other way. He had come up for no reason anybody could give and he was going down for no reason anybody could give, and he was on that half-landing at about half past three on the twenty-second of July with his hands in his pockets, and he watched a man of fifty-nine carry four bags up a flight of steps and watched a woman of sixty-seven thank him for a corridor in June.

**He is the only person in that block who knows both of those things at the same time. That is the whole of what he got out of it.**

If anything is ever asked about that stairwell, the person anybody would ask is him, and not because he is in charge of anything, and not because anybody knows he was there, and not because anybody wants anything from him. He would be asked because he is the one who was in the stairwell, and he would answer correctly, and he has never in his life been the person anybody brings an answer back to.

---

**AND HE HAD A WORD FOR IT IN JUNE, AND IT IS NOT HIS, AND HE DID NOT USE IT THEN AND HE IS NOT GOING TO USE IT NOW, AND A WORD IS NOT A THING YOU CAN USE TWICE.**

It belongs to a person of nineteen who is owed nothing and is owed it and got it for nothing, and she had it on a footbridge in May about something entirely different, and she was right about it, and it is hers. He had it once in a yard in July and he did not say it and he is not going to say it here, and saying it here would make it his, and if it were his then he would be the man who named what a porter of sixty-three did with his morning and what a man of fifty-nine did with his Wednesday.

**And there was no other word. There is no second one. He looked for one on that half-landing for about as long as it takes to go down a flight of stairs and there was not one, and not having one is not better and it is not a principle and it did not occur to him as anything at all. It is a Tuesday, and the difference between a word that stops a thing happening and no word at all is not a moral position. It is only that the bag got carried either way.**

---

Nobody in that block was asked anything and there is no form in this borough on which a person could record that a man of fifty-nine carried four bags up four floors on the twenty-second of July, and there is not going to be one, and the reason there is not going to be one is that there is nothing to be in a form about. The two thank-yous in that stairwell were about a corridor in June and about four bags on a Wednesday, and they happened about four minutes apart, and nobody joined them, and that is all.

The register of narrowings is at nineteen and the blank line under the nineteenth is two hundred and thirty-eight days old and a Wednesday is not a reason to write on it and nobody is obliged. The card in the slot is a hundred and fifty-two days old. The letter in the tray on the floor above is a hundred and fifty-four days old, it has two pieces of post lying on it, and it has not been opened in front of a second person and is not going to be.

**And a person who is not counted is not a person who is helped, and a man of fifty-nine carried four bags because he was going down anyway, and a woman of sixty-seven said thank you for a corridor, and a man of thirty-two is on a half-landing with nothing in his hands and he is the only one in that block who knows both halves of it, and nobody is going to ask him, and he is not going to offer.**
