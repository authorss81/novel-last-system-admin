# Chapter 0155 — Nine Of The Same Answer

Friday the twelfth of June 2015, from about two o'clock in the afternoon, a car park, Tideglass.

**He asked no question of anybody on that day and nobody asked him anything. One man went round nine doors over three evenings and asked one question, and the answer at all nine of them was one word, and the answer was correct, and a piece of paper now exists in a folder that goes into a building. Nobody thanked anybody, nobody refused anything, and nothing was helped.**

---

**THE ONLY LATTICE NOTICE AT THE EDGE OF THAT CAR PARK, on the wall of the stair tower where the bins are, and it defines nothing and nothing on it defines *holder*, and it requires nothing of anybody:**

> **A STANDPIPE ON THIS ROAD IS OPEN WHENEVER IT IS OPEN.**
> **NO PERSON IS REQUIRED TO BE AT A STANDPIPE, OR NEAR ONE, OR TO ANSWER FOR ONE.**
> **A PERSON WHO SAYS THAT A STANDPIPE HAS BEEN USED IS NOT ASKED WHEN IT WAS LAST USED.**

---

It had been hot since the middle of May and it had not broken, and the twelfth of June was the fourth week of it, and a car park in this borough in the fourth week of it is a car park in a heat.

About nine of the same people were in it. That is the whole of the arithmetic: nine of the fifty who stood in that car park on the twenty-third of May, and about nine of the same nine, because there is no other car park.

The sun had come round and was on the wall behind the stair tower, and the tarmac under the cars had a colour it does not have in October.

**Callum Nkemdirim, 38**, from the water office, was there with a folder and a one-page sheet on a clipboard and a pen he had not uncapped. He had been in that car park twice in the heat and had got a result both times, and he had come a third time because a result you have got twice is not yet a position, and a position is a thing that can be repeated by somebody who was not there.

He had three columns on that sheet and a space at the bottom for a name and a space for a day-time number.

---

**AND THE PERSON WHO HAD GONE ROUND THE NINE WAS NOT HIM AND WAS NOT ANYBODY ANYBODY HAD ASKED.**

**Ronnie Peart, 52**, who lives at number fifty-eight and has lived there nineteen years, and who has no position of any kind in this borough and is on nothing and is not going to be, and who has a dog.

He had been round the nine over three evenings, on foot, with the dog not coming because it was too hot for the dog, and he had knocked on nine doors and asked nine people one question, and he had written down what they said on the back of a C5 envelope because that is what was in the drawer in the kitchen.

The question was this, and he said it in that car park the way a man says something he has already said nine times:

"**If I write down what you tell me, what do I write.**"

**And the answer was the same at all nine of them, and the answer was one word, and it was correct.**

"**Nothing.**"

---

**AND IT IS CORRECT, AND THE REASON IT IS CORRECT IS THE SAME REASON IN NINE KITCHENS, AND TWO OF THEM SAID IT OUT LOUD AND THE OTHER SEVEN SAID IT THE SAME WAY WITHOUT SAYING IT.**

There is nothing anybody could do with it. And if it is written down then there is a piece of paper about a house that says the house needs something, and in a year's time somebody will come to the door and ask whether it is still needed, and there will be no way of saying no that does not sound like ingratitude.

**A woman of about seventy, who had come on a bus on the twenty-third of May and had ended an argument about four streets in about four seconds, said it as a sentence and then said it again as a reason:**

"**There's nothing to write. And if you write it, then you've made it a thing, and then somebody's got to come back and find out if it's still a thing, and they won't want to, and then they'll have to pretend to, and by the end of August there'll be a bit of paper about nine houses saying that nine houses asked for something in June, and in a year's time that's not a thing anybody can take back.**"

---

**AND THE RESISTANCE WAS ON THE OTHER SIDE OF THAT CAR PARK, AND IT WAS CORRECT, AND IT WAS A MAN WITH A CLIPBOARD.**

Callum Nkemdirim was right about the whole of it and he said it in the order a person says things when they have said them before.

"**A borough does not act on a conversation. A borough acts on a sheet. I go back on Monday with nothing and there is no delivery, because there is no record of anybody asking, and in September somebody in an office asks me what happened on the four streets in June and the true answer is that nine people told me nothing in a car park, and I could not show them a single thing.**"

"**You could write that down.**"

"**I could write that down. Nine houses, said nothing, the twelfth of June. And that is not the same as nine houses that do not need anything, and I know it is not the same, and here is the whole of the difficulty and I am not going to pretend it away. The sheet says they do not need anything. That is what a sheet does. A sheet takes what a person said to you and it makes it a fact, because that is the only thing a sheet knows how to do, and I have never in my working life been able to stop it.**"

---

**AND THEN HE SAID THE PART THAT NOBODY IN THAT CAR PARK HAD THOUGHT OF, AND HE WAS CORRECT, AND HE SAID IT BECAUSE IT WAS HIS JOB.**

"**I am not putting a name on it. That is the honest part and I want it on the record that I said it out loud in a car park. A name turns a street into a set of telephone numbers. The first thing anybody does with nine names is find out whether the four streets on them are the four streets anybody is being helped on, and the second thing anybody does is ring them, and the third thing is ask them why they have not been helped yet, and none of those three is a thing anybody in that car park has asked to happen to them.**"

He put the pen back in his pocket.

"**So the names column stays empty. And if the names column stays empty then the sheet cannot be used to find anybody, and it can be used to say a position, and a position is the only thing I am allowed to leave in a building.**"

---

**AND THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE WITH A BARROW ANSWERED IT THE SAME WAY AS THE OTHER EIGHT, AND SHE WAS CORRECT, AND THEN SHE SAID THE THING SHE HAS TO SAY.**

She had a barrow and four containers on it and a carrier bag, and she had been asked the same question on Wednesday evening standing on a doorstep, and she said the same word.

Then she said the other thing, and she said it to Ronnie Peart and not to the man with the clipboard.

"**You've got nine of them on the back of an envelope and you're going to hand that to him.**"

"**I'm not.**"

"**I know you're not. I'm telling you why you shouldn't, and the reason isn't that it's unsafe. The reason is that nine lines in a row is not nine people. It's a street with a shape. And once it's a street with a shape then it's somewhere you can point, and I don't need anybody pointing at that street. I have got nine lines of my own on a bit of paper about the size of a receipt, and there is nothing at the top of it, and it is not for him and it is not for you, and I am not showing it to a man with a clipboard in a car park in the sun.**"

---

**AND HERE IS THE THING THAT MADE THE CAR PARK GO QUIET FOR ABOUT A MINUTE, AND IT WAS NOT THE MAN WITH THE CLIPBOARD.**

Ronnie Peart had the envelope in his hand and he turned it over, and he said the sentence that was the whole of the afternoon, and he was not the person with the form, and he had no standing of any kind, and he had walked nine doorways to find it out.

"**Nine of them said nothing. Put nine nothings in a row and it isn't nine nothings any more. It's a street with *nothing* written against it, and the *nothing* is the thing about the street, and in about four years somebody's going to be able to say that in the worst heat in nineteen years, on four streets, nine households were asked what they wanted and the record says they wanted nothing, and there is not one thing anybody can do about that sentence.**"

He handed the envelope back to himself and put it in his pocket, which is where it had been.

"**That's not me saying it's a lie. It isn't a lie. That's the trouble with it. It's just a row, and a row is a different animal from a doorstep.**"

---

**AND HERE IS WHAT HAPPENED, AND IT IS THE WHOLE OF THE WEEK, AND NOTHING IN IT WAS A CRIME AND NOBODY DID ANYTHING WRONG.**

Callum Nkemdirim wrote the four streets down the left in his own hand, and he wrote what nine people had said in a line beside each of the nine, and he left the name column empty and the day-time column empty, and he did it standing up in the sun in a car park because that is where the answers had been given.

**That was the whole of what three evenings came to.**

Not because of the man with the clipboard. Because a correct answer, said on a doorstep by a person who was owed nothing and who was owed nothing the next day as well, came back into a room and became a line, and a line under a street is a fact about a street, and a fact about a street can be repeated by somebody who was not there and will be repeated by somebody who was not there.

**Nine people gave the correct answer to a correct question in a heat, and not one of them has been counted, and not one of them is going to be, and the answer was right, and being right about it is not a thing anybody can hold.**

---

Nobody refused anything. Nobody was harmed and nobody was helped and nobody in that car park had been on the receiving end of a single thing that a machine in a building was going to do for them, because there was nothing in it for a machine to do.

The register of narrowings is at nineteen entries and the blank line under the nineteenth is blank and it is not going to be written on, and a heat wave is not a reason to write a form and nobody is obliged to. The rota has still not been rebuilt and cannot be. The board stands at seven of nine with two chairs empty and no chair of that body at all and has not met since the middle of January. **The lowland is not on the layer and cannot be seated, and the reason has not been fixed, and the four streets in that car park are not on the layer either, and the reason is the same one, and nobody in that car park has ever asked to be on anything.**

**And a person who is not counted is not a person who is helped. Nine people said so themselves, in nine kitchens, in the correct words, in a heat, and none of them has been on a piece of paper since.**
