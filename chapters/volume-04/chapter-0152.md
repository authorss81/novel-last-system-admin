# Chapter 0152 — What A Person Can Be Held To

Friday the twenty-second of May 2015, from about two in the afternoon, a first-floor corridor over a shopfront in Tideglass.

**She asked him one question and he did not answer it. That is the first time anybody has asked him anything since a physician asked him a question in a day room on Monday and he answered that one. He asked no question of anybody. Nobody opened a drawer. Nobody thanked anybody.**

---

**NELL ARDENT, 39. Founder of the Open Covenants Legal Clinic, which is two rooms and a corridor on the first floor over a shopfront, and which challenges revoked permissions and coerced signatures, and which has been going for six years and has never had a result anybody wanted.**

**She maintains the public charter record. That is the other job and it is the one she does not talk about in the first ten minutes.**

---

**And the first thing in that corridor was the heat, and it was the whole of the first ten minutes, and neither of them got to the other thing until the fan was on.**

The corridor has two doors, a fire door at one end that is not locked and has not been locked since about the spring, and a window at the other end which is open, wedged with a piece of card, because the room behind it has no air at all and neither does this.

There is a jug of water on the sill. It is not a jug with a tap on it. It is a jug somebody filled in a kitchen and carried up a flight of stairs, and there are two glasses beside it upside down on a windowsill, and the glasses are from a café four doors down.

**A fan is on a stool. It is a hotel fan with a hotel's label still on the back of the stand, which means it came from a hotel, which means somebody from this corridor is sleeping in a hotel, and nobody is going to ask her about that either.**

She had a lever-arch file under her arm. She had put it down on the windowsill, on the dry side, away from the sun, before she said anything, and she had done that without thinking about it, which is a lawyer's habit and not a clerk's.

---

"**You are in a thing with no form on it,**" she said.

"**Yes.**"

"**I have four pages about you and every one of them says the same three words, and the three words are *no record located*. I have had two people try to find out what the thing is and neither of them could find out anything, and I have written *no record located* four times, and that is the only sentence in a file I am allowed to write, and it happens to be true, and I would like you to know that I have got very tired of it being true.**"

"**Then there is not much I can tell you.**"

"**I know there is not. That is why I asked.**"

---

**AND THEN SHE OPENED THE FILE, ON THE WINDOWSILL, IN THE AIR, WITH THE JUG BESIDE IT, AND HE LOOKED AT IT AND DID NOT TOUCH IT.**

Nine sheets. The public charter record. Each one has a box at the top of it.

The box says **PARTY**.

**On four of the nine sheets the box is empty. On five of them it has a name in it, or a company, or a trust, and one of them has a company with a number as well.**

"**That is the whole of the record,**" she said. "**Nine. Five years. A record of what this city has promised and to whom, and the promise and the person go on the same sheet, and that is not an accident. That is the whole design of it and I did it in the first month and I have defended it in a room four times.**"

"**It is the right design.**"

"**It is. That is the trouble, and I have been sitting with it for about two years and I have not got anybody to say it out loud to, and that is why you are sitting on this windowsill.**"

---

**AND HERE IT IS, AND IT IS NOT A LECTURE AND IT IS NOT A COMPLAINT, AND IT IS THE WHOLE OF WHAT IS DIFFICULT ABOUT HER.**

"**I want you to tell me the opposite, and I do not want you to be polite about it, and I have asked three other people the same question this year and two of them were polite at me.**"

"**I have not got the opposite.**"

"**Then say the honest thing, which is the hard thing, and it is this. If somebody is on a sheet, then somebody can be held to it. And that is the point of it. That is what it is for. If nobody can be held to a promise then a promise is a mood, and I have spent six years in rooms being told that a promise is a mood, and I have watched what happens to a person who is owed something when the owing is a mood.**"

"**That is right.**"

"**It is right, and here is the other half of it and you have got it, and I have got it, and we are the only two people in this city who have got it, and neither of us is on a board. A person who can be held to a promise can be *found*. Every one of those nine sheets is a place where a person is slightly easier to reach than they were the day before, and nobody asked them, and I did that on purpose, and I would do it again on Monday.**"

"**I know you would.**"

"**You do not know that. You have known me one hour.**"

---

**AND THEN SHE ASKED HIM THE QUESTION. AND IT IS NOT AN ACCUSATION, BECAUSE SHE HAS NEVER HEARD OF A DRAWER AND SHE IS NOT GOING TO.**

She turned round and sat down on the floor with her back against the wall under the window, which is a thing you do when the chair in the room behind her has no air in it.

"**So I am going to ask you the question I have been not asking for six years, and I am asking it of you because you are the only person in this city who does not work for anybody and I have run out of people who work for somebody.**"

"**Go on.**"

"**What is a person in this borough held to.**"

---

**And the answer is a drawer.**

**There is a drawer under a counter on the ground floor of a building about two miles from that windowsill, with a tray sitting on top of it, and the tray is not a list, and the drawer has no key on it and no list of what is in it, and one hand puts things in it, and every thing in it has a date on it and no name on it and no reference and no box, and a man can walk in off the street and stand in front of a woman for about as long as it takes to say good morning and be told what was true on a day, and that is the whole of the answer, and that is the cheapest privacy device anybody in this city has ever built, and it cost nothing, and nobody designed it, and there is no form for it, and a man walks out with a piece of paper in his coat and it cannot be paid and cannot be returned and cannot be filed and cannot be thrown away, and it goes in a pocket, and in a few years it is going to be handed to a frightened person in a kitchen by somebody who thinks they are helping.**

**And he is not allowed to say one word of that.**

Nobody in this borough is allowed to tell her there is a drawer. She is not on a rota, she is not on a board, she does not work for anybody, and there is no instrument in this city by which anybody could tell her anything, and if he said it she would go and look, and if she went and looked she would be a woman who went and looked, and the whole of what a drawer is — that nobody can be asked about it, that there is no list, that there is no instrument that can go and find a thing like that and ask what it is — depends entirely on nobody ever going and looking.

**So he said nothing.**

---

**And it took him a while, and she watched him not say it, and she did not press him, and that is the part of it that is going to be in his own head for a long time: she did not press him. She had asked a question she has wanted the answer to for six years and he did not give it to her and she let him not give it to her, and at no point in the next half hour did she say *you know something*.**

"**Right,**" she said.

"**Right.**"

"**That is not a no.**"

"**It is not a no and it is not a yes and I am not going to improve on it for you, because I have spent nine months watching people improve things for other people in rooms and it is the only thing I know how to do and I have watched what it costs.**"

"**Fair.**"

---

**AND HERE IS WHAT SHE ASKED FOR INSTEAD, AND IT IS SMALLER, AND IT IS THE ONLY THING SHE ASKED HIM FOR IN AN HOUR.**

"**The empty box. What goes in it.**"

"**I do not know.**"

"**You have thought about it.**"

"**I have thought about it and the answer I have is that a node with no party is not a party, and a place is not a person, and that is true and it is correct and it is not going to be fixed. And it is also the reason that four of your nine boxes are empty, and it is the reason they are empty, and it is not a gap in your record. It is what your record has to say.**"

"**And if I leave them empty?**"

"**Then you have built a record that cannot be pointed at a place and say *that one is in*, and in a few years somebody who is frightened is going to come to this corridor and ask you whether there is a thing about them, and you are going to be able to say *I do not know, and there is no box I can look up, and I could not tell you even if I wanted to, and I have built this so that I could not*. That is not a weakness. That is the only thing you have that anybody else in this city does not have.**"

---

**AND THEN SHE SAID THE THING THAT IS THE REAL SUBJECT OF THE CHAPTER, AND SHE SAID IT FLATLY, AND SHE DID NOT ASK HIM FOR AN ARGUMENT.**

"**I want a box on it. I want a box on all of it. I have wanted one for about two years and I have not got one and I am not going to get one, and it is not unkind of me to want one, and that is the difficulty, and I have not found a way round it, and I have run out of people who work for somebody.**"

"**It isn't unkind.**"

"**I know it isn't. That is what makes it difficult. If I were a person who did not care I would have got a box on the thing inside a week and I would have been the only person in this city with an instrument and everybody would have come to me, and I would have been *useful*, and I would have been a service, and a service is a thing that can be given to somebody and taken away again, and I do not want to be that.**"

---

**And he could have warned her. That is the thing. He worked it out standing at that windowsill with a hotel fan going and a jug of water sweating on the sill.**

**In about four seconds he could have said: a record with a box on it is what a person can be held to, and there is a drawer under a counter in this city with no key on it and no list of what is in it, and one hand puts things in it, and in a few years somebody is going to be able to describe exactly what is in it, and that drawer is not yours, and nobody is going to be able to point at you and say you did it, and you are going to be all right.**

**He is not going to tell her. And the reason is the one that has been true since the eighth of April, and it is not a new reason and it is not a good one: if he warned her, she would be the woman who was warned.**

---

She picked the file up off the sill at about half past three and put her hand flat on the top of it.

"**I am going to keep the four boxes empty,**" she said. "**And that is the whole of what I have decided this afternoon and I have a hearing on Thursday about a different thing and I have not told you one word about it, and you are not going to ask.**"

"**Somebody will.**"

"**Yes. Before the year is out.**"

---

**AND THEN SHE WENT DOWN THE STAIRS WITH THE FILE UNDER HER ARM AND HE STAYED ON THE WINDOWSILL, AND THAT IS THE END OF IT, AND IT IS NOT A SMALL THING AND IT IS NOT GOING TO BE RESOLVED.**

**She asked a question and he did not answer it, and the reason he did not answer it is that the answer is a drawer, and nobody is allowed to tell her there is one, and there is no instrument in this city by which anybody could.**

**He asked no question of anybody that afternoon. Nobody opened a drawer. And in a few years there is going to be a piece of paper in a kitchen somewhere with a hand on it and no name on it, and a woman with a lever-arch file and four empty boxes is going to be the only person in this city who could have said anything about it, and she is not going to be asked, and she would answer, and nobody is going to tell her that there is a question.**
