# Chapter 0188 — Which One Is In Front

**FOUR PEOPLE AND A QUESTION, AND THE QUESTION WAS NOT DIFFICULT, AND THAT IS NOT THE SAME AS BEING EASY TO ANSWER.**

The question was: **a street in this district is closed. A market is on it. The man of about thirty-eight, who is a continuity field manager, said clause four, because a closure is a safety thing and safety goes first, and he said it fast.

Councillor Sore said clause one, because clause four is about closing a district and clause one is about deciding, and a thing that decides is always in front of a thing that closes, and she said it slower.

"You are both right about your own service," said the man of thirty-two at the back of the room.

**AND HE SAID THE WHOLE OF IT IN ABOUT THREE SENTENCES, AND HE WAS RIGHT, AND HE DID NOT KNOW HE WAS RIGHT UNTIL HE HAD FINISHED.**

"**Clause four says a district may be closed. It does not say that a closure may not be taken account of. **AND THE POWER AND THE COST ARE THE SAME SENTENCE AND IT HAS NO WORD IN IT THAT ANYBODY IN THIS BOROUGH IS ALLOWED TO USE.**

A man with no department is a person who cannot be asked what he did.

That is it. That is the whole of what happened in that room, and it is not a slight. **If he had a department, there would be a file, and the file would have his decision in it, and the woman of seventy-two could be told that a decision was made at ten o'clock by an officer of something, and she could be angry at him, and she could ask to see it, and she could go to a place and say that this city decides things about her market in rooms she is not in. **And the reason he has no department is that there is no body in this city that can require anybody to do anything, and a body that cannot require anybody cannot be told what it will do, and both of those sentences are on a card on a wall in a Methodist hall in this borough, and they were put there by a woman who understood them, and neither of them can be argued out of the other one, and a man standing in a room with no department in front of him is what those two sentences look like when they are standing in a room.**
