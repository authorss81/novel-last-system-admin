# Chapter 0189 — Selby Row

Saturday the fifth of September 2015, from about half past six in the morning, Selby Row, a street of shops and flats off a parade of shops in Tideglass.

**The street closed at six o'clock and it was the right closure, and it is a closure, and nobody in this borough was heard before it and nobody in this borough can appeal it. The cost of it fell in three positions that nobody has argued out. The person who would have been able to say what the three positions are is a short walk away with all three answers in a building with the lights on, and he is not permitted to put them anywhere, and it is not because anybody forbade him. Nobody died and nobody was rescued.**

---

**THE ONLY LATTICE NOTICE ON THAT STREET, printed and fixed to the railings at the top end at about the height of a person of average height, and it defines nothing, and nothing on it defines *holder*:**

> **A CLOSED STREET IS A CLOSED STREET.**
> **NO PERSON IS REQUIRED TO APPEAL A CLOSURE.**
> **THE STREET OPENS WHEN THE THING ON IT IS FINISHED.**

---

**THE WALL IS BEHIND THE BACKS OF NUMBER EIGHT TO NUMBER FOURTEEN AND IT IS MOVING.**

It came off the top course about a foot on Saturday morning at about half past five and it did not fall and it did not have to. There is a bulge in it you can see from the road with your arms down. It is a boundary wall about nine feet high holding up a bank that carries the back gardens of nine houses, and the bank has been wet since the first of August, and there has been a hole in the middle of Selby Row's drainage since the twenty-ninth of July that three different contractors have looked at and none of them has written down.

**At about six o'clock a person put a barrier across the bottom of Selby Row and a piece of A4 on it with the hour on it, and the street is closed, and a closure is not required to be heard before it is made, and this one was right.** If that wall goes over it goes over nine gardens and a row of flats that is about thirty years old and a pavement. It is the correct decision and it was made in about four minutes by a person who had been awake since five.

**Nothing was minuted and no hearing was held and no hearing is available. There is no body in this city that can require anybody to do anything, and a body that cannot require anybody cannot be told what it will do, and both of those sentences are on a card on a wall in a Methodist hall in this borough and neither of them can be argued out of the other one, and between them they mean that this closure is not appealable in the same way that a wall is not appealable, which is to say that it cannot be, at all, by anybody, ever, and that is what makes it quick.**

---

**AND BY HALF PAST SEVEN THERE WERE ABOUT A DOZEN PEOPLE ON THE PAVEMENT AT THE BOTTOM OF IT WITH THEIR BAGS, AND THREE POSITIONS NOBODY HAS ARGUED OUT.**

Most of them went to a sister and a friend and a car park. That is what happens and it is fine and nobody in this borough thinks it is remarkable, and a dozen people standing on a pavement with bags at half past seven in the morning is a thing that happens in this borough about four times a year and it is handled by about four people who are not required to be there.

**Three of them could not be handled that way, and they are not the same three, and nobody has put them together and nobody is going to.**

---

**ONE. A WOMAN OF ABOUT EIGHTY-TWO IN THE TOP FLAT AT NUMBER TEN, WHO CANNOT GET DOWN FOUR FLIGHTS AT ANY TIME.**

She came down on the morning of the twenty-eighth of August, when she was told, and she came down at her own speed and somebody helped her with the bag, and she has been at her daughter's since. **Her daughter is on a day shift at a place four miles from here and does not get back until about six in the evening, and the woman of eighty-two has been at that daughter's since the twenty-eighth and is at that daughter's now, and her daughter did not know she was coming and has not been told by anybody that this is what happened, and she will find out at about six this evening from her mother.**

That is one position and it is a position somebody in this borough could argue about. It is a tenancy, a postcode, a key, a person of eighty-two and a person on a day shift, and there is a place in this city where that is a conversation, and it is not going to happen this morning.

**Two. A MAN OF ABOUT TWENTY-FIVE WHO IS IN THE BACK ROOM AT NUMBER FOURTEEN AND WHO IS THERE ON A LICENCE.**

The licence is a room. It is not a tenancy and he is not a lodger and he is not on anything and the paper that says he may be there is in the hands of an office with a telephone number on it. **He went out at about seven with a bag and there is nowhere to go and he has not asked anybody for anything and he is not going to, and he has been on that street since the second of May and no document in this borough says he is on that street at all.**

**That is the second position and nobody in this borough can argue it, because arguing it means saying that a person is in a room, and a person in a room is a thing a building can be asked about.**

---

**Three. A MAN OF ABOUT EIGHTY-THREE WITH A VAN IN A YARD AT THE BACK OF THAT STREET.**

The yard is reached through Selby Row. The van is inside it. It is a van with a ladder in it and a coil of rope, and there is nothing else in it, and he has a job on the Monday morning that starts at about eight.

**He is eighty-three. The van is on the wrong side of a barrier and the barrier came off the top of that street at about six o'clock this morning, and nobody has put it back, and the closure is right and the van is behind it.**

He has been sitting on a wall at the bottom of Selby Row since about half past seven with a cup of tea from a flask and he has rung the depot twice and been told twice that the barrier is not the depot's and he has not rung anybody else, and he is not going to, and **he will lose the Monday job because the van is in a closed street and the van is the only way he does that job, and nobody in that street knows it and the depot knows it and the depot cannot do anything about it because the van is not a service and the order is not about vans.**

---

**AND THE PERSON WHO HAS ALL THREE ANSWERS IS FOUR HUNDRED YARDS AWAY IN A BUILDING WITH THE LIGHTS ON, AND HE PUT A PIECE OF A4 ON A BARRIER IN AUGUST, AND HE IS THE ONLY PERSON WHO COULD WRITE THE THREE POSITIONS DOWN, AND NOTHING IN THE INSTRUMENT ASKS HIM TO.**

The man of about thirty-eight has the district in front of him and has walked it since June. **He does not have a system. He has three sentences: that there is a tenancy at number fourteen and it is a tenancy and not a licence; that the woman at number ten cannot get down four flights at any time; and that there is a man in a back room at number fourteen who is there on a licence and has been there since the second of May. All three were said to him, one each, over about three weeks, in a doorway and on a forecourt and once at a counter while he waited for a van, by people who wanted something to do with their week, and not one of them was said in a room, and not one of them was written down, and that is the exact arrangement this borough has been running on for nine months.**

**And clause two says the field holds four things: a person's name, the hour the person was called, the hour the person went home, and the thing the person was called for. Nothing else goes in the field.**

Nobody was called on to find out where three people were going to sleep. The thing those three people were called for is not a thing anybody called them for. **The order is the only instrument anybody in this city has ever made that could hold a person's name and say what the person was for, and it says what the person was for, and what the person was for is a thing that goes in a field, and a tenancy is not a thing that goes in a field, and there is no clause in the whole of it about a room with a licence on it, and there is no clause in the whole of it that says a person who has been told a thing has to write it down, and it does not need one, because there is nothing in this city that would enforce a clause like that.**

**He is in that building and he has the answers and he is not permitted to put them anywhere, and it is not because anybody forbade him. It is because the instrument is silent. Silence is not permission and it is not a prohibition either, and a document that cannot tell those two things apart is not a document anybody can be asked a question about. He could put three correct positions on a piece of paper this morning, and the piece of paper would not go anywhere, because there is nothing in this city that a piece of paper like that can be put into — and if there were, he is the man who went out on the morning of the twenty-second of August and put a barrier across a lane with the hour written on it, and a man with an hour written on a barrier who then produces a list of what the barrier cost is a man with a record, and nothing in this borough hands out records.**

**And it may not be put in front of the seat that is fifty-four and unpaid and can be found at a shopfront and at an address, because the seat that is fifty-four can empty that body, and a body that has been emptied cannot be asked to do anything, and if that happened today then nobody in this city could ever require anybody to do anything again, and the two sentences on the card in the Methodist hall are both true and neither can be argued out of the other, and the whole of this borough's capacity is standing on both of them at once.**

---

**AND A MAN OF THIRTY-TWO STOOD ON THE PAVEMENT AT THE BOTTOM OF SELBY ROW FROM ABOUT HALF PAST SEVEN UNTIL ABOUT TEN, AND HE COULD HAVE DONE SOMETHING, AND HE DID NOT, AND HE WAS NOT ASKED AND HE ASKED NOTHING.**

He could have gone up and knocked on the door of number fourteen and asked the man of twenty-five what he wanted, and the man would have told him, in a sentence, and that would have been a fact about a man in a room in this borough, and he is not going to be the man who makes that fact exist.

He could have gone to the woman of eighty-two's daughter at her place of work and said that her mother was at her flat and had been since the twenty-eighth.

**He could have asked the man of thirty-eight, in that building with the lights on, one question, and got three correct answers, and put three correct answers on a piece of paper, and a piece of paper with three positions on it is the beginning of a list, and a list of people who cannot be found is the one thing this borough has spent nine months not building and has succeeded in not building.**

**So he bought four coffees from a place on the parade and gave three of them away and had one himself, and he was on that pavement for about two and a half hours, and nothing happened, and that is what a street that cannot appeal is.**

---

Nobody died. Nobody was rescued. The man of eighty-three moved his van at about four in the afternoon when the engineers said the wall would be tied and the top of the street would be walkable, and the Monday job was gone and the depot said so in a sentence and did not say sorry, and nobody in that depot is a villain and the depot is not a villain and the depot is a building.

**The man of twenty-five was still on that pavement at about four in the afternoon and still had a bag, and at about half past four a woman of about forty-seven, who was on that pavement with a bag and whose sister had a room going, said to him, in front of a barrier, that there was a room going in a house in Kilburn Street because the woman who was in it had gone into a home, and he said yes, and that is not a rescue, because nobody arranged it, and it is the only thing that happened for him, and he is not on anything and the room is not on anything and there is no paper with his name on it, and there will not be.**

The register of narrowings is at nineteen and the blank line under the nineteenth has been blank for two hundred and eighty-three days, and a closure is not a narrowing. The card in the slot on the ground floor is a hundred and ninety-seven days old. The letter in the tray on the floor above is a hundred and ninety-nine days old, with two pieces of post on it, and it has not been opened in front of a second person and is not going to be.

**And a person who is not counted is not a person who is helped, and this order counts about nine people a week and can be rung, and it does not have one place in the whole of it where a woman of eighty-two who cannot get down four flights could be put, and a man in a back room on a licence is not in it, and a van behind a barrier is not in it, and the lowland is not on the layer and cannot be seated, and there is nobody in this borough to be asked about any of it, and a street that cannot appeal cannot even be told that it was wrong.**
