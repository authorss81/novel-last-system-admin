# Chapter 0187 — The Call

Saturday the twenty-second of August 2015, from about seven in the morning, a street near the market.

**Nine days after the order was made, a person made a call, and about nine people in this borough are now people somebody can ring about. They went because they were asked to. They will be back because they are asked to. They are all right. It is the end of the thing this borough built. Nobody was hurt, nobody was paid less, and the machinery works.**

---

**THE ONLY LATTICE NOTICE ON THAT STREET, printed and fixed to a lamp standard at about the height of a person of average height, and it defines nothing, and nothing on it defines *holder*, and it requires nothing of anybody:**

> **A PERSON WHO HAS BEEN CALLED ON HAS BEEN CALLED ON.**
> **THE HOUR THEY WERE CALLED IS THE HOUR THEY WERE CALLED.**
> **NOTHING ON THIS NOTICE IS A DUTY.**

---

**AND CLAUSE THREE IS FOUR SENTENCES LONG AND IT IS THE WHOLE OF WHAT HAPPENED ON THAT STREET.**

A person in the district may be called on to work. A person who has been called on may not decline. The call is made by a person. The call is not required to be in writing and there is no form for it.

**Four sentences, and they were drafted before the thirteenth and argued over for a fortnight, and the fortnight was the argument, and on the morning of the twenty-second the thing happened at about seven, and it happened because a man of about thirty-eight who is a continuity field manager for a company that supplies this city's services stood in a doorway on a street near the market and asked about nine people whether they could come and do some work today.**

He did not send anybody. He did not post anything. He asked, and about nine of them said yes, and eight of them were at the end of the street inside twenty minutes.

---

**AND THE MAN OF ABOUT TWENTY-THREE SAID YES IN THE TIME IT TOOK TO ASK, AND HE CAME, AND HE WAS ENTIRELY ALL RIGHT.**

He has a car and he does this and that. He was asked to carry four boxes of water from a builder's merchant to the end of a street and then to carry a chair up two flights because a woman on the second floor could not get it up alone, and he did both, and he was thanked by the woman on the second floor, and he said it was nothing.

**He is one of the nine. He is about twenty-three and he is a person who can be rung about now, and there is a place in this borough where his name is and what he was called for and the hour he was called and the hour he went home, and the four things in that place are correct, and his name in it is a door.**

**He does not know that is what it is and he was not asked and he is not going to be. He will answer if somebody rings him because he said he would, and he said it out loud, and a thing said out loud in a doorway in the morning is the whole of the mechanism of this borough and it has taken nine months and about four people to get back to it.**

---

**AND A MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY-THREE CAME AT SEVEN IN THE MORNING TO HOLD A GATE OPEN, AND HE HELD IT OPEN FOR NINE HOURS.**

He is seventy-three and he is not a volunteer of anything and he has not been in a hall about anything. He was asked to stand at a gate on a compound where a lorry was coming and going and to let vehicles out and to shut it behind them, and he stood at it from about half past seven until about half past four, and at about half past four somebody else's car came and he let it out, and then he went home.

**He was paid. He was paid the going rate for a day, which is less than he would have made, and he took it, and it is in his hand, and the person who paid him has a piece of paper with his name on it and the thing he was called for on it and both of those are correct.**

**He can be rung tomorrow. He will come tomorrow. He said he would, and he will, and that is the finding of this whole morning and it is not a small one.**

---

**AND THE MACHINERY WORKS. THAT IS THE FINDING AND IT IS NOT A RELIEF.**

Every correct thing that has happened in this borough in nine months happened because somebody who was not required to do it did it. Nine kitchen doors in June. A laundry round with the last four stops done first. A hall opened at eleven by a woman with a key. A tap granted in about four seconds in a doorway on the sixteenth of July. A bench nobody looked at, and a slat a woman of seventy-one wiped with a cloth out of her own pocket because she was going to sit on it. A crate on a pavement that nobody brought and a lid on that crate that nobody put on.

**And the reason all of that was possible is not humility and not caution and not a rule. It is that there was no way to reach any of them afterwards.**

This morning there was a way. **Nine people said yes to a correct question and they were written down, and being written down is what it is for, and the writing down was correct, and the work got done at seven in the morning on a Saturday in the last week of a heat, and no human being in this borough has ever arranged nine people at seven in the morning before.**

---

**AND THE PRICE IS THE NINE, AND THE NINE IS THE END OF A THING THIS BOROUGH BUILT.**

A list of people who cannot say no is the only reason anything works in this borough, and it has now been used on purpose, and it worked on the first morning, and nobody argued with it, and the whole of the argument this borough has had for nine months was about how to stop it.

**Clause three does not require anybody to do anything. It requires a person who has been asked to be unable to refuse. That is a different sentence and it took nine months to arrive at it and it arrived in four sentences, and it is the correctest clause anybody in this city has ever written, and it is correct in the way a tap left unlatched is correct.**

So the man of twenty-three is now a person somebody can ring about, and the man of seventy-three is a person somebody can ring about, and there are about seven more, and they are not on anything else and they are not on a rota and nobody drew up a rota and no rota exists and cannot be built and the order did not build one.

**They are findable. Not on a form. On a field with four things in it, one of which is a name, and the name is a door, and the door is on the other side of it.**

---

**AND THE MACHINERY WORKED SO WELL THAT BY TEN O'CLOCK THERE WAS A STREET CLOSED, AND A MAN WITH A VAN WAS ON IT.**

A service had come off on Ossot Lane — a gully, a water main, a thing under a road that nobody in that street could name and did not need to name — and under clause four the street was closed, and a closure is not required to be heard before it is made, and a closure is posted on the street it closes, and it was posted on the street it closes on a piece of A4 at about half past eight in the morning with the hour on it.

**At about ten, a white van was stopped at the top of Ossot Lane and a man of about fifty-three was standing in the road looking at a barrier.**

He has a card on the dashboard with a telephone number on it and a list of eight things under the number, and the list of eight things includes *fridges* and *wardrobes* and *lofts* and nothing else. He has been on that street before and the street was open then, and there is a house at the bottom of it with a wardrobe in a bedroom that is going to a house three miles away on the Monday, and the man has been going to be paid at the end of it, and he is standing in a closed street at ten in the morning.

**He got out of the van and looked at the piece of A4 on the barrier, and he did not photograph it, and he did not ring anybody, and he did not go and find out who closed it or why or on what authority, and he got back in the van and drove to the bottom of the street and parked on the wrong side of the barrier and went and knocked on a door and asked the woman of the house whether she was moving on the Monday.**

She said she was. He said he would come at about nine on the Monday and she would have to be out. She said she would be out. **That is the whole of what happened to that street on the twenty-second of August, and the barrier came down on the Sunday afternoon, and the man did the wardrobe on the Monday, and nobody in this borough has ever heard any of it.**

**He is a man who has been findable for years, by a card with a telephone number on it, and being findable has been the best thing that has ever happened to him and the worst, and it is on his dashboard and it is not in a building and it is not in this order and nobody has ever needed to make it be in this order.**

---

**AND THE THING THE ORDER DID NOT DO IS THE PART OF THE MORNING NOBODY WILL MENTION AGAIN.**

It did not open the drawer under the ground-floor counter. It has no key on it and it has no list of what is in it and a tray sits on top of it that is not a list, and the order put about nine names in a field and did not once ask what is in that drawer, and nobody in this borough knows that it did not, and nobody would have noticed either way.

**And the reason it did not is not discretion. Asking is what a building does to a drawer.**

---

**AND THE PRINTED ROW, WHICH IS NINE LINES READING *no requirement stated*.**

It went round a building folded in four on the nineteenth of June and it is in nine other buildings and it has been there nine weeks, and it went round a second time on the tenth of July in a tray with the lid off with one column added, and the June sheet was not withdrawn, and there is no name box on either of them and nobody in any of the nine buildings knows there are two.

**It is still there. It says that this borough is going to do nothing about nine households on four named streets, with a date on it, and the date has gone by twice, and it cannot be got out of, and nobody has ever been asked to sign it, and nobody ever will be.**

The machinery this morning was not that. The machinery this morning was nine people at seven o'clock and a gate held open for nine hours by a man of seventy-three who is not a volunteer of anything, and a card on a dashboard, and a barrier on a lane.

**And the two of those are not the same kind of thing and neither of them is a form, and the second copy of the printed sheet is still the emptiest and most dangerous object in this borough, and it is still correct, and the column with *no name, no initials* printed under it is still empty and is going to stay empty, and nothing that happened this morning put anything in it and nothing that happened this morning was going to.**

---

Nobody in this borough has been told that any of it happened. The register of narrowings is at nineteen and the blank line under the nineteenth has been blank for two hundred and sixty-nine days, and a Saturday is not a reason and an order is not a reason, and the order did not add a twentieth entry and the register was at nineteen before it and at nineteen after it and nobody in this borough is available to be surprised by that. The card in the slot on the ground floor is a hundred and eighty-three days old. The letter in the tray on the floor above is a hundred and eighty-five days old, with two pieces of post on it, and it has not been opened in front of a second person.

**And a person who is not counted is not a person who is helped, and a person who is counted is a person somebody can ring about, and this morning nine people in this borough were counted, and they are all right, and nothing that happened to any of them was a help, and one of them is about twenty-three and one of them is about seventy-three and both of them will answer the telephone, and about a mile away a man of about fifty-three with a card on his dashboard finished a job on a closed street and drove away, and he has been answerable to that card for nine years and nobody had to invent him.**
