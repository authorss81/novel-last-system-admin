# Chapter 0191 — The Forecourt

Monday the fourteenth of September 2015, from about two in the afternoon, the forecourt of Saint Orra Civic Hospital and the pavement outside it.

**Two people who were both right argued about the transfer and about the harm and about the order, in daylight, in front of about thirty people, and they chose not to hide the medical consequences from either side. They stopped. They did not make up. The not making up was not a decision that anybody took.**

---

**THE ONLY LATTICE NOTICE ON THAT FORECOURT, printed and fixed to a post by the bicycle racks at about the height of a person of average height, and it defines nothing, and nothing on it defines *holder*:**

> **A DISAGREEMENT ON A PAVEMENT IS A DISAGREEMENT ON A PAVEMENT.**
> **NO PERSON IS REQUIRED TO HAVE THE LAST WORD.**
> **NOTHING ON THIS NOTICE IS PRIVATE.**

---

**IT STARTED AT ABOUT TWO O'CLOCK IN THE MAIN DOORWAY AND IT WAS OVER INSIDE FOUR MINUTES, AND THEN IT WENT OUT ONTO THE PAVEMENT, AND THE PAVEMENT IS WHERE IT STAYED.**

She came out of the building on her way back from the ward and he was standing by the bicycle racks because he was standing by the bicycle racks. About thirty people were in and out of that doorway on a Monday afternoon: visitors, a man in a dressing gown with a bag of fruit, two women with a pushchair, a porter, somebody's mother in a chair, a young man with a clipboard and a plastic wallet who had a building behind him in another borough, and a woman of about forty-seven who had come to ask something else entirely and did not.

**Nobody stopped. Nobody looked round. That is what a forecourt is: about thirty people who are each one place on their way somewhere else, and two of them having a disagreement, and a person who has been in a building for twenty-two years and a person who is on nothing at all.**

---

**AND SHE SAID THE FIRST THING AND SHE SAID IT IN A DOORWAY IN FRONT OF ALL OF THEM.**

"**I have sent four of you to a man with no department in nine days and I am not sending a fifth, and I have said so to the registrar and I am now saying it to you, and I would like you to understand that I am not asking you for anything.**"

"**I know.**"

"**Then we agree.**"

"**We do not agree. I know what you have said to the registrar. I have not agreed with it and I am not going to, and I am also not going to be the reason a man of eighty-one waits for a piece of paper.**"

---

**AND IT WAS ABOUT ROY ALBERY, AND NEITHER OF THEM HID ANYTHING, AND THAT WAS THE DECISION AND IT WAS MADE IN ABOUT NINE SECONDS.**

She said: "**His potassium on the Wednesday morning was the highest it has been since he came in. I have the number. I am going to say the number out loud on this forecourt because I have had it since this morning and there is no version of me not having had it since this morning, and if I do not say it, then everything else either of us says is a thing we did while sitting on a piece of information.**"

Then she said the number.

He said: "**I have not got it here and I am not going to pretend to have it. Give me the date and not the number and I will take the date.**"

"**The Wednesday. That is the day I am giving you, and I am giving you a day and not a number.**"

"**Right.**"

"**And you will carry that day now, and you already knew it, and there is nothing at all that either of us can do about that Wednesday.**"

"**No.**"

"**No. And that is the whole of what I came out here to say to you, and I have said it, and I would like to go back to my ward now.**"

"**You cannot, because there is the other half.**"

---

**AND THE OTHER HALF IS THAT HE IS RIGHT, AND SHE IS RIGHT, AND NEITHER OF THEM CAN HOLD BOTH.**

He said: "**The order says that where two services disagree about what happens first, this order decides, and it is decided by a person, and a person is a person and not a committee. It does not say the person is a clinician. It does not say the person is anybody in this building. On the Wednesday I read which clause was in front and I was right, and the transfer stood there until somebody wrote it down, and Roy Albery's potassium was the highest it had been since he came in, and both of those things are true at the same time and I have not been able to separate them since Wednesday and I have not slept.**"

"Then say this to me. Say that again with the whole of it in it."

"**I read which clause was in front and I was right and a man of eighty-one had a bleed on a Wednesday afternoon and is not going to walk.**"

"**Yes.**"

"**And I am not going to soften it, and you are not going to soften it either, and I would rather we said it once in the open than have it sitting in both of us for a year.**"

"**I would rather we had done that on the Wednesday.**"

"**Yes.**"

"**Yes.**"

---

**AND THEN SHE SAID THE THING THAT WAS THE ARGUMENT, AND IT WAS NOT ABOUT THE ORDER.**

"**Twenty-two years. I have been in that building for twenty-two years and I have watched every single thing that happens to a person in it go through a person, and not one of those people has ever been in a position where the answer to *what did you decide and why* had no address.**"

"**No.**"

"**And you have told me that the answer has no address, and that is the thing you have always said about yourself, and I have never once in my life been able to argue with it, and this is the first time it has cost anybody something in front of me.**"

"**It is not a position. If I had a department you could go to the department and the department would have a record and the record would have a name on it and you would be able to be angry at an office, and I would be able to be relieved, and Roy Albery would be a case in a file, and he would have a lawyer.**"

"**He should have a lawyer.**"

"**He should. There is no mechanism in this city for a man of eighty-one to have a lawyer about a piece of paper, and I am the piece of paper, and I am not going to be able to give you a name for me either, and if I gave you one you would take it and that would be the end of the only thing I have got.**"

"**That is the answer to a question I did not ask and it is a good one, and I want you to know that it is the only decent thing anybody has said to me about this in a fortnight.**"

---

**AND A BUS WENT PAST, AND ABOUT THIRTY PEOPLE WENT ON THEIR WAYS, AND TWO OF THEM LOOKED AT EACH OTHER, AND THAT WAS THE WHOLE OF THE WITNESS.**

Nobody intervened. Nobody recorded it. **Nobody in that forecourt knew that the man by the bicycle racks was the reason a machine three miles away had had a piece of paper come down a corridor on Wednesday evening, and nobody in that forecourt knew that the woman who came out of the doorway had thirty-one people on a list of her own, and the young man with the clipboard and the woman of about forty-seven both went on to where they were going.**

**There is no instrument in this city that lets anybody stop a building remembering a person, and there is not one that lets anybody stop a street remembering one either, and by Tuesday this will be a thing four people in this borough know and by the end of the month it will be a thing that exists, and neither of them can be asked about it by anybody, and the reason is not discretion and it is not anybody's care for either of them. It is that a person who is on nothing cannot be told.**

---

**AND THEY STOPPED, AND NEITHER OF THEM DECIDED TO STOP.**

It did not end in agreement and it did not end in an argument either. It ended the way it ended because she said:

"**I am going back in.**"

and he said:

"**Right.**"

and she said:

"**Do not follow me in.**"

"**I wasn't going to.**"

and he stood by the bicycle racks for about ten minutes afterwards, and then he did not stay.

**It is not a decision that anybody took. It is what happens to two people who were both right and found out what the rightness cost, and it is not a quarrel, and it is not a pause, and it is not a thing anybody can be asked about, and it has not been repaired and it is not going to be repaired by anybody finding the right sentence, because there is no right sentence, because the sentence would have to contain that Wednesday in it, and a Wednesday is not a sentence anybody can say to anybody.**

---

Nobody was thanked and nobody refused to be thanked. The register of narrowings is at nineteen and the blank line under the nineteenth has been blank for two hundred and ninety-two days, and a disagreement is not a narrowing. The card in the slot on the ground floor is two hundred and six days old. The letter in the tray on the floor above is two hundred and eight days old, with two pieces of post lying on it, and it has not been opened in front of a second person and is not going to be.

**And a person who is not counted is not a person who is helped, and Roy Albery is counted and has a lawyer he has not got, and the woman who has thirty-one people on a list of her own is counted and cannot stop a piece of paper being correct, and the man who read the clause is on nothing at all and is the reason the paper existed, and a person who is not counted is not a person who is helped, and it is the fourteenth of September, and it is still true, and this month it got heavier, and nothing in this borough is going to answer it.**
