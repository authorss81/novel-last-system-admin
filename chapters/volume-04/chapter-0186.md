# Chapter 0186 — Page Two

Thursday the thirteenth of August 2015, from about half past ten in the morning, a first-floor room over a shop on a parade of shops in Tideglass.

**A valid instrument was read out in a room, in daylight, with a date on it and a blank at the end of it, and it is the answer to a question this city refused to answer, and it works by putting a person in a field. It is not an expiry. It asks a question in ninety days, and the question has a line at the bottom of it for a person to agree to. He was in that room and he drafted nothing and signed nothing and administered nothing and delivered nothing and opposed nothing, and he was not on the agenda, and nobody asked him anything.**

---

**IT IS TWO PAGES OF PAPER AND IT IS ON THE TABLE BY NINE FORTY, AND FOUR PEOPLE ARE IN THE ROOM.**

Councillor Iven Sore, who brought it. The woman of about fifty-eight, who has the keys to the room and opens it on a Saturday. **A man of about sixty-four, who is a duty clerk at a works depot on the far side of the tracks and who is the person a printing run has to go through before it exists.**

And a man of thirty-two at the back who is on nothing.

**There is no chair of that body and he may not chair anything. Nobody took minutes, there was nothing in that room anybody was required to minute, and he was required to minute nothing.**

---

**AND SHE READ IT OUT, ALL OF IT, INCLUDING THE PARTS SHE THOULD HAVE LEFT OUT.**

She did not summarise it and she did not say the interesting part first and she read the whole thing at the speed of somebody reading somebody else's words, and the room was four people and a window open.

> **CENTRAL CONTINUITY ORDER**
> **Made the thirteenth of August 2015. Page one of two.**
>
> **1. Precedence.** Where two services in this district disagree about what happens first, this order decides which one goes first. It is decided by a person. A person is a person and not a committee.
>
> **2. The field.** This order is administered in a field. The field holds four things: a person's name, the hour the person was called, the hour the person went home, and the thing the person was called for. **Nothing else goes in the field.**
>
> **3. The call.** A person in this district may be called on to work. A person who has been called on may not decline. The call is made by a person. The call is not required to be in writing and there is no form for it.
>
> **4. Closure.** Where a hazard is inside a district boundary, the district may be closed. A closure is not required to be heard before it is made. A closure is posted on the street it closes.
>
> **5. Transfer.** Where a person is to be moved from one site to another, the sending site holds precedence over the receiving site until the receiving site has confirmed capacity in writing.
>
> **6. The second call.** A person whose name is in the field may be called again at any time, without limit, and no person may decline a second call.
>
> **7. Renewal.** This order stands for ninety days from the date of making. At the end of that period it is renewed or it is not renewed. Page two.
>
> ---
>
> **Page two.**
> **This order is renewed on the eleventh of November 2015, or it is not renewed.**
> **Agreed by —**
>
> *and the line goes down the page and stops about two inches from the bottom edge and there is nothing after it.*

---

**AND THE ROOM HEARD IT, AND THE ROOM WAS QUIET, AND SOMEBODY HAD TO SAY SOMETHING, AND IT WAS NOT HER.**

"It is a good document," said the woman of about fifty-eight, and she was not being kind, and she went on: "**And I want to say the thing about it, because nobody else in this room is going to say the thing about it and one of us should.**"

"What thing."

"**That it is the first one anybody in this city has written down that would actually work.**"

She said it flatly, the way you say a thing you have checked twice in your own head. "**It has a date on it and it has a person in it and it says what the person was for. Every correct thing that has happened in this borough in nine months happened because somebody who was not required did it, and not one of them can be shown to anybody, and I have been the one who can show it for about six weeks and I have not been asked for it once and I am not going to be.**"

Nobody said anything to that. She picked up her keys and went out on the landing and left the door open behind her because it is a hot day and because she has the keys.

---

**AND THE ARGUMENT WAS THAT IT WORKS BY PUTTING A PERSON IN A FIELD, AND THE PERSON WHO SAID IT WAS THE ONLY PERSON WHO HAD READ IT TWICE.**

The man of about sixty-four had the folder on his knee and he had had it since Wednesday, because a thing that is going to be read out gets read by the person whose job it is to know what a document is, and his job is that, and he is not a councillor and does not want to be.

"**Then it is the answer,**" he said. "**And I have been the person who gets asked whether there is a way round a thing for twenty-nine years and there is not one. This city has never once managed to say who did what, and it has spent the whole of that being right about it, and the whole of being right about it is that the boy who carried your shopping is not a person a building can go to.**"

"**And this thing is a box with a boy's name in it.**"

"**Yes.**"

"**And the box is in a building.**"

"**It is.**"

"**Then we have spent nine months being right about a thing and it turns out we were right about a mechanism and not about a value.**"

Nobody answered him and he did not press it, and he said the other half himself about ten seconds later, and he said it more quietly:

"**I have not got an argument against it. I have got a thing I want somebody else to hold for me. Clause three says a person who has been called on may not decline. That is the whole instrument. Everything else on that page is a formality around clause three, and clause three has never existed anywhere in this city, and it works, and I have known since Wednesday that I would vote for it and I have not got a way of saying why that is frightening.**"

---

**AND THE MAN OF ABOUT SIXTY-FOUR WORKED OUT WHAT THE SECOND PAGE WAS IN ABOUT THREE SECONDS, AND THE THREE SECONDS WERE NOT AVAILABLE TO ANYBODY ELSE IN THAT ROOM.**

He had it open. He turned two pages, went back one, put his thumb on the line, and looked at the woman of about fifty-eight in the doorway and said:

"**This is not an expiry.**"

"**It says ninety days.**"

"**It says ninety days and it says a date, and the date is the eleventh of November, and the eleventh of November is not when this stops. This is when somebody is asked. There is a line at the bottom of page two with a name going on it and the name is not there, and a page that only stopped something would not have a line on it.**"

Nobody in that room had read the thing twice. She had written the parts she could write. The man of thirty-two at the back was in a room in Tideglass on the twenty-fourth of June when a page was read out that he was not asked to read and did not read, and he is reading the second page of this for the first time this morning with four other people in the room, and that is not an advantage and it is not a place he is standing.

**The woman of fifty-eight said the only sentence in the room that was about what the line would do, and she said it from the landing with her keys in her hand:**

"**Nine months of this borough have spent all their time finding out what a blank does. The blank under the nineteenth one. The empty box at the bottom of your page from May. The column nobody writes in. The row at the hospital that will not say the word. And this is the first one anybody has written down with a date sitting on top of it, and nobody in this borough has ever noticed what a date with a blank after it does to a room.**"

"**And nobody is required to put a name on it,**" said Councillor Sore. "**That is deliberate and I will not have it improved on. There is no body in this city that can require anybody to do anything, and that is the only reason a document like this one can be made at all, and I am not going to pretend to you that the same thing that makes it possible is not what will make it permanent.**"

**Nobody said that last part out loud in any other room in this borough, and four people heard it, and it was correct, and it has not been written down.**

---

**AND HE DID NONE OF IT.**

He did not draft it. He did not sign it. He did not witness it. He did not administer it, and nobody asked him to, and there is no way in this city to hand an instrument to a man with no department, and he is not a party to it and has not been since the second of June. He did not deliver it and he did not oppose it, and he did not say one word about any of the seven clauses at any point in that room, and nobody asked him for one word, and there was no agenda with his name near it and no minute and no seat and no form.

**He has asked no question of anybody since the twenty-fifth of February 2015 and he asked none that morning, and being in the room where the only working instrument in this city was read out is not standing and is not a promotion and is not going to be anything.**

---

**IT IS VALID. THAT IS THE FINDING AND IT IS NOT A RELIEF.**

It is valid, and it is readable, and it is the answer to a question this city refused to answer in nine months, and on the afternoon it was made it did what it was made to do. **It works because it is the cheapest possible way of doing one thing: it makes a person's name into the address of a piece of work, and every correct thing anybody in this borough has done for nine months was done by a person whose name was on nothing.**

The register of narrowings is at nineteen and it did not move on the day the order was made, and it did not move on the day the order was read out, and the blank line under the nineteenth has been blank for two hundred and sixty days, and an order that decides what happens first in a district of four hundred thousand people is not a reason to write on the nineteenth. The card in the slot on the ground floor is a hundred and seventy-four days old and nobody has taken it out. The letter in the tray on the floor above is a hundred and seventy-six days old and it has two pieces of post on it and it has not been opened in front of a second person.

**And a person who is not counted is not a person who is helped, and a person who is counted is a person somebody can ring, and this order did the second one on a Thursday morning in August and it is going to do the first one as well without ever meaning to, and the difference between the two sentences is a line two inches from the bottom of page two, and that line is the only future this document has.**

**Ninety days from a Thursday in August is a Wednesday in November. Nobody in this borough has written anything on anything about the eleventh of November 2015 and there is a blank on page two with a date over it and the whole of this borough knows what a blank is, and not one person in this city is going to be asked to put anything in it, and it is going to stay like that until somebody in a room decides to be the one.**
