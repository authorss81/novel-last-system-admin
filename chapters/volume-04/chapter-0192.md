# Chapter 0192 — The Sixth Clause

Friday the eighteenth of September 2015, from about half past ten in the morning, a parade of shops in Tideglass, in daylight.

**One clause of a valid instrument was stopped in front of everybody by a man who could produce nothing at all, and the city was saved, and he took public responsibility for it in daylight without a piece of paper, and there is nowhere to put it. Then somebody who is right about the need put the shape of the whole thing out where anybody could read it, and that is the last thing that happened, and nobody is going to be able to put that back either. The renewal point falls due on a Wednesday in November and nobody in this borough answers it.**

---

**THERE WERE ABOUT THIRTY PEOPLE ON THAT GROUND AT ELEVEN O'CLOCK AND EVERY ONE OF THEM WAS THERE BECAUSE SOMEONE HAD RUNG A TELEPHONE.**

A main came off under a parade of shops at about ten to nine in the morning and it is still off, and about thirty people had been called on to a piece of ground outside a hardware shop, and the work is not difficult and it is not skilled and it takes two days, and **clause three is how thirty people came to be standing on that pavement at about half past ten on a Friday morning in September, the hottest day this borough has had since June, with no emergency of any kind.**

They had been called because there is a hole in the road. That is all. **There is no longer any reason on earth for a hole in the road to put thirty people on a pavement, and there was one in August, and the reason is a piece of paper with four things in a field on it and one of the four is a name.**

---

**AND THE MAN OF ABOUT TWENTY-THREE HAD BEEN CALLED FIVE TIMES SINCE THE SECOND OF SEPTEMBER AND HE WAS ON THAT PAVEMENT FOR THE FIFTH.**

He had been called on the second of September and on the eleventh and on the sixteenth, and on the seventeenth at about nine in the evening, and he had not declined any of them and he had not said no to one and he had not been asked whether he could, and he was on that pavement because at about nine this morning a person rang him and said come if you can, and he said he could.

**And the man of about seventy-three was there too, for the fifth time, and he has been called nine times since the twenty-second of August and has not declined one, and on Wednesday morning he was called at about five.**

---

**AND JONAS WAS THERE BECAUSE A PRECEDENCE QUESTION HAD ARRIVED AT THE PLACE HE HAPPENS TO BE AT, WHICH IS WHAT HAPPENS NOW, ABOUT TWICE A WEEK, AND THERE IS NO LIST OF WHERE HE IS.**

A question about which of two things goes first, on a road, with thirty people on it, and no answer was going to arrive from anywhere else, because there is no body in this city that can require anybody to do anything and therefore nobody can be instructed to do anything, and the only person anybody can ask to read a document twice and answer for it is a man of thirty-two who is on nothing and has never been asked to be on anything.

**He has answered every question that has come to him since the thirteenth of August and he has not declined one. He cannot be instructed, cannot be given a department, cannot be given a form, and cannot be thanked, and there is no rota of him and no way in this city to take this off him.**

---

**AND HE SAID THE CLAUSE OUT LOUD, IN DAYLIGHT, AT ABOUT ELEVEN FIFTEEN, IN FRONT OF THIRTY PEOPLE AND TWO COUNCILLORS AND A MAN WHO HAD COME IN A VAN.**

**"I am not going to apply clause six."**

"**You cannot do that.**" said the man of about thirty-eight. "**That is not yours to do.**"

"**No. It is not. I have no authority you could write down and I am not going to produce any and I am not asking anybody to notice that I have not.**"

"**Then it does not stop.**"

"**It stops for everything it touches, this morning, and it will start again on Tuesday unless somebody in this city with a department says it does not, and there are not nine of those and there are two, and I have said this in front of thirty people, so put it in front of two hundred.**"

---

**AND HE SAID WHY, AND THE REASON HE SAID WAS TRUE, AND IT WAS NOT THE WHOLE OF THE REASON.**

"**Clause six says a person whose name is in the field may be called again at any time, without limit, and no person may decline a second call. Clause three says a person who has been called on may not decline. Those two together are the only reason this borough has thirty people on a pavement at half past ten in September with a hole in the road that was dug on Wednesday.**"

"**That is the whole of the argument for it. It works. It worked at seven o'clock on the twenty-second of August. It has worked every day since and there is nothing wrong with any of it.**"

"**And here is the other half and I am not going to pretend it is a technicality. A person who cannot say no to a second call cannot be asked anything at all. Not cannot say no — cannot be asked. Once you can be called without limit, there is no question left that anybody in this city is allowed to put to you, because every question you are asked after that is a question you are not allowed to answer.**"

"**This borough has spent nine months finding that out the slow way and I have watched it happen, and this order knows nothing about it, and it does not need to, because this order was written for a hole in a road.**"

"**I am stopping clause six. I am not stopping anything else. The other six clauses are in force and they should be and I will answer every question that comes to me about them, and the one I am stopping is the one that makes people permanent.**"

---

**AND IT WAS THE RIGHT THING AND IT SAVED SOMETHING, AND NOBODY IN THAT STREET KNEW WHAT.**

The second call went off at about a quarter past eleven. Not cancelled — **there is no instrument in this city that cancels anything.** The man of about thirty-eight rang four people between about twenty past eleven and about half past eleven and said one sentence: *not this time, not for a while, and I cannot tell you when.* And four people accepted that, because a person who has said yes three times and is told no once knows exactly what the yes was worth.

**And the field in that building stopped growing, and it is closed, and a number of names in it are in it and that number is not given to anybody and is not going to be, and it has been there since the second of August and nobody has counted it and nobody is going to.**

**And that is what was saved. Not the water on that parade, which will be on by the middle of next week either way, and not the road, which has been dug twice before it. The thing that was saved is that the answer to *who is this person* is still a sentence somebody has to say out loud to somebody who happens to be standing there, and not an address.**

**And a man at the end of a lane who is sixty pays for every drop of his own water and is not on anything, and nobody in this borough is going to go up that lane, and he does not know that anything happened on a parade of shops in Tideglass this morning and he is not going to be told.**

**And there are places in this city that are not on the drawing, and there is nobody who could be shown one and nobody who would know what to say about it, and that was true at about ten to twelve this morning and it will be true on the eleventh of November, and it is the whole of the reason a man of thirty-two said one sentence out loud on a pavement in front of thirty people.**

**And a hole in a record is still the only honest thing anybody in this borough has produced about a person in nine months, and nobody has filled it and nobody is going to, and there is nothing at the bottom of it and nobody in this city is going to be told what is.**

---

**AND HE TOOK RESPONSIBILITY FOR IT IN DAYLIGHT, AND HE DID NOT HAVE A FORM FOR IT, AND THERE IS NOWHERE TO PUT IT.**

"**That was me,**" he said, to the two councillors and to the thirty people and to the whole of a parade of shops in daylight at about ten to twelve. "**I read it and I did not apply it. Nobody told me not to and nobody has backed me up and there is nobody in this city I could hand this to. I am not a member of anything, I have no department, I have no grade, and I am not going to be given one for this, and if anybody in this city ever tries to give me one for it I am going to hand it back.**"

Councillor Sore said: "**I made that instrument and I would like it minuted — no, there is nobody to minute it, and I would like it said in front of these people that I said it: the man has no authority, and I am not going to say so again, and he has just stopped a clause of my document with a sentence, and the sentence was right, and the order stands, and I am not going to pretend to anybody that anything has been cancelled today, because nothing has been cancelled. It has been bounded. That is a different word and it is the one that is true.**"

"**You are going to have to explain that to about nine people who were going to be called on again,**" said the man of about thirty-eight.

"**Yes. And I will do it, and it will take me a year, and the year is mine and not his, and I would like that said in the same breath as the other thing because the other thing is going to be the only one anybody repeats.**"

---

**AND THEN, AT ABOUT TWENTY PAST TWELVE, A WOMAN HE HAD NOT SEEN BEFORE SAID SOMETHING, AND SHE WAS NOT AFRAID OF ANYBODY.**

She is about forty-six. She is a committee clerk at a committee whose letterhead does not have a name on it, and she has been on the side of the order since the spring, and she is not Councillor Sore and she is not anybody who has been in this borough before, and she had been standing at the edge of that pavement since about half past eleven with a folder.

"**You are going to want to know whether you should have done that,**" she said. "**It went out at about nine this morning. It is in four places and it will be in forty by tonight.**"

He said nothing.

"**It is the shape of it. That is all it is. The nodes, the eight districts that hold a term, which clause sits above which, where the term in each of them starts and where it stops, and the fact that until the thirteenth of August this city had never once in nine months put a person's name into a piece of paper on purpose, and that on the thirteenth of August it did, in one morning, by reading out two pages in a room over a shop.**"

"**And the part nobody is going to like is this: it is not a scandal. It is a drawing. It is a drawing of how the things are put together, and it has been on the wall of a room in this city that people have walked past for a decade without being shown it, and I have put it where anybody can look at it, and I am right that we cannot renew what we cannot see, and there is a date in November on the end of that document with nothing under it, and a city that cannot see its own shape has no business being asked in November whether it wants to keep the shape.**"

---

**AND THERE IS ONE SQUARE ON THAT DRAWING THAT HAS NOTHING IN IT, AND HE READ IT WHILE SHE WAS STILL TALKING.**

He read the sheet the way he reads everything, which is once and properly and with the whole of it at once, and there is one square on it with nothing in it, and it is not a district and it is not a seat, and it is not on any of the four sheets that will be in four places tonight. **He did not say so then and he did not say so afterwards, and there is nobody in this borough who is going to be able to say so either, and that is the last thing in this borough that is still unspent, and it is now in a woman's hands who does not know she is holding it.**

---

**AND HE COULD HAVE STOPPED HER AND HE DID NOT, AND HE WAS NOT GOING TO, AND HE KNEW EXACTLY WHY SHE HAD DONE IT.**

It is the first irreversible act anybody in this borough has produced in nine months. Everything correct that has happened here was done by a person who could not be found afterwards, and every one of them was undone by the next fortnight or held only by about four people, and none of them could be pointed at, and that is why nothing in this borough has ever been proved. **She has made a thing that cannot be taken back, on purpose, having worked out that it could not be taken back, and she is right about the need, and she is not a villain, and she is not wrong, and every word she said on that pavement was correct.**

---

**AND THEN EVERYBODY WENT BACK TO THE HOLE IN THE ROAD, BECAUSE THERE WAS STILL A HOLE IN THE ROAD.**

Thirty people. Two councillors. A man of about thirty-eight with a telephone. A man of about seventy-three, who had been called nine times and had not declined one and had been told once, in one breath, that there would not be a second time for a while, and who said, to nobody: "**That's fair.**"

**A man of about twenty-three.** A clerk of about forty-six. A man of about thirty-two with nothing in his hands and nothing in his name.

Nobody was thanked. He was not given a title, a grade, a department, a chair, a seat, a membership, a form, a rota row, a thank-you or a way of being thanked, and nobody offered him one, and he did not have to refuse anything, and there is nowhere in this borough to put the fact that he did it, and he is not going to look for somewhere.

---

**NINETY DAYS FROM A THURSDAY IN AUGUST IS A WEDNESDAY IN NOVEMBER. THE ELEVENTH OF NOVEMBER 2015.**

**Nobody in this borough has written anything about that date on anything. There is a line at the bottom of page two with a date over it and there is a district of four hundred thousand people that will be asked in the middle of that morning whether it wants to keep the only instrument that has ever worked, and the answer to that is a name, and no body in this city can require anybody to give one, and nobody in this city is going to ask.**

The register of narrowings is at nineteen and the blank line under the nineteenth has been blank for two hundred and ninety-six days, and an order that bounds a call is not a narrowing and a date in November is not a reason, and nobody is obliged. The card in the slot on the ground floor is two hundred and ten days old and nobody has taken it out of it. The letter in the tray on the floor above is two hundred and twelve days old, with two pieces of post lying on it, and it has not been opened in front of a second person and is not going to be.

**And a person who is not counted is not a person who is helped, and it is the eighteenth of September, and about thirty people went back to a hole in a road because they had been asked, and a woman who sorts the bins on a street of terraces is not a person anybody in this borough can name, and nine people said yes to a correct question in a heat in August and it has not helped them, and in August a city learned how to count everybody it needs, and it did not help anybody, and nothing in this borough is going to be asked about that in November.**

---

**AND THE LAST OF IT, WHICH IS A QUESTION, AND IT IS NOT ABOUT A PERSON, AND NOT ABOUT A FLOOD BARRIER, A SCHOOL CROSSING, A COUNTER-AMENDMENT, A SECOND SIGNATURE, A PROVISIONAL CIVIC STANDING, A DATE IN NOVEMBER, AN EXPIRY OR AN ORDER.**

So the two of them are opposites, and both of them worked, and only one of them can be repeated.

The one that can be repeated is a page with a person's name in a field on it. It was read out in daylight in a room over a shop, and it is in force, and it did what it was made to do, and it will do it again on any morning that anybody chooses to make it.

The one that cannot be repeated has been going for nine months, and it has about four people in it, and none of the four can be found, and it has cost this borough nothing at all, and there is nothing anywhere in this city that a person could be shown, and no building in it has ever been asked.

**A city that can count everybody it needs, and a city nobody can be found in, cannot both go on. So which of the two is the first one this city is going to have to stop, and what is the thing a person has to be holding in order to be allowed to stop the first one, and what is the thing a person has to be holding in order to stop the second one — which is nothing, and has always been nothing, and is the only thing anybody in this borough has managed to keep.**
