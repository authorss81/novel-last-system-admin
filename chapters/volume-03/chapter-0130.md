# Chapter 0130 — What Is On The Floor, In February

Wednesday the eleventh of March 2015, from about ten past ten in the morning, the vestibule, ground floor, the Old Ward. In daylight, in a room eleven feet by nine, with about eleven people in it, all of them standing sideways.

**There is no chair. There was no chair on the twenty-eighth of January and there is no chair now, and the reason has not changed and is not going to, and it is the first thing anybody who has ever come into that room has to work out and it takes about four seconds: a body of nine seats has one power, which is to require a party holding continuity to answer, receive the answer, and be asked to consider a matter. A chair is not one of the three parts of it. There is no chair of that body and there never was.**

**There was no question of his in that room until the Friday morning, and it was five words, and it was not a question anybody had prepared, and it came out of him because the man in front of him had said *the other one* and had not said what the other one was. He has not asked one in that room about the number of people who come, because on the twenty-third of February he was told that number is on the back of a hand and he has not asked for it since and is not going to.**

---

Before anything else was said, the room said the things that are true, because that is what it does now, and it used to take about nine minutes standing up, and this morning it took about four, and the reason it took four is that the ninth seat said *go on* twice and everybody in that room has learned by now that the only two things that body can do are require a party holding continuity to answer and ask whether a matter may be considered, and both of those had been done in January.

**The lowland cannot be seated.** It is not among the districts the layer draws, a node with no party is not a party, a place is not a person, and there is no instrument in this city by which a ninth district could be constituted in a Methodist hall by anybody in a bad mood. The registrar said that in a hall in Tideglass on the thirteenth of January and said it is correct and has been correct since the first of March and is not going to be fixed, and nobody corrected it, and nobody has fixed it, and it was not on the agenda and it was not going to be.

**The board stands at seven of nine.** The seventh seat has been empty since Wednesday the eighth of October 2014. The eighth has been empty since the evening of Wednesday the twenty-sixth of November 2014. Nobody can fill either and nobody can unfill either and there is no power in this city that could, and the woman who keeps the minutes read both dates out and did not editorialise.

**Four of the nine have said they will stop if it turns into a job.** One of them gave a reason when she was asked and asked for it not to be written down, and it is not in the minute, and that was agreed and both of those were correct. Nobody has stopped. Nobody has been asked again.

A man of forty-four in a building in Gantry Ward cannot be asked one question about any of it, ever, and that is not a protection anybody arranged for him. That is what he is. A man of forty-nine can be asked one question tomorrow morning about anything, for ever, by anybody, and has not been, and is not going to be, and is not untouchable, and there is no argument in this borough in which not asking him is the correct thing to do, and he is not going to be asked anyway.

**A woman of thirty-four is in about eleven people in a Methodist hall in Tideglass and in no document at all.** There is no list of who has it and there is not going to be one, and there is a drawer in that building with no key on it, and nobody has been to the bottom of that street since the nineteenth of January.

A woman of seventy-two is behind a door with a sheet of A4 in a plastic wallet on it, and she is a great deal more frightened than she was on the sixth of May and she is no more findable than she was on the sixth of May, and there is no reason anybody would recognise.

A woman of sixty-one is on no board, in no field and on no rota, and has nine hundred and eighty-six Wednesdays written on a card under a rubber band in a biscuit tin, and it is a number of Wednesdays and it is not a number of people, and nobody has ever asked her about it and nobody is going to.

**And the ninth seat of that body is fifty-four and unpaid and findable at a shopfront and has been since the fourth of July, and she is the only one of the nine that anybody can write to, and nobody in that room has ever told her that, and nobody in that room is going to, and the reason is a card in a plastic sleeve on a wall in a Methodist hall and not anybody's kindness.**

---

And at about twenty past eleven on the Friday morning, the thirteenth of March, the man of about fifty-six asked the question.

He is not a member and did not come as a member and is not on any list. He asked it on the twenty-eighth of January and got five items and about four seconds, and he has not come since, and nobody asked him to, and he came in daylight and stood at the wall with his back to the slot in the counter, which has had a card in it since the twentieth of February that nobody has taken out and nobody has put up.

He did not apologise for asking and he did not say *may I*.

"**You told me what it is made of. I have been carrying that for six weeks. It has not once told me what it is for and I have stopped wanting to know, because I have worked out that I am not the person it is for.**"

"**No,**" said Jonas Mercer. "**You are not.**"

"**So I am going to ask you the other one, and it is not the same question, and you have never been asked it, and I want you to know that I know that, because I have read the minutes and there is a finding of a borough safety panel in one of them that was said once in this room six weeks ago by a man who is not on the agenda either, and nobody has resolved it and nobody is going to.**"

"**What is the other one.**"

"**What it costs.**"

---

And he did not decline it, which is the first thing anybody has noticed about him since the third of April, and it took about four seconds, and then he said it, and it is a sentence and not a scheme, and it is not the sentence anybody in that room was expecting, and it is not the five things again, and it is not going on a wall.

"**A service floor costs the people who are on it and not the people who use it.**"

Nobody in that room said anything, and the room is eleven feet by nine and eleven people were standing sideways in it.

"**I want to be exact about who I mean, because I have got nine minutes and about eleven people and a woman with a sheet and I am going to be exact.**"

"**The trough by the lock-ups is still full of something that is not water. The man with the bowser has still got a man on the bowser and he is still paid on Fridays out of his own pocket. The market standpipe has not run since Friday the twenty-eighth of November and there is no party in this city who can be made to make it run, and the six of them are still eleven thousand two hundred and eighty pounds a year and that is a winter figure and that is one boiler each, and nobody has been asked to pay any part of it and nobody is going to be.**"

"**Four hundred and twenty women in this borough have their instruments out of a room on level zero and there is a paragraph number in a public report and there is no person in front of the paragraph number and there will not be, and the machine is still running and somebody is still putting its own strip in a drawer, and I have not found that person and I am not going to.**"

"**A man of thirty-eight had a plastic wallet in the glovebox of a van by the middle of February and nobody in this city can say how many pieces of paper are in it, and he has worked out on his own that a person can ask a question in this borough and get a date, and nobody in this borough taught him that, and nobody in this borough is able to tell him what to do with what he has found out, and he is thirty-eight and he works nights.**"

"**That is the cost. None of it is the price. The price is the next part, and the next part is the only reason I have not slept much since the twenty-sixth of January, and I have not written it down in a place where anybody can find it, and I am going to say it once in a room in daylight and then I am going to stop.**"

---

"**The price is findability.**"

"**Every person in this borough who has ever stood at that counter is now slightly easier to find than they were, and none of them was asked, and every single one of the reasons it is better is a real reason and none of the reasons it is worse is a complaint.**"

"**It is better because a person can be wrong in public on a date, and being wrong in public on a date is worth more than being right in a van. I have watched a man of fifty-eight work that out in about nine seconds at a counter in February and it is the single most useful thing I have seen anybody do in this borough in nine months.**"

"**And it is worse because a person who is findable is a person who can be asked, and that sentence was given to me in a kitchen in December by a woman of fifty-four and I did not write it down, and I have been arguing with it for three months and I have not beaten it, and I am not going to, and I am not going to improve it, and I am not going to put it in front of the ninth seat in this room.**"

"**Here is what it costs, specifically, and I am going to say all of it because you asked me the price and not the description.**"

"**A woman of fifty-four who has stood in that shopfront since the fourth of July and has not been paid a penny and who is the only door in a building of thirty-one rented rooms is now, if I have done my eight months properly, very slightly easier to find than she was in June. And she has not been told that and she is not going to be, and she cannot un-find herself, and neither can I.**"

"**A records supervisor of about fifty who is not on a rota and cannot be made to be on one and who keeps a drawer under a counter with a date and a hand on everything in it and no reference and no name on any of it is now a person whose method is knowable, and her method is knowable because a man of thirty-eight worked it out in a kitchen on a Sunday, and in about four years there will be a person who can describe exactly what that counter does, and that person will not be her, and that is the correct outcome and it is also the thing that is going to happen to her.**"

"**A man of forty-eight who said a correct thing in a corridor in August is now a man somebody could ask about, except that nobody is able to, because the only instrument that keeps a person from being askable is a person who does not know, and I said that out loud in a corridor at a hospital on the sixth of February, and it is the only thing in nine months that I am going to be judged on.**"

"**A man of forty-four is a wall and a man of forty-nine is a door and I made one of them in August and the other one in November and neither of them was in a room and neither of them has been told anything and I cannot unmake either of them and I am not going to try.**"

"And nobody has been asked whether they can afford that. Not one person. Not her, not him, not the thirty-eight-year-old, not the sixty-one-year-old with the card under the rubber band, not the woman of thirty-four in a drawer, not the woman of seventy-two behind a door, not the fourth hundred and twentieth woman behind a paragraph number. Nobody has been asked, and this borough has no instrument for asking, and the only instrument it has ever had is a form, and a form has a box on it, and the box is where the answer would go, and a person's answer to *can you afford to be found* does not fit in a box and if it did it would not be true."
