# Chapter 0124 — The Second Asker

**Wednesday the eleventh of February 2015, a corridor, second floor, the civic offices. And Friday the thirteenth of February, about ten past ten in the morning, the vestibule, ground floor, the Old Ward.**

---

**He had a plastic folder and it was the fourth one in nine months, and he did not fill it, and the reason he did not fill it is the reason he has spent eight months building things nobody can put anything in.**

The minute of the fifteenth of January had gone out on the Thursday and it is public and anybody may have it at that counter for nothing, and Jonas Mercer had a copy of it in the folder and had read the item about the lowland four times, and here is the whole of that item:

> **Item 6. A person attended, not on the agenda, not a party, not a member and not a departmental officer, and asked that it be recorded that a thing exists at a place where a thing is needed. He stated the condition of five nodes in his own words and declined to state the sixth, and the declining is recorded below. He was asked one question, which was answered.**
>
> **Recorded: that the state of each node was given and is recorded here as a statement and not as a return, and that no number was given and none is recorded, and that no name appears in this minute and this minute has no box for one.**
>
> **Recorded: that a refusal was given at the end of the sixth and was not asked for again, and that the person giving it is not to be asked where the sixth is, by this body or by anybody.**
>
> **Recorded: that this body has no party to serve, no seat to refer the matter to, no money, and no power to require any person to do anything.**
>
> **Recorded: that this body has done nothing, in the correct words, and that the doing of nothing is not a defect in the minute.**
>
> **Nothing in this item requires any person to do anything.**

**A minute that says a person asked and names nobody cannot be put beside a bowser in February.**

That is the sentence. He had it on the Wednesday morning in a corridor outside the records office and he turned it over about nine times and it did not get better. The minute is a statement and not a return. It binds nobody. It requires nothing. It has a date on it and no name on it and no number in it and it cannot be produced at a water point, because the water points are in the open and a piece of paper about six of them is not a thing you can hold up at a trough, and **the man who asked for it is not a party, and a body that cannot require anybody to do anything cannot be told what it will do, and both of those sentences are on a card on a wall in a Methodist hall and neither of them may be argued out of the other.**

---

**On the Wednesday he worked out why this was the second asker and not the first, and it is the most important piece of arithmetic anybody in this borough has done in nine months and it is four sentences long.**

**A man who asked once could still believe it was an accident.**

Abu Bakayoko went into a hall on the thirteenth of January, in daylight, in front of about nine people, and said what he knew about five of six water points in about two minutes, and asked for a line in a minute saying that a thing exists at a place where a thing is needed, and he got it, and the minute went out, and it is a beautiful piece of paper and it does absolutely nothing.

**He has had four weeks to hold it.** Four weeks is long enough for a man to stop believing that a thing which happened once can happen again by accident. Four weeks is long enough to read a document that says *this body has done nothing* and to work out that the nothing is not going to stop being nothing, because nothing in the item requires anybody to do anything and there is no box anywhere in this borough in which anybody's name could be written to make it require something.

**The second asker is not a bigger asker. A second asker is a man who has run out of accident.** And the terrible thing, and Jonas Mercer said it out loud in that corridor to nobody at all on the second floor of the civic offices at about twenty past eleven on a Wednesday in February, is that **he is right to, and there is no arrangement in this borough that can make him not right to, and the reason he is right to is the reason nine seconds in a corridor in January was the right length and eleven seconds at a counter on the twenty-sixth was the right length.**

---

**On the Friday morning at about ten past ten he came in off the street with the minute in a plastic wallet, and he had it out before he had got to the counter, which Jonas Mercer understood completely and which cost the man something to do.**

"**I have got your minute.**"

"You have got the copy of it," said Nina Calder, "and anybody may have it for nothing, and you are welcome, and I am not going to pretend I do not know what you are going to do with it, and I would rather you did it in this room than in a queue outside a water office."

He put it flat on the counter. It was folded in four. He put his finger on the sixth numbered line and read the words *as a statement and not as a return* out loud, twice, slowly, the way a man reads a thing he has read before to somebody he is trying to convince.

"**A statement and not a return. That is what you are telling me. In January I told nine people what was at five places and eleven thousand two hundred and eighty pounds a year came back in a piece of paper saying a person asked, and it did not ask anybody to do anything, and there is no name on it, and I cannot even prove I was the one who asked.**"

"That is correct."

"**Then what is it for.**"

---

**And she said the answer, and it was nine seconds, and it is the answer of the whole of Volume 03 to the question of what happens next in this borough, and nobody wrote it down.**

"**It is a statement, and it stands, and a statement is a thing that is true, and it is on the public record, and nobody may take it off. It is not a return. A return is a thing that goes to somebody who has to do something about it, and there is no somebody and there has not been one since the first of March and there is not going to be one this year. So you have got what you asked for and it is worth exactly what you asked for, and I want to be exact about that, because you did ask for it and I would not want you to think you asked for the wrong thing.**"

"**Then I am back where I was on the thirteenth of January.**"

"**You are back where you were on the thirteenth of January with one thing added, which is that a statement of a thing existing at a place is now a public document and cannot be argued with. That is the whole of the addition and it is worth more than it sounds.**"

---

**And then she did the other thing, and it is the first thing anybody in this borough has offered anybody in about four months, and it took eleven seconds, and it is worth nothing.**

She wrote a date on the top of a slip and turned it round and pushed it across, and there was no box on it and no reference and no name on it. It was the third she had given to the same man and the second about the same trough, and she said:

"**As of this morning, and I am held to this morning and to nothing else: the market standpipe on a post is not running. The trough by the lock-ups is not frozen and there is something in it that is not water and I am not held to what. The stand with a bucket on a chain and forty slots on the board is being used, and it works, and the man who turns the bucket is not findable and I cannot help you with that and I have never been able to help you with that. The stand at the back of the school wall runs for forty minutes at a quarter to eight and then it is a pipe. The tap by the yard is dry. And the sixth is not in this and you did not tell me and I did not ask you and there is no box on it.**"

He did not pick it up for about four seconds.

"**That is what was true this morning.**"

"**That is what was true at about ten past ten this morning. On the second of February I gave you a piece of paper saying what was true at ten past ten on the second of February, and it is in your inside pocket now, and I cannot produce it to anybody but you, and there is no reference on it and no box on this counter that says I wrote it, and that is the whole of what a piece of paper is.**"

"**And on the third of March I could come in here and say that is not what it was.**"

"**Yes.**"

"**And you would not tell me I was wrong.**"

"**I would tell you what is true this morning and put a date on it, and if that is the same as the date you have, then the two pieces of paper are the same piece of paper twice, and if it is not, then the one in your pocket is a thing that was true and has stopped being true, and both of those are free and neither of them costs you anything but a walk.**"

---

**And he stood at that counter in a room eleven feet by nine for a while and worked it out, and Jonas Mercer was four feet away and watched a man of fifty-eight discover, in about nine seconds, the thing that nine months of arguing in this borough has not produced.**

**Nothing about the six water points has changed.** Four pounds a bucket is four pounds a bucket. The tap by the yard is dry and it will be dry in June. The market standpipe has not run since Friday the twenty-eighth of November and there is no party in this city who can be asked to make it run, and a node with no party is not a party, and that reason is correct and has been correct since the first of March.

**And what has changed is that on Tuesday he will drive to four of the six and not to five, and he will know which four, and if the state he has been given is wrong he can come back to a counter in a vestibule and be told he is wrong in eleven seconds and be right, in front of a person who is required to answer him.**

That is the whole of it, and it is the thing this borough has been looking at since the third of April without anybody naming it, and it took a man with a bowser on a Friday morning:

**A man who can be wrong in public is not a man who has to be right in a van.**

And he is still not going to get his Tuesday back. Nobody in this borough is going to get their Tuesday back. **What he has got is a date, and a date is the cheapest thing in this city, and it is worth what it costs, which is eleven seconds, and it works, and the reason it works is that it is not a service and there is no arrangement behind it and nobody is required to keep giving it.**

He put the slip in the same inside pocket as the other two and said, "**That is the third one.**"

"Yes."

"**Then there are three.**"

"**There are three that I have given to you. I am not going to tell you how many I have given to anybody, and you have not asked me, and if you ask me I shall answer you, because a counter is required to answer, and I shall answer you in a way that is a counter's answer, and I would rather you did not spend your question on it.**"

---

**And Jonas Mercer asked nothing in that vestibule on the Friday morning. He had wanted to ask one thing and he was not going to, and there were no questions in the corridor outside either, and the counting has been right in every month of this year.**

He wanted to ask her how many of them there are. He has wanted to since the second of February. **The reason he is not going to is the reason she is not going to tell, and it is the reason a shelf is not a list and a list is not a series and a series is a rota: the number of what a counter does is the one thing in this borough that has to stay where it is, and the only place it can stay is the back of a hand, and a hand is not a record and cannot be produced and cannot be asked after by a person with a folder.**

---

**And he did the arithmetic in the corridor, and he did not do a total, because he is not entitled to a total, and what he did instead was an interval, and the interval is the whole of what this chapter is.**

Three pieces of paper in this borough mention that trough. The oldest is dated the twenty-sixth of January and mentions it in a line between four others, and it went into a man's inside pocket in the rain and he has not looked at it once. The second is dated the second of February and mentions nothing else. This one is dated the thirteenth and mentions nothing else.

**The gap that matters is the second to the thirteenth. That is eleven days.**

**A fortnight is the shortest word anybody in this borough has for an interval like that one.** A week is a word, and a week means something to everybody in this city because a Saint Orra west term is six days and is read on a Saturday night, and everybody who has ever worked in a hospital knows what a week is. **Eleven days has no word, and a thing with no word has a date on it twice and nothing else, and the whole of the difference between a piece of paper and a series is that a series has a word for the gap.**

And there is no form in this borough, on any counter, on any tray, in any office, on which anybody could write **this is the second of these and not a series.** There is no such form. It does not exist. It has never been designed, it could not be designed by anybody honest, and if it were designed it would be a form with a box on it saying *not a series*, and a box that says *not a series* is a series.

**And the person making them is not on them and is not paid and cannot be made to be.** She is not a records clerk; she is a records supervisor of about fifty who comes down from the second floor at about ten and about three because nobody has ever told her to and nobody could. There is no rota with her on it. There is no board. There is no box anywhere in this city that says who gave the state on the second of February or the state on the thirteenth, and there is not going to be one, and **the first of these three facts is the only thing standing between a shelf of paper and a schedule, and the second is the only thing standing between a shelf of paper and a service, and the third is the reason nobody will be able to find her in about four years to ask her what she thinks she was doing.**

The board at the end of the counter read nineteen entries and a blank line, and the line is the only fixed point in that building, and the two conditions were sixteen inches apart above it and neither of them governs anything in the room.

**And there is going to be somebody in this borough who works out what eleven days is.**
