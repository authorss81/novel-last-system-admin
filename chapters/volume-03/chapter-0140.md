# Chapter 0140 — What A Service Floor Is, In April

Monday the twenty-seventh of April 2015, from about ten past ten in the morning, the vestibule, ground floor, the Old Ward. And Wednesday the twenty-ninth of April, about twenty to eleven in the morning, the same room.

**He asked no question of anybody in that room on either morning, and he has not asked one since the twenty-fifth of February. He was asked a question in it on the Monday morning, in daylight, by somebody who is not on the agenda either, and it was the third question anybody has asked him, and he answered it, and the counting has been right in every week of this year and in every room of it.**

---

**It is eleven feet by nine. There is no chair and there was no chair and there is not going to be one, and a body of nine seats has one power, which is to require a party holding continuity to answer, receive the answer, and be asked to consider a matter, and a chair is not one of the three parts of it.**

**Here is who was in that room on the Monday morning, and there is no list of them, and this is not one, and nobody in that room was called anything.**

**The man of about fifty-six, who is not a member, did not come as a member, and is not on any list, and who has asked the only three questions anybody has asked.**

**The woman who keeps the minutes, who is also the woman who stands at that counter at about ten and at about three.** She is not named in this account and is not going to be. Her name is in a box on the sheet that says *minute taken by*, and there is no other box anywhere in this borough that says who wrote a thing down, and there is no box that says the two of those are the same person, and there is not going to be one, and a box that said so would be a rota with one name in it and the name would be hers.

**A woman of about sixty who has been in that corridor every morning for about a year reading a board with an empty row on it and has never once been asked about it, and who came in on the Monday morning with a plastic cup of tea and has been in the room about four minutes.**

**And eight other people who are on no board, who have not been asked anything, and who are not going to be, and one of whom has been standing in that room since before he came.**

**And here is who was not in that room, and none of them was left out, and every one of them was left out on purpose by somebody standing at a wall.**

The ninth seat of that body is fifty-four and unpaid and findable at a shopfront since the fourth of July and she is the only door in a building of thirty-one rented rooms, and she has not been told what that is, and she is not in that room, and she is not going to be told.

The man of forty-four is not in that room and could not have been asked to come and nobody was ever going to ask him.

The man of forty-nine is not in that room and could be asked one question tomorrow morning, about anything, for ever, by anybody, and has not been.

The man of forty-eight is not in that room, and nobody has asked him, and Jonas Mercer has said out loud in a corridor that he is not going to, and he is not going to.

A woman of thirty-four is not in that room, and is not in any room, and is in about eleven people and in no document at all, in a drawer with no key on it and in a handbag in a kitchen off a landing, and neither has been opened and neither is going to be.

A woman of seventy-two is not in that room and is behind a door and is a great deal more frightened than she was in May and is no more findable than she was in May.

The four hundred and twentieth woman behind a paragraph number is not in that room, and there is no person on the near side of that paragraph, and there is not going to be one.

A man of thirty-eight is not in that room. He works nights and he is not on anything, and he worked out a thing on his own in a kitchen in February and gave it away to three people and told nobody, and one of those three works somewhere in this borough that has a name, and Jonas Mercer does not know which one, and is not going to find out, and has not written it down.

---

**At about half past ten the man of about fifty-six asked it, and he did not apologise and he did not say *may I*, and he has not apologised or said *may I* on either of the other two occasions either.**

"**You told me what it is made of, twelve weeks ago, and what it costs, six weeks ago, in this room, in daylight, and I have carried both of them, and it is the other one I am asking for and I have never been told whether I am allowed to.**"

Nobody said anything.

"**What is it for.**"

---

And Jonas Mercer did not answer that, and he said so, and it took about four seconds.

"**That is a question about a thing. I am going to turn it round, and it will still be a question, and then I am going to answer it, and it will be the shortest thing I have ever said in a room, and I am not going to improve it afterwards, and I am not going to say it again this year.**"

"**Turn it round.**"

"**Who is it for.**"

"**That is the same question.**"

"**No. It is not. It is the other one. And I have answered the one you asked, twice, out loud, in daylight, in this room, and I am not going to say either of them a third time, and neither of them is going to get better the third time and I have found that out the hard way over nine months.**"

---

And then he said it, and it is one sentence, and it is not a scheme, and it is not the five items, and it is not what anything costs, and it is not on a wall and it is not going on one.

"**It is for the people the borough cannot count.**"

---

Nobody in that room said anything for about four seconds, and it is eleven feet by nine and about eleven people were standing sideways in it, and he could have named three of them and did not name any of them, and there is no sheet anywhere on which the other eight could be put.

The man of about fifty-six did not ask a second question. He has asked three questions of anybody in this borough in nine months and he has got three answers and he did not ask a fourth.

**And nobody repeated it, and nobody improved it, and nobody has put it on a wall, and the only place it has ever been is the fourth item of a sheet that requires nothing, and the woman who keeps the minutes did not ask him whether he wanted it read back, and he did not ask her to.**

And the man of about fifty-six has five words in biro on the back of his left hand that nobody has ever asked him about and nobody is going to.

---

**And here is what the eleven seconds bought, and it is worth nothing and it is correct, and nobody in that room was made to do anything by it.**

A floor has been given a purpose in a room, in daylight, by a man who is not on the agenda, and the purpose names a set of people, and **not one of that set was in the room, and not one of them was asked, and this borough has no instrument for asking, and the only instrument it has ever had is a form, and a form has a box on it, and a person's answer to *can you afford to be found* does not fit in a box.**

**And naming a purpose is not producing one.** A floor that is for the people the borough cannot count is not a floor that has counted them, and does not seat them, and does not put them in a register, and does not require anybody to do anything, and cannot. **The lowland is still not on the layer, is still not in the eight districts, still cannot be seated, and the reason is still that a node with no party is not a party, and a place is not a person, and that reason is correct and is not going to be fixed.**

**And a person who is not counted is not a person who is helped. That sentence has been true in this borough since the second of June and it has not been refuted, and it is not refuted this morning, and it is not going to be, and saying out loud in daylight what a floor is for has not made one single person in this city any closer to being helped, and everybody in that room knows it, and that is the correct outcome and it is not a victory.**

Four of the nine seats have said they will stop if it turns into a job. One of them gave a reason when she was asked and asked for it not to be written down, and it is not in the minute, and that was agreed and both of those were correct, and nobody has stopped, and nobody has been asked again.

---

The woman who keeps the minutes read back, standing up, at the end of the counter, which is what she does, and what is in it is this, and there are no names in it, and it went out on the Friday, and it requires nothing.

> **Item 4. A person present, not on the agenda, not a party, not a member and not a departmental officer, was asked a question in the room, in daylight, by a person who is also not on the agenda. He declined the question as asked, put another question in place of it, and answered that one.**
>
> **Recorded: that the purpose of this floor is people this borough cannot count.**
>
> **Recorded: that no person so described was in the room, that none of them has been asked whether they can afford it, that this body has no instrument for asking, and that the reason is that this body cannot require any person to answer anything, including that.**
>
> **Recorded: that this is the third answer given in this room in nine months and that none of the three has been repeated and that none of the three has been placed on a wall.**
>
> **Recorded: that this minute has no box for the name of a person, and that the sheet is the minute until about four o'clock and after that it is only a sheet.**
>
> **Nothing in this item requires any person to do anything.**

---

**And on the Wednesday morning, the twenty-ninth of April, about twenty to eleven, the whole of the position of this borough is as follows, and it is the whole of what six weeks has produced, and none of it was produced by him and none of it was undone by him.**

**A counter in a vestibule eleven feet by nine answers in about eleven seconds.** It gives a state instead of a list, and a date, and nothing else, and it is not on a rota, it does not scale, it cannot be given to anybody, nobody is required to keep it going, and it works because a person is standing at it and that person is not findable and is not on anything.

**The register of narrowings is at nineteen entries and the blank line under the nineteenth is blank.** It has been blank since Wednesday the twenty-sixth of November 2014, which is a hundred and fifty-four days today, and it is the only fixed point in the building, and entry nineteen is a yard light, the maintenance is a bulb, and the bulb is out.

**The slot in that counter has had a card in it since Friday the twentieth of February.** It is sixty-eight days in there. Nobody has taken it out, nobody has put it up, and the person at the counter has not read it and is not going to, because a slot that gets emptied is a rule and a rule is a service.

**The drawer under that counter is a drawer with no key on it and no list of what is in it.** There is a hand in it and a date on everything in it and a reference, a number, a crest, a form, a box and a name on none of it, including hers. And there is no form in this borough, on any counter, on any tray, in any office, on which anybody could write that these are not a series, and a box that said *not a series* would be a series, and there is not going to be one. **Nine slips is a count of documents. About eleven people a day is a count of people. They are two numbers about two different things and they were never added and they are never going to be.**

**On a second floor there is a tray with post on top of something in it.** It came in on Wednesday the eighteenth of February at about half past nine, not on borough stock, with a departmental reference in the correct typeface, about four lines long, and it is not a reason column, and it is the smallest request anybody in this borough has made since the first of March. **It has been there seventy days. Nobody has opened it in front of a second person, nobody has told anybody it is there, and nobody is going to, and what is in it is not known in this account and may not be said in a room.**

**Two conditions are sixteen inches apart on a wall and have never been reconciled and are not going to be.** A contradiction is not a pause. A contradiction is a floor that is not level, and everybody crosses a floor that is not level faster than a floor that is.

**The autumn compliance report stands with paragraph 4.11 on it in front of four hundred and twenty women.** It was never answered, by anybody, and it is not going to be. The bench steriliser on level zero is still running on four hundred and ten pounds a doctor bought herself, and about four instruments an hour come out of it, and its own strip goes in a drawer in that room about forty times a day, and the hand that puts it there is not on a form and has not been asked and has not been found.

**The board stands at seven of nine.** Two chairs, empty since the eighth of October and the evening of the twenty-sixth of November. Nobody can fill either and nobody can unfill either and there is no power in this city that could, **and there is no chair of that body at all and there never was.**

**A man of forty-four has a fortnight somewhere in this city with a bucket under a tap, and nobody in this borough is able to ask him whether he has got a coat.** A man of forty-nine can be asked one question tomorrow morning and has not been and is not going to be. A man of forty-eight has not been asked and is not going to be. A man of thirty-eight has given a thing he worked out on his own to three people and told nobody, and it has reached a place with a name on it, and nothing has been written down.

A woman of thirty-four is in about eleven people and in no document, and a hole in a record is the only honest thing anybody in this borough has produced about a person in nine months. A woman of fifty-four is findable at a shopfront and has not been told. A woman of seventy-two is behind a door. **A woman of sixty-one is on no board and in no field and on no rota, and has nine hundred and eighty-six Wednesdays written on a card under a rubber band in a biscuit tin, and that is a number of Wednesdays and it is not a number of people, and nobody has ever asked her about it and nobody is going to.**

**The west-clinic gate goes up at seven on a Sunday because two people who are not on anything were in a room with four chairs in it at ten o'clock on the Saturday night, and there is no date on any of those Saturdays, and there is no form on which anybody could record that one happened, and the next one is the second of May, and the rota has still not been rebuilt and cannot be.**

**The claims box is closed and is empty. The sealed envelope is in a drawer on a lower floor and has not been opened and is not going to be. The Weir is on no layer and cannot be seated and nobody on it has told anybody anything. Margo Doré has not been asked anything since the first of June and is not angry and has not forgiven him and nobody went up that path. The blank line at the bottom of that board is blank.**

**And a finding of a borough safety panel of the twenty-third of April 2014 was stated out loud once, in a room, in daylight, on the twenty-eighth of January, by a man with no standing to state it, and it is in a minute, and nothing in the minute requires any person to do anything, and it is not resolved, and it is not going to be opened again, and what is left of it is its consequence, and nobody in this borough has been made to live under that consequence yet, and nobody is going to be.**

---

**And Jonas Mercer is not a member of anything, is not a party, is not a departmental officer, has no grade, no department and no probation, and may not chair anything, and is in a gallery, in a corridor, or is not in the room, and has been all three, and is not going to be any of the other things.**

**And nothing in the six weeks from the sixteenth of March to the twenty-ninth of April was written down by anybody that anybody can be held to, and the register did not move, and eleven standing refusals stand exactly as they stood on the thirteenth of March, none of them argued out, none of them softened, none of them revisited, and a request refused twice is one request and not two.**

---

**And the question that is left, and it is not his and he is not going to answer it and nobody in this borough is entitled to answer it in April, is this.**

**A floor is for the people the borough cannot count. There is a card in a slot that has been in it since the twentieth of February. There is a letter in a tray that has been there since the eighteenth. There is a register with a blank line under it that is the only fixed point in the building, and it has been blank a hundred and fifty-four days. There is a Saturday night every week with no date on it, and two tired men who read it aloud, and neither of whom has ever been asked. And about eleven people a day walk under two conditions that do not agree, and get a date, and go out again.**

**So the counting is the thing that is missing, and the counting is not a job anybody can be given, and nobody in this city may be required even to be told, and a form has a box on it, and the box is where the name would go, and the name is the whole of what has been refused for nine months and it is the only thing anybody in this borough has ever actually needed.**

**And nobody has ever written down who is standing under it.**
