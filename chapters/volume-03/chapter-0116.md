# Chapter 0116 — Six Of Them

**Tuesday the thirteenth of January 2015, about half past two in the afternoon, the Methodist hall, Carrow Lane, Tideglass. In daylight, which is not how that room is used, and the reason is that the day was put there and the light through the door at half past two goes all the way to the back wall and everybody in the room had to stand sideways anyway.**

There were nine chairs in a horseshoe and seven of them had somebody in them and two of them had not had anybody in them since the eighth of October and the evening of the twenty-sixth of November, and a stack of nine folding chairs against the wall that had been there since September and had never once been brought out.

**Jonas Mercer was against the left-hand wall with nothing in his hands and he did not ask a single question in that room that afternoon, and the woman who keeps the minutes recorded that a person was present at the invitation of nobody, which is the phrase she has used for him since September and which nobody has ever asked her to change.**

The card in the plastic sleeve was nine feet from the door.

> **A SEAT IS A DUTY. A DUTY IS AN ACT AND NOT A PLACE. NOBODY ON THIS BODY CAN BE REQUIRED TO BE TOLD THAT THEY ARE A SEAT, AND NOBODY ON THIS BODY CAN BE REQUIRED TO TAKE ONE.**

---

**And the price came up at about ten past three, because Winifred Crabbe asked for it, and she asked for it in the voice of a woman who has read a laundry list aloud to a room of about nine people for nineteen years, and she had the minute of the nineteenth of May in front of her and page eleven open.**

"**Six water points on the lowland at Tier 1. Eighteen hundred and eighty pounds each. Eleven thousand two hundred and eighty pounds a year.** That is paragraph 6.1 of the nineteenth of May and it is recorded as received and not as evidence and no party has been asked for any part of it, and the chair wrote in the margin that she will not require a person at six water points, and the reason nobody has asked is the reason this Committee cannot answer."

"It is a number nobody has ever asked anybody to pay," said Ibrahim Sesay.

"**It is a number on a piece of paper in a minute from May, and it is still a number in January, in a hall, with the heating on.**"

Gloria Nye put her pen down.

"That is one boiler," she said. "One boiler in a small flat. That is the price of a boiler, and I have fitted nine in two years and the cheapest anybody quoted me was seventeen hundred and ninety and I would not recommend it and I told him so at the time. So there are six boilers' worth of water in this borough that nobody is going to buy, and the six of them are the only six things in this borough that cannot be bought at all, and the reason is not that the people who need them are poor. **The reason is that we are not on the map.**"

"The six are not on the layer," said Jonas Mercer, from the wall, and it was not a question.

"**The six are not on the layer, and there are nine service districts, eight of them named and drawn and the ninth not drawn and never drawn, and the drawing layer has no delete operation and no add operation that anybody in this city can find. And a cooperative steward board may be constituted for the districts the layer draws.**" Dermot Fahey had the sheet in front of him. "So the lowland is not in the eight districts. So there is no seat for it. **And the reason there is no seat is not that the lowland cannot pay. A district that cannot pay has a seat and a person in it and has not thereby lost its vote — that is in the registrar's minute of the second of June and it is right. The reason there is no seat is that a node with no party is not a party, and a place is not a person, and there is no instrument in this city by which a ninth district could be constituted in a Methodist hall by anybody in a bad mood.**"

"That reason is correct," said Oren Pell, from the back, where he had been standing for about an hour and had not sat down, because a registrar who sits down in a body that cannot minute him is a registrar who has joined something. "**And I am going to say, because I am the only person in this room who can be asked to say it, that it is correct, and that it has been correct since the first of March, and that I am not going to be the man who makes it wrong in January because the weather has turned.**"

Nobody argued with him, and he put his hands back in his pockets, and the registrar went out at about ten past three and did not come back in, because a registrar who comes back into a room is a registrar who is in it.

---

**And then a man came in at about ten past three, and he was not late, and he had come in out of the cold, and he was not on any list.**

He was fifty-eight and he had a plastic bag with a thermos in it and he had come forty minutes on a bus and he was not on the agenda and nobody knew who he was.

The woman who keeps the minutes said the standing sentence at the top of the afternoon, which she says for everybody now, including people who are not known: "You are not on the agenda, not a party, not a member, not a departmental officer, you are welcome to stay, and nobody is entitled to the answer to anything you may say, and you may decline to say it."

"**Then I'll say it, because I have come about a number,**" he said. "And before anybody says anything — I know what this room is. I have read the minute. I know you cannot make anybody do anything. I am not asking you to make anybody do anything."

"Who are you," said a voice, and it was Gloria Nye's and she said it the way a person says a thing across a room rather than to anybody in it.

"**My name is Abu Bakayoko. I have had a stand at the top of the market for twenty-six years. I get there at about five in the morning. I have a bucket, two urns and no drain, and I pay a man on the lowland to come and empty them twice a day, and I have paid that man every day since about 1989, and I do not know what his name is, and I could find out in about four minutes, and I have never once asked.**"

"**Then sit down,**" said Yolanda Fofana, without turning round, "**and there is a chair, and take the one at the end.**"

He sat down.

"**Now say the number,**" said Winifred Crabbe.

"No. **I want to be counted. That is what I came for.** I have read the number in the minute and I have read the record that went out in November and I can check the number in it, and I am not here to check the number. **I am here because there is a page in this borough that says what was done and it does not say where, and I know where five of the six I am going to talk about are, and I know them because I have been next to them, and in about four years somebody is going to come with a piece of paper about that market and there is going to be nothing on the table that says anything was there.**"

"Mr Bakayoko—" said Dermot Fahey, and stopped, because the body he is in cannot require anybody to do anything, including to stop talking.

"**I know. I am sitting down. What I am asking for is a line in your minute that says a thing exists at a place where a thing is needed. That is all. I do not want a seat. I do not want money. I do not even want to be a number, and I would have said that in November if this room had existed in November.**"

---

**And then the room did the correct thing, which is the thing this chapter is about, and the correct thing is that it tried.**

"**State of the supply at each of the six.**" Dermot Fahey had a pen. "In your own words. Not a list, not a count. What is there."

And Abu Bakayoko said it, and he said it well, and he said it in about two minutes without stopping, and it was the best piece of testimony anybody had given in that hall in four months and there was nothing at all wrong with it.

"The top of the market one is a standpipe on a post and it has a tap on it and it runs when somebody has paid the water. It has not run since the Friday of the twenty-eighth of November. I know that because I have been carrying water in a bowser from the one at the church since the twenty-eighth of November and that is four pounds a bucket and I have not got four pounds a bucket." Somebody said *oh* and he went on, because he had not come to be pitied. "The one by the lock-ups is a trough and it is frozen, and it has been frozen since about the twenty-second of December, and it is a trough and it is not going to be anything else in February. The one on the corner by the bus turning head is a stand with a bucket on a chain and a board with forty slots on it and it works, and it works because somebody puts money in the slot and takes the bucket and washes it and puts it back, and that is not a service, that is a man, and I could not tell you his name either. The one at the back of the school wall is a stand and it works on a timer for forty minutes at a quarter to eight in the morning and then it is a pipe. The one by the yard is a tap. And there is a sixth one."

He stopped.

"There is a sixth one," he said, "**and I am not going to say where it is, and nobody in this room is going to ask me, and if anybody in this room does ask me then I am going to say no, and I want the no written down, because there is somebody on that street who has told the county that nothing is there and she is right to and I am not going to be the person who takes it off her.**"

Yolanda Fofana said: "**Nobody is going to ask you. And that refusal is the same shape as several other refusals in this borough and none of them is mine and I am not going to put mine next to anybody's, including yours, and I am not going to improve it. Write down that a refusal was given at the end of the sixth and do not write down anything else.**"

"**Mr Mercer.**" A man near the back. "You are the only person in this room who is good at this. **Is this a seventh shape?**"

And Jonas Mercer, against the left-hand wall, with nothing in his hands, said the only thing he said in that room that afternoon, and he said it because he was the only person in it who had spent eight months on the difference between a shape and a person, and he had not been asked to, and it was seventeen words, and it was not a question.

"**A shape has to have a place you can point at. That has a person in it.**"

---

**And the room wrote it down, in the correct words, and what it wrote down is the whole of what a service floor is made of, and it took about eleven seconds to say and about four minutes to type, and the woman who keeps the minutes did the typing later, on a machine on a table at the back with a cloth over it, because that is where the typing gets done.**

**There is no chair in that hall and there has never been one. A body of nine seats has one power and a chair is not one of the three parts of it, and anybody who has been in that hall on a Wednesday has already worked that out by standing up. The ninth seat has said in a room that she is not one and that nobody is to make her into one, and she said it in that order on purpose.**

"Is there a party to whom this body may serve anything? **No party.** Is there a seat to which this may be referred? **The lowland is not among the districts this body may be constituted for.** Is there any money? **This body has no money and cannot obtain any.** Is there any person this body may require to do anything? **No person may be required to do anything, and this body has twice asked for that to be minuted and it has been.** Then what can this body do."

"Nothing," said Ibrahim Sesay.

"Nothing," said Oren Pell. "**And the reason it can do nothing is not that it is a small body. It is that the reason nobody has asked anybody to pay the eleven thousand two hundred and eighty pounds is the same reason the body cannot answer a question, and the chair of that other Committee wrote it in the margin in May and she was right, and I have been a registrar for nineteen years and I have never seen a body put that sentence in a minute.**"

"So we write down that we did nothing," said the ninth seat, "**in the correct words, and we do not apologise for it, and we do not put a name on it, and nobody in this borough is going to be told that we did nothing well.**"

"Minutes," said the woman who keeps the minutes.

---

**It went out on Thursday the fifteenth of January 2015 and it is a public document and anybody may ask for it at a counter for nothing, and this is the whole of what is in it.**

> **Item 6. A person attended, not on the agenda, not a party, not a member and not a departmental officer, and asked that it be recorded that a thing exists at a place where a thing is needed. He stated the condition of five nodes in his own words and declined to state the sixth, and the declining is recorded below. He was asked one question, which was answered. He was not asked a second question and made no further statement.**
>
> **Recorded: that the state of each node was given and is recorded here as a statement and not as a return, and that no number was given and none is recorded, and that no name appears in this minute and this minute has no box for one.**
>
> **Recorded: that a refusal was given at the end of the sixth and was not asked for again, and that the person giving it is not to be asked where the sixth is, by this body or by anybody.**
>
> **Recorded: that this body has no party to serve, no seat to refer the matter to, no money, and no power to require any person to do anything, and that the reason nobody has been asked to pay is the reason this body cannot answer.**
>
> **Recorded: that this body has done nothing, in the correct words, and that the doing of nothing is not a defect in the minute.**
>
> **Nothing in this item requires any person to do anything.**

**There is no number in it and there is no name in it, and about four hundred people in this borough have added their own names to a page in November which said the same sentence out loud, and the price of that page is four hundred arguments instead of one document that nobody can touch.**

---

**And there is a seventh shape question and it was answered in a Methodist hall in about seventeen words and the answer was no, and the reason was that a shape has to have a place you can point at and that one has a person in it, and that is now in a minute, and it is in nobody's document about the lowland, and the sixth shape is where it has been since the twenty-eighth of October, which is a street that will not be named in this volume in any document that leaves it.**

**The seventh is not resolved and it is not going to be resolved by a person sitting in a hall in January, and nobody in this borough is entitled to ask him again, and there is a kitchen wall in this borough with three names written on it in biro and a fourth line with a ring of pencil round a blank space and nothing in the ring, and it is exactly as it was in the spring, and the trap at the top of that three-hundred-metre street is packed and lost for good.**

---

**And the minute goes out, and there is no number in it, and there is no name in it, and there is a woman of about thirty-four in this borough who has been counted in a room and has not been written down, and the two of those are different problems, and the difference between them is the whole of this volume, and neither of them is going to be solved by anybody in a hall.**

He got the extract on the Thursday at ten past eleven, at the counter, on the second floor, and it is the only arithmetic anybody in this borough can be held to, and he asked his one question of the day, and it was three words.

"Is it postable."

"To the address on it, at that counter, for nothing, as of the date on it, and the clerk signs it," said the woman at the counter, "**and anybody may do it, and about eleven people have, and two of them have written back and been wrong, and the clerk had to write *enclosure received, no comment*, and that is in the file now, and it is the best thing that counter has ever done.**"

On the wall above that counter there was a printed sheet in a plastic wallet, and it was the only thing in the room with a crest on it, and it had a reference on it and an issue date and it said, in the typeface that comes off a system:

> **CONTINUITY SCHEDULE — TIER 1. SIX NODES. £1,880 PER NODE PER YEAR. £11,280 PER YEAR.**
> **NO PARTY ANSWERING. TERM TO 06:00, SUNDAY 7 JUNE 2015.**
> **THIS NOTICE MAY NOT BE MATCHED AGAINST ANY LIST OF ADDRESSES.**
