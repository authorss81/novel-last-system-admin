# Volume 02, Batch 0002 — Chapters 60–70 (11 chapters)

## Batch goal

Take the mechanism into a room and make it cost somebody in public. The batch carries the **Monday 19 May hearing** of the Standing Committee on Civic Continuity, in a room with a gallery, a minute and a public box, and it does **not** defeat Schedule 4. It establishes what the city can and cannot do with a form that has a hole in it, pays for the Saint Orra west decision that Halvorsen has been carrying since Chapter 50, and produces **the first transfer in the series that succeeds — and shows what it costs the person who receives it.**

## Mid-batch turn (Chapter 65)

**The form is used against the people who filled it in.** Marguerite Oyelaran's four blocks are named on a return, and the returns are being read aloud, and the arithmetic she defended in a room on 28 April is now legible to the people who have to pay it — and a trade association writes to her, and a councillor asks in a chamber whether the association should be in the room at all. **The instrument of visibility becomes an instrument of exposure, exactly as Nell Ardent warned it would on the first of April**, and Nina Calder has to decide whether the return is a public document or a form.

## Batch climax (Chapter 70)

**The 19 May hearing.** Not a victory. The committee cannot define *holder* and says so on the record; Schedule 4 stands and is referred on; **nobody succeeds at beating a price with a sentence, and a registrar's record, a form with a hole in it, and a return with *nobody* in it are the only three things the city puts into the record.** Jonas is in the gallery and is not on anything, is asked nothing, asks one question, and it is the number: **six lowland water points, £1,880 each, £11,280 a year, and nobody has asked.** And the answer he gets comes from the chair, and it is a procedural one, and it is not nothing: the committee requires the schedule to be answered, in public, by every party holding continuity, on the form.

## Lattice notices budget

**Four notices across eleven chapters, at most one each, in Chapters 61, 65, 68 and 70 or fewer.** Candidate conditions, in the established register, each a condition and never a solution:

1. A node with a holder that has not been renewed for its term serves the term it was last read into, and not the term somebody expected.
2. A return made by a party holding maintenance is evidence of a state, and is not evidence of a person, a household, or a number.
3. A node that is on a schedule at a tier is not held by the party that pays; it is held by the party that answers, and a party that answers may be asked what it will do and not what it will cost.
4. Where two terms apply to one use and neither has precedence, the use is performed and entered, and nobody is blamed, and the entry is made twice.

**Do not** print a notice that defines *holder*. The committee cannot define it and the batch must not let a plate do it.

## Cards

### Chapter 0060 — The Number in a Chair
- **POV / when:** Jonas. The Old Ward porch, then the shop. **Thursday 1 May into Friday 2 May.**
- **Goal:** get the returns somewhere they can be argued with, and find out what Rook actually said in the pre-sitting.
- **Resistance:** the returns are on a board and are not evidence of anything, because a form is not adopted; the parties holding maintenance are not required to return anything; and **Kofi Sarr has been asked by the council flood forum for the second time and has said no again, and Nora Bell's third asking is recorded.**
- **Major turn:** **Ibrahim Sesay's school gate comes back on a return with *a key* in the holder field, and it is the first line anybody has ever had to explain in public.** Doina Serkis put a man in a field because there was nowhere else to put him and the caretaker's answer is that the line is wrong and the line is his.
- **Consequence:** the return is not a register. A holder field on a form is not a holder.
- **Ending hook:** Halvorsen telephones at about seven in the evening and says he has written to the mortuary porter and got an answer and the answer is *I still have not got the people who do not come in a van*, and that he is going to have to decide on Saturday, and that Jonas is not going to be in the room when he does.

### Chapter 0061 — Two Pounds Sixty
- **POV / when:** Jonas, then Marguerite Oyelaran, then the Carrow Lane blocks. **Friday 2 May into Saturday 3 May.**
- **Goal:** find out what the participation money actually bought, block by block, and who is paying it.
- **Resistance:** **the fee does not stop**; the association has no money and Block C is a void; and one of the nineteen who signed has come to the door to ask whether she can be let out, and **the answer is that she can leave the agreement on paper in a fortnight and cannot leave the term for a year**, and Marguerite Oyelaran has to say that to a face.
- **Major turn:** **the block's consumption is what the term is sized on, and the block's consumption went up in April, because the nineteen people who were sheltered in Wren Court in March cooked in that block's courtyard.** The fee was priced on a year in which the block was empty for eleven days and has been paid for a year in which it was full.
- **Consequence:** a notice about a node serving the term it was last read into, not the term somebody expected.
- **Ending hook:** twelve of the hundred and twenty-eight have asked to leave the agreement. **Nell Ardent says a mass withdrawal is a data set, and Marguerite Oyelaran says it is a hundred and twenty-eight people finding out on the same day.**

### Chapter 0062 — The Nineteenth of May Is Not a Hearing Yet
- **POV / when:** Jonas, Oren Pell, Nina Calder. **Saturday 3 May into Sunday 4 May.**
- **Goal:** find out what is actually before the committee on Monday, because the tabling says one thing and the covering submission says another.
- **Resistance:** the agenda is in the hands of the chair and the chair will not take instructions; **Fenella Marsh will not let Jonas see the papers and tells him why in a corridor, in a sentence, and it is not unkind.**
- **Major turn:** **nobody has moved that *holder* be defined.** It is Rook's prediction that somebody will, and the prediction has become a fact by being written about in a corridor. The motion has not been tabled. **Nell Ardent wants to table it and is told by a member's office that a member may table it and she is not a member.**
- **Consequence:** the batch's biggest fear is a paperwork error, which is the correct way for it to arrive.
- **Ending hook:** **the clerk's cover note for the papers** — one paragraph, four sentences, and the fourth sentence says what the *return* cannot establish, and it is going to be read out in a gallery.

### Chapter 0063 — The Saturday Decision
- **POV / when:** Jonas at a distance, then the Saint Orra west corridor. **Saturday 9 May morning.**
- **Goal:** be in the corridor and not in the room when Halvorsen decides.
- **Resistance:** he is not in the room and says so; the three unnamed nodes are the staff stair, the car park barrier and the service riser, and **the site has found £4,200 and has also found that the service riser is not a service and can be removed from the list by a person who has standing, and that person is Halvorsen.**
- **Major turn:** **the decision is not which node is worth £4,200. It is that two of the four are not services at all, and finding that out takes a morning and a half and nobody in the city has asked the question because the schedule made it look like a menu.** Tier 2 goes to the mortuary gate, which has a hollow term on it and is therefore the most expensive gate in Nacre and the only one anybody is arguing about.
- **Consequence:** the care entrance plate prints that 6-41 is on a schedule at Tier 2, and the *west clinic's* care term is now priced. **This is the batch's most consequential institutional act and nobody voted for it.**
- **Ending hook:** Mina Okafor asks, in the corridor, what the care entrance costs now, and Jonas has to answer her, and she writes it in the box.

### Chapter 0064 — A Council Flood Forum
- **POV / when:** Jonas. Kofi Sarr's platform, then a room in the civic offices. **Friday 2 May, then Tuesday 6 May.**
- **Goal:** stop a council body turning eight local people into a resilience panel, without making any of them refuse twice.
- **Resistance:** **the second question has been asked a fourth time and the fourth asking was Nora Bell's, and the answer was a man, not a refusal.** The body has a name, a budget and a membership process, and the membership process has eleven places and no street on it.
- **Major turn:** **Nora Bell takes the seat and then takes it off her own hands inside four minutes, in public, and refuses the badge again**, and the body's chair does something unforgivable and also correct: he leaves her name on the roll and does not remove the seat, and says the roll is a list and the body will hold the list.
- **Consequence:** a new, worse instrument exists and it is a list.
- **Ending hook:** Dev Singh's empty-crossing clause, and a man from a flood forum asking Jonas whether the west lane is on the body.

### Chapter 0065 — The Return Is Public
- **POV / when:** Jonas. The Old Ward, then Carrow Lane. **Wednesday 7 May into Thursday 8 May.**
- **Goal:** stop the returns being used as an attendance register.
- **Resistance:** Nina Calder built the form so that it could be argued with, and it is being argued with, and **four of the four blocks are named on it, and the trade association has written to Marguerite Oyelaran, and a councillor has asked in a chamber whether the association should be in the room at all.**
- **Major turn (mid-batch):** the return is a public document and it is an instrument of exposure, and **Nell Ardent's 1 April sentence is what happens** — *the count is the day somebody comes with a clipboard.* Nina Calder has to decide, and her decision is the wrong one on the facts and right on the principle, and it costs Marguerite Oyelaran her afternoon.
- **Consequence:** a notice — a return is evidence of a state and is not evidence of a person, a household or a number.
- **Ending hook:** the twelfth withdrawal letter at Carrow Lane, and a person in a queue who is not on any list and wants to be.

### Chapter 0066 — Nine Hundred Pounds
- **POV / when:** Jonas and Dr. Sanaa Iqbal, and Gunnar Halvorsen. **Thursday 8 May into Friday 9 May.**
- **Goal:** nothing. She sends a message and it is not about the second clinician this time.
- **Resistance:** the nine hundred pounds is expected to last until somebody in that building spends it on a machine, and **the machine has been ordered.**
- **Major turn:** Halvorsen has to choose between a service riser that is not a service and a steriliser, and the second clinician's line is funded out of the same dead activity budget, and **the money that bought her relief is the money that would have paid for the thing that sterilises instruments**, and the person who has to make that choice is a service manager who is not a villain and has nineteen years in post and about four hours to make it in.
- **Consequence:** Sanaa is put in a position where the price of her own relief is a clinical one, and she handles it and does not put it in a pocket.
- **Ending hook:** she says one sentence about June and it is not what he expected and it is not a request.

### Chapter 0067 — What Two Clinicians Cost
- **POV / when:** Jonas. Saint Orra west, the corridor, at ten at night. **Thursday 15 May.**
- **Goal:** watch a mending he is not allowed to make, and be useful anyway.
- **Resistance:** **the handover at 22:00 now has two named clinicians, and the plate's fallback names a route and not a man, and for the first time in five weeks the door is opened by a person who is neither Ruth Nwosu, nor Bram Osei, nor Ezra Mbeki, nor a declaration.** Priya Ranganathan, forty-one, part-time, on the roster since 2 May, does a handover she was not rostered for and refuses to be thanked for it.
- **Major turn:** **she has read the term properly and she asks the question nobody has asked, which is what a *declining* reduction is.** The care entrance's Tier 2 tier has an expiry and the expiry is a date, and the date falls on a Sunday, and the four people who would normally do a ten o'clock handover cannot all do a Sunday.
- **Consequence:** the two-refusal rule, live and dated, meets a priced node.
- **Ending hook:** nobody has offered to take the Sunday and Jonas is not allowed to offer.

### Chapter 0068 — The First Transfer That Works
- **POV / when:** Jonas. The lowland, a chamber, and a garden in Tideglass. **Saturday 17 May into Sunday 18 May.**
- **Goal:** move one held duty from a lapsed node to a willing, informed person, and do it properly.
- **Resistance:** **Royden Achebe** is the receiving holder and he understands it and accepts it and it attaches to a man in his own name, and **he tries to hand it back within a week and cannot, and that is the batch's cost.**
- **Major turn:** the transfer is of a *held claim* on a lowland outfall inspection duty, not a water point, because a plate will not take a category and a water point is not a duty. **The transfer succeeds, and the first thing the Lattice does with it is put Jonas's name on the record as the person who moved it**, and legal responsibility follows the transfer and not the person who received it, and he learns that the difference is the whole of Stage 3 and it is not a gift.
- **Consequence:** a notice about a tier and about who answers and not what it costs.
- **Ending hook:** Achebe says he would rather have had it before the post, again, and this time it is a different sentence.

### Chapter 0069 — Six Points and One Number
- **POV / when:** Jonas. The Old Ward, and a room with a gallery. **Sunday 18 May into Monday 19 May, 07:30.**
- **Goal:** get one number into a room without spending it early.
- **Resistance:** the number has been in Marguerite Oyelaran's hand since 28 April; the gallery is forty-one people; **the claims box closes on 18 July and a claim there establishes nothing**; and Jonas has been told twice that the count is the clipboard.
- **Major turn:** he does not put the number on a card or in a claim or in a speech. **He gives it to the chair, in advance, in writing, with the source on it — a housing association's board, a chief officer's hand, a date — so that when he says it in the gallery he is reading a document that already existed and is not producing a list.**
- **Consequence:** it is on the record before it is said, which is the only way he has found to say a number in a room without becoming the man who counted.
- **Ending hook:** **the chair reads it out in full, and does not comment on it, and it goes in the minute, and that is the whole of it.**

### Chapter 0070 — The Nineteenth of May
- **POV / when:** Jonas, in a gallery. **Monday 19 May, 10:00 to about 16:00.**
- **Goal:** to be in a room and to be useful in it in the smallest possible way.
- **Resistance:** the room is full. Marsh in the chair. Sore, and four other members. Pell in attendance. Rook and Vaughan for CivicCore. **The gallery is full at 10:55 and Jonas is in it, and he did not come for Tomas Renn, and Tomas Renn is not in the room, and both of those facts are on the page.**
- **Major turn:** **nobody succeeds at beating a price with a sentence.** The committee cannot define *holder* and minutes that it cannot. Schedule 4 stands and is referred on with a requirement that every party holding continuity answer a Return of a Deferred Node in public. **Sore moves something and it is not the thing anybody expected, and she explains it in four sentences and does not explain it.**
- **Consequence:** the three things the city puts into the record are a registrar's record, a form with a hole in it, and a return with *nobody* in it. **And the chair says, of the hundred and twenty-eight rooms and the six water points and the eleven of nineteen, one sentence, and it is procedural, and it changes what has to happen next: a party that answers may be asked what it will do, and not what it will cost.**
- **Ending hook:** the volume's question, on the page and unresolved — **can a city keep a shared service floor when urgent access is legally priced, and who bears the cost when a resident refuses the price?** Forward pull into Chapters 71–80: the heat, the narrowings, the first transfer that has to be repeated, and a cooperative steward board that does not exist yet.
