## How to write it

Write ten complete scenes in order, each with a goal, resistance, a change from the scene, a finished beat, and an ending that opens something without naming any of it. Do not pad and do not rush. End each chapter on something a person does or decides not to do. **Do not open or end with a ledger of state. Do not end two chapters on a thing being put away, and do not end two chapters on a thing being left where it is.** Put facts in scenes or in the roster line under the dateline.

Dialogue is required in every chapter. Do not write every line as a report of a conversation. **At least seven of the ten get a second exchange, so that resistance outlasts the first answer.** Attribute with what somebody does rather than with said. **In 0055 said fell to 28 across ten chapters and that is the lowest in three blocks; keep it there or lower.** Declare one ordinary word in the band file before any prose, count it, and publish the count. Spent words that may not be re-declared are usually, anyway, like, just, enough, still, kind, quite, small, round, one, week, almost, anything, and then, nearly, and away, and **count**. Do not reprint their figures as settled.
