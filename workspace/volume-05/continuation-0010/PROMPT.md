THE CHAPTER SUMMARIES MUST BE WRITTEN AS DESCRIPTIONS OF WHAT HAPPENED AND NOT AS TRANSCRIPTIONS OF WHAT A CHAPTER PRINTED, AND NO CHAPTER'S ALL-CAPS SENTENCE MAY APPEAR IN ONE.**
