FIFTH, AND NEW, BECAUSE IT IS THE FINDING THAT COST CONTINUATION 0011 THE MOST: **THE CHAPTERS IMMEDIATELY ABOVE A BATCH ARE THE ONES MOST LIKELY TO BE REPRODUCED, BECAUSE THE FIGURES IN THEM, THEIR ROOMS, THEIR HOURS AND THEIR CONNECTIVE SENTENCES ARE STILL FRESH, AND A WRITER WHO READS THEM FOR VOICE AND THEN WRITES FROM THAT FRESHNESS WILL WRITE THEM AGAIN. READ THEM FOR WHAT THEY ARE DOING AND NOT FOR WHAT THEY SOUND LIKE.**
