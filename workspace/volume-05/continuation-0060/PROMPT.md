## The family for this block, declared before any chapter exists, and it is a writer's and not a human's

**THE FAMILY IS A GOOD REPAIR AND WHAT THE PERSON DOES WITH IT AFTERWARDS.**

Somebody finds something wrong in their own place and means to deal with it their own way. Somebody else arrives, takes the second half, and does it properly. **THE REPAIR IS GOOD AND IT STAYS GOOD. THE METHOD IS GOOD AND IT HAS NO HOLE IN IT. THE FAULT IS NOT IN EITHER METHOD AND IT IS NOT IN THE REPAIR. THE FAULT IS IN THE PERSON.** The person does not leave the job alone. They lean on it, they stop doing the other thing that was keeping the rest of it right, they go straight back to what they were doing before, they do it again themselves because they do not like it, they keep a hand on it, they carry on the one habit the repair was meant to make unnecessary, or they hand it on to somebody else and it gets done the other way round. **Every chapter ends with a repair that is better than it was and a person who is worse off in relation to it, and nobody in any of the ten can say whether the repair was worth it.**
