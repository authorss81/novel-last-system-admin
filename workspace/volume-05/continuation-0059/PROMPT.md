## The family for this block, declared before any chapter exists, and it is a writer's and not a human's

**THE FAMILY IS A REPAIR THAT WORKS.** Somebody finds something wrong in their own place and means to deal with it their own way. Somebody else arrives, takes the second half, and does it properly. **Both methods are sound, neither of them is the visitor's fault, and neither of them is why the place is worse afterwards.** The fault in every chapter is not in a method at all. It is in the position, the choice, or the moment: a repair put where the place cannot take one, a repair that takes something out and puts nothing back, a repair that puts something in where nothing was, a repair measured by the thing before it and not by the thing after it. Every chapter ends with a thing that is better and a place that is worse, and nobody in any of the ten can say which of the two was the job.

**Why this and not 0058, which is the nearest of them, and the difference is the whole point.** In 765 to 774 the adopted method was right for the job and had a hole in it, and the fault was in the method and the host took it on and kept it. **In 775 to 784 the adopted method has no hole in it and it is still wrong, because the method was never the question. The fault is in the repair itself.** A later writer may not go back to a fault in a method in either direction, that is, neither the visitor's method being wrong (0057) nor the adopted method having a hole in it (0058).
