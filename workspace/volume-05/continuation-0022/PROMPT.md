## THE TEN CARDS

**0405 — A THING TWO PEOPLE FIND AT THE SAME TIME AND HANDLE DIFFERENTLY. *Goal:* let two people come across one object within the same hour, one of whom puts it back and one of whom moves it, and let neither of them know the other was there at all, and let the object end up somewhere neither of them would have chosen. *Resistance:* a street, a room over a room, a man in his forties, a woman who works in a shop, a man in his thirties, **and two people handling one thing is not an agreement and not a handover and not a meeting, nobody is put on anything, and a thing that is in the wrong place because two people moved it is not a thing anybody is owed.** *Major turn:* **THEY NEVER FIND OUT THEY WERE THERE, THE OBJECT ENDS UP ABOUT ELEVEN FOOT FROM WHERE EITHER OF THEM PUT IT, AND NEITHER HAS SAID A WORD AND NEITHER WILL.** It may not be a door, a hinge, a paper strip, a pencil mark, a rail, a bracket, a floor, a chair, a tin, a box, a churn, a bench, a lamp, a comb, a coil of cable, a bundle of laths, a band of slates or an awning.

**0406 — A THING A MAN IS TOLD ABOUT BY SOMEONE WHO IS NOT ENTITLED TO TELL HIM. *Goal:* let a person who is not responsible say one true sentence about another person's building, and let the man it is said to believe it and act on it, and let it turn out to be true, and let nobody ever correct the record. *Resistance:* a cellar or a store, a man in his forties, a man in his thirties, a man in his sixties, **and a thing that is true and is not a finding and not a report and not a claim, nobody is caught out, nobody is named, and being wrong about a man is not the same as being right about a building.** *Major turn:* **HE ACTS ON IT, IT IS TRUE, HE NEVER LEARNS THAT HE WAS TOLD BY SOMEONE WHO COULD NOT KNOW, AND THE PERSON WHO TOLD HIM HAS SAID NOTHING SINCE.** It may not be a rail, a bracket, a hardboard floor, a vinyl floor, a chair, a plaster ceiling, a tin, a box, a churn, a bench, a lamp, a comb or a coil of cable.

**0407 — A THING DONE AND FOUND OUT ABOUT THE SAME EVENING BY A PERSON WHO WAS NOT THERE. *Goal:* let a man do a piece of work and let somebody hear about it before he has finished the day, and let him find out that they know, and let him neither confirm nor deny. *Resistance:* a yard, a man in his thirties, a man in his forties, the woman who works in a shop, **and hearing about a thing is not being asked about it and not being reported, nobody is accused, nobody owes anybody a sentence, and a man who has heard about his own work is not a man who has been caught.** *Major turn:* **HE FINDS OUT BEFORE HE GETS HOME, HE SAYS NEITHER YES NOR NO, AND NEITHER OF THEM RAISES IT AGAIN.** It may not be a rail, a bracket, a chair, a hardboard floor, a vinyl floor, a tin, a box, a bench, a lamp, a comb, a coil of cable, a bundle of laths or a length of slates.

**0408 — A THING A MAN FINDS OUT ABOUT HIMSELF AND PUTS DOWN. *Goal:* let a man of about sixty-odd look at something of his own for the first time in years and see a mark he cannot account for, and let him decide it is nothing, and let a second man see the same thing and not say anything, and let neither of them ever come back to it. *Resistance:* a room in a house of flats, a man in his sixties, a man in his thirties, a man in his forties, **and a thing a man finds out about himself is not a confession and not a discovery and not a finding about anybody else, nobody is accused, and deciding that a thing is nothing is not the same as being right.** *Major turn:* **HE PUTS IT DOWN AND GOES ON WITH HIS DAY AND HE IS WRONG, AND THE OTHER MAN SAW IT AND SAID NOTHING AND HAS NOT SPOKEN OF IT.** It may not be a rail, a bracket, a chair, a hardboard floor, a vinyl floor, a tin, a box, a bench, a lamp, a comb or a coil of cable.

**0409 — A THING ONE MAN GIVES AND ANOTHER MAN DOES NOT NOTICE IS GONE. *Goal:* let a man in his forties take a thing out of a building for a reason of his own, and let the person it belongs to come into that room, and let him look round it, and let him say nothing, and let it stay gone. *Resistance:* a room, a ledge, a man in his forties, a man in his sixties, a man in his thirties, **and a thing that is not there is not a theft and not a loss and not a mystery, nobody has been done out of anything they were using, and a man looking round a room and saying nothing about what is missing is a man who does not want the answer.** *Major turn:* **HE LOOKS ROUND THE ROOM TWICE, HE SAYS NOTHING TO ANYBODY, AND HE DOES NOT ASK, AND WHAT THE OTHER MAN DID WITH IT IS NEVER FOUND OUT BY ANYBODY IN THIS BOROUGH.** It may not be a rail, a bracket, a chair, a hardboard floor, a vinyl floor, a tin, a box, a bench, a lamp, a bundle of laths or a length of slates.

**0410 — TWO MEN WORK ON THE SAME THING FROM OPPOSITE ENDS AND ONE OF THEM IS WATCHED BY A THIRD WHO SAYS NOTHING. *Goal:* let two men work on one long job in one building over two days and let a third person be in that building the whole of the time and let her watch both of them and say nothing to either, and let the job finish. *Resistance:* a workshop, a man in his forties, a man in his thirties, a woman who works in a shop, **and being in a room while a thing is done in it is not being a party to it and not being asked and not being owed, nothing is minuted, and a woman who has watched two men work is not a woman who has anything to report.** *Major turn:* **SHE WATCHES BOTH OF THEM, NEITHER OF THEM KNOWS SHE WAS THERE, AND THE JOB IS FINISHED AND NOBODY IS ASKED HOW IT WENT.** It may not be a rail, a bracket, a chair, a hardboard floor, a vinyl floor, a tin, a box, a bench, a lamp or a coil of cable.

**0411 — A THING A MAN CARRIES BACK THAT HE WAS GIVEN AND DID NOT WANT. *Goal:* let a man in his forties be handed a small object he has no use for, carry it for a fortnight, and put it down somewhere he was not to, and let the person who gave it not come looking, and let neither of them raise it. *Resistance:* a shop off a parade, a van, a man in his forties, a man in his thirties, the woman who works in a shop, **and a thing that was pushed into a man's hand is not a gift and not a debt and not an instruction, nobody is thanked, nobody refuses to be thanked, and a man who puts a thing down in the wrong place is not a man who has admitted anything.** *Major turn:* **HE PUTS IT DOWN SOMEWHERE HE WAS NOT TO AND TELLS NOBODY, AND THE OTHER ONE HAS NOT COME LOOKING AND IS NOT GOING TO, AND NEITHER OF THEM WILL RAISE IT.** It may not be a rail, a bracket, a chair, a hardboard floor, a vinyl floor, a tin, a box, a bench, a lamp, a comb or a coil of cable.
