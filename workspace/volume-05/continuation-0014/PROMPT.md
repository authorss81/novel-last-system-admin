**THE SIX RUNS MUST BE RUN AGAIN AND **ALL SIX** FIGURES WRITTEN DOWN AND LABELLED, WITH EVERY WINDOW NAMED, BECAUSE A FIGURE THAT IS SCOPED AND THEN READ AS THOUGH IT WERE THE WHOLE CHECK IS A FIGURE THAT HIDES A DEFECT: **RUN ONE** ON THE CHAPTERS' OWN STANDALONE ALL-CAPS BLOCKS AGAINST EVERY NON-CHAPTER FILE; **RUN TWO**, THE ONE THAT MATTERS, ON THE TEN CHAPTERS AGAINST EACH OTHER, EACH CHAPTER COMPARED WITH THE **OTHER NINE**; **RUN THREE** ON THE TEN CHAPTERS AGAINST **315–324**, ON THE SAME TEN AGAINST **305–314**, AND ON THE SAME TEN AGAINST **295–304**, ALL THREE NAMED, **AND THE 315–324 WINDOW IS THE ONE THAT MATTERS MOST BECAUSE IT IS THE WINDOW THE CHAPTERS IMMEDIATELY ABOVE THIS ONE ARE IN, AND IT IS THE WINDOW NO EARLIER PROMPT NAMED FOR THE BATCH THAT FOLLOWS**; **RUN FOUR** ON THE TEN CHAPTERS AGAINST **THE SEVEN STATE FILES THIS BATCH WRITES**, **AT THE END AND NOT AT THE START, AND TAKEN AGAIN AFTER EVERY REPAIR UNTIL IT READS NIL, BECAUSE IN 0013 IT HAD TO BE TAKEN EIGHTEEN TIMES**; **RUN FIVE** ON THE WHOLE TEXT OF THE TEN CHAPTERS AGAINST EVERY NON-CHAPTER FILE; AND **RUN SIX**, WHICH IS THE COUNT OF RUNS, NOT A RUN. **EVERY FIGURE MUST NAME ITS WINDOW IN THE SAME BREATH, AND EVERY FIGURE MUST BE GIVEN AS TWO NUMBERS — THE RAW SPAN COUNT AND THE COUNT OF REUSES OF PROSE — BECAUSE THE SECOND NUMBER IS THE ONE THAT BINDS AND A RAW COUNT ALONE HIDES THE ANSWER. **AND THE SCRIPT MUST COMPARE TOKENS, NOT CHARACTERS: A SCRIPT THAT SLICES CHARACTERS AND COMPARES THEM WILL REPORT NIL IN EVERY WINDOW IN THIS REPOSITORY, AND A FALSE NIL IS WORSE THAN A LARGE NUMBER BECAUSE IT CLOSES THE PASS. BEFORE A WRITER BELIEVES A FIGURE, THE SCRIPT MUST BE ASKED FOR THE LONGEST RUN IT FINDS BETWEEN TWO FILES THAT ARE KNOWN TO SHARE A SENTENCE, AND IF IT RETURNS NOTHING THE SCRIPT IS WRONG, NOT THE FILES.**** FOR REFERENCE, THE SIX FIGURES AT THE END OF CONTINUATION 0013, MEASURED ON CHAPTERS 315–324, WERE NIL, 18 RAW AND NIL, 17/11/7 RAW AND NIL, NIL, NIL, AND NIL, AND EVERY SURVIVING SPAN IN EVERY ONE OF THOSE SIX WAS A FIGURE'S OWN BY-NAME TAG OR A BOROUGH'S OWN RECURRING PLACE NOUN, WHICH WERE DETECTED AGAINST THE CHAPTERS' OWN DATELINE LINES AND THE CHAPTERS' OWN TAGS RATHER THAN AGAINST A LIST OF WORDS, AND A WRITER WHO DETECTS THEM BY A LIST OF WORDS WILL EITHER CHASE A DATELINE OR MISS A NEW BY-NAME TAG. A CHAPTER IS NEVER INCLUDED IN THE SET IT IS COMPARED AGAINST: AN UNSCOPED COMPARISON RETURNS A SELF-MATCH AT THE WHOLE LENGTH OF THE CHAPTER, WHICH IS WHY AN UNSCOPED FIGURE IN THIS REPOSITORY IS NOT A SMALL NUMBER BUT A CONSTANT. COUNT THE LONGEST RUN IN EACH SPAN, NOT EVERY WINDOW INSIDE IT, AND A WINDOW INSIDE ITS OWN RUN IS NOT COUNTED TWICE.**** DATELINES, A BOROUGH'S OWN RECURRING NOUNS SUCH AS *IN THE KITCHEN AT THE BACK OF THE PARADE* AND *A FIRST-FLOOR LANDING IN A BUILDING BY THE WATER*, AND A FIGURE'S OWN BY-NAME DESCRIPTION ARE EXCLUDED AND RECORDED AS A COUNT, NOT CHASED, BECAUSE BREAKING THOSE WOULD MEAN MISIDENTIFYING SOMEBODY. **THE SECOND RUN AND THE FOURTH RUN ARE THE ONES THAT MATTER AND BOTH RETURNED NIL REUSES AT THE END OF CONTINUATION 0013, AND WHAT BINDS A LATER WRITER IS THE METHOD, THE WINDOW, THE THRESHOLD AND THE CONCLUSION, IN THAT ORDER, AND NOT THE COUNT.**

**AND THE PROSE LIMIT CARRIED INTO THIS BATCH, WHICH IS BINDING: A CHAPTER USES NO MORE THAN FIVE STANDALONE ALL-CAPS PARAGRAPHS IN WHOLE, COUNTING ANY DIEGETIC NOTICE, AND USES `---` ONLY AS A SCENE BREAK AND AT MOST TWICE, AND THE DRAMA IS CARRIED BY WHAT PEOPLE SAY AND DO. THE CAPS ARE THE SOUND OF THIS BOOK AND THEY ARE NOT BEING BANNED: A COLD OPEN, A TURN, A CONSEQUENCE, A FINDING AND A CLOSING LINE MAY CARRY THEM WHEN A CHAPTER HAS EARNED THEM, AND A PIECE OF PAPER IN THE SCENE IS NOT THE NARRATOR AND DOES NOT COUNT AGAINST THE FIVE. WHAT IS BANNED IS THE OTHER THING, WHICH IS A RUNNING COMMENTARY: A PARAGRAPH THAT SAYS WHAT THE SCENE MEANS INSTEAD OF SOMEBODY DOING SOMETHING, A CLOSING BLOCK THAT RESTATES THE STATE OF THE BOROUGH ITEM BY ITEM, AND ANY PASSAGE THAT COULD BE LIFTED VERBATIM OUT OF `state/current.md` OR OUT OF THE HAND-OVER BLOCK AND LOST NOTHING. **IF A CHAPTER'S LAST THREE PARAGRAPHS COULD BE THE STATE FILE, IT IS WRITTEN WRONG. A FINDING IN THIS BOOK IS A THING THAT HAPPENED TO A PERSON AND NOT A PARAGRAPH ABOUT A THING. A CARD MAY GIVE A CHAPTER ITS FINDING. THE CHAPTER MUST STILL ARRIVE AT IT THROUGH SOMEBODY DOING SOMETHING, AND A SENTENCE THAT COULD BE LIFTED INTO `state/continuity.md` IS NOT THE ARRIVAL, IT IS A LABEL ON THE ARRIVAL, AND ONE OF THEM IS ENOUGH.****

**AND THE REVIEW'S OTHER RULES, ALL OF WHICH ARE WORTH CARRYING FORWARD. FIRST, A CHAPTER MAY NOT OPEN WITH AN ALL-CAPS THESIS PARAGRAPH AND CLOSE WITH A PARAPHRASE OF IT: EVERY CLOSING BLOCK IN CONTINUATIONS 0006, 0007, 0008, 0009, 0010, 0011, 0012 AND 0013 NOW LANDS ON A CONSEQUENCE, AN OBJECT OR A PERSON WHO IS THE SUBJECT OF A VERB, AND NONE OF THEM PRINTS ITS OWN OPENING'S THESIS, ITS OWN FINDING, A VERDICT, A DAY-COUNT OR A FIGURE ITS OWN BODY HAS ALREADY PRINTED. SECOND, *A FRIDAY* IS A MOTIF AND NOT A TIC AND IS NOW MEASURED IN EVERY CHAPTER. THIRD, **THE WRITING OF THE CHAPTER SUMMARIES MATTERS MORE THAN THE FIGURES, BECAUSE A WRITER WHO WRITES THEM BY QUOTING THE CHAPTERS WILL REPEAT THE CHAPTERS' OWN PROSE AND THE FIGURE WILL BE A COMPLETELY DIFFERENT NUMBER FROM THE ONE IT SHOULD BE. THE CHAPTER SUMMARIES MUST BE WRITTEN AS DESCRIPTIONS OF WHAT HAPPENED AND NOT AS TRANSCRIPTIONS OF WHAT A CHAPTER PRINTED, AND NO CHAPTER'S ALL-CAPS SENTENCE MAY APPEAR IN ONE.** FOURTH, **A CHAPTER MAY NOT DESCRIBE A FIGURE'S ACTION IN A STATE-FILE PHRASE THAT THE CHAPTER ITSELF THEN REUSES, WHICH IS THE SAME FAILURE AS A STATE FILE HOLDING A CHAPTER'S WORDS ONLY IT IS POINTED THE OTHER WAY, AND THE CHEAPEST TEST IS THE RUN FOUR RUN AT THE END AGAINST THE SEVEN STATE FILES AND NOT AT THE START. FIFTH, **THE CHAPTERS IMMEDIATELY ABOVE A BATCH ARE THE ONES MOST LIKELY TO BE REPRODUCED, BECAUSE THE FIGURES IN THEM, THEIR ROOMS, THEIR HOURS AND THEIR CONNECTIVE SENTENCES ARE STILL FRESH, AND A WRITER WHO READS THEM FOR VOICE AND THEN WRITES FROM THAT FRESHNESS WILL WRITE THEM AGAIN. READ THEM FOR WHAT THEY ARE DOING AND NOT FOR WHAT THEY SOUND LIKE. SIXTH, **AND THE ONE THAT COST CONTINUATION 0013 THE MOST: A CARD THAT ASKS FOR A SCENE THAT WAS WRITTEN IN THE BATCH ABOVE MUST SAY WHAT IS NEW ABOUT IT, BECAUSE A SCENE THAT WAS WRITTEN ONCE MAY BE WRITTEN AGAIN WITH A DIFFERENT DATE ON IT AND NOBODY WILL NOTICE. SEVENTH, **AND A CLOSING ALL-CAPS BLOCK THAT REPEATS ITS OWN CARD, OR A STATE-FILE PHRASE THE CHAPTER ITSELF LIFTS, IS THE SAME DEFECT AT TWO SCALES AND IS CAUGHT BY THE SAME TWO RUNS. EIGHTH, AND THE ONE that cost Continuation 0013 its second-largest repair: **A PASSAGE THAT REPORTS WHAT WAS SAID IN A ROOM IS THE PASSAGE MOST LIKELY TO COME OUT OF A CHAPTER AS A NEAR-TRANSCRIPTION, BECAUSE THE CHAPTERS PRINT IT IN QUOTATION MARKS AND A FILE IS TEMPTED TO UNQUOTE IT AND HANDLE IT AT LENGTH. §10 OF THE CONTINUITY BLOCK AND §10 OF THE OPEN-THREADS BLOCK BOTH CAME OUT THAT WAY IN 0013 AND BOTH HAD TO BE REWRITTEN RATHER THAN PATCHED.****
