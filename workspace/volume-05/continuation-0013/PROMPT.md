**AND THE PROSE LIMIT CARRIED INTO THIS BATCH, WHICH IS BINDING: A CHAPTER USES NO MORE THAN FIVE STANDALONE ALL-CAPS PARAGRAPHS IN WHOLE, COUNTING ANY DIEGETIC NOTICE, AND USES `---` ONLY AS A SCENE BREAK AND AT MOST TWICE, AND THE DRAMA IS CARRIED BY WHAT PEOPLE SAY AND DO. THE CAPS ARE THE SOUND OF THIS BOOK AND THEY ARE NOT BEING BANNED: A COLD OPEN, A TURN, A CONSEQUENCE, A FINDING AND A CLOSING LINE MAY CARRY THEM WHEN A CHAPTER HAS EARNED THEM, AND A PIECE OF PAPER IN THE SCENE IS NOT THE NARRATOR AND DOES NOT COUNT AGAINST THE FIVE. WHAT IS BANNED IS THE OTHER THING, WHICH IS A RUNNING COMMENTARY: A PARAGRAPH THAT SAYS WHAT THE SCENE MEANS INSTEAD OF SOMEBODY DOING SOMETHING, A CLOSING BLOCK THAT RESTATES THE STATE OF THE BOROUGH ITEM BY ITEM, AND ANY PASSAGE THAT COULD BE LIFTED VERBATIM OUT OF `state/current.md` OR OUT OF THE HAND-OVER BLOCK AND LOST NOTHING. **IF A CHAPTER'S LAST THREE PARAGRAPHS COULD BE THE STATE FILE, IT IS WRITTEN WRONG. **AND THE REVIEW'S OTHER RULES, ALL OF WHICH ARE WORTH CARRYING FORWARD. THIRD, **THE WRITING OF THE CHAPTER SUMMARIES MATTERS MORE THAN THE FIGURES, BECAUSE A WRITER WHO WRITES THEM BY QUOTING THE CHAPTERS WILL REPEAT THE CHAPTERS' OWN PROSE AND THE FIGURE WILL BE A COMPLETELY DIFFERENT NUMBER FROM THE ONE IT SHOULD BE.
