## How to write it

Write ten complete scenes in order, each with a goal, resistance, a change from the scene, a finished beat, and an ending that opens something without naming any of it. Do not pad and do not rush. End each chapter on something a person does or decides not to do. Do not open or end with a ledger of state. Put facts in scenes or in the roster line under the dateline.

Dialogue is required in every chapter. Do not write every line as a report of a conversation. Declare one ordinary word in the band file before any prose, count it, and publish the count. Spent words that may not be re-declared are usually, anyway, like, just, enough, still, kind, quite, small, round, one, week, almost, anything, and then, nearly, and away. Do not reprint their figures as settled.
