## Central pressure

A heat wave and a chemical spill make a citywide emergency out of a borough that has spent nine months discovering it cannot name the word *holder*. Into that emergency a central Continuity Order is proposed, with a ninety-day renewal, and it is proposed by **Iven Sore**, who has a constituency and a reason and is right about the need.

The order's pressure is not that it is evil. It is that **it is the only thing anybody has ever written down that would work**, and it works by putting a person in a field.

**The volume's question, stated by the mechanism and not by a villain: what does it cost to be the only person in the room who can make a district function, and who pays for it?**
