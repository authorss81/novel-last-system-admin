## Central pressure

**A river disaster in and around an estuary city, and mutual-aid permissions signed with neighbouring cities during it, whose clauses are all correct and whose effect is to export labour, patients, and future claims.** Nothing in the agreements is a fraud. That is the pressure: a bargain that works exactly as written and takes something out of the city that cannot see it leaving.

## The volume's five movements, as `outline/series.md` has them

- **Major movement:** Jonas travels with **Tomas Renn**, **Nell Ardent** and **Sanaa Iqbal** through three regional systems, each of which reflects a different bargain between public obligation and private ownership.
- **Midpoint reversal:** one neighbour's apparently benevolent shared rule is quietly draining this city's repair crews through inherited claims, and the regional trade is a political weapon.
- **Climax:** Jonas renegotiates the compact in public, sacrificing the city's emergency priority in exchange for a mutual right of return.
- **Resolution:** the regional pact holds, and this city learns the original Lattice was part of a wider civic experiment.
- **New question:** what happened to the other first administrators?
