# Volume 02, Batch 0001 — Chapters 49–59 (11 chapters)

## Batch goal

Establish the priced-access mechanism as a **document**, show it working, and give Jonas a refusal he cannot argue with. The batch does not defeat anything. It ends with a lowland water main repaired under a written emergency declaration — because a price is a term and a burst pipe has no priced term — and with the city adopting, on its own paper, the first return of a deferred node.

## Starting condition

Sunday 20 April, 22:20. The care term is running to 06:00 Tuesday 21 April. The Weir stand has been dead since 06:00 that morning and there is no term, no holder and no claim on it. The map in Jonas's coat has a name in biro in the middle of it. A register with one name in it cannot be removed. The committee sits on 19 May and cannot define *holder*.

## Mid-batch turn (Chapter 55)

**The fix and the disease are the same money.** Sanaa Iqbal's second named clinician — five refusals, four of them about a rota line being money — exists because the west site administration found a participation line. The thing that made the witness structure survivable is the instrument that prices it. On the same day Tomas Renn learns, from a contractor's covering document, that his finding is already being cited.

## Batch climax (Chapter 58)

**Tuesday 29 April, 23:40, a lowland main.** A commissioning clamp left on a joint after the 18 April flood works; the works order was closed on paper on 20 April by a crew that never came back. Jonas and Emeka Rusk find and shift it in forty minutes in the dark, and the restoration cannot be done on a declaration, because the spur's restoration route is a priced term and there is nobody on the tier. **A CivicCore field manager offers, in a car park, at twenty to one in the morning, to put the lowland on Tier 2 for eleven minutes**, and it would work, and nobody in Nacre can turn it into a favour without breaking a rule that costs somebody else money. They do not buy it. Nine hours. Marigold Adjei, seventy-seven, with a chest, gets a bottle, carried by Aurrie Nkemdirim, and nobody writes it down.

## Chapter 0049 — Not a Service

- **POV / location / when:** Jonas. The Weir and the lowland road. **Monday 21 April, morning into afternoon.** (unchanged)
- **Goal:** get the Weir stand back onto a term, or at least get somebody to say out loud what the stand is now that no term is on it.
- **Resistance:** there is nothing to mend. A plate with no term is not a fault; it is a plate. Margo Doré has already decided what the stand is. Perdita Sow is out of hospital with a sling and eleven weeks of anger and she is the person who tells him what five weeks of writing on walls looks like from a road.
- **Major turn:** Margo Doré says she burned a letter from the water authority in March about her own supply and will not tell him what was in it, and asks him to write that down in full. Jonas cannot read the Margin because there is nothing on it. He refuses to act, correctly, and it is the first refusal of the volume.
- **Consequence:** three people clear nine more inches of silt by hand, unpaid, and it is not a repair. Jonas understands, without having the word for it, that there is nobody to ask for anything.
- **Ending hook:** Margo Doré: *say that again in a fortnight in a room where it matters.*
- **Continuity to hold:** trap has eighteen inches out and a foot ten in; bank is in the channel; Royden Achebe holds the unclaimed burden; claim on the stand was known to be false; nothing on that street tells anybody anything.

## Chapter 0050 — Six Days Is Not a Term Any More

- **POV / when:** Jonas. Saint Orra west corridor, then Halvorsen's office. **Monday 21 April 22:00 into Tuesday 22 April morning.**
- **Goal:** get the care term renewed and understand why the corridor has been running on thirty-two-hour terms since Saturday.
- **Resistance:** the renewal is fine and uninteresting. The resistance is a piece of paper: **CivicCore's assessment on the Saint Orra west site chain**, and four nodes on that chain with no continuous term — 6-44 mortuary van gate, staff stair 2, car park barrier 3, service riser 1.
- **Major turn:** the site can find £4,200. Tier 2 on one node is £4,200. Tier 2 on four is £16,800. **Halvorsen asks Jonas which node is worth £4,200 a year and Jonas refuses to answer and tells him who should.**
- **Consequence:** the four nodes are unfunded and deferred from **00:01 on Saturday 26 April**. The first notice prints on the care-entrance plate at the 22:00 handover.
- **Ending hook:** Jonas opens the box on 6-44 and finds the gate is mechanically perfect, and the thing that is wrong with it is a field nobody has ever filled in.

## Chapter 0051 — The Field Nobody Fills In

- **POV / when:** Jonas. Mortuary yard. **Tuesday 22 April into Wednesday 23 April, 07:20.**
- **Goal:** find out what a deferred node serves, and record it before Friday.
- **Resistance:** the answer is a **last verified essential use**, and there is no such record, and Halvorsen cannot write one because he is a site manager and not a person who stands in a mortuary yard at four in the morning. Neither can Jonas, for reasons he states: he is not on the rota, nobody is on the rota, and a technician's record of what a mortuary gate is for is the wrong kind of witness.
- **Major turn:** the notice on 6-44 says what happens with no record, and the answer is *the node's maintenance*, and the maintenance on a mortuary gate is a man with a key.
- **Consequence:** somebody has to stand in that yard, and the hospital is a hospital, and everybody knows it will be the night orderly.
- **Ending hook:** Jonas goes and does something else instead, on a pad, in a shop, while Bea Osunde tells him he does not get to be modest on behalf of a mortuary gate. The registrar's office is asked whether a record made that way can be struck and says the registrar will look at it on the Friday.

## Chapter 0052 — The Appeal That Everybody Expected to Fail

- **POV / when:** Jonas. Cooper's Reach panel room. **Wednesday 23 April, 09:30 to 13:10.**
- **Goal:** Nell Ardent's appeal. It fails. That is known. The chapter is what it fails *on*.
- **Resistance:** the panel upholds the order. The £2,000 bond is payable. Joseph Anum, eighty-one, flat nine since 1991. Ferdy Oduya on the second key. Yasmin Rohan.
- **Major turn:** the building's water is a **supply and not a service**, so no civic claim attaches to it and the panel has nothing to weigh; and **Nell Ardent finds rule 4(c)** — a participation-agreement signature is a consent mark and may not be withdrawn except on the same date next year — in a document she has been carrying since 2 April, and the finding is that the people who signed were not deceived and cannot get their signatures back.
- **Consequence:** a CivicCore continuity field manager attends and puts a written Tier 2 offer on four blocks on the table. **Yasmin Rohan refuses for the third time, in a third room, and this time the sentence is longer, and the growth is that she gives a reason she has not given before.**
- **Ending hook:** the chair's helpful, worse finding: the only route that would win this is a location of record, and the Weir is not in the room and Brine Wharf is not the Weir.

## Chapter 0053 — Nine Seconds on a Delivery Note

- **POV / when:** Jonas. Saint Orra west corridor and the mortuary yard. **Wednesday 23 April 23:40 into Thursday 24 April, 07:20.**
- **Goal:** find out what was written in the mortuary yard at twenty to twelve last night.
- **Resistance:** **Ivo Bamba**, 29, night orderly, on since ten, not on the rota, not a holder. He wrote a last verified essential use on the back of a laundry delivery note in nine seconds because a woman of eighty-four's mortuary van had been in this corridor on 15 April and the box on the wall said forty minutes.
- **Major turn:** the record is **a hollow term**, and Jonas has to explain to a man of twenty-nine, in a corridor, what he has given a hospital's mortuary gate — and cannot take it back, and must not be asked to.
- **Consequence:** the gate is saved. The narrowing does not happen. It is also now the legal basis on which the mortuary gate keeps opening while four small nodes are unfunded, and the first person to make that observation will be a contractor.
- **Ending hook:** Pell's answer, and it is that a hollow term functions and striking it narrows the node to its maintenance.

## Chapter 0054 — The Meeting in the Community Room

- **POV / when:** Jonas. Wren Court, Salter's Yard. **Thursday 24 April, 20:00 to 23:00.**
- **Goal:** see rule 4(c) where it was signed, without being given a list of thirty-one people.
- **Resistance:** Yolanda Fofana's three conditions, and the third is the one that matters — **the fee does not stop.** She shows him the file because she chooses to.
- **Major turn:** **nineteen of thirty-one signed; four lapsed in April; and eleven of the nineteen people sheltered there on 18 April had a lapsed agreement.** And Yolanda Fofana signed her own agreement as steward and her own lapsed on 1 April and the block went to the default and she did not tell the residents why the water went.
- **Consequence:** a fee nobody can stop is the mechanism, and the mechanism is not a lie.
- **Ending hook:** the notice on the meter cupboard. And Jonas realises his own city's hollow term and this one are the same shape.

## Chapter 0055 — The Second Named Clinician

- **POV / when:** Jonas, and Dr. Sanaa Iqbal. Saint Orra west, a corridor, at her initiative. **Saturday 25 April, 09:40.**
- **Goal:** nothing. She has sent a message and he does not know what it is.
- **Resistance:** she is not reconciled, she is not on the card, she does not let him thank her, and she will not be asked again to be a witness as a favour.
- **Major turn:** the second named clinician exists. It exists because the west site administration found a **participation line**, and the fifth refusal reversed for money, and the money is the mechanism of this volume.
- **Consequence:** she gives him the four refusals' reasons because he is the only person who will do work with them, and says so in those words. She tells him she will be in a room in June explaining the clinic's own witness structure. He almost uses the second-clinician information as an argument and stops himself mid-sentence and she lets him.
- **Ending hook:** the fix and the disease are the same money, and there is a participation line in a hospital's budget and a participation line in a housing block, and they are the same line.

## Chapter 0056 — Do Not Come

## Chapter 0057 — Nobody Has Asked Her To

- **POV / when:** Jonas. The Carrow Lane Housing Association. **Monday 28 April, 10:00 to 11:30.**
- **Goal:** find out who put the price on the residents, and whether it can be taken off.
- **Resistance:** **Marguerite Oyelaran**, 47, chief officer, four blocks, 128 rooms, bought Tier 2 in March because the blocks lost lifts in 2011 and 2017. She can defend every line of her arithmetic. She is not Rook's instrument and she has never met him.
- **Major turn:** Jonas's first transfer attempt fails, and it fails on Volume 01's own rule. He drafts the transfer of a lowland point to a named holder; she names herself; she then refuses, because a housing association officer findable at six water points on a street with no address is a document with a name on it, and a plate will not take a bare role. **The draw ends. The job does not.**
- **Consequence:** the notice on the meter room wall, and the fact that nobody in the city can appeal their own bill.
- **Ending hook:** she tells him the six lowland points could be put on a schedule for £1,880 a year each and nobody has ever asked her to. And Jonas, who has spent five weeks going round asking people for things, does not ask.

## Chapter 0058 — Nineteen Days

- **POV / when:** Jonas. Lowland road, the dip, a car park. **Tuesday 29 April 23:40 into Wednesday 30 April 07:40.**
- **Goal:** get water back to a lowland that has none.
- **Resistance:** a commissioning clamp on a joint after the 18 April flood works, a works order closed on paper on 20 April, and a restoration route that is a priced term with nobody on the tier.
- **Major turn:** **Wendell Pryce**, 38, CivicCore continuity field manager, in a car park at twenty to one, offers to put the lowland on Tier 2 in eleven minutes, and says plainly that it is not a favour, it is a purchase, and that nobody in this city can make it a favour without breaking a rule that costs somebody else money. **And that it is the best argument anybody has made against Jonas in five weeks.**
- **Major consequence:** they do not buy it. Nine hours. Marigold Adjei, seventy-seven, with a chest, at the top of some stairs, gets a bottle, carried by Aurrie Nkemdirim, and nobody writes it down. Emeka Rusk shifts the clamp at about ten past three with a bar and a torch.
- **And:** the Weir stand, having no term, narrows to its maintenance — which is the pump that serves the trap — and the trap can no longer be cleared by anybody, ever, and Jonas understands the rule for the first time, six days after Margo Doré told him to say it in a room where it matters.
- **Ending hook:** the notice at the standpipe, and Jonas's discovery that the restoration route is a price and the price is a term and he cannot mend a term whose holder is a schedule.

## Chapter 0059 — The Return of a Deferred Node

- **POV / when:** Jonas. Old Ward porch, then a stairwell. **Wednesday 30 April, 10:40 to 16:20.**
- **Goal:** get one thing into the record before 19 May. He is not allowed to be in the room and is not asked.
- **Resistance:** the pre-sitting runs four hours on the schedule amendment; CivicCore's operations director is in attendance; the city cannot force CivicCore to publish anything because the schedule is not a contract term.
- **Major turn:** **Nell Ardent and Nina Calder, separately and neither deferring to the other, arrive at the same instrument** — the city cannot make the Lattice publish a narrowing, but the city can publish one on its own paper. Pell says so and declines to draft it because he is a registrar and not a clerk. **Adopted 30 April: the Return of a Deferred Node.**
- **Consequence:** the first return comes back the same afternoon. **Eight nodes have a holder. One does not.** It is a lowland water point. The form has no line for it. Nina Calder leaves a blank line at the bottom of the form and says it is not a design feature.
- **And Rook, in a stairwell, twenty minutes, at the end.** He argues the volume's best case, in full, and he is right about the queue and wrong about the claim, and he says so himself. He agrees to publish his own narrowings, because he is a decent operator and visibility is not defeat. And he says the register will be used against Jonas inside two years, and that in June somebody will try to define *holder* and the definition will be drafted by whoever puts a schedule on the table.
- **Ending hooks:** the claims box at the Old Ward board closes **Saturday 18 July**. The Standing Committee sits **Monday 19 May**. The schedule is tabled and not defeated. The form has a hole in it and the hole is the honest part.
