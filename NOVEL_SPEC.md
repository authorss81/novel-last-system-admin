# Novel Specification

Title: The Last System Admin
Repository slug: novel-last-system-admin
Genre: urban fantasy / civic progression
Male lead: A municipal network technician who repairs magical permissions
Length target: 720 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship or no romance, decided in the bible phase.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A modern city gains a magical permissions system, and the technician responsible for repairing it slowly becomes responsible for every broken rule.

## Ending
The city must choose between permanent central control and a distributed civic system that can survive its founder.
